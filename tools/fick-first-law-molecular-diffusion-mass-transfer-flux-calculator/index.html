Adolf Fick 1855 Mass Transport Differential Law

Fick's First Law ($J = -D abla C$) establishes that mass flows down chemical potential gradients in direct analogy to Fourier's law of heat conduction and Ohm's law of electrical current.

Frequently asked questions

What is the physical meaning of the negative sign in Fick's Law?

The negative sign indicates that chemical species naturally diffuse spontaneously from regions of high concentration to regions of low concentration.
