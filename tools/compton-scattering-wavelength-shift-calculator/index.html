Arthur Compton's 1923 Particle Nature of Light Proof

Compton treated X-ray photons as relativistic billiard balls colliding elastically with electrons, proving that light carries discrete relativistic particle momentum (p = h/λ) and earning the 1927 Nobel Prize.
