Origin of Rule of 72

The Rule of 72 provides a quick mental estimation derived from natural logarithms: ln(2) ≈ 0.693. Because 72 has many convenient divisors (2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12), it offers an exceptionally accurate approximation for rates between 4% and 15%.
