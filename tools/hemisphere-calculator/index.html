How to use Hemisphere Calculator

  1. Enter the radius of the hemisphere.
  2. Choose the pi value for your calculation.
  3. Click Process to get CSA, TSA, and volume with formulas shown.

Key Features & Privacy

A hemisphere is half of a sphere. This tool calculates: Curved Surface Area (2πr²), Total Surface Area including the flat circular base (3πr²), and Volume (2/3 πr³). All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use this for the Surface Areas and Volumes chapter and problems involving domes, bowls, and half-spherical objects.
