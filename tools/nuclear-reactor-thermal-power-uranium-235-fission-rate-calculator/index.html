Frequently asked questions

Where does the 200 MeV of fission energy go?

$sim 168 ext{ MeV}$ as fission fragment kinetic heat, $5 ext{ MeV}$ prompt neutrons, $7 ext{ MeV}$ prompt gammas, $13 ext{ MeV}$ delayed beta/gamma decay, and $12 ext{ MeV}$ lost to neutrinos.
