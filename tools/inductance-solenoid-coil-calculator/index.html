N-Squared Inductance Scaling (N²)

Inductance scales quadratically with turns (L ∝ N²): doubling the number of turns quadruples the total inductance for the same physical geometry.

Frequently asked questions

Why do ferrite cores increase inductance?

High magnetic permeability materials (ferrites with μ_r ≈ 100-1000) concentrate magnetic flux lines, multiplying inductance by hundreds of times.
