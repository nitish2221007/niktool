Ludwig Prandtl & Hermann Glauert 1928 Compressibility Rule

As aircraft approach the speed of sound, air density no longer remains constant; air compresses ahead of the airfoil, steepening pressure gradients and amplifying lift and pitching moments.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the Prandtl-Glauert rule fail near Mach 1?

As M approaches 1, the formula approaches infinity; transonic shock waves form, requiring non-linear transonic formulations.
