T⁴ Temperature Fourth-Power Radiation Scaling

Because blackbody power scales with temperature to the fourth power ($L propto T^4$), a hot blue-white star (e.g. 10,000 K) radiates nearly 9 times more energy per square meter than our Sun, dominating the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram.

Frequently asked questions

What is our Sun's luminosity?
