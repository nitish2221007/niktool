Why the Old "Multiply by 7" Rule is Wrong

University of California San Diego (UCSD) genomic researchers discovered that dogs mature extremely rapidly in their first two years (a 1-year-old dog has the molecular DNA age of a 31-year-old human), but cellular aging slows down significantly in later years.

Frequently asked questions

How old is a 4-year-old dog in human years?

Human Age = 16 × ln(4) + 31 = 16 × 1.386 + 31 ≈ 53.2 years old.
