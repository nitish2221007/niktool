Frequently asked questions

Why is JWST placed at Sun-Earth L2 instead of LEO?

Sun-Earth $L_2$ keeps the Sun, Earth, and Moon all in one continuous direction behind the spacecraft's 5-layer tennis-court-sized sunshield, allowing cryo-cooling of infrared sensors to 40 K.
