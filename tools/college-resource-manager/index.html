How to use College Resource Manager

  1. Enter the title and URL of the resource (e.g., a YouTube lecture, a PDF drive link, or a GitHub repo).
  2. Optionally add a subject tag and personal notes to help you find it later.
  3. Click Save Resource to store it securely in your browser's local memory.

Key Features & Privacy

College life involves juggling hundreds of links, from assignment submission portals to reference papers. This manager acts as your personal digital binder. Because it relies entirely on LocalStorage, your academic research and private notes are never exposed to the internet.

Common Use Cases

Engineering and medical students use this to compile syllabus links, store references for final year projects, save important faculty announcement links, and maintain a curated list of tutorial videos for exam preparation.
