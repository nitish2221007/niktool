Frequently asked questions

Why is standard RC servo frequency 50 Hz?

A 50 Hz refresh rate ($20 ext{ ms}$ period) allows ample quiet time after a $1 ext{ to }2 ext{ ms}$ pulse for analog integrator circuits to settle.
