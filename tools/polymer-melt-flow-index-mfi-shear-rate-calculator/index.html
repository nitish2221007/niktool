How to use Polymer Melt Flow Index (MFI / MFR) & Capillary Shear Rate Calculator

  1. Enter Melt Flow Index (MFI / MFR) in grams per 10 minutes (g/10 min).
  2. Enter molten polymer melt density in $g/ ext{cm}^3$ (typically 0.74 to 0.78 $g/ ext{cm}^3$ at 190°C–230°C).
  3. Enter standard test piston load in kg (e.g. 2.16 kg or 5.0 kg per ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133).
  4. Inspect apparent wall shear rate $dotgamma_w$ in $s^{-1}$ and volumetric flow rate Q in $mm^3/ ext{s}$.

ASTM D1238 / ISO 1133 Extrusion Plastometer Standard

MFI is inversely related to molecular weight; high MFI resins (>15 g/10min) have lower molecular weights and viscosity suited for fast injection molding thin-wall packaging, while low MFI resins (<1.0 g/10min) provide high melt strength for blown film extrusion and pipe manufacturing.

Frequently asked questions

Why is MFI measured at 10-minute intervals?

Historical legacy standard: early plastometers required 10 minutes to collect sufficient extrudate mass for accurate physical weighing on laboratory balance scales.
