Frequently asked questions

Why is relativistic correction mandatory in 200 kV electron microscopes?

At 200 kV, electrons travel at 69.5% the speed of sound ($c$), increasing their momentum by 39% ($gamma = 1.391$) and shortening the wavelength from classical 2.74 pm down to relativistic 2.51 pm.
