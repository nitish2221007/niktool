Frequently asked questions

Why do tokamaks elongate the plasma vertically (κ ≈ 1.7)?

Elongating the plasma increases cross-sectional area and allowable plasma current $I_p propto rac{1+kappa^2}{2}$ at the same safety factor $q$, dramatically improving energy confinement.
