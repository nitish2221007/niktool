Enzyme Pharmacological Inhibition Kinetics

Competitive inhibitors (statins, ACE inhibitors) can be overcome by high substrate concentrations ($V_{max}$ unchanged), whereas noncompetitive inhibitors reduce catalytic turnover ($V_{max}$ decreased).

Frequently asked questions

What is the Lineweaver-Burk intersection for competitive inhibition?

Competitive inhibitor lines intersect at the same point on the Y-axis ($1/V_{max}$) with differing X-axis intercepts ($-1/K_m^{ ext{app}}$).
