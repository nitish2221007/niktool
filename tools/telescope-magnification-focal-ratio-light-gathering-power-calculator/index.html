Amateur Astronomy Optical Geometry

Light gathering power scales with the square of aperture ($LGP propto D^2$), allowing large mirrors to reveal faint deep-sky nebulae and galaxies invisible to the human eye.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum useful magnification for a telescope?

Rule of thumb: maximum useful magnification is approximately $2 imes$ the aperture diameter in millimeters ($2 imes D_{ ext{mm}}$).
