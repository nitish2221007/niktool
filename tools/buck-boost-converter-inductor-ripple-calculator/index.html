Inverting Buck-Boost Energy Transfer Mechanism

During the ON time, energy stores exclusively in the magnetic inductor core; during the OFF time, the collapsing magnetic field dumps stored inductive energy into the output filter capacitor with inverted negative voltage polarity.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if L is smaller than L_crit?

The converter enters Discontinuous Conduction Mode (DCM), where inductor current drops to zero before the next switching cycle, altering duty cycle linearity.
