Why Diastole Counts Twice as Much as Systole

Because the resting human cardiac cycle spends approximately 2/3 of its time in diastole (filling) and only 1/3 in systole (pumping), MAP weights diastolic pressure double ($MAP = (2cdot DBP + SBP)/3$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum safe MAP in intensive care units (ICU)?

Surviving Sepsis guidelines recommend maintaining MAP ≥ 65 mmHg to ensure adequate glomerular filtration in kidneys and coronary perfusion.
