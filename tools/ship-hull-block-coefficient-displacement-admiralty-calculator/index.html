Archimedes Principle in Naval Architecture

The block coefficient ($C_b = rac{ abla}{LBT}$) quantifies hull fullness: slow bulk carriers have full block hulls ($C_b approx 0.85$) for maximum cargo capacity, whereas high-speed naval ships use slender forms ($C_b approx 0.50$) to cut wave-making drag.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Displacement and Deadweight (DWT)?

Displacement is the total weight of the ship plus everything aboard; Deadweight (DWT) is the payload cargo carrying capacity.
