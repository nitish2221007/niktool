Archimedes 1:3 Volume Ratio

Discovered by Archimedes in antiquity, the volume of any cone is always exactly one-third (⅓) the volume of a circumscribed cylinder sharing the same base and height.

Frequently asked questions

What is the slant height of a cone with r=5 and h=12?

By the Pythagorean theorem: Slant Height = √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13.0 units.
