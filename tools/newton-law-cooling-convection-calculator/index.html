How to use Newton's Law of Cooling & Convective Heat Transfer Calculator

  1. Enter convective heat transfer coefficient h (Free air = 5-15, Forced air fan = 25-100, Forced water = 500-2000 W/(m²·K)).
  2. Enter total fin surface area A in square meters.
  3. Enter hot surface temperature T_s and cooler ambient fluid temperature T_∞.
  4. Inspect convective heat dissipation in Watts and convective thermal resistance in °C/W.
