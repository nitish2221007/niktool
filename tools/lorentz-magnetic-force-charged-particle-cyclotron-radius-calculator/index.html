Hendrik Lorentz 1895 Magnetic Force Vector Law

Because the magnetic force acts strictly perpendicular to the particle velocity vector ($ ec{F} = q ec{v} imes ec{B}$), it performs zero mechanical work on the particle, bending its path into a pure circle ($r = rac{mv}{qB}$) used in mass spectrometers and CERN synchrotrons.

Frequently asked questions

Why does an electron have a much smaller cyclotron radius than a proton at the same speed?

Because a proton is 1,836 times more massive than an electron ($m_p gg m_e$), giving it much higher momentum and inertia to resist magnetic bending.
