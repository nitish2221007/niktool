Why memorize algorithm complexities?

In interviews, you're often asked to state and justify the time/space complexity of your solution. This cheat sheet helps you build that intuition.

FAQ

Which sorting algorithm should I default to?

Use your language's built-in sort (typically Timsort or Introsort — O(n log n)). For interviews, know Merge Sort and Quick Sort inside-out.

What is the difference between greedy and DP?

Greedy makes the locally optimal choice at each step (no backtracking). DP considers all sub-problems and combines results. DP is more general but slower.
