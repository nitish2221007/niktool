Why is Ellipse Perimeter Complex?

Unlike a circle, an ellipse has no elementary algebraic closed-form formula for its circumference and requires elliptic integrals. Srinivasa Ramanujan discovered extraordinary high-precision approximation formulas with negligible relative error (< 0.001%).
