Conservation of Mass Tie-Line Lever Principle

Uses inverse lever arm proportions to determine the exact relative quantities of coexisting phases in two-phase regions of binary alloy phase diagrams.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the solid fraction calculated using the liquidus arm length?

Mass balance dictates that the phase fraction is proportional to the OPPOSITE lever arm distance.
