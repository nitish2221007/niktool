Permutation vs Combination

A Permutation is an arrangement of items where sequence/order is significant (e.g. locker lock combinations, race positions). A Combination is a selection of items where order does not matter (e.g. lottery numbers, card hands).

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula for nCr?

nCr = n! / (r! × (n - r)!)

What is the formula for nPr?

nPr = n! / (n - r)!
