Lorentz Force Helical Gyromotion

Charged particles are trapped by magnetic fields into tight circular orbits around magnetic field lines; keeping the ion Larmor radius much smaller than the vessel radius is the fundamental principle of magnetic plasma confinement.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the electron Larmor radius so much smaller than an ion's?

Because electrons have 3,600× less mass than deuterons, their Larmor radius is 60× smaller.
