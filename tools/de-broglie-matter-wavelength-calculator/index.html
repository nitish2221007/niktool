Louis de Broglie's 1924 Wave-Particle Duality Hypothesis

De Broglie proposed that all moving matter possesses wave properties; an electron moving at 10⁶ m/s has a wavelength comparable to atomic lattice spacings (0.7 nm), enabling Transmission Electron Microscopes (TEM) to resolve individual atoms.

Frequently asked questions

Why do everyday macroscopic objects not exhibit visible wave diffraction?

Because macroscopic objects have huge mass m, their de Broglie wavelength is infinitesimal (~10⁻³⁴ meters), making quantum wave diffraction imperceptible.
