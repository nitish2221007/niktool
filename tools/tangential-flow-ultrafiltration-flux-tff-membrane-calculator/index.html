Tangential Crossflow Ultrafiltration & Diafiltration

Unlike dead-end filters that rapidly clog with cake fouling, TFF sweeps fluid parallel to the membrane surface at high crossflow velocity ($Delta P_{ ext{channel}}$), creating hydrodynamic shear that prevents gel-layer polarization and concentrates fragile therapeutic monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) to $>150 ext{ g/L}$.

Frequently asked questions

What is the definition of TMP in Tangential Flow Filtration?

$ ext{TMP} = rac{P_{ ext{feed}} + P_{ ext{retentate}}}{2} - P_{ ext{permeate}}$ represents the average net hydraulic pressure gradient driving solvent through the membrane pores.
