Airbreathing Momentum Thrust Equation

Net thrust equals rate of momentum change ($F_n = dot{m}(v_e - v_0)$); high-bypass turbofans accelerate huge masses of air moderately rather than small masses violently, dramatically improving propulsive efficiency and cutting TSFC.

Frequently asked questions

Why do turbofans have lower TSFC than pure turbojets?

High-bypass turbofans match exhaust speed closer to flight speed ($v_e approx v_0$), minimizing wasted kinetic energy in the jet wake.
