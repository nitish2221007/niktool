Klaus von Klitzing 1980 Nobel Integer Quantum Hall Effect

At low temperatures and high magnetic fields, Landau levels create perfectly flat transverse Hall resistance plateaus ($R_H = h / u e^2$) invariant to sample impurities, establishing the international SI metrological standard for electrical resistance based on the exact von Klitzing constant ($R_K approx 25,812.807 Omega$).

Frequently asked questions

What happens to longitudinal resistance Rxx during a Quantum Hall plateau?

During a Hall plateau ($R_{xy} = R_H$), the longitudinal resistance drops to exactly zero ($R_{xx} = 0$), indicating dissipationless chiral edge state transport.
