High Specific Heat of Water

Water possesses an unusually high specific heat capacity (4,184 J/kg·°C), making it an extraordinary thermal coolant in automotive radiators and planetary climate moderator.

Frequently asked questions

How much energy to boil 1 kg of water from 20°C to 100°C?

Q = 1 kg × 4,184 J/kg·°C × 80°C = 334.72 kJ (~0.093 kWh).
