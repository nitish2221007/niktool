How to use Logarithmic Decrement Structural Damping Ratio (δ & ζ) Calculator

  1. Enter initial displacement vibration peak amplitude $x_0$ in mm from an accelerometer or LVDT sensor free-decay record.
  2. Enter damped peak amplitude $x_n$ measured n cycles later in mm.
  3. Enter integer cycle count n elapsed between peaks.
  4. Inspect logarithmic decrement $delta$ and exact modal viscous damping ratio $zeta$ in %.

Experimental System Identification Damping Test

Measuring free vibration decay ring-down traces provides the definitive experimental validation of structural damping ($zeta$) for bridges, wind turbines, and aerospace structures.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

Why is multiple cycles (n > 1) used instead of just one cycle?

Measuring over 5 to 10 cycles averages out measurement noise and sensor digitizer discretization errors.
