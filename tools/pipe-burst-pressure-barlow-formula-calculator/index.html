Peter Barlow's 1836 Thin-Walled Cylinder Law

Barlow's formula relates internal hydrostatic pressure to circumferential hoop stress (P = 2·S·t/D), establishing the worldwide code standard for pressurized oil, gas, and steam piping design.

Frequently asked questions

What is Barlow's formula for pipe burst pressure?

P = (2 · S · t) / D, where S is yield strength, t is wall thickness, and D is outside diameter.
