Frequently asked questions

Why is pull force always lower than push force in double-acting cylinders?

The steel piston rod occupies cross-sectional area in the rod chamber, reducing effective pressure area ($A_{ ext{pull}} = rac{pi}{4}(D^2 - d^2)$).
