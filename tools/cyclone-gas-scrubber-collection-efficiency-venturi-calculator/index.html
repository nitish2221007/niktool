Frequently asked questions

What is the trade-off of high collection efficiency in Venturi scrubbers?

Capturing sub-micron particles requires extreme gas velocities ($v_t > 90 ext{ m/s}$), demanding huge fan electrical power to overcome high pressure drops ($Delta P > 30 ext{ in. w.g.}$).
