Joseph Fourier's 1822 Analytical Theory of Heat

Frequently asked questions

What is thermal resistance R_th?

R_th = ΔT / q = L / (k·A), representing a component's resistance to heat flow analogous to Ohm's electrical resistance (R = V/I).
