Frequently asked questions

What determines the theoretical limit of c*?

$c^* = rac{sqrt{gamma R T_c}}{Gamma(gamma)}$ depends purely on high chamber flame temperature ($T_c$) and low product molecular weight (R), making LOX/Liquid Hydrogen ($c^* approx 2,400 ext{ m/s}$) superior to LOX/Kerosene ($c^* approx 1,790 ext{ m/s}$).
