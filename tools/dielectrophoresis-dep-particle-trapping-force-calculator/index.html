How to use Dielectrophoresis (DEP) Cell Sorting & Trapping Force Calculator

  1. Enter biological cell spherical radius r in micrometers ($mu ext{m}$) (e.g. 7.5 $mu ext{m}$ for Circulating Tumor Cells CTCs, 3.5 $mu ext{m}$ for red blood cells).
  2. Enter real part of the Clausius-Mossotti polarizability factor $ ext{Re}[K(omega)]$ (-0.5 to +1.0).
  3. Enter electric field gradient $ abla|E|^2$ in $V^2/m^3$ generated across interdigitated microelectrodes.
  4. Inspect Dielectrophoretic force in piconewtons (pN) and determine if cells experience positive trapping (pDEP) or negative repulsion (nDEP).

Frequently asked questions

What is the DEP Crossover Frequency?

The crossover frequency is the AC electric field frequency where $ ext{Re}[K(omega)] = 0$; below this frequency cells experience nDEP, while above it they switch to pDEP.
