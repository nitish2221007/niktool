Specific Fuel Consumption Part-Load Curves

Internal combustion diesel engines operate at peak thermodynamic efficiency when loaded above 60-75% rated power; running generators at ultra-light loads (<30%) causes incomplete fuel combustion and wet-stacking carbon buildup.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.
