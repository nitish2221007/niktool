How to use Microbial Colony Forming Units (CFU / mL = Colonies · Dilution / Volume) Calculator

  1. Enter number of visible colony forming units counted on agar plate.
  2. Enter volume of diluted culture inoculated onto the plate in mL (e.g. 0.1 mL for $100 mu ext{L}$).
  3. Enter cumulative serial dilution factor (e.g. $10^5$ for a $10^{-5}$ dilution tube).
  4. Inspect calculated stock bacterial titer concentration in CFU/mL.

Standard Plate Count (SPC) Microbiological Method

Serial dilutions ensure accurate colony counting in the statistically valid 30–300 colony range, quantifying viable living organisms in food safety, water testing, and clinical microbiology.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why are plates with fewer than 30 colonies considered TFTC?

Random Poisson distribution sampling errors become unacceptably high when counting fewer than 30 colonies.
