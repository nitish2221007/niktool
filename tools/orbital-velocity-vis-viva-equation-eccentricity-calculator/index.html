Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between circular velocity and escape velocity?

At any radial distance r, escape velocity is exactly $sqrt{2} approx 1.414$ times the circular orbital velocity ($v_{ ext{esc}} = sqrt{2} , v_{ ext{circ}}$).
