Claude Shannon 1948 Mathematical Theory of Communication

Shannon entropy measures the fundamental lower bound on lossless data compression; according to Shannon's Source Coding Theorem, no compression algorithm (like ZIP, JPEG, or MP3) can compress a message stream to fewer than $H(X)$ bits per symbol without losing information.

What probability distribution maximizes entropy?

The uniform distribution ($p_i = 1/N$) maximizes entropy ($H = log_2 N$), representing maximum unpredictability and total lack of bias.
