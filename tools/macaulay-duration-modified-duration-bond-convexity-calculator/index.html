Frederick Macaulay 1938 Interest Rate Risk Metric

Duration measures the effective price sensitivity of a bond to interest rate fluctuations ($ rac{Delta P}{P} approx -D_{ ext{mod}} Delta y$); matching asset and liability durations is the cornerstone of pension fund immunization.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Macaulay duration shorter than the maturity for coupon bonds?

Because intermediate coupon cash flows returned before maturity shorten the weighted average time until investment capital is recovered.
