Aerodynamic Stall Flight Envelope Boundary

Exceeding the critical angle of attack ($alpha_{ ext{crit}} approx 14^circ ext{–}18^circ$) causes flow separation and loss of lift; during steep $60^circ$ turns, the load factor doubles ($n = 2.0 ext{g}$), increasing stall speed by $41.4%$ ($v_s propto sqrt{n}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why do deployment of wing flaps reduce stall speed?

Flaps increase effective wing camber and area, raising $C_{L,max}$ from $sim 1.6$ to $sim 2.4$, which lowers touchdown stall speed significantly.
