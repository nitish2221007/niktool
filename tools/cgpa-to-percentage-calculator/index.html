How to use CGPA to Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter your CGPA value.
  2. Select a conversion method, or choose custom and enter your own multiplier.
  3. Click Process to see the percentage and the exact formula used.

Key Features & Privacy

Different boards and institutions convert CGPA to percentage using different multipliers, which is a common source of confusion. This tool lets you choose the rule rather than assuming one universal formula, and it clearly reminds you to verify with your institution. All processing stays in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it to convert their CGPA into a percentage for forms and applications, and to compare results under different conversion rules.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Enter your CGPA, select a conversion method, and click Process to see the percentage and the formula used.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Which multiplier should I use?

It depends on your board or institution. CBSE has commonly used 9.5, but always verify the official rule that applies to you.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
