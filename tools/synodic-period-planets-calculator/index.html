Mars Launch Windows Every 26 Months

Because Earth orbits faster than Mars, Earth "laps" Mars once every 780 days (~26 months); interplanetary spacecraft must launch during this exact planetary alignment window to intercept Mars with minimal fuel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the synodic period between Earth and Mars?

S = (365.25 × 686.98) / (686.98 - 365.25) ≈ 779.9 days (~2.14 years).
