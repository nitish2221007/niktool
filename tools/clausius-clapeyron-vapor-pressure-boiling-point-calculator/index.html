Rudolf Clausius & Benoît Paul Émile Clapeyron 1834 Equation

The Clausius-Clapeyron equation describes exponential growth of saturated vapor pressure with temperature ($ rac{d ln P}{dT} = rac{Delta H_{ ext{vap}}}{RT^2}$), forming the engineering foundation of vacuum rotary evaporation and steam turbine power plants.

Frequently asked questions

Why does water boil at lower temperatures at high altitudes?

Atmospheric pressure is lower at high altitudes; boiling occurs when saturated vapor pressure equals ambient atmospheric pressure.
