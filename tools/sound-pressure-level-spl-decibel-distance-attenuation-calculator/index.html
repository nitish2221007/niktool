Acoustic Inverse Square Spherical Divergence Law

Sound pressure decays by exactly $6.02 ext{ dB}$ for every doubling of distance in a spherical free field ($ ext{SPL} propto 1/r$), defining plant perimeter setback distances.

Frequently asked questions

What is the acoustic reference sound pressure p0 in air?

$p_0 = 20 mu ext{Pa}$ ($2 imes 10^{-5} ext{ Pa}$), representing the absolute human threshold of hearing at 1000 Hz.
