Marion M. Bradford 1976 Coomassie Dye Binding Assay

Binding of Coomassie Brilliant Blue G-250 to basic and aromatic amino acid residues shifts the dye absorbance maximum from red ($465 ext{ nm}$) to blue ($595 ext{ nm}$), providing a rapid, sensitive colorimetric protein quantitation assay.

Frequently asked questions

Why is absorbance measured specifically at 595 nm?

595 nm is the absorption peak of the anionic blue form of Coomassie dye bound to protein complexes.
