Why Speed of Sound Decreases with Altitude

Speed of sound depends directly on absolute air temperature (c ∝ √T). At 10,000 meters altitude where air temperature is -50 °C, the speed of sound drops from 1,235 km/h at sea level down to ~1,078 km/h.

Frequently asked questions

What Mach number defines hypersonic flight?

Speeds of Mach 5.0 and above (over 6,000 km/h) are classified as hypersonic.
