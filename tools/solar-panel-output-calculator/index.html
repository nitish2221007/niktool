How to use Solar Panel Output & kWh Calculator

  1. Enter the rated capacity of your solar panel array in Watts (e.g. 10 panels of 400W = 4,000 Watts).
  2. Enter your region's average daily Peak Sun Hours (typically 4.0 - 5.5 hours).
  3. Click Calculate to inspect estimated daily, monthly, and annual clean kWh electricity output.

Solar Derate Factor Considerations

Real-world solar systems experience ~20% energy loss due to inverter conversion losses, DC-AC wiring resistance, temperature coefficients, and atmospheric dust accumulation.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 Peak Sun Hour?

One peak sun hour equals 1,000 Watts per square meter of solar irradiance for 1 hour.
