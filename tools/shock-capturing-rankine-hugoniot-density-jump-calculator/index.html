Frequently asked questions

What is the asymptotic maximum density jump limit for air (γ=1.4)?

As Mach approaches infinity ($M_1 o infty$), the maximum theoretical density ratio for air reaches an asymptotic limit of $ rac{gamma+1}{gamma-1} = rac{2.4}{0.4} = 6.0 imes$.
