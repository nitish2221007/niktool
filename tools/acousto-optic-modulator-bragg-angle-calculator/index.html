Travelling Acoustic Wave Phase Grating

Frequently asked questions

What is the frequency of the diffracted laser beam?

The 1st-order diffracted beam undergoes a Doppler frequency shift equal to the RF drive frequency ($f_{ ext{laser}} pm f_{ ext{RF}}$).
