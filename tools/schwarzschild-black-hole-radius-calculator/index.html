General Relativity and Karl Schwarzschild

Karl Schwarzschild solved Einstein's field equations in 1916 while serving on the Russian front in World War I, discovering that any mass compressed inside r_s = 2GM/c² collapses inevitably into a gravitational singularity.

Frequently asked questions

What size would Earth be as a black hole?

To become a black hole, Earth's entire mass would have to be squeezed into a sphere roughly the size of a marble (radius ~8.87 mm).
