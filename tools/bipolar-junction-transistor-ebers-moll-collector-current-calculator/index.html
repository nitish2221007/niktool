Frequently asked questions

Why is BJT transconductance higher than MOSFET transconductance?

BJTs follow an exponential $I$-$V$ characteristic ($e^{V_{ ext{BE}}/V_t}$) whereas MOSFETs follow a quadratic square law ($(V_{ ext{GS}} - V_{ ext{th}})^2$), giving BJTs much higher gain per unit current.
