Merrell Fenske 1932 Shortcut Distillation Column Sizing

The Fenske equation sets the absolute lower bound on distillation tower height ($N_{min}$); chemical plant designers use the Fenske-Underwood-Gilliland shortcut method to optimize the economic trade-off between capital column height cost vs operating reboiler steam reflux costs.

Frequently asked questions

What happens as relative volatility (α) approaches 1.0?

When $alpha o 1.0$ (close-boiling mixtures or azeotropes), the required stages $N_{min} o infty$, making simple distillation impossible without extractive solvents or entrainers.
