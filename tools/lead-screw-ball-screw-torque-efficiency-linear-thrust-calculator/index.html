  2. Enter screw nominal diameter d in mm (e.g. 16 mm for SFU1605).

  4. Select screw mechanical drive efficiency $eta$ (Ball screw $approx 90%$, ACME bronze $approx 45%$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between lead and pitch in multi-start screws?

Pitch is the axial distance between adjacent threads; Lead is the linear travel per revolution ($Lead = Pitch imes Number of Starts$).
