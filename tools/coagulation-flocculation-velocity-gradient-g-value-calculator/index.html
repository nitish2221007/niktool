Thomas R. Camp 1943 Velocity Gradient Standard

Balances particle collision frequency ($G$) against hydraulic shear stress to achieve microsecond coagulant chemical dispersion in rapid mix tanks ($G > 700 ext{ s}^{-1}$) and gentle floc growth in flocculators ($G approx 30 ext{ s}^{-1}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why must G be decreased in tapered flocculation basins?

As floc aggregates grow larger, their structural shear strength weakens; decreasing G prevents hydrodynamic shear tearing of flocs.
