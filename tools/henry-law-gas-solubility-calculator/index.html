William Henry's 1803 Law and Scuba "Bends"

At high underwater pressure, nitrogen gas dissolves rapidly into diver blood and lipid tissue; ascending too quickly without decompression stops causes nitrogen to bubble out of solution like soda fizz (Decompression Sickness).

Frequently asked questions

Why do opened soda cans fizz?

Opening the can releases the 3 atm CO₂ pressure down to atmospheric 0.0004 atm, drastically reducing CO₂ solubility and causing bubbles to escape.
