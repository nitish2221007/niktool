George Gamow & Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin (WKB) Tunneling

Quantum wavefunctions do not stop abruptly at energy barriers but decay exponentially into the classically forbidden barrier ($e^{-kappa x}$); in Scanning Tunneling Microscopes (STM), measuring sub-angstrom tunneling current variations ($I propto e^{-2kappa d}$) maps individual atoms on conductive surfaces.
