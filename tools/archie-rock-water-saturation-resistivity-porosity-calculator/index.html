How to use Archie's Law Well Logging Water Saturation (S_w = (a·R_w / (Φᵐ·R_t))^(1/n)) Calculator

  1. Enter reservoir formation fractional porosity $Phi$ (e.g. 0.22 for $22%$).
  2. Enter true deep formation resistivity $R_t$ from deep induction/lateral log in $Omegacdot ext{m}$.
  3. Enter formation connate water resistivity $R_w$ in $Omegacdot ext{m}$.
  4. Enter cementation exponent m (typically 2.0 for sandstone, 2.1 for carbonates) and saturation exponent n (typically 2.0).
  5. Inspect Water Saturation $S_w$ and net Hydrocarbon Saturation $S_{hc} = 1 - S_w$.

Gus Archie 1942 Petrophysical Law

Hydrocarbons are non-conductive electrical insulators ($R_t gg R_o$); Archie's equation calculates commercial oil and gas volume in place from wireline resistivity logging.
