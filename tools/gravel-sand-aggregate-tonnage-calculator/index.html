How to use Gravel, Sand & Aggregate Tonnage Calculator

  1. Select landscape aggregate material (Gravel, Crushed Stone, Sand, Topsoil, or Mulch).
  2. Enter driveway or garden bed length and width in feet, and aggregate depth in inches.
  3. Inspect required material volume in cubic yards and delivery order weight in US tons and metric tonnes.

Material Density Ordering Accuracy

Landscape suppliers sell bulk material by the ton or cubic yard; crushed gravel typically weighs ~2,800 lbs per cubic yard (~1.4 US tons/cu yd).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How many tons of gravel are needed for a 30 ft × 10 ft driveway with 3" depth?

Volume is ~2.78 cubic yards; at 1.4 tons/cu yd, you will need approximately 3.89 US Tons of crushed gravel.
