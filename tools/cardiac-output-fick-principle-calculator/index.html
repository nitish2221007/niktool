How to use Cardiac Output (Fick Principle) Hemodynamic Calculator

  1. Enter metabolic oxygen consumption rate $VO_2$ in mL/min (or estimated 125 mL/min/m² × BSA).
  2. Enter arterial oxygen content $C_a$ in mL O₂ / dL blood.
  3. Enter mixed venous oxygen content $C_v$ sampled from pulmonary artery catheter in mL/dL.
  4. Enter patient body surface area (BSA) in m².
  5. Inspect Cardiac Output in L/min and Cardiac Index ($L/min/m^2$).

Adolf Fick's 1870 Mass Conservation Law

The direct Fick method remains the definitive clinical reference gold standard for measuring human heart pumping capacity by tracking whole-body oxygen consumption against arteriovenous blood extraction.

Frequently asked questions

What is normal resting cardiac output?

Normal adult resting cardiac output is 4.0 to 8.0 L/min (Cardiac Index 2.5 to 4.0 L/min/m²).
