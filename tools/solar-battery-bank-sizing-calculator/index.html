How to use Off-Grid Solar Battery Bank Capacity Calculator

  1. Enter total daily electrical consumption in Watt-Hours (Wh) (sum of all lights, fridge, laptop, appliances).
  2. Enter desired days of autonomy (backup cloudy days buffer).
  3. Select system DC voltage (12V, 24V, 48V) and battery chemistry DoD.
  4. Inspect required battery capacity in Amp-Hours (Ah) and storage in kWh.

Why 48V Systems Reduce Cable Losses

At 48 Volts, current is 4x lower than a 12V system for the exact same power (P = V·I), dramatically reducing I²R cable heating losses and allowing much thinner copper wiring.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Depth of Discharge (DoD) critical?

Discharging Lead-Acid batteries past 50% severely shortens their lifespan; LiFePO4 lithium batteries safely deliver 80-90% usable depth of discharge without degrading cycle life.
