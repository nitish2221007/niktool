The 1239.84 nm·eV Optoelectronic Conversion Constant

Because photon energy equals $E = hc/\lambda = 1239.84/\lambda\text{ eV}$, longer wavelength photons carry less energy per photon; for fixed quantum efficiency $\eta$, responsivity increases linearly with wavelength up to the semiconductor bandgap cutoff ($R = \eta\lambda / 1239.8$).
