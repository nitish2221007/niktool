How to use Boiling Point Elevation Calculator

  1. Enter the ebullioscopic constant (Kb) for the solvent (Water Kb = 0.512 °C·kg/mol).
  2. Enter solute molality and Van 't Hoff factor.
  3. Inspect the elevated boiling point temperature.

Vapor Pressure Lowering Principle

Non-volatile solute molecules occupy surface space at the liquid-gas interface, reducing solvent vapor pressure and requiring higher thermal kinetic energy to match atmospheric pressure.

Frequently asked questions

Does adding salt to cooking water boil pasta faster?

The minor boiling point elevation (typically less than 0.5 °C for standard culinary salt amounts) is negligible for cooking time.
