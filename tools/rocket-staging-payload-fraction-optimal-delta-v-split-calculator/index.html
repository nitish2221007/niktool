  2. Enter Stage 1 booster average Specific Impulse in seconds (sea level).

  5. Inspect overall vehicle payload mass fraction $lambda_{ ext{pl}}$ and optimal $Delta v$ velocity split per stage.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Single-Stage-To-Orbit (SSTO) so difficult on Earth?

Earth's deep gravity well requires $9.4 ext{ km/s}$; with structural fractions $epsilon approx 8%$, single-stage payload fraction is virtually zero ($< 0.5%$).
