Frequently asked questions

What is Oil Formation Volume Factor (B_o)?

B_o measures reservoir barrels of oil with dissolved solution gas required to produce exactly one standard Stock Tank Barrel of stabilized oil at surface (typically 1.1 to 1.5 RB/STB).
