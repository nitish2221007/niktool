IEEE 519-2022 Harmonic Control Standards

Excess harmonic distortion causes severe overheating in distribution transformers, false tripping of circuit breakers, and acoustic whining in induction motors; utility interconnection standards strictly cap voltage THD below 5.0%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the THD of a modified sine wave inverter?

Modified sine wave inverters have an extremely high THD of 25% to 45%, which can damage sensitive electronics and motors.
