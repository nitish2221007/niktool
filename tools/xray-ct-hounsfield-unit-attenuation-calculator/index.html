Sir Godfrey Hounsfield 1972 CT Numbers

The Hounsfield scale normalizes raw scanner attenuation relative to distilled water ($0 ext{ HU}$) and vacuum air ($-1000 ext{ HU}$), providing a universally standardized quantitative scale for detecting internal brain hemorrhages ($+80 ext{ HU}$) and soft tissue tumors.

Frequently asked questions

Why is acute hematoma clotted blood hyperdense (+80 HU)?

As a blood clot retracts, globin protein and iron hemoglobin concentrate, increasing physical density and photoelectric X-ray absorption compared to circulating blood (+40 HU).
