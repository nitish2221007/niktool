How to use Aircraft Takeoff Ground Roll Distance (S_G) & Takeoff Run Calculator

  1. Enter aircraft Takeoff Wing Loading $W/S$ in $ ext{kg/m}^2$ (e.g. 50–100 $ ext{kg/m}^2$ general aviation, 400–650 $ ext{kg/m}^2$ commercial jet airliner).
  2. Enter Takeoff Thrust-to-Weight ratio $T/W$ (typically 0.25 to 0.35).
  3. Enter maximum takeoff flap configuration lift coefficient $C_{L,max}$ (1.8 to 2.6).
  4. Enter runway ambient air density $ ho$ in $ ext{kg/m}^3$ (accounting for airport elevation and temperature).
  5. Inspect takeoff ground roll distance $S_G$ in meters/feet and liftoff speed $V_{ ext{LOF}}$ in knots.

Frequently asked questions

Why do high-altitude airports require much longer runways?

Low air density $ ho$ raises liftoff speed ($V_{ ext{LOF}} propto 1/sqrt{ ho}$) and reduces jet engine thrust $T$, quadratically increasing takeoff roll distance ($S_G propto 1/ ho^2$).
