How to use Sound Decibel Distance Calculator

  1. Enter the known sound pressure level (dB) and the reference measurement distance (d₁).
  2. Enter the target listener distance (d₂).
  3. Click Calculate to inspect the expected decibels based on the inverse square law.

The 6 dB Drop Rule of Thumb

In an open free acoustic field, sound pressure level drops by exactly 6 dB every time the distance from a point source is doubled (L₂ = L₁ - 20 log₁₀(d₂/d₁)).

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

What sound level is considered harmful to human hearing?

Sustained exposure to sound levels above 85 dB can cause permanent noise-induced hearing loss.
