Thomas K. Sherwood 1937 Mass Transfer Analogy

Represents the ratio of convective mass transfer to diffusive mass transport rate ($Sh = rac{k_c D}{D_{AB}}$), serving as the exact mass-transfer analog to the heat-transfer Nusselt number.

What is the Chilton-Colburn j-factor analogy?

It equates the dimensionless heat transfer factor $j_H = St_H Pr^{2/3}$ with the mass transfer factor $j_D = St_M Sc^{2/3} = f/2$.
