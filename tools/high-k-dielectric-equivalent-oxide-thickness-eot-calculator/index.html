Frequently asked questions

Why is EOT used instead of physical thickness in CMOS design?

EOT normalizes gate capacitance per unit area ($C_{ ext{ox}} = kappa_{ ext{SiO}_2} epsilon_0 / ext{EOT}$), allowing circuit designers to compute transconductance and drive currents independently of the specific dielectric chemistry.
