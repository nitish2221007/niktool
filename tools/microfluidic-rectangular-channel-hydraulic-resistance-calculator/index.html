Fluidic Ohm's Law Analogy in Microfluidics

At low Reynolds numbers ($Re ll 1$), microchannel flow is strictly linear and Stokes-governed; hydraulic resistance ($R_h$) acts exactly like electrical resistance ($Delta P = Q cdot R_h$), enabling engineers to design complex passive fluidic splitters and resistor networks.
