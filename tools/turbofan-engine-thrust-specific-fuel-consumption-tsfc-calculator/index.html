Gas Turbine Propulsive Efficiency Standard

Increasing bypass ratio accelerates a larger mass of air at lower velocity ($F = dot{m} Delta v$), maximizing propulsive efficiency ($eta_p = rac{2}{1 + v_j/v}$) to slash airline fuel burn.

Frequently asked questions

Why do fighter jets use low bypass turbofans (BPR < 1.0)?

Fighter jets prioritize high exhaust jet velocity ($v_j$) and compact frontal cross-sectional area to achieve supersonic speed with minimum supersonic wave drag.
