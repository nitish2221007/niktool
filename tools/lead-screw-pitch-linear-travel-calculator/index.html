How to use CNC Lead Screw Pitch & Steps per mm Calibration Calculator

  1. Enter lead screw lead in millimeters (linear distance traveled per 1 full rotation; T8x8 = 8mm lead, T8x4 = 4mm, T8x2 = 2mm).
  2. Select motor full steps (200 steps standard) and driver microstepping (1/16).
  3. Inspect calibrated Steps/mm parameter to enter into Marlin, Klipper, or GRBL CNC firmware configuration.

Pitch vs Lead Multi-Start Thread Distinction

Lead equals pitch multiplied by the number of thread starts (Lead = Pitch × Starts); a standard 4-start T8 screw with 2mm pitch moves exactly 8.0 mm linear distance per revolution.
