Frequently asked questions

Why does U-238 have a 4.5-billion-year half-life while Polonium-212 has 0.3 microseconds?

Uranium-238 emits 4.2 MeV alphas (thick barrier), while Po-212 emits 8.8 MeV alphas (narrow barrier, tunneling faster).
