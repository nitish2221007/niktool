IEEE 802 MAC Address Structure

A 48-bit MAC address consists of a 24-bit Organizationally Unique Identifier (OUI) assigned to network card manufacturers (e.g. Intel, Apple, Cisco) and a 24-bit Network Interface Controller (NIC) serial identifier.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Broadcast MAC Address?

FF:FF:FF:FF:FF:FF sends packets to all connected devices on the local Layer 2 broadcast domain.
