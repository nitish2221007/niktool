General Relativity in Everyday GPS

Clocks deeper in a gravitational well tick slower. GPS satellites at 20,200 km experience weaker Earth gravity, causing their atomic clocks to gain +45.9 microseconds per day relative to Earth's surface.

Frequently asked questions

Why did 1 hour equal 7 years on Miller's Planet in Interstellar?

Miller's planet orbited exceptionally close to the event horizon of the supermassive black hole Gargantua, producing an extreme gravitational time dilation factor of ~61,320.
