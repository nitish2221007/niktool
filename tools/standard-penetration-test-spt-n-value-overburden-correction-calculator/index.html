How to use Standard Penetration Test (SPT N₆₀ & (N₁)₆₀ Overburden Correction) Calculator

  1. Enter raw measured field SPT N blow count (blows per 300 mm / 1 ft penetration).
  2. Enter hammer kinetic energy delivery efficiency percentage $E_m$ (typically $60%$ for donut hammer, $75% ext{ to }80%$ for safety/automatic hammer).
  3. Enter effective vertical overburden pressure $sigma^prime_v$ in kPa.
  4. Inspect standardized $N_{60}$ and overburden-normalized $(N_1)_{60}$ values.

ASTM D1586 Standard Penetration Test Normalization

Standardizes borehole blow counts to a baseline $60%$ hammer energy and $100 ext{ kPa}$ (1 atm) overburden stress, providing universal correlations for soil liquefaction triggering and pile bearing capacity.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is overburden correction C_N required?

Higher confining pressure at depth increases blow counts even if soil density is identical; $C_N$ normalizes all depths to $100 ext{ kPa}$ equivalent.
