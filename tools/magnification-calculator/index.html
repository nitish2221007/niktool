How to use Magnification Calculator

  1. Choose what you want to find: Magnification from heights, Magnification from distances, Image Height, or Object Height.
  2. Enter the two known values as prompted by the placeholders.
  3. Click Process to see the result along with the nature of the image (real/virtual, erect/inverted).

Key Features & Privacy

This tool calculates magnification (m) and explains what the sign and magnitude of m mean for the resulting image. It helps students connect the mathematical result to the physical properties of the image formed by lenses and mirrors. All calculations run locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use this to verify ray optics numericals involving image sizes and magnification factors.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
