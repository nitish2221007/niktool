Dielectric Relaxation & Molecular Dipole Friction

At high frequencies, molecular polar dipoles cannot rotate fast enough to follow the alternating electric field ($epsilon = epsilon' - jepsilon''$), dissipating RF electrical energy as internal thermal Joule heat ($ andelta = epsilon''/epsilon'$); switching from lossy FR-4 to PTFE Teflon prevents high-power RF transmitters from catching fire.

Frequently asked questions

Why is tan δ also called Dissipation Factor (DF)?

Dissipation Factor is defined as the ratio of active real resistive power loss to reactive stored energy ($DF = ext{ESR} / X_C = andelta$).
