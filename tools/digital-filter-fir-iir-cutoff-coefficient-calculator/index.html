Frequently asked questions

What is a windowed sinc FIR filter?

A windowed sinc filter truncates an ideal infinite sinc impulse response using a window function (like Hamming or Blackman) to prevent Gibbs oscillations.

Why must filter taps N be odd?

Odd length symmetric FIR filters produce Type-I linear phase filters with exact integer group delay.

What is the cutoff attenuation of a Hamming window?

A Hamming windowed FIR filter provides approximately -53 dB of stopband attenuation.
