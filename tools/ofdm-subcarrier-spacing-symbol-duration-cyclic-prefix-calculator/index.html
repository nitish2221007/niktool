Frequently asked questions

What is the purpose of Cyclic Prefix (CP) in OFDM?

CP copies the end of an OFDM symbol to its beginning to eliminate Intersymbol Interference (ISI) caused by multipath delay spread.

How does 5G NR flexible subcarrier spacing work?

5G scales subcarrier spacing as 15 kHz * 2^μ (15, 30, 60, 120, 240 kHz) to handle millimeter wave and low-latency applications.

What is subcarrier orthogonality?

Peak of each subcarrier occurs exactly at zero-crossings of all other subcarriers, preventing crosstalk without guard bands.
