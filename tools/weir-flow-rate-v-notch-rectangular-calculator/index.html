James Thomson's 1858 Triangular Weir Standard

V-notch weirs provide highly accurate open-channel flow measurements across wide dynamic ranges because the triangular geometry contracts naturally at low flows, preserving high measurement sensitivity.

Frequently asked questions

Why is V-notch discharge proportional to H^(5/2)?

Flow area scales with H² while velocity scales with √H (H^(1/2)); multiplying area by velocity yields Q ∝ H^(5/2).
