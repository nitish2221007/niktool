Yoshihisa Okumura 1968 & Masaharu Hata 1980 Empirical Model

The Okumura-Hata model fits extensive Tokyo field measurements into closed-form equations, accounting for multi-path building clutter reflections and diffraction over urban rooftops.

Frequently asked questions

Why is path loss significantly lower in rural areas?

Open rural areas lack dense multi-story concrete structures, reducing destructive multipath fading and shadowing losses by ~25 to 30 dB.
