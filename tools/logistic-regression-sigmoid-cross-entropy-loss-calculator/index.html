Frequently asked questions

What is the logit function?

The logit function is the inverse of the sigmoid: $ ext{logit}(p) = ln( rac{p}{1-p}) = z$, converting probabilities $[0, 1]$ into unconstrained real numbers $(-infty, +infty)$.
