Stimulated Emission Optical Resonance Condition

Lasing occurs when stimulated optical gain in the active quantum well exactly balances round-trip cavity mirror transmission and waveguide scattering losses ($g_{ ext{th}} = alpha_i + alpha_m$).

Frequently asked questions

How does High-Reflectivity (HR) facet coating reduce threshold current?

Applying dielectric HR coating ($R > 95%$) drops mirror loss $alpha_m$ toward zero, dramatically reducing threshold current $I_{ ext{th}}$.
