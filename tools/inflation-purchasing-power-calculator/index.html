How to use Inflation Purchasing Power Calculator

  1. Enter your current savings or capital balance.
  2. Enter the estimated average annual inflation rate (e.g. 6%).
  3. Enter the forecast horizon in years.
  4. Click Calculate to inspect the eroded real value and the target future capital needed.

Silent Wealth Erosion by Inflation

Inflation continuously reduces the quantity of goods and services a fixed sum of cash can buy. A 6% annual inflation rate cuts real purchasing power in half in approximately 12 years.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Rule of 72 for inflation?

Dividing 72 by the annual inflation rate gives the approximate number of years it takes for your money's purchasing power to be halved (72 / 6% = 12 years).
