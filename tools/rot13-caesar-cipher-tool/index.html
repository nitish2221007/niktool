Symmetry of ROT13

Because the English alphabet has 26 letters, shifting by 13 is its own inverse: applying ROT13 twice restores the original text without requiring separate encryption and decryption algorithms.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

Is ROT13 secure for passwords?

No, ROT13 is a simple obfuscation cipher for spoilers and puzzle games; it provides zero cryptographic security.
