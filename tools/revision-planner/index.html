How to use Revision Planner

  1. Enter the number of days left until your exam.
  2. Enter the total number of chapters you need to revise.
  3. Enter your daily study hours to calculate your daily chapter target.

Key Features & Privacy

This planner breaks down a massive syllabus into manageable daily targets, telling you exactly how many chapters to finish each day and how much time to spend on each. It is a planning aid that runs entirely locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use this 30 to 60 days before board exams to create a realistic, math-backed daily revision timetable.
