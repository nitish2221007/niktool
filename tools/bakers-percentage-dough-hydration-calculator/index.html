How to use Baker's Percentage & Sourdough Dough Hydration Calculator

  1. Enter total recipe flour weight in grams (always established as 100% baseline).
  2. Enter desired dough hydration percentage (typically 65% for sandwich loaves, 72-80% for open-crumb artisanal sourdough).
  3. Enter salt percentage (standard 2.0%) and levain / sourdough starter percentage (15-20%).
  4. Inspect exact scaled ingredient gram weights and total final dough batch mass.

Professional Baker's Percentage Standard

In commercial bakery production, all ingredients are expressed as a percentage of total flour weight (Flour = 100%), allowing bakers to scale recipes from a single loaf up to 500 kilograms instantaneously.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is 75% hydration dough?

It means the dough contains exactly 75 grams of water for every 100 grams of flour in the recipe.
