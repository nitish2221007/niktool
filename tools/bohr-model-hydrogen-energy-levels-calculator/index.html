Niels Bohr's 1913 Quantized Angular Momentum Model

Bohr proved that electrons exist in discrete quantized orbital shells (L = n·ħ); transitions between energy states emit photons with exact energies matching the Balmer spectral lines of interstellar hydrogen nebulae.
