How to use Solar Cell Fill Factor (FF), Open-Circuit Voltage (V_oc) & Efficiency (η) Calculator

  1. Enter open-circuit voltage $V_{ ext{oc}}$ in Volts measured under zero load.
  2. Enter short-circuit current $I_{ ext{sc}}$ in Amperes measured under zero voltage drop.
  3. Enter Maximum Power Point ($P_{max} = V_{ ext{mp}} imes I_{ ext{mp}}$) peak power in Watts.
  4. Enter active photovoltaic cell surface area in $ ext{cm}^2$.
  5. Inspect Fill Factor (FF) squareness percentage and total Standard Test Condition (STC) Power Conversion Efficiency $eta%$.

IEC 60904 Photovoltaic Performance Standard

Evaluates parasitic series and shunt resistance losses in solar cells; higher Fill Factor ($FF > 80%$) indicates minimal internal ohmic power dissipation.

What is the Shockley-Queisser theoretical efficiency limit?

The single-junction silicon solar cell theoretical thermodynamic efficiency limit under standard AM1.5G sunlight is approximately $33.7%$.
