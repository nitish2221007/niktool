How to use Trip Budget Planner

  1. Enter the total number of travelers participating in the trip.
  2. List your expected expenses line by line in the format: Item Name, Estimated Cost.
  3. Click Calculate Budget to see the total cost and the exact amount each person needs to contribute.

Key Features & Privacy

Planning a trip involves many moving parts and costs. This planner helps you consolidate flights, accommodation, food, and activities into one clear summary. Because it runs entirely in your browser, your travel plans and financial estimates remain strictly private.

Common Use Cases

Backpackers planning multi-country tours, families estimating vacation costs, student groups organizing college fests, and corporate teams budgeting for offsite retreats all use this tool to get a quick, clear financial picture before booking.
