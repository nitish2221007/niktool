How to use U.S. Navy Body Fat Calculator

  1. Select gender (female calculations include hip circumference).
  2. Measure waist at narrowest point / navel level and neck just below the larynx.
  3. Click Calculate to inspect body fat percentage and lean muscle mass.

Accuracy of the U.S. Navy Method

Developed by Hodgdon and Beckett at the Naval Health Research Center, this circumference equation correlates within 3-4% of dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DEXA) scans without needing expensive medical equipment.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is healthy body fat for men vs women?

Essential fat is 2-5% for men and 10-13% for women. An athletic/fitness range is 6-17% for men and 14-24% for women.
