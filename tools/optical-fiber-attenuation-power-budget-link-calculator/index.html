How to use Optical Fiber Attenuation & Power Budget Link Calculator

  1. Enter optical fiber link length in kilometers.
  2. Enter fiber attenuation coefficient $\alpha$ in dB/km (e.g., 0.35 dB/km @ 1310nm, 0.20 dB/km @ 1550nm).
  3. Enter combined total connector and fusion splice insertion loss in dB.
  4. Enter optical transmitter launch power $P_{tx}$ in dBm and receiver sensitivity $P_{rx}$ in dBm.
  5. Click Process to compute total loss, available budget, and link power margin.

Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle \text{Loss}_{\text{total}} = L \cdot \alpha + \text{Loss}_{\text{connectors}}, \quad \text{Margin} = (P_{tx} - P_{rx}) - \text{Loss}_{\text{total}}$$

Frequently asked questions

What optical attenuation is standard for single-mode fiber?

Single-mode fiber typically exhibits ~0.35 dB/km at 1310 nm and ~0.20 dB/km at 1550 nm.

What is a safe fiber power margin?

A safety margin of 3 dB to 6 dB is standard to allow for fiber aging, repairs, and temperature variations.

What causes fiber attenuation?

Rayleigh scattering, material absorption (OH- ion peak), microbending, and splice imperfections.

Yes, no data is transmitted to external servers.
