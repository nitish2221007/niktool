How to use Projectile Motion Calculator

  1. Enter the initial launch velocity in meters per second (m/s).
  2. Enter the launch angle in degrees relative to the ground (45° gives maximum theoretical range on level ground).
  3. Optionally specify an initial elevation height (h₀).
  4. Click Calculate to inspect range, maximum altitude, and flight duration.

Kinematic Trajectory Equations

In classical mechanics ignoring air drag, horizontal velocity remains constant (vx = v0 cos θ), while vertical motion experiences constant downward acceleration due to gravity (vy = v0 sin θ - gt).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What angle maximizes projectile range on flat ground?

45 degrees produces the maximum range when launch height equals landing height.
