How to use Manning's Equation Open Channel Flow & Normal Depth (Q = 1/n·A·R_h^(2/3)·S^(1/2)) Calculator

  1. Enter channel bottom bed width b in meters.
  2. Enter uniform normal water flow depth y in meters.
  3. Enter trapezoidal side slope z (horizontal distance per 1 vertical unit; enter 0 for rectangular channel).
  4. Enter longitudinal channel bed slope S (m/m, e.g. 0.002 for $0.2%$).
  5. Enter Manning's roughness coefficient n (e.g. 0.013 for smooth concrete, 0.030 for natural river channel).
  6. Inspect total volumetric discharge Q in $ ext{m}^3/ ext{s}$, velocity v, and hydraulic radius $R_h$.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most hydraulically efficient open channel cross-section?

A trapezoidal channel with side slopes of $60^circ$ ($z = 1/sqrt{3} approx 0.577$), forming a semi-hexagon that maximizes hydraulic radius $R_h = y/2$.
