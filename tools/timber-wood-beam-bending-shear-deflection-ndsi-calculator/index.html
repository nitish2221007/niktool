National Design Specification (NDS) for Wood Construction

Wood is anisotropic with natural grain defects; calculating horizontal shear parallel to grain ($f_v = rac{1.5 V}{A}$) is critical because timber shears along annual growth rings at much lower stress than tension/compression.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard residential floor joist deflection limit?

Residential building codes specify a maximum live-load deflection limit of $L / 360$ to prevent drywall ceiling cracking and floor bouncing.
