How to use Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) Neuron Firing Rate (F-I Curve) Calculator

  1. Enter injected continuous somatic depolarizing current $I_{ ext{inj}}$ in picoamperes (pA).
  2. Enter neuron input membrane resistance R in megaohms ($MOmega$).
  3. Enter action potential voltage threshold $V_{ ext{th}}$ above reset in millivolts (mV).
  4. Enter absolute refractory dead-time period $t_{ ext{ref}}$ in milliseconds.
  5. Inspect steady-state spiking output frequency in Hz, Inter-Spike Interval (ISI), and minimum rheobase threshold firing current.

Louis Lapicque 1907 Integrate-and-Fire Model

The LIF model is the workhorse of computational neuroscience and neuromorphic computing (Intel Loihi / IBM TrueNorth), providing an analytically tractable spiking neuron abstraction that accurately models gain modulation ($f ext{–}I$ curve).

Frequently asked questions

What is the Rheobase current?

Rheobase is the minimum continuous injected current required to charge the membrane up to the action potential threshold ($I_{ ext{rheo}} = V_{ ext{th}} / R$).
