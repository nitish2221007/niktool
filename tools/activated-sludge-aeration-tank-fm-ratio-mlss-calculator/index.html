Biological Wastewater Treatment Process Control

Controlling the F/M ratio between $0.20 ext{ and }0.50$ ensures bacteria have sufficient organic substrate to oxidize contaminants while starving them just enough to form dense, rapidly settling biological flocs in secondary clarifiers.

Frequently asked questions

What is MLSS vs MLVSS in activated sludge?

MLSS (Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids) measures total suspended solids; MLVSS (Volatile portion, $sim 75 ext{–}80%$ of MLSS) represents the living active bacterial biomass.
