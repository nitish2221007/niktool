AC Phasor Impedance Analysis

In AC circuits, inductors resist high frequencies ($X_L propto f$) while capacitors block low frequencies ($X_C propto 1/f$); calculating vector impedance $Z = R + j(X_L - X_C)$ is fundamental to audio crossover filters and power grid transmission.

Frequently asked questions

What is the power factor of an AC circuit?

Power factor is the cosine of the phase angle ($ ext{PF} = cosphi = R / Z$), representing the fraction of apparent electrical power that performs real work.
