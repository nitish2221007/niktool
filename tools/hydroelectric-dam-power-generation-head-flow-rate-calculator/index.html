Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Francis, Pelton, and Kaplan turbines?

Pelton wheels are used for high head ($>300 ext{ m}$) low flow; Francis turbines for medium head ($50 ext{–}300 ext{ m}$); Kaplan propellers for low head ($<50 ext{ m}$) high flow rivers.
