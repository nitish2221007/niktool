Fixed Income Internal Rate of Return

Yield to Maturity (YTM) represents the total expected annualized return if the bond is held until maturity and all coupon payments are reinvested at the same rate, enabling direct comparisons between discount and premium bonds.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Current Yield and YTM?

Current Yield ($ ext{Coupon} / ext{Price}$) only measures immediate annual interest income; YTM includes both coupon income and capital gains/losses at maturity.
