Multi-Class Probability Normalization

Softmax exponentiates logits so larger values dominate exponentially while constraining output sum strictly to 1.0, serving as the standard final layer of classification neural networks (ResNet, Vision Transformers).

Frequently asked questions

Why is max(z) subtracted during Softmax computation?

Subtracting $max(z)$ prevents floating-point exponential overflow ($e^{z}$) without altering output probabilities.
