Escape Velocity Derivation

Escape velocity is the speed at which the kinetic energy of an unpropelled ballistic projectile equals its gravitational potential energy (1/2 m v² = G M m / r), giving v = √(2GM/r).

Frequently asked questions

What is Earth's escape velocity?

From Earth's surface, escape velocity is approximately 11.19 km/s (roughly 40,270 km/h or Mach 33).
