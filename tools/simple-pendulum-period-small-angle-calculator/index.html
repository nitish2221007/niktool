Galileo Galilei 1602 Isochronism of the Pendulum

For small amplitudes ($ heta < 15^circ$), pendulum period depends exclusively on length and gravity ($T = 2pisqrt{L/g}$), completely independent of the hanging bob mass or swing angle, providing the historical foundation for mechanical pendulum grandfather clocks.
