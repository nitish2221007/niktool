Second Law of Thermodynamics Limit

Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot proved in 1824 that no heat engine operating between two thermal reservoirs can be more efficient than a reversible Carnot engine: η_max = 1 - (T_cold / T_hot).

Frequently asked questions

Can any heat engine achieve 100% thermal efficiency?

No, achieving 100% efficiency would require a cold reservoir at absolute zero (0 Kelvin), which violates the Third Law of Thermodynamics.
