How to use Freelance Hourly Rate to Salary Calculator

  1. Enter your hourly consulting or freelance contracting rate.
  2. Enter estimated billable hours per week (freelancers typically average 25-35 billable hours due to admin overhead).
  3. Enter active working weeks per year (e.g. 48 weeks allows 4 weeks vacation/sick time).
  4. Click Calculate to inspect your annualized gross income.

Freelance Billable Utilization Rule

Full-time standard employment assumes 2,080 hours (40 hrs × 52 weeks). However, independent contractors spend 25-30% of their time on unbillable client acquisition and invoicing, making 1,400 - 1,600 billable hours standard.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good rule of thumb for hourly rate from salary?

A standard rule of thumb is to divide target annual salary by 1,000 (e.g. a $100k salary translates to a ~$100/hr freelance rate to account for taxes and healthcare).
