  4. Inspect minimum theoretical equilibrium separation stages $N_{min}$ at total infinite reflux.

Merrell Fenske 1932 Distillation Column Standard

The Fenske equation calculates the absolute minimum number of theoretical distillation trays required to achieve a specified chemical separation; real industrial columns operating at finite reflux typically require $1.5 imes$ to $2 imes$ $N_{min}$.

Frequently asked questions

What is Total Reflux in distillation?

Total reflux means all condensed distillate vapor is returned back into the top of the column ($R = infty$), maximizing separation with zero net product withdrawal.
