Albert Einstein 1915 Gravitational Equivalence Principle

Clocks deeper in a gravitational potential well tick slower than distant clocks; GPS atomic satellites in orbit run $45.9 mu ext{s}$ faster per day due to General Relativity (and $7.2 mu ext{s}$ slower due to Special Relativity), requiring a net $+38.7 mu ext{s/day}$ frequency compensation to maintain 1-meter navigational accuracy.

What happens to light emitted at the Schwarzschild event horizon (r = rs)?

The gravitational redshift becomes infinite ($z o infty$), shifting light wavelengths infinitely red until photon energy drops to zero.
