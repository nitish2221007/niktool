How to use Duplicate Word Finder

  1. Paste your essay, article, or notes into the input area.
  2. Click Process to scan for repeated words.
  3. Review the list of duplicates to improve your writing variety and reduce repetition.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool identifies words that appear multiple times in your text, ignoring very short common words (like "the", "is", "a") to focus on meaningful repetition. It helps you polish your writing. All analysis is done locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students and writers use it to find overused words in essays and improve vocabulary variety.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.
