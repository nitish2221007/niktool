Frequently asked questions

What is the speed of a tsunami in the deep ocean (d = 4000m)?

In 4,000 meter deep ocean, long-period tsunamis travel as shallow-water waves at c = sqrt(gd) ≈ 198 m/s ≈ 713 km/h.
