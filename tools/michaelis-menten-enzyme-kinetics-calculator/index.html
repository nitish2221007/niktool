Leonor Michaelis & Maud Menten 1913 Catalytic Model

Enzyme catalysis follows hyperbolic saturation kinetics; at low substrate concentrations ($[S] ll K_m$) reaction rate is first-order, while at high substrate concentrations ($[S] gg K_m$) enzyme active sites become fully saturated (zero-order kinetics).

Frequently asked questions

What does a low Km value indicate?

A lower $K_m$ indicates higher substrate affinity, meaning the enzyme achieves high catalytic velocity even at low substrate concentrations.
