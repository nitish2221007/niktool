How to use Drinking Water Chlorine Disinfection (CT Value) Calculator

  1. Enter measured free available residual chlorine concentration C in mg/L (ppm).
  2. Enter T₁₀ effective baffled contact time in minutes (time for 10% of water to pass through pipe/clearwell).
  3. Select pathogen inactivation target (3-log Giardia or 4-log Virus).
  4. Inspect achieved CT disinfection product in mg·min/L and EPA compliance ratio.

Chick-Watson Chemical Disinfection Law

Under EPA Safe Drinking Water Act rules, pathogen disinfection follows the Chick-Watson law: germicidal kill depends on the product of disinfectant concentration C and contact exposure time T₁₀ ($CT = Ccdot T_{10}$).

Frequently asked questions

What is T₁₀ contact time?

T₁₀ is the detention time through a clearwell basin at peak hourly flow through which 90% of water remains in the tank (10% has exited), accounting for short-circuiting.
