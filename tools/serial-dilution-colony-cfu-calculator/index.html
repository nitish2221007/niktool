How to use Serial Dilution & Colony Forming Units (CFU/mL) Calculator

  1. Enter the number of distinct visible colonies counted on the petri dish.
  2. Enter reciprocal dilution factor (e.g. 10⁴ = 10,000 for a 10⁻⁴ dilution).
  3. Enter volume spread onto agar plate in mL (0.1 mL = 100 μL).
  4. Inspect viable microbial stock concentration in CFU/mL.

The 30 to 300 Colony Statistical Standard

Microbiological plate counts are statistically valid only between 30 and 300 colonies per plate: fewer than 30 introduces high sampling error (TFTC), while over 300 causes colony overlap and nutrient competition (TNTC).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is 142 colonies on a 10⁻⁴ plate spread with 0.1 mL?

CFU/mL = (142 × 10,000) / 0.1 mL = 14,200,000 = 1.42 × 10⁷ CFU/mL.
