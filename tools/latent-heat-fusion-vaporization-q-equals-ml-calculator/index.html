Isothermal Phase Change Physics

During phase transitions, heat energy breaks intermolecular hydrogen bonds rather than increasing kinetic molecular vibration, keeping the substance at a constant temperature ($0^circ ext{C}$ for melting ice, $100^circ ext{C}$ for boiling steam).

Frequently asked questions

Why is the latent heat of vaporization of water so much larger than fusion?

Vaporization ($2,260 ext{ kJ/kg}$) completely breaks all intermolecular hydrogen bonds, while melting ($334 ext{ kJ/kg}$) only disrupts crystal lattice order.
