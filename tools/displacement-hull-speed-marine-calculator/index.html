Bow Wave Length Trapping Barrier

As a displacement vessel accelerates, it generates a bow wave and stern wave; at hull speed, the wavelength of the bow wave equals the boat waterline length (Fn ≈ 0.40), trapping the boat in its own wave trough unless it possesses sufficient power to plane.

What is the hull speed of a 36-foot waterline yacht?
