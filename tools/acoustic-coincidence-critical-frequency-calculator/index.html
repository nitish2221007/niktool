Bending Wave Acoustic Trace Velocity Matching

At the critical coincidence frequency ($f_c$), free flexural bending waves in the solid panel propagate at the exact same velocity as airborne acoustic waves, causing the panel to become acoustically transparent and destroying sound isolation.

Frequently asked questions

How do acoustic engineers eliminate the coincidence dip in windows?

Using laminated glass with a damping acoustic Polyvinyl Butyral (PVB) interlayer absorbs flexural bending energy and smooths out the transmission loss dip.
