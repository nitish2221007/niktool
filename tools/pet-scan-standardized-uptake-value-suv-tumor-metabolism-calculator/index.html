How to use PET Scan Standardized Uptake Value (SUV_bw) Tumor Metabolic Activity Calculator

  1. Enter Region of Interest (ROI) tissue radiotracer activity concentration $C_{ ext{tissue}}$ in $ ext{kBq/mL}$ from PET scanner voxel data.
  2. Enter initial injected dose of $^{18} ext{F}$-FDG radiotracer in MBq ($1 ext{ mCi} = 37 ext{ MBq}$).
  3. Enter uptake incubation time between injection and scan acquisition in minutes (standard $60 ext{ min}$).
  4. Enter patient body weight in kg.
  5. Inspect Standardized Uptake Value ($ ext{SUV}_{ ext{bw}}$) and diagnostic malignancy stratification.

Positron Emission Tomography Quantitative SUV Standard

Normalizes radiotracer uptake for patient size and physical radioisotope decay ($t_{1/2} = 109.8 ext{ min}$), enabling oncologists to quantify tumor glucose consumption (Warburg effect) and assess response to chemotherapy.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical cutoff for malignant tumors on FDG-PET?

Lesions with $ ext{SUV} > 2.5$ have high likelihood of malignancy (e.g. solitary pulmonary nodules), though active infections (tuberculosis, abscesses) can also yield high SUV.
