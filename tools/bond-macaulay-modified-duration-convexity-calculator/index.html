Frederick Macaulay 1938 Duration & Convexity Curvature

Modified duration estimates linear interest rate price sensitivity ($Delta P/P approx -D_{ ext{mod}}Delta y$); adding the second-order positive convexity term ($ rac{1}{2} C (Delta y)^2$) captures the non-linear curvature of bond pricing, showing that bond prices gain more when rates fall than they lose when rates rise.

Frequently asked questions

Why is positive convexity desirable in bond investing?

Bonds with high positive convexity experience greater price appreciation when interest rates drop and lesser price declines when interest rates climb.
