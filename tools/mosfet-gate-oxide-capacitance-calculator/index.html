How to use MOSFET Gate Oxide Capacitance (C_ox) & Transconductance Parameter Calculator

  1. Enter gate insulator dielectric physical thickness t_ox in nanometers.

  3. Enter channel electron inversion layer surface mobility μ_n in cm²/V·s.
  4. Inspect gate capacitance density C_ox in fF/μm² and CMOS transconductance process parameter k_n'.

High-k Dielectrics & Quantum Gate Tunneling

Thinning standard SiO₂ below 1.5 nm causes excessive quantum tunneling gate leakage; replacing SiO₂ with high-k hafnium dioxide provides identical high gate capacitance C_ox with much thicker physical barriers.

Frequently asked questions

What is Equivalent Oxide Thickness (EOT)?

EOT = t_high-k × (3.9 / κ), representing the thickness of a SiO₂ layer that would produce the exact same gate capacitance.
