Frequently asked questions

What is a pi-pulse in quantum computing?

A $pi$-pulse ($t = pi / Omega_R$) drives the qubit exactly half a full oscillation, flipping the state from $|0 angle$ to $|1 angle$ (equivalent to a quantum NOT / Pauli-X gate).
