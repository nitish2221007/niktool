  1. Enter any word, passport number, postal code, or flight callsign.

ICAO and NATO Aviation Clarity

The International Radiotelephony Spelling Alphabet (NATO phonetic alphabet) prevents critical misunderstandings over radio and phone communications where letters like B and D or M and N sound nearly identical.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 9 spelled "Niner"?

In German "Nein" means "no", so aviation standards adopted "Niner" to avoid fatal confusion over communication channels.
