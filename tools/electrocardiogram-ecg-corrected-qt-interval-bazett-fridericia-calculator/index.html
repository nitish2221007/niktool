Frequently asked questions

Why is Fridericia formula often preferred over Bazett at high heart rates?

Bazett formula overcorrects at tachycardic heart rates ($HR > 85 ext{ bpm}$), generating false-positive long QT alarms; Fridericia provides superior accuracy across all heart rates.
