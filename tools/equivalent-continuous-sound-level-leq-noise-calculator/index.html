Frequently asked questions

What is the OSHA 5-dB exchange rate vs NIOSH 3-dB rule?

OSHA uses a 5 dB trading ratio (halving allowable exposure time for every 5 dB rise above 90 dBA); NIOSH uses a stricter 3 dB rule.
