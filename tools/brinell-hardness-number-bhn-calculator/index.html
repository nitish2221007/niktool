How to use Brinell Hardness Number (HBW / BHN) Calculator

  1. Enter testing indentation load force in kgf (e.g. 3,000 kgf standard for steels, 500 kgf for aluminum).
  2. Enter spherical indenter ball diameter D in millimeters (10 mm standard).
  3. Enter microscope-measured indentation impression diameter d in millimeters.
  4. Inspect Brinell Hardness Number (HBW) and empirical Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS).

Johan August Brinell's 1900 Metallurgy Standard

Brinell hardness testing provides a reliable average hardness over larger heterogeneous microstructures (like cast iron and forged steel) by pressing a spherical tungsten carbide ball into the surface.

Frequently asked questions

How does Brinell hardness correlate to steel tensile strength?

For structural carbon steels, Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS in MPa) is approximately 3.45 × HBW (or UTS in psi ≈ 500 × HBW).
