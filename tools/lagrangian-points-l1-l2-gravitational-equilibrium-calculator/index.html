Frequently asked questions

Why is JWST placed at Sun-Earth L2 instead of low Earth orbit?

At L2 ($1.5 ext{ million km}$ from Earth), Earth and Sun remain in the same direction, allowing a single sunshield to permanently keep instruments at $-233^circ ext{C}$ ($40 ext{ K}$).
