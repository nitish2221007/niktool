The 20:1 Carbonic Acid Buffer Equilibrium

Normal physiological human blood pH of 7.40 is maintained when the ratio of basic bicarbonate to dissolved acidic carbon dioxide equals exactly 20:1 ($ ext{pH} = 6.10 + log_{10}(20) = 6.10 + 1.30 = 7.40$).
