How to use Carreau-Yasuda Polymer Melt Shear-Thinning Viscosity Calculator

  1. Enter zero-shear Newtonian plateau viscosity $eta_0$ in Pa·s.
  2. Enter characteristic polymer chain relaxation time constant $lambda$ in seconds.
  3. Enter power-law index n ($n < 1.0$ for pseudoplastic shear-thinning melts).
  4. Enter process shear rate $dotgamma$ in $s^{-1}$ (e.g. $10 ext{ s}^{-1}$ for extrusion, $1000 ext{ s}^{-1}$ for injection mold gates).
  5. Inspect apparent dynamic viscosity $eta(dotgamma)$ in Pa·s and percentage shear-thinning reduction.

Molecular Chain Uncoiling & Alignment

Under high shear rates ($dotgamma > 1/lambda$), entangled polymer random coils stretch and align parallel to the flow streamlines, drastically reducing intermolecular friction and dropping viscosity by 90%+ during rapid injection molding cavity filling.

Frequently asked questions

Why is shear-thinning crucial for plastic injection molding?

It allows high-molecular-weight polymers with excellent mechanical strength to flow freely through narrow mold gates without requiring extreme hydraulic pressures.
