William Henry 1803 Gas Solubility Law

Henry's law ($C = k_H P_{ ext{gas}}$) governs oxygen dissolution into ocean ecosystems, sparkling beverage carbonation, and hyperbaric scuba diving decompression sickness ("the bends").

Frequently asked questions

Why is CO2 so much more soluble in water than O2 or N2?

Carbon dioxide has a Henry's constant nearly 30 times higher than oxygen and chemically reacts with water to form carbonic acid ($H_2CO_3$).
