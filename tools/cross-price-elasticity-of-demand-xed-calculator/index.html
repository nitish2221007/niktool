How to use Cross-Price Elasticity of Demand (XED) Calculator

  1. Enter percentage price increase or decrease for Product B (%ΔP_B).
  2. Enter resulting percentage change in quantity demanded for Product A (%ΔQ_A).
  3. Inspect Cross-Price Elasticity coefficient (XED) and identify economic product substitution relationship.

Cross-Price Sign Interpretation

Positive XED indicates Substitute goods (e.g., Butter and Margarine); Negative XED indicates Complementary goods used together (e.g., Hamburgers and Buns).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.
