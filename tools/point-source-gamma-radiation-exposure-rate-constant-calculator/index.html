Frequently asked questions

What is the Rule of Thumb 6CEN formula for gamma exposure?

For common gamma emitters, $dot{X} ext{ (R/hr @ 1 ft)} approx 6 imes C imes E imes n$, where C is Curies, E is energy in MeV, and n is photon yield.
