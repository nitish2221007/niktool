Class 3 Musculoskeletal Anatomical Levers

Human skeletal joints operate as Class 3 levers where muscles insert very close to the joint fulcrum; this trades mechanical advantage for high limb speed and range of motion, requiring the biceps tendon to generate over 800 N of force to hold a modest 10 kg dumbbell.

Frequently asked questions

Why are joint compression forces so large during heavy lifting?

Because muscle tendon pull angles are nearly parallel to bones, large tendon tensions pull joint surfaces tightly against each other, compressing cartilage.
