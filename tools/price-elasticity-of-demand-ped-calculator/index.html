Total Revenue Rule of Elasticity

For elastic goods (|PED| > 1), raising prices decreases total business revenue because unit volume drops by a greater percentage than the price increase; for inelastic goods (|PED| < 1), raising prices increases total revenue.

Frequently asked questions

Why use the midpoint method instead of simple percentage changes?

The midpoint formula gives the exact same elasticity value whether prices are rising from P₁ to P₂ or falling from P₂ back to P₁.
