  1. Enter applied uniaxial tensile stress $sigma$ in MPa.
  2. Enter angle $phi$ between tensile load axis and slip plane normal vector in degrees.

  4. Inspect resolved shear stress $ au$ and Schmid Factor ($m le 0.50$).

Erich Schmid 1924 Dislocation Glide Criterion

Plastic deformation in single crystals initiates only when resolved shear stress on the primary slip system reaches the Critical Resolved Shear Stress ($ au ge au_{ ext{CRSS}}$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum possible Schmid factor?

The theoretical maximum Schmid factor is exactly $0.50$, occurring when $phi = lambda = 45^circ$.
