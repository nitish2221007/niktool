Frequently asked questions

What is the Nyquist Sampling Theorem?

It states that an analog signal can be perfectly reconstructed if sampled at a rate greater than twice its highest frequency.

What is aliasing in digital signal processing?

Aliasing occurs when high frequencies disguise themselves as lower frequencies due to under-sampling.

Why is CD audio sampled at 44.1 kHz?

Human hearing extends up to ~20 kHz, so 44.1 kHz provides >40 kHz Nyquist rate plus a guard band for antialiasing filters.
