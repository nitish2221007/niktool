Jacobus Henricus van 't Hoff (First Chemistry Nobel Laureate)

The Van 't Hoff equation quantitatively demonstrates Le Chatelier's principle: heating an exothermic reaction (ΔH < 0) suppresses product formation (driving equilibrium back to reactants), explaining the high-temperature trade-offs in Haber ammonia synthesis.

Frequently asked questions

How does temperature affect an endothermic reaction?

Heating an endothermic reaction (ΔH > 0) increases the equilibrium constant K, shifting equilibrium towards higher product yields.
