How to use Workout Sweat Rate & Athletic Hydration Calculator

  1. Weigh yourself before exercise in minimal clothing (Pre-Workout Weight).
  2. Weigh yourself after exercise and record fluid consumed during the session.
  3. Inspect your exact hourly sweat rate and fluid deficit to customize athletic race hydration.

The 2% Dehydration Performance Threshold

Losing greater than 2% of body mass through sweat impairs cardiovascular output, reduces muscular endurance, and elevates core body temperature.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is typical sweat rate in hot conditions?

Athletes typically sweat between 0.8 and 2.0+ Liters per hour in hot or humid environments.
