Gustaf de Laval 1888 Supersonic Nozzle Geometry

A converging-diverging de Laval nozzle accelerates subsonic combustion gas to Mach 1 at the narrowest throat, then expands supersonic gas in the divergent bell to convert thermal pressure into massive kinetic exhaust velocity.

Frequently asked questions

Why do vacuum rocket engines have huge expansion ratios (ε > 100)?

In the vacuum of space, expanding the nozzle bell to huge ratios captures every drop of pressure energy without risking flow separation.
