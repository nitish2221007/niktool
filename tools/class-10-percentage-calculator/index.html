How to use Class 10 Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter the subject name, obtained marks, and maximum marks for each subject.
  2. Click + Add Subject to add more rows or Remove to delete subjects.
  3. Click Calculate Percentage to see your total marks and overall percentage.

Key Features & Privacy

This calculator is designed specifically for Class 10 students. All calculations happen locally in your browser — your marks and academic data never leave your device. The tool supports any number of subjects with custom maximum marks, making it perfect for board exams, unit tests, and internal assessments.

Common Use Cases

Students use this tool to calculate their board exam percentage, track progress across multiple tests, verify their school's percentage calculations, and plan target scores for competitive exams.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply enter your subject names, marks obtained, and maximum marks for each subject, then click Calculate Percentage to see your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all processing happens locally in your browser. Your marks are never sent to any server.

Can I add more than 5 subjects?

Yes, click the Add Subject button to add as many subjects as you need.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
