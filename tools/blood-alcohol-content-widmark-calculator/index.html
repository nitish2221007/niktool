Hepatic Alcohol Metabolism Rate (β = 0.015%/hr)

The human liver metabolizes alcohol at a steady linear rate of approximately 0.015% BAC per hour (zero-order elimination kinetics), unaffected by coffee, cold showers, or exercise.

Frequently asked questions

What is the legal driving limit in the US and UK?

In the United States and England/Wales, the legal limit for non-commercial driving is 0.08% BAC; in Scotland and most European countries, it is 0.05% or zero-tolerance.
