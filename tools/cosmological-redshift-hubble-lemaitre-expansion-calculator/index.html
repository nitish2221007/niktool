Edwin Hubble & Georges Lemaître 1929 Expanding Universe

Cosmological redshift is not a Doppler shift through space, but the stretching of photon wavelengths as space-time itself expands across billions of years ($lambda_{ ext{obs}} = lambda_{ ext{emit}} [1 + z]$), allowing the James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) to peer back $13.5 ext{ billion years}$ to the dawn of the first stars.

Frequently asked questions

What is the redshift of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB)?

The CMB was emitted at $z approx 1089$ when the universe cooled to $3,000 ext{ K}$, stretching visible orange recombination light into $2.725 ext{ K}$ microwave background radiation today.
