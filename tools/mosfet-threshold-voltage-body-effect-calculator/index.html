Frequently asked questions

What is the Body Effect in integrated circuits?

When the transistor source is at a higher potential than the substrate body ($V_{ ext{SB}} > 0$), depletion charge increases, raising threshold voltage $V_{ ext{th}}$ and slowing down stacked pass gates.
