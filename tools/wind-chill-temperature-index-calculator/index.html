NWS 2001 Joint Action Group Model

Developed by the National Weather Service, the modern wind chill index models convective heat loss from the human face (standing 5 ft off the ground into wind) to establish medical frostbite risk guidelines.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Does wind chill affect inanimate objects like car engines?

No, wind chill only accelerates the rate of cooling down to ambient air temperature; an object cannot cool below actual air temperature.
