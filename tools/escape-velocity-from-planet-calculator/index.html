Zero-Energy Parabolic Trajectory Threshold

Escape velocity represents the minimum initial speed an unpropelled projectile needs to completely break free from a planet's gravitational well to infinity without ever falling back (kinetic energy balances gravitational potential energy: ½mv² = GMm/r).
