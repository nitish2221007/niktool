William John Macquorn Rankine Steam Power Cycle

The Rankine cycle powers over $80%$ of global base-load electricity generation across coal, nuclear, and concentrated solar power plants; superheating steam ($h_1$) increases enthalpy drops and minimizes turbine blade water droplet erosion.

Frequently asked questions

What is Heat Rate in power plant engineering?

Heat Rate measures thermal energy input required to generate 1 kilowatt-hour of electricity ($ ext{kJ/kWh}$); lower heat rate indicates higher thermodynamic efficiency.
