Ductile Yield Failure Criteria

Frequently asked questions

Why is Tresca always more conservative than Von Mises?

The Tresca hexagon yield locus is completely inscribed inside the smooth Von Mises ellipse, predicting yielding at up to $15.5%$ lower stresses.
