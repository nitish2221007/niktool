How to use Rockport 1-Mile Walk Test VO2 Max Calculator

  1. Walk 1 mile (1,609 meters) as fast as possible on a flat track.
  2. Record exact completion time in decimal minutes and immediate heart rate upon crossing the finish line.
  3. Inspect your clinical VO2 Max aerobic fitness classification.

Sub-Maximal Aerobic Capacity Testing

Developed at the Department of Exercise Science at UMass Amherst, the Rockport walk test allows safe, non-invasive estimation of VO2 max without requiring maximal treadmill exhaustion testing.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good VO2 max for a 30-year-old male?

Between 45 and 52 mL/kg/min represents excellent cardiovascular aerobic health.
