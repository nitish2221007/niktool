The T₂ Bound in Quantum Computing

Energy loss fundamentally limits quantum phase retention; no qubit can possess a transverse coherence time longer than twice its relaxation time (T₂ ≤ 2·T₁).

Frequently asked questions

What causes T1 vs T2 decoherence in superconducting qubits?

T₁ is caused by energy dissipation into material dielectric substrate defects, while T₂ dephasing is driven by low-frequency flux and charge noise.
