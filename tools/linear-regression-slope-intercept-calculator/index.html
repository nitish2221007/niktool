Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression

OLS minimizes the sum of squared vertical residuals (distances between observed data points and the regression line) to create the optimal linear predictor.

Frequently asked questions

What does R² = 0.95 mean?

It indicates that 95% of the variance in the dependent variable Y is explained by the independent variable X.
