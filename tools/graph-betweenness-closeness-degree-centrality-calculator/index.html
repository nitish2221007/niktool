Social Network & Structural Graph Centrality

High closeness centrality indicates a node can rapidly disseminate information across a network; high betweenness centrality marks bridge nodes that control communication between isolated clusters.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

What does a closeness centrality of 1.0 mean?

A closeness centrality of 1.0 means the node is directly connected to every single other node in the graph (a star network hub).
