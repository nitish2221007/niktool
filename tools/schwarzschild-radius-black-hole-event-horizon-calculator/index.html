Frequently asked questions

Why is Hawking radiation colder for supermassive black holes?

Hawking temperature is inversely proportional to mass ($T_H propto 1/M$); supermassive black holes have temperatures of $10^{-14} ext{ K}$, far colder than the $2.7 ext{ K}$ Cosmic Microwave Background.
