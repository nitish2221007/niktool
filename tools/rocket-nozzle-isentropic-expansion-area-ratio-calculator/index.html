Frequently asked questions

Why do vacuum rocket engines have much larger nozzles than sea-level engines?

Vacuum engines expand exhaust against near-zero ambient backpressure ($epsilon > 100 ext{–}200$), whereas sea-level nozzles are restricted ($epsilon approx 15 ext{–}25$) to avoid flow separation.
