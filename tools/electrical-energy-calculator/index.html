How to use Electrical Energy Calculator

  1. Enter the power in watts.
  2. Enter the time and choose seconds or hours.
  3. Click Process to see the energy in joules, watt-hours, and kilowatt-hours.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator applies E = P x t and converts between joules and kWh, which is the unit used on electricity bills. Showing all three units helps connect the Class 10 physics formula to real-world energy measurement, and everything runs locally.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it for electricity numericals, and it also bridges naturally into estimating appliance energy use and bills.
