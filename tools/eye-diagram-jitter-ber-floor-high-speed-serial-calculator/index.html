Frequently asked questions

What is an Eye Diagram in digital design?

An eye diagram overlays consecutive waveform sweeps to assess noise margin, jitter, and signal integrity of high-speed serial links.

What is Unit Interval (UI)?

Unit Interval is the time duration of one single data bit period.

What causes eye closure?

Intersymbol interference (ISI), crosstalk, dispersion, channel loss, and random jitter cause eye closure.

Is this calculation browser-based?

Yes, processing is performed 100% locally in your web browser.
