How to use EPA Chlorine Disinfection Concentration-Time (CT Table & Log Inactivation) Calculator

  1. Enter measured free chlorine residual concentration C in mg/L at clearwell exit.
  2. Enter hydraulic contact time $T_{10}$ in minutes ($10%$ of water passes faster, accounting for tank baffling factor).
  3. Enter water temperature in $^circ ext{C}$ and water pH.
  4. Inspect achieved CT value in $ ext{mg}cdot ext{min/L}$ and EPA 3-log Giardia ($99.9%$) inactivation compliance.

EPA Surface Water Treatment Rule (SWTR) Disinfection Standard

Ensures municipal water supplies achieve mandated 3-log ($99.9%$) Giardia cyst inactivation and 4-log ($99.99%$) virus reduction to prevent waterborne pathogen outbreaks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the baffling factor in contact time calculations?

Unbaffled tanks experience short-circuiting ($T_{10}/T approx 0.1$); adding serpentine baffles increases effective contact time ($T_{10}/T approx 0.7$).
