ASTM E90 & E413 Architectural Soundproofing Standard

The Acoustic Mass Law dictates that doubling wall surface mass increases sound transmission loss by $6 ext{ dB}$; building codes mandate minimum $ ext{STC } 50$ between adjacent apartment condominium units.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum STC required by the International Building Code (IBC)?

IBC Section 1207 mandates a minimum laboratory rating of $ ext{STC } 50$ (or field test $ ext{FSTC } 45$) for walls and floor-ceiling assemblies separating dwelling units.
