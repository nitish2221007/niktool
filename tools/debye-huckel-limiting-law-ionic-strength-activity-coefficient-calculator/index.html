Frequently asked questions

Why is the Debye-Huckel limiting law only valid for dilute solutions (I < 0.1 M)?

At higher concentrations, finite ion sizes and short-range non-Coulombic van der Waals interactions require extended Debye-Hückel or Pitzer virial equations.
