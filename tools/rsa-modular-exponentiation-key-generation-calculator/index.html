Rivest-Shamir-Adleman 1977 Public-Key Cryptosystem

RSA relies on the asymmetric trapdoor difficulty of integer prime factorization: while computing $n = p cdot q$ takes microseconds, factoring 2048-bit n back into primes without knowing private key d would take supercomputers billions of years.

Frequently asked questions

Why is e = 65537 commonly used in commercial RSA certificates?

$65537 = 2^{16} + 1$ is a Fermat prime with only two binary "1" bits, allowing ultra-fast modular exponentiation with only 17 multiplications.
