Sampling Without Replacement Precision

Unlike the binomial distribution which assumes replacement and independent trials, the hypergeometric distribution accounts for changing probabilities as items are removed from a finite population.

Frequently asked questions

What is the probability of getting exactly 1 Ace in a 5-card poker hand?

P(X = 1) = C(4, 1) · C(48, 4) / C(52, 5) = (4 × 194,580) / 2,598,960 ≈ 29.95%.
