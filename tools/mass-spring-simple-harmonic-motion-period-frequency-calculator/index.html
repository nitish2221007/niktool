Robert Hooke Restoring Force Linear Elasticity Law

Because the restoring spring force is strictly proportional to displacement ($F = -kx$), the resulting differential equation ($mddot{x} + kx = 0$) produces sinusoidal harmonic motion with constant frequency regardless of amplitude.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to the oscillation period if the mass is quadrupled?

Quadrupling the mass doubles the oscillation period ($T propto sqrt{m}$, so $sqrt{4} = 2$).
