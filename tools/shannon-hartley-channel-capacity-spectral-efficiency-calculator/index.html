Claude Shannon 1948 Mathematical Theory of Communication

Shannon proved that error-free digital transmission is possible over any noisy channel up to the fundamental capacity limit ($C = B log_2(1 + ext{SNR})$); modern LDPC and Polar codes in 5G approach within $0.1 ext{ dB}$ of this universal mathematical law.

What is the absolute Shannon Limit for zero error communication?

The minimum signal-to-noise ratio per bit required for error-free transmission as bandwidth approaches infinity is $ rac{E_b}{N_0} = ln 2 approx -1.59 ext{ dB}$.
