Warren L. McCabe & Ernest W. Thiele 1925 Distillation Method

Graphical and numerical stepping between equilibrium curve ($y = rac{alpha x}{1 + (alpha-1)x}$) and operating lines ($y = rac{R}{R+1}x + rac{x_D}{R+1}$) determines the exact number of column trays needed for petrochemical separations.

Frequently asked questions

What is the consequence of operating below R_min?

Operating at or below $R_{min}$ creates a pinch point requiring an infinite number of column trays to achieve the specified separation.
