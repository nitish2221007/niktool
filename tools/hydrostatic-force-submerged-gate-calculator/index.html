Why the Center of Pressure Lies Below Centroid

Because hydrostatic water pressure increases linearly with depth (P = ρ·g·h), the lower portion of a gate experiences higher pressure than the top, shifting the resultant force point (Center of Pressure) strictly below the geometric centroid.

Frequently asked questions

Where should hinge supports be placed on dam floodgates?

Hinges and structural reinforcement ribs are positioned at or near the Center of Pressure to prevent rotational tipping moment instability.
