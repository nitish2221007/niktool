Frequently asked questions

What causes maximum ground-level concentration to occur some distance downwind?

Directly at the base of the stack, the elevated plume ($H$) hasn't diffused down yet; concentration peaks at $x_{max} approx H / sqrt{2}$ before wind dilution reduces it.
