Ary Sternfeld 1934 Bi-Elliptic Transfer Principle

When raising an orbit by a factor greater than 11.94 ($r_2 / r_1 > 11.94$), boosting to a high intermediate apogee $r_b$ reduces plane-change and circularization speeds so drastically that a 3-burn bi-elliptic transfer uses strictly less propellant $Delta v$ than a direct Hohmann transfer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the downside of a bi-elliptic transfer?

Bi-elliptic transfers take vastly longer elapsed flight times (often days or months longer) than direct Hohmann transfers.
