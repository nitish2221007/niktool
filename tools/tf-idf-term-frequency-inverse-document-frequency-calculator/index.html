  1. Enter term count in current document and total document word count.

  3. Enter number of corpus documents containing the term (Document Frequency DF).
  4. Inspect normalized Term Frequency (TF), Inverse Document Frequency (IDF), and final TF-IDF relevance score.

Karen Spärck Jones 1972 Information Retrieval Standard

TF-IDF offsets common stop words ("the", "is") by penalizing high document frequency while rewarding rare, domain-specific keywords.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

Why is the logarithm used in the IDF formula?

Logarithms dampen the effect of corpus size and provide linear scaling for term rarity.
