How to use Henderson-Hasselbalch Buffer Calculator

  1. Enter the pKa of your weak acid.
  2. Enter the molar concentrations of conjugate base [A⁻] and weak acid [HA].
  3. Click Calculate to inspect buffer pH.

Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation

The Henderson-Hasselbalch equation relates pH to acid dissociation constant pKa: pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA]). When [A⁻] = [HA], pH = pKa, which provides maximum buffer capacity.

Frequently asked questions

What is buffer capacity?

Buffer capacity is the amount of strong acid or base a buffer can absorb before significant pH changes occur; it is highest within ±1 pH unit of pKa.
