Robert Hooke 1678 Elasticity Law

Within the elastic limit, strain is directly proportional to stress ($F = kx$); the area under the force-extension graph equals stored elastic strain energy ($E_p = rac{1}{2}kx^2$), essential for vehicle suspension springs, trampolines, and archery bows.

Frequently asked questions

What happens beyond the elastic limit of a spring?

The spring suffers plastic deformation, permanently altering its structure so Hooke's law no longer applies.
