Vaswani et al. 2017 "Attention Is All You Need"

Dividing by $sqrt{d_k}$ prevents large dot products from pushing the softmax function into regions with vanishingly small gradients, enabling deep Transformer models (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini) to train stably.

Frequently asked questions

Why do dot products grow large for high dimensions d_k?

For independent zero-mean unit-variance components, the dot product $Q cdot K$ has mean 0 and variance $d_k$; scaling by $sqrt{d_k}$ restores unit variance.
