How to use Solubility Product (Ksp) & Molar Solubility Calculator

  1. Select salt stoichiometry (AB, AB₂, AB₃, or A₂B₃).
  2. Enter solubility product equilibrium constant Ksp (scientific notation like 1.8e-10 is supported).
  3. Enter formula molar mass in g/mol.
  4. Inspect molar solubility in mol/L and dissolved concentration in mg/L.

Predicting Precipitation Reactions

Precipitation occurs whenever the instantaneous ion product quotient Q exceeds the equilibrium solubility constant (Q > Ksp).

Frequently asked questions

What is the molar solubility of Silver Chloride (AgCl)?

With Ksp = 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁰, s = √(1.8×10⁻¹⁰) ≈ 1.34 × 10⁻⁵ mol/L (~1.92 mg/L in water).
