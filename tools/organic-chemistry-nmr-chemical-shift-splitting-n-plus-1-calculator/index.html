Proton Spin-Spin J-Coupling in Organic Chemistry

Nuclear magnetic spin states ($I = 1/2$) of neighboring protons split local magnetic fields, creating characteristic multiplet peak patterns ($N+1$) that reveal chemical connectivity and spatial stereochemistry in organic molecular structure determination.

Frequently asked questions

Why do alcohol -OH and amine -NH protons usually appear as singlets?

Rapid intermolecular chemical proton exchange with trace water averages out spin coupling, collapsing the peak into a broad singlet.
