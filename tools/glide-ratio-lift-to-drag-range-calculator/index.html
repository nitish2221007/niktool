The Gimli Glider & Miracle on the Hudson Aerodynamic Proof

Without engine thrust, gravity acts as the propulsive engine component along the flight path slope; gliding range depends exclusively on aerodynamic (L/D)_max, completely independent of aircraft gross weight.

Frequently asked questions

Does aircraft weight affect maximum glide distance?

No! Heavier aircraft fly faster at their best glide speed (V_g), but travel the exact same horizontal distance as lighter aircraft with the same L/D.
