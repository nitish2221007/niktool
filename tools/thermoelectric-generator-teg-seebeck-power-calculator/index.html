Frequently asked questions

What is the Dimensionless Figure of Merit (ZT)?

$ZT = rac{S^2 sigma}{kappa} T$ quantifies thermoelectric performance; high ZT requires high electrical conductivity ($sigma$) and Seebeck coefficient ($S$) paired with low thermal conductivity ($kappa$).
