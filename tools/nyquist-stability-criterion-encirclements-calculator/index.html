Frequently asked questions

How does Nyquist handle an open-loop unstable plant (P > 0)?

To stabilize an open-loop unstable plant ($P > 0$), the Nyquist contour must encircle $(-1 + j0)$ exactly $P$ times in the COUNTER-CLOCKWISE direction ($N = -P$), making $Z = 0$.
