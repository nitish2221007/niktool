Arterial Pressure Waveform Integration

Because the cardiac cycle spends approximately two-thirds of its time in diastole and one-third in systole, MAP weights diastolic pressure twice as heavily ($MAP = rac{SBP + 2cdot DBP}{3}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is MAP >= 65 mmHg the standard ICU resuscitation target?

A MAP of $ge 65 ext{ mmHg}$ maintains coronary, cerebral, and renal capillary perfusion gradients.
