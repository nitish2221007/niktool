How to use Optical Fiber Numerical Aperture (NA), Acceptance Angle & V-Number Calculator

  1. Enter fiber glass core refractive index $n_{ ext{core}}$ (e.g. 1.468).
  2. Enter cladding refractive index $n_{ ext{clad}}$ (e.g. 1.463).
  3. Enter core radius a in micrometers (e.g. $4.1 mu ext{m}$ for single-mode, $25 mu ext{m}$ for multimode).
  4. Enter laser wavelength $lambda$ in nanometers (e.g. 1310 nm or 1550 nm).
  5. Inspect light-gathering Numerical Aperture (NA) and Normalized Frequency V-number.

Determines the single-mode cutoff condition ($V < 2.405$ based on the first zero of the $J_0$ Bessel function), eliminating multi-modal pulse dispersion across transoceanic telecom fiber links.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when V-number exceeds 2.405?

Higher-order transverse spatial modes ($ ext{LP}_{11}, ext{LP}_{21}$) begin propagating at different group velocities, causing severe intermodal pulse spreading.
