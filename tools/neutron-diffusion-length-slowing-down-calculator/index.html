Thermalization & Neutron Economy

Frequently asked questions

What is Fermi Age (τ)?

Fermi age ($ au$) is the slowing-down area representing the mean squared distance a fast 2 MeV fission neutron travels while slowing down to thermal energy (0.025 eV).
