M.B. Standing 1947 California PVT Correlation

The bubble point pressure ($P_b$) is the thermodynamic threshold where the first bubble of dissolved gas nucleates out of liquid crude oil; producing below $P_b$ releases free gas in the reservoir pore space, reducing oil relative permeability.

Frequently asked questions

Why is producing above bubble point pressure preferred?

Above $P_b$, single-phase oil flows with minimum capillary resistance and maximum relative permeability.
