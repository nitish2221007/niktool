George Brayton 1872 Constant-Pressure Thermodynamic Cycle

The Brayton cycle models jet engines and power-plant gas turbines; higher compressor pressure ratios ($r_p$) directly increase thermal efficiency ($1 - r_p^{- rac{gamma-1}{gamma}}$).

Frequently asked questions

What limits the maximum pressure ratio in real jet engines?

Extremely high pressure ratios increase compressor exit temperature $T_2$, leaving less margin for fuel combustion before exceeding turbine blade melting limits.
