Frequently asked questions

What is a kinematic singularity in a 2R robot arm?

Singularities occur at full extension ($r = l_1 + l_2$) or full retraction ($r = |l_1 - l_2|$), where the arm loses a degree of freedom and the Jacobian determinant becomes zero.
