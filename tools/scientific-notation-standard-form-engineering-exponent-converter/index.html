Handling Astronomical and Subatomic Scales

Scientific notation prevents counting errors when working with vast numbers like the speed of light ($3.0 imes 10^8 ext{ m/s}$) or minuscule constants like the elementary electron charge ($1.602 imes 10^{-19} ext{ C}$).

Frequently asked questions

How does Engineering Notation differ from Scientific Notation?

In Engineering Notation, the exponent must always be a multiple of 3 ($10^3, 10^6, 10^{-3}, 10^{-6}$), directly matching metric prefixes like kilo, mega, milli, and micro.
