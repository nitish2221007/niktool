Édouard Roche 1848 Gravitational Tidal Limit

When a moon crosses inside its parent planet's Roche limit, the differential gravitational tidal pull across the moon's diameter exceeds its own self-gravitational binding force, tearing the moon apart into millions of fragments that spread into planar planetary ring discs.

Frequently asked questions

Why are Saturn's rings located inside its Roche limit?

Saturn's prominent main rings (A, B, C) lie entirely within Saturn's fluid Roche limit of ~140,000 km, preventing ring particles from coalescing into a single large moon.
