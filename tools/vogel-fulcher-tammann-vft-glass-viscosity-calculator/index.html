H. Vogel, G.S. Fulcher & G. Tammann 1925 Glass Viscosity Law

Unlike simple Arrhenius liquids, supercooled glass melts show steep non-Arrhenius viscosity curves modeled by VFT ($log_{10}eta = A + rac{B}{T-T_0}$); controlling the "working range" ($log_{10}eta = 4 ext{ to }7.6$) is critical for automated blow molding of billions of soda-lime beverage bottles.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Littleton Softening Point?

The Softening Point ($log_{10}eta = 7.6 ext{ dPa}cdot ext{s}$, $sim 720^circ ext{C}$ for soda-lime) is the standardized temperature where a 0.65 mm glass fiber elongates under its own weight at 1.0 mm/min.
