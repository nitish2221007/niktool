How to use CAPM Expected Return (E(R_i) = R_f + β_i·(E(R_m) - R_f)) & Jensen Alpha Calculator

  1. Enter risk-free benchmark discount rate $R_f$ in % (e.g. 10-Year US Treasury yield).
  2. Enter asset systematic covariance market Beta $eta$ ($eta=1.0$ matches the market index).
  3. Enter expected broad market equity return $E(R_m)$ in %.
  4. Enter actual annualized investment portfolio return in %.
  5. Inspect CAPM required hurdle rate / cost of equity $E(R_i)$ and risk-adjusted Jensen's Alpha $alpha$.

William Sharpe 1964 Capital Asset Pricing Model

CAPM establishes that investors only receive compensation for non-diversifiable systematic market risk ($eta$); idiosyncratic firm-specific risk can be eliminated entirely through portfolio diversification without expected return sacrifice.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Beta > 1.0 indicate?

A Beta of 1.25 means the stock is 25% more volatile than the broad market; when the index rises 10%, the stock is expected to gain 12.5%.
