What is the bearing capacity of pure saturated clay under undrained loading (phi = 0)?

Under undrained conditions ($phi = 0$), $N_q = 1.0$, $N_gamma = 0$, and $N_c = 5.14$, yielding $q_{ult} = 5.14 c_u + gamma D_f$.
