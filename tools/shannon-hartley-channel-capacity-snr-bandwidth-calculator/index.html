Frequently asked questions

What is the Shannon-Hartley Theorem?

It establishes the absolute mathematical upper limit on data rate for an AWGN communication channel of specified bandwidth and SNR.

Can real systems exceed the Shannon limit?

No, Shannon capacity is a strict physical upper bound that error-correction codes approach but cannot exceed.

What happens if you increase bandwidth to infinity?

Capacity approaches a ultimate limit C_max = 1.44 * (P / N0) as bandwidth approaches infinity.
