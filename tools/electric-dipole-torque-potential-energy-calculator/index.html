Microwave Oven Dielectric Heating Principle

Microwave ovens oscillate high-frequency electric fields at 2.45 GHz, applying alternating torque on polar water molecules in food; the resulting molecular friction rapidly heats food throughout.

Frequently asked questions

When is dipole potential energy minimized?

When the dipole moment aligns perfectly parallel with the electric field (θ = 0°), potential energy reaches its minimum U = -p·E.
