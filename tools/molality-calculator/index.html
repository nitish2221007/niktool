Why Molality is Temperature-Independent

Unlike Molarity (moles/Liter), which changes as liquid volume expands or contracts with temperature, Molality (moles/kg) depends strictly on mass and remains completely invariant to temperature shifts.

Frequently asked questions

When is molality required over molarity?

Molality is mandatory when calculating colligative properties like boiling point elevation and freezing point depression.
