How to use Batch Liquid Media Thermal Sterilization Del Factor (∇ = ln(N₀ / N_t)) Calculator

  1. Enter steam sterilization temperature in Celsius (typically 121°C for standard autoclaves, 140°C for Continuous HTST Sterilizers).
  2. Enter total initial heat-resistant bacterial spore bioburden count $N_0$ across the liquid batch (e.g. $10^{11}$ spores in a $10 ext{ m}^3$ fermenter).
  3. Enter maximum acceptable statistical contamination failure risk probability $N_t$ (typically $10^{-3} = 1 ext{ in }1,000$ batches).
  4. Inspect required Del Sterilization Factor ($ abla = ln(N_0/N_t)$) and necessary isothermal sterilization holding time in minutes.

Aiba & Humphrey Arrhenius Thermal Spore Inactivation

Sterilization kinetics follow first-order logarithmic spore death ($dN/dt = -k N$); high-temperature short-time (HTST 140°C for 30 seconds) sterilization delivers the required Del factor ($ abla approx 32$) with significantly less vitamin and protein thermal nutrient degradation than 121°C autoclaving for 20 minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Nt set to a fraction less than 1 (e.g. 10⁻³)?

$N_t < 1$ represents the statistical probability of a surviving spore; $N_t = 10^{-3}$ guarantees a $99.9%$ confidence that zero viable spores remain in the batch.
