Gravitational Potential Energy Conversion

Hydroelectric turbines convert the gravitational potential energy of falling water (E = mgh) into rotational shaft torque with exceptional thermodynamic conversion efficiencies exceeding 90%.

Frequently asked questions

What power is generated by 1 m³/s falling through a 100m head at 85% efficiency?

P = 0.85 × 1,000 kg/m³ × 9.81 m/s² × 1 m³/s × 100m ≈ 833.8 kW.
