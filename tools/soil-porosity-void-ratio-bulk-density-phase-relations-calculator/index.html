Frequently asked questions

What is the theoretical range of porosity n vs void ratio e?

Porosity $n$ is always strictly bounded between $0%$ and $100%$ ($n < 1$), whereas void ratio $e$ can exceed $1.0$ (e.g. $e > 2.0$ for soft organic clays).
