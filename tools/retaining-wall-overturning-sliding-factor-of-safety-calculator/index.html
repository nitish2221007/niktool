How to use Retaining Wall Stability Factor of Safety (FS_overturn & FS_slide) Calculator

  1. Enter total restoring stabilizing moment $M_R$ about the footing toe in $ ext{kN}cdot ext{m/m}$.
  2. Enter total destabilizing overturning moment $M_O$ in $ ext{kN}cdot ext{m/m}$.
  3. Enter total vertical downward foundation weight $R_v$ in kN/m.
  4. Enter total lateral horizontal soil thrust $P_h$ in kN/m.
  5. Enter base concrete-to-soil friction coefficient $mu$ (typically 0.45 to 0.60).
  6. Inspect structural safety verification factors for Overturning ($FS ge 2.0$) and Base Sliding ($FS ge 1.5$).

Geotechnical Retaining Wall Stability Verification

Building codes (IBC / Eurocode 7) mandate strict minimum safety margins ($FS_{ ext{overturn}} ge 2.0, FS_{ ext{sliding}} ge 1.5$) to guard against soil saturation, hydrostatic water pressure buildup, and earthquake ground shaking.

Frequently asked questions

How do civil engineers fix a sliding failure (FS_slide < 1.5)?

By extending the footing heel/toe width, casting a concrete shear key beneath the base slab, or installing tieback soil anchors.
