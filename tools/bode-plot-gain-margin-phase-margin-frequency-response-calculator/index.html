How to use Bode Plot Gain Margin (GM) & Phase Margin (PM) Frequency Stability Calculator

  1. Enter open-loop phase angle $angle G(jomega_{gc})$ in degrees at the Gain Crossover Frequency (where $|G| = 0 ext{ dB}$).
  2. Enter open-loop logarithmic magnitude $|G(jomega_{pc})|$ in dB at the Phase Crossover Frequency (where $angle G = -180^circ$).
  3. Inspect Phase Margin ($PM = 180^circ + angle G$) and Gain Margin ($GM = -|G|_{ ext{dB}}$) to verify closed-loop asymptotic stability.

Hendrik Wade Bode 1938 Frequency Response Criterion

Bode stability margins measure how close a feedback loop is to the critical Barkhausen point (Gain $= 1 = 0 ext{ dB}$, Phase $=-180^circ$); maintaining $PM ge 45^circ ext{ to }60^circ$ guarantees stable flight control and robotics trajectories.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if Phase Margin is negative (PM < 0)?

A negative phase margin means the feedback signal is inverted into regenerative positive feedback before loop gain drops below 1, driving violent instability.
