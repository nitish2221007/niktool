  4. Inspect resulting capacitance in picoFarads (pF) or nanoFarads (nF).

Dielectric Dipole Polarization

Inserting a dielectric insulator between capacitor plates causes molecular electric dipoles to align against the applied electric field, reducing internal field strength and allowing more charge to accumulate for the same applied voltage.
