Albert Atterberg & Arthur Casagrande Soil Consistency

Atterberg limits quantify how fine-grained soils transition between solid, semi-solid, plastic, and liquid states; Casagrande A-line (PI = 0.73(LL - 20)) mathematically separates true cohesive clays from inorganic silts.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Liquidity Index (LI) greater than 1.0 indicate?

When LI > 1.0, natural moisture exceeds the liquid limit, indicating extremely sensitive quick clays prone to catastrophic structural liquefaction upon disturbance.
