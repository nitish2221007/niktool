Pappus's Centroid Theorem for a Torus

Pappus's Theorem states that revolving a circle of area (π·r²) around an axis distance R sweeps a volume equal to: Area × Distance traveled by centroid = (π·r²) × (2π·R) = 2π²·R·r².

Frequently asked questions

What happens if r equals R in a torus?

When r = R, the central hole closes, forming a horn torus.
