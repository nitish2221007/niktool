Integrated Rate Laws & Half-Life Physics

For first-order processes (like radioactive decay and pharmacological drug clearance), half-life is strictly constant and independent of initial concentration ($t_{1/2} = rac{ln 2}{k}$), whereas for second-order reactions half-life doubles as reactant is consumed.

Frequently asked questions

Why is first-order half-life independent of concentration?

Because the decay rate is strictly proportional to the number of remaining molecules, yielding an exponential decay function whose half-life depends purely on the rate constant k.
