Hans R. Stoll 1969 Put-Call Parity Theorem

Put-Call Parity is a model-free fundamental law of quantitative finance: holding a fiduciary call and zero-coupon bond produces the exact same terminal payoff as a protective put and stock, preventing cross-market arbitrage pricing discrepancies.

Frequently asked questions

Does Put-Call Parity apply to American options with early exercise?

No; for American options with early exercise rights, Put-Call Parity becomes an inequality ($S - K le C - P le S - K e^{-rT}$).
