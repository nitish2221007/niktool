How to use Soil Swell, Bulking & Shrinkage Factor Calculator

  1. Select soil material type (Common Earth, Heavy Clay, Sand/Gravel, Blasted Rock).
  2. Enter in-situ virgin bank excavation cut volume (BCY or BCM).
  3. Inspect loose truck haul volume (LCY) and compacted embankment fill volume (CCY).

Earthwork Cut-to-Fill Mass Balancing

Virgin soil expands when dug out because air voids are introduced (Swell/Bulking), and shrinks below original bank volume when rolled and compacted with heavy vibratory compactors on an embankment.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1,000 BCY of heavy clay in loose haul volume?

With 25% swell: LCY = 1,000 × 1.25 = 1,250 Loose Cubic Yards.
