Henri Pitot 1732 Differential Pressure Airspeed Standard

Pitot-static tubes convert fluid stagnation kinetic energy into readable pressure differences ($Delta P = rac{1}{2} ho v^2$), driving all aircraft primary airspeed indicators (ASI).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Indicated Airspeed (IAS) and True Airspeed (TAS)?

IAS measures dynamic pressure assuming standard sea-level density; TAS is the actual physical speed through the airmass ($v_{ ext{TAS}} = v_{ ext{IAS}} / sqrt{ ho/ ho_0}$).
