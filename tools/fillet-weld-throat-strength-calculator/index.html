0.707 Throat Dimension Geometry

For a standard 45° equal-leg fillet weld, failure occurs along the minimum root-to-face throat dimension (t_e = w · cos(45°) = 0.707 · w); E70XX weld metal delivers 1.392 kips/inch for every 1/16th inch of leg size.
