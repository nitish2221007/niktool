Sir Arthur Eddington 1924 Mass-Luminosity Law

Massive stars burn through core hydrogen at rates proportional to $M^{3.5}$; a $10 M_odot$ blue supergiant shines $3,000 imes$ brighter than the Sun and exhausts its nuclear fuel in only $30 ext{ million years}$ before going supernova.

Frequently asked questions

Why do low-mass red dwarfs live for trillions of years?

Red dwarfs ($0.1 M_odot$) have low core temperatures, burning hydrogen at a minuscule rate while remaining fully convective, enabling lifespans exceeding $1 ext{ to }10 ext{ trillion years}$.
