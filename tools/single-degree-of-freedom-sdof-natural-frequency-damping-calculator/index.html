How to use Single Degree of Freedom (SDOF) Natural Frequency & Damping Calculator

  1. Enter total lumped tributary floor mass in Tonnes.
  2. Enter lateral column structural spring stiffness k in kN/m.
  3. Enter structural modal damping ratio $zeta$ in % (typically 2% for bare steel, 5% for reinforced concrete, 10%+ for isolated structures).
  4. Inspect fundamental natural period $T_n$ in seconds, natural frequency in Hz, and critical damping threshold.

Harmonic Oscillator Resonance Physics

Determining a building's fundamental natural period ($T_n approx 0.1 imes ext{Number of Stories}$) is the critical first step in earthquake structural design to avoid catastrophic dynamic resonance matching peak seismic ground acceleration frequencies.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 5% damping standard for civil concrete structures?

Micro-cracking in concrete, structural joint friction, and architectural partition walls dissipate mechanical vibration energy, providing ~5% equivalent viscous damping.
