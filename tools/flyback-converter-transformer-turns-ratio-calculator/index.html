Galvanic Isolation with Coupled Energy Storage

Unlike standard power transformers that transfer energy instantaneously, Flyback transformers are coupled gapped inductors that store energy in the core during the primary ON-time and release it to the secondary isolated load during the OFF-time.

Frequently asked questions

Why is maximum duty cycle typically capped at D_max = 0.45 in flybacks?

Capping duty cycle below 50% allows the transformer core sufficient demagnetization reset time during the OFF interval to prevent magnetic saturation staircase runaway.
