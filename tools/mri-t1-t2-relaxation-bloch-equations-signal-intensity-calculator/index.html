Frequently asked questions

Why does CSF appear black on T1 and bright white on T2?

Pure fluid (CSF) has long $T_1$ ($4000 ext{ ms}$), so it recovers very little signal during short TR on T1-weighted scans, but has long $T_2$ ($2000 ext{ ms}$), retaining strong signal on long-TE T2-weighted scans.
