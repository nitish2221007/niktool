OSHA 5 dB Exchange Rate Standard

OSHA regulations enforce a 5 dB halving rule: maximum permissible daily exposure is 8 hours at 90 dBA, 4 hours at 95 dBA, 2 hours at 100 dBA, and only 1 hour at 105 dBA.

Frequently asked questions

What is the OSHA Action Level for hearing conservation?

An 8-hour TWA of 85 dBA (equivalent to a 50% daily noise dose) mandates mandatory annual audiometric hearing testing and employee training.
