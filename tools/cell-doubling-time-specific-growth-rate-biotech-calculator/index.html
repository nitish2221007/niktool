Frequently asked questions

What is typical doubling time for mammalian CHO cells vs E. coli bacteria?

Mammalian CHO cells double every $18 ext{ to }24 ext{ hours}$, whereas *E. coli* bacteria double rapidly every $20 ext{ to }30 ext{ minutes}$.
