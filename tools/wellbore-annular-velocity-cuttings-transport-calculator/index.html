Preventing Stuck Drill Strings & Pack-Offs

Maintaining an annular mud velocity of at least 120-180 ft/min ensures drill cuttings are carried upward faster than their gravitational slip velocity, preventing cuttings bed accumulation in deviated shale wells.

Frequently asked questions

What is the annular velocity for 500 GPM in an 8.5" hole with 5" pipe?

AV = (24.5 × 500) / (8.5² - 5.0²) = 12,250 / (72.25 - 25) = 12,250 / 47.25 ≈ 259.3 ft/min.
