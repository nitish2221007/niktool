How to use Glover-Dumm Agricultural Subsurface Drain Spacing Calculator

  1. Enter soil saturated hydraulic conductivity K in m/day.
  2. Enter drainable porosity / specific yield $mu$ (typically 0.05 for clay, 0.12 for loam).
  3. Enter equivalent depth to impermeable barrier d in meters.
  4. Enter target drawdown time t in days (typically 1–2 days after heavy rainfall).
  5. Enter target water table head reduction ratio ($h_t / h_0$, e.g. 0.50 for $50%$ drop).
  6. Inspect calculated drain lateral pipe spacing L in meters.

Robert E. Glover & L. D. Dumm 1954 Drainage Design Standard

Frequently asked questions

Why is rapid 24-48 hour water table drawdown essential for crops?

Plant root systems suffocate without oxygen when submerged in waterlogged soils for longer than 48 hours.
