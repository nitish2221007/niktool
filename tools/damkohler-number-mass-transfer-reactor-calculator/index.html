Gerhard Damköhler's 1936 Dimensionless Number

The Damköhler number relates the chemical reaction rate timescale to the fluid convective transport timescale; when $Da_I > 2$, reactors achieve over 85-95% chemical conversion.

Frequently asked questions

What is the conversion in a CSTR when Da = 1.0?

For a first-order reaction, $X = Da / (1 + Da) = 1.0 / (1 + 1.0) = 50.0%$ conversion.
