OpenGL & Direct3D Frustum Mapping

The 4x4 perspective projection matrix maps a truncated view frustum into a canonical (-1 to +1) Normalized Device Coordinate cube; dividing by homogeneous coordinates (w = -z) shrinks distant geometry to create realistic 3D perspective depth.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why does setting near plane too close cause Z-fighting?

Perspective depth precision is non-linear (1/z distribution); placing the near plane too close consumes nearly all 24-bit depth buffer precision near the camera.
