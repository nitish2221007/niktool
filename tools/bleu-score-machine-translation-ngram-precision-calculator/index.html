Kishore Papineni et al. 2002 BLEU Metric

Evaluates n-gram overlap while penalizing artificially truncated outputs via Brevity Penalty, providing the benchmark standard for neural machine translation research.

Frequently asked questions

What is considered a good BLEU score in machine translation?

BLEU scores above 40 indicate high-quality, fluent translations; human translators typically score between 50 and 60 against another reference translator.
