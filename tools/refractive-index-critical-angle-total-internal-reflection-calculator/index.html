How to use Refractive Index, Critical Angle & Total Internal Reflection (TIR) Calculator

  1. Enter refractive index of dense core medium $n_1$ (e.g. 1.50 for glass, 2.42 for diamond, 1.48 for fiber core).
  2. Enter refractive index of rarer cladding/outer medium $n_2$ (e.g. 1.00 for air, 1.33 for water, 1.46 for fiber cladding).
  3. Inspect critical angle $ heta_c = arcsin(n_2 / n_1)$ and light propagation speed in the medium.

Fiber Optic Communications & Diamond Optics

When light strikes a boundary from a denser medium at an incidence angle greater than the critical angle ($ heta_i > heta_c$), zero refraction occurs and 100% of light undergoes lossless Total Internal Reflection (TIR), enabling high-speed transoceanic internet optical fiber cables.

Frequently asked questions

Why do diamonds sparkle with intense brilliance?

Diamond has an extremely high refractive index ($n = 2.42$), resulting in a tiny critical angle ($ heta_c = 24.4^circ$) that traps and reflects light repeatedly before exiting the top facets.
