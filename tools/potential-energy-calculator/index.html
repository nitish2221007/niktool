How to use Potential Energy Calculator

  1. Enter the mass of the object in kilograms.
  2. Enter the height in metres and optionally adjust the value of g.
  3. Click Process to calculate potential energy using PE = mgh.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator applies PE = mgh with a configurable gravity value (defaulting to 9.8 m/s²), showing the formula and every substitution. It validates inputs and runs fully locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it for the Work and Energy chapter to check numericals involving objects at height.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
