Frequently asked questions

What causes inductive flyback voltage spikes when opening a circuit?

Attempting to instantly interrupt inductor current ($dt ightarrow 0$) creates a huge back-EMF spike ($V = -L rac{dI}{dt}$) that can spark switches or destroy transistors without a flyback snubber diode.
