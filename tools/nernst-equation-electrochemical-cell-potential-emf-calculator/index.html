Walther Nernst 1889 Electrochemistry Formulation

The Nernst equation connects chemical concentration gradients with electrical potential ($E = E^circ - rac{RT}{nF} ln Q$), providing the operating principle for lithium-ion batteries, biological neuron action potentials, and laboratory pH glass electrodes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a dead battery in thermodynamic terms?

A dead battery has reached chemical equilibrium ($Q = K_{ ext{eq}}$), making the cell potential $E = 0 ext{ V}$ and $Delta G = 0$.
