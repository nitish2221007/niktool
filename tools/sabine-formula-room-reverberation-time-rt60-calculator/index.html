Frequently asked questions

What is the Eyring equation modification for highly absorbent rooms?

Carl F. Eyring modified Sabine's formula for dead rooms ($alpha > 0.3$) to $RT_{60} = rac{0.161 V}{-S ln(1 - alpha)}$.
