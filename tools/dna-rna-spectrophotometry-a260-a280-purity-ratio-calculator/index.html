UV-Vis Beer-Lambert Nucleic Acid Photometry

Aromatic nitrogenous purine/pyrimidine bases absorb strongly at $260 ext{ nm}$, whereas aromatic amino acids (Trp, Tyr) absorb at $280 ext{ nm}$, providing a non-destructive method to quantify DNA purity.

Frequently asked questions

What does a low A260/A230 ratio (< 1.8) mean?

A low $A_{260}/A_{230}$ ratio indicates residual guanidine thiocyanate salts, phenol, or carbohydrates from column extraction.
