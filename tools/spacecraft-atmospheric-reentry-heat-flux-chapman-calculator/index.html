H. Julian Allen & A. J. Eggers 1953 Blunt Body Concept

Allen and Eggers discovered that blunt heat shields ($q propto 1/sqrt{R_n}$) push the detached bow shock wave away from the vehicle hull, dissipating $99%$ of kinetic friction heating into the surrounding shock layer air.

Frequently asked questions

Why is heat flux lower on blunt capsules than sharp pointed missiles?

A blunt nose radius creates a thick detached shock layer that acts as a thermal cushion, transferring only $1%$ of total kinetic energy to the shield.
