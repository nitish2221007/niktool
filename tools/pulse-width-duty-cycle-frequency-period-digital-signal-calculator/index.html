Frequently asked questions

What is Duty Cycle in PWM signals?

Duty cycle is the percentage of one full signal period during which the digital signal remains active (HIGH).

How is PWM used in microcontroller systems?

PWM is used for motor speed control, LED dimming, switching power supplies, and digital-to-analog audio output.

What happens at 100% duty cycle?

At 100% duty cycle, the signal remains constantly HIGH, behaving as a continuous DC voltage.
