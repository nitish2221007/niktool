How to use Aspect Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter the original width and height in pixels (e.g. 1920 × 1080).
  2. Inspect the simplified aspect ratio (16:9).
  3. Enter a new target width or height in the second box to auto-calculate the proportional dimension.

Standard Screen Aspect Ratios

Common aspect ratios include 16:9 (High-Definition TV & YouTube), 4:3 (Legacy TV & iPad displays), 21:9 (Ultrawide Cinema), 9:16 (Instagram Reels & TikTok videos), and 1:1 (Square social posts).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How is aspect ratio calculated?

Divide both width and height by their greatest common divisor (GCD).
