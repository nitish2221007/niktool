How to use Marks Needed Calculator

  1. Enter your current marks and the total marks for exams/tests you've completed.
  2. Enter your target percentage (e.g., 85 for 85%).
  3. Enter the total marks available in remaining exams/tests.
  4. Click Calculate Required Marks to see how many marks you need.

Key Features & Privacy

This calculator helps you plan your exam strategy by showing exactly how many marks you need to achieve your target percentage. All calculations happen locally in your browser, keeping your academic goals private.

Common Use Cases

Students use this tool to set realistic targets, understand how much they need to score in upcoming exams, and plan their study priorities based on remaining marks available.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Enter your current marks, current total, target percentage, and remaining total marks, then click Calculate Required Marks.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser.

What if the target is not achievable?

The tool will clearly show that your target requires more marks than are available in remaining exams.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline.
