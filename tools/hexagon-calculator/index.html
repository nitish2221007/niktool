Regular Hexagon Formulas

A regular hexagon consists of 6 congruent equilateral triangles. Its internal angles are each 120°, and its area equals (3√3 / 2) × a².

What is the sum of interior angles in a hexagon?

The sum of all 6 interior angles in any hexagon is 720 degrees.
