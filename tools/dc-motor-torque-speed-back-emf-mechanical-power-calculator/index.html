How to use DC Motor Torque-Speed Curve, Back-EMF & Mechanical Power Calculator

  1. Enter motor DC terminal power supply voltage in Volts.
  2. Enter armature coil winding phase resistance R in Ohms.
  3. Enter motor Torque Constant $K_t$ in $ ext{N}cdot ext{m}/ ext{A}$ (equivalent to Back-EMF constant $K_e$ in $ ext{V}/( ext{rad/s})$ in SI units).
  4. Enter applied mechanical load torque in $ ext{N}cdot ext{m}$.
  5. Inspect operating motor shaft speed in RPM, mechanical shaft power output in Watts, and electrical efficiency.

Electromechanical Energy Conversion & Back-EMF Law

Generates the linear DC motor operating curve ($omega = omega_0 - rac{R}{K_t K_e} T$), balancing mechanical torque against induced opposing electromagnetic Back-EMF voltage.

Frequently asked questions

At what operating speed is maximum mechanical power achieved?

Maximum mechanical output power occurs exactly at $50%$ of no-load speed and $50%$ of stall torque ($P_{max} = rac{1}{4} T_{ ext{stall}} omega_0$).
