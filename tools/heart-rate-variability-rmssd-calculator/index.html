Vagal Nerve Parasympathetic Activity

Higher Heart Rate Variability (RMSSD > 50ms) reflects healthy vagal tone, high physical recovery, and readiness for athletic strain; low HRV signals accumulated physical exhaustion, illness, or systemic stress.
