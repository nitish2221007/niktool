Why is 15 keV the optimal temperature for D-T fusion?

At 15 keV ($sim 170 ext{ Million }^circ ext{C}$), the D-T fusion cross-section $langlesigma v angle$ reaches its maximum peak relative to thermal bremsstrahlung loss.
