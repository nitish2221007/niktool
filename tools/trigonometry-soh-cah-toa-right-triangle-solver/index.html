SOH-CAH-TOA High School Trigonometry Mnemonic

SOH ($sin heta = rac{O}{H}$), CAH ($cos heta = rac{A}{H}$), and TOA ($ an heta = rac{O}{A}$) allow students to resolve any right triangle or 2D physics vector into perpendicular x- and y-components.

Frequently asked questions

What are the exact trigonometric values for a 30-60-90 triangle?

For $30^circ$: $sin 30^circ = 1/2 = 0.5$, $cos 30^circ = sqrt{3}/2 approx 0.866$, and $ an 30^circ = 1/sqrt{3} approx 0.577$.
