Frequently asked questions

What is the specific gravity of a 50% solids slurry with ore SG = 2.70?

SG_slurry = 100 / ((50 / 2.70) + 50) = 100 / (18.52 + 50) = 100 / 68.52 ≈ 1.459 SG.
