Ernest Thiele 1939 Heterogeneous Reaction Theory

When chemical reactions occur rapidly compared to molecular pore diffusion ($phi > 3$), reactants consume before penetrating the catalyst pellet core, reducing overall catalyst utilization ($eta ll 1$) and wasting expensive precious metals (Pt/Pd).

How do chemical engineers solve strong diffusion limitations?
