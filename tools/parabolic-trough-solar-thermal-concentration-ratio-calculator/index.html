Concentrated Solar Thermal Power (CSP) Engineering

Parabolic trough mirrors focus direct sunlight ($DNI$) onto linear evacuated receiver tubes at 26× concentration, heating synthetic thermal oil or molten salt to 400°C–550°C to drive steam turbine generators with multi-hour thermal energy storage.

Frequently asked questions

Why is DNI used instead of Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) in CSP?

Focusing optical mirrors can only reflect parallel direct sunlight beams; diffuse scattered sky radiation cannot be concentrated by parabolic optics.
