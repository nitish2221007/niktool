Magnetic Gyro-Resonant Electrodynamics Standard

Electrons corkscrewing around magnetic field lines emit microwave synchrotron radiation ($f_{ce} approx 28 ext{ GHz/Tesla}$), which is re-absorbed by the plasma or used for high-power gyrotron microwave heating (ECRH).

Frequently asked questions

Why is cyclotron radiation mostly self-absorbed in dense tokamak plasmas?

High plasma opacity traps fundamental cyclotron emission inside the core, so only higher harmonic microwave frequencies escape.
