Frequently asked questions

What is the Three-Polarizer Paradox in quantum physics?

Crossed $0^circ$ and $90^circ$ polarizers block $100%$ of light; inserting a third $45^circ$ polarizer between them allows $12.5%$ of light to pass through by rotating the quantum state vector.
