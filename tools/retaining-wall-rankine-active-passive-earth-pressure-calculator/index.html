William John Macquorn Rankine 1857 Earth Pressure Theory

Rankine assumed a frictionless vertical wall retaining cohesionless soil in plastic equilibrium, demonstrating that triangular soil pressure creates a resultant thrust located at one-third height ($H/3$), which must be balanced to prevent sliding and overturning collapse.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Active and Passive earth pressure?

Active pressure ($K_a < 1$) occurs when the wall tilts away from the backfill; passive pressure ($K_p > 1$) occurs when the wall pushes into the soil mass, developing enormous resistance.
