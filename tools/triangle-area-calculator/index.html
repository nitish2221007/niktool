How to use Triangle Area Calculator

  1. Choose the method: Base and Height, or Three Sides using Heron's formula.
  2. Enter the required values for the chosen method.
  3. Click Process to see the area with the formula and substitution shown.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator supports both standard Class 10 approaches and validates that three sides actually form a triangle before applying Heron's formula. It shows the semi-perimeter and every step, and runs fully locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use it for the Triangles and mensuration chapters, to verify homework, and to understand when Heron's formula is the right approach.
