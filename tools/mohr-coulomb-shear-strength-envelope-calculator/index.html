Charles-Augustin de Coulomb 1776 Shear Failure Law

Soil shearing resistance derives from two physical mechanisms: interparticle cementation/electrostatic bonding (cohesion c) and mechanical grain interlocking friction that increases linearly with confining overburden pressure.

Frequently asked questions

What is the friction angle of pure dry sand?

Clean dry sand has zero cohesion (c = 0) and an internal friction angle ϕ typically between 30° (loose) and 40° (dense compacted).
