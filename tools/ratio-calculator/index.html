How to use Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter two or more positive integers separated by commas.
  2. Click Process to simplify the ratio using the HCF.
  3. Review the simplified ratio and an equivalent ratio for verification.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator finds the HCF of all values and divides each term to produce the simplest whole-number ratio, then shows an equivalent ratio to confirm the relationship. Worked steps help you learn the method, not just the answer. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it to simplify ratios in mensuration, trigonometry, and word problems, and to check homework answers quickly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
