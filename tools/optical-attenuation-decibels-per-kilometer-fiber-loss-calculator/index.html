How to use Optical Fiber Attenuation (dB/km) & Link Budget Power Loss Calculator

  1. Enter transmitter laser optical launch power in dBm (0 dBm = 1.0 mW).
  2. Enter total single-mode fiber route length L in kilometers.
  3. Enter fiber cable attenuation $alpha$ in dB/km (typically 0.20 dB/km at 1550 nm, 0.35 dB/km at 1310 nm).
  4. Enter number of optical patch connectors along the link (0.5 dB loss each).
  5. Enter receiver optical sensitivity in dBm (e.g. -24 dBm for $10^{-12}$ BER).
  6. Inspect received optical power $P_{ ext{rx}}$ and net optical safety link margin in dB.

Frequently asked questions

Why is fiber attenuation lowest at 1550 nm?

1550 nm sits at the fundamental minimum where short-wavelength Rayleigh scattering ($propto 1/lambda^4$) balances long-wavelength silica infrared vibrational absorption.
