How to use Margin & Markup Calculator

  1. Enter the Cost Price of your product or service.
  2. Enter either your target Selling Price or desired Profit Margin percentage.
  3. Click Calculate to instantly view your gross profit, markup, and margin.

Difference between Margin and Markup

Margin is the ratio of profit to revenue (Selling Price), whereas Markup is the percentage added on top of the Cost Price. For instance, a 50% markup on a $100 product gives a $150 selling price, which translates to a 33.33% margin.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the margin formula?

Margin = ((Selling Price - Cost) / Selling Price) × 100

What is the markup formula?

Markup = ((Selling Price - Cost) / Cost) × 100
