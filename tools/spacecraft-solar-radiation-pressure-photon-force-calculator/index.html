James Clerk Maxwell 1873 Photon Momentum Standard

Photons carry momentum ($p = E/c$), creating a continuous propellantless radiation pressure force ($4.54 mu ext{N/m}^2$ for absorption, $9.08 mu ext{N/m}^2$ for reflection) utilized by solar sails (NASA ACS3, JAXA IKAROS).

Frequently asked questions

Why is photon force doubled for a reflective mirror vs black absorber?

Reflecting a photon reverses its momentum vector from $+p$ to $-p$, transferring double momentum ($Delta p = 2p$) via elastic collision compared to absorption ($Delta p = p$).
