Frequently asked questions

What causes a Right Shift in the Oxyhemoglobin Dissociation curve?

Increased temperature, acidosis (low pH / Bohr effect), elevated $CO_2$, and high 2,3-DPG shift the curve right (increase $P_{50}$), releasing more $O_2$ to exercising tissues.
