Historical Clinical Pediatric Dosing Rules

Frequently asked questions

Why is mg/kg dosing preferred over Clark or Young rules in modern pediatrics?

Modern clinical pediatrics doses medications strictly per kilogram ($mg/kg$) or body surface area ($mg/m^2$) for precise therapeutic index control.
