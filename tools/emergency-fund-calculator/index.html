Why Emergency Funds Are Liquid

Emergency funds should be held in high-yield savings accounts or liquid money market funds to guarantee immediate accessibility without market volatility risk during job loss or medical crises.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How many months of expenses should an emergency fund cover?

Financial planners recommend 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses for salaried employees, and 9 to 12 months for freelancers or volatile commissions.
