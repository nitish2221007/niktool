How to use Simply Supported Beam Distributed Load Deflection Calculator

  1. Enter uniformly distributed load w in kiloNewtons per meter (kN/m).
  2. Enter clear span length L in meters.
  3. Enter Young's Modulus E (GPa) and beam section moment of inertia I (cm⁴).
  4. Inspect midspan maximum sag deflection (mm) and structural building code serviceability limit ratio (L/360).

Fourth-Power Span Sensitivity (L⁴)

Because uniform load deflection scales with span to the fourth power (δ ∝ L⁴), increasing floor beam span by just 25% more than doubles center deflection (1.25⁴ ≈ 2.44x).

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard residential floor deflection limit?

Building codes typically enforce a maximum live load deflection limit of L / 360 (e.g. max 16.7 mm for a 6-meter span).
