Double Integration Quadratic & Cubic Error Growth

Inertial navigation calculates position by integrating acceleration twice; uncompensated accelerometer bias errors grow quadratically with time ($x propto t^2$), while gyro angular drift tilts the gravity vector to cause cubic error growth ($x propto t^3$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is Kalman filtering (INS/GNSS coupling) used in aviation?

Extended Kalman Filters (EKF) fuse high-frequency IMU acceleration data with absolute GPS fixes to continuously estimate and subtract out sensor bias errors.
