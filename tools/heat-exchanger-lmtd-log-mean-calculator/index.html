  1. Select heat exchanger flow configuration (Counter-Flow or Parallel-Flow).

  3. Inspect the Logarithmic Mean Temperature Difference (LMTD) driving the heat transfer process.

Why Counter-Flow Yields Superior LMTD

In counter-flow heat exchangers, the exiting hot stream transfers heat to the coldest entering fluid; this maintains a uniform temperature difference throughout the unit, producing a higher LMTD and requiring less surface area than parallel-flow designs.

Frequently asked questions

Can a heat exchanger cold fluid exit hotter than the hot fluid exit?

Yes, in counter-flow heat exchangers, the cold fluid outlet temperature T_c,out can exceed the hot fluid outlet temperature T_h,out.
