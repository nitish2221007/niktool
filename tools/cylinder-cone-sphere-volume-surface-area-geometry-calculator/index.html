Archimedes Sphere and Cylinder Volume Ratio

Archimedes considered his greatest mathematical discovery to be the proof that a sphere has exactly two-thirds the volume and surface area of its circumscribing cylinder ($V_{ ext{sphere}} = rac{2}{3} V_{ ext{cylinder}}$).

How is the slant height of a cone calculated?

Using the Pythagorean theorem: Slant Height $l = sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$.
