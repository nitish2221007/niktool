  2. Enter the critical ultimate oscillation gain (K_u) and measured oscillation wave period (P_u) in seconds.

John Ziegler and Nathaniel Nichols' 1942 Method

The Ziegler-Nichols heuristic provides a standard closed-loop tuning methodology that achieves rapid setpoint tracking with approximately quarter-decay ratio (25% initial overshoot).

Frequently asked questions

What is the full PID tuning formula?

K_p = 0.60 · K_u, T_i = 0.50 · P_u (K_i = K_p / T_i), and T_d = 0.125 · P_u (K_d = K_p · T_d).
