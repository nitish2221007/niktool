R² Quadratic Gravitational Tidal Energy Scaling

Because both water volume and hydrostatic head increase with tidal amplitude, energy generation scales with the square of the tidal range; estuaries with 10 m tides produce 4 times more energy than 5 m tides.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the world's largest operational tidal barrage?

Sihwa Lake Tidal Power Station in South Korea is the world's largest at 254 MW capacity, followed closely by France's 240 MW La Rance Barrage.
