Adipose Tissue Volume of Distribution (Vd)

Adipose tissue possesses ~40% of the extracellular fluid volume of lean vascularized muscle tissue, making 0.4 the standard clinical correction coefficient.

Frequently asked questions

When is AdjBW clinically recommended?

When a patient's actual body weight is greater than 120% of their calculated Devine Ideal Body Weight.
