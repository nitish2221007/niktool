Sir James Jeans 1902 Gravitational Instability Criterion

When gas cloud mass exceeds the Jeans Mass ($M > M_J$), internal thermal pressure can no longer balance self-gravity, causing catastrophic gravitational collapse and stellar cluster birth.

Frequently asked questions

Why do cold, dense clouds collapse into stars while hot clouds do not?

Jeans mass scales as $M_J propto T^{3/2} / sqrt{ ho}$; cold dense gas has low thermal pressure, lowering the mass needed for gravity to trigger collapse.
