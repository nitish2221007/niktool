Beer-Lambert Photon Attenuation Law

High atomic number (Z) and high density materials like Lead ($Z=82, ho=11.3 ext{ g/cm}^3$) maximize photoelectric absorption and Compton scattering cross-sections, compressing required shield wall thickness by 10× compared to concrete.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Tenth-Value Layer (TVL)?

A TVL is the shield thickness required to reduce radiation intensity by 90% (to $1/10 ext{th}$ of original dose), equal to approximately $3.322 imes ext{HVL}$.
