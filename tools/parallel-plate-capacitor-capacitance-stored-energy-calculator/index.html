Electrostatic Energy Storage Physics

Capacitors store electrical potential energy in the organized polarization of the electric field between conductive plates ($U = rac{1}{2} C V^2$), enabling instantaneous high-current power release in camera flashes and medical defibrillators.

Frequently asked questions

Why does inserting a dielectric increase capacitance?

Dielectric molecules polarize and create an opposing internal electric field, reducing voltage for the same charge and thereby increasing capacitance by factor $kappa$ ($C = kappa C_0$).
