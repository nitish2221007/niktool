AISC 360 Plastic Limit State Structural Design

Compact steel sections develop complete plastic hinging across the entire depth before local buckling occurs ($M_p = F_y Z_x$), unlocking an extra $12% ext{ to }15%$ reserve load capacity beyond first yield ($M_y$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the Shape Factor of an I-beam vs a solid rectangle?

A standard wide-flange I-beam has a shape factor of $sim 1.12 ext{–}1.15$, whereas a solid rectangular beam has a shape factor of $1.50$.
