Lester Eli Dubins 1957 Non-Holonomic Shortest Paths

Dubins proved that the shortest path between any initial 2D pose $(x_1, y_1, heta_1)$ and target pose $(x_2, y_2, heta_2)$ for a forward-only vehicle with minimum turning radius $R_{min}$ is always composed of a combination of maximum-curvature circular arcs (C) and straight line segments (S) (e.g. RSR, LSL, RSL, LSR).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Dubins and Reeds-Shepp paths?

Dubins paths permit only forward motion, whereas Reeds-Shepp paths allow both forward and reverse gear motion.
