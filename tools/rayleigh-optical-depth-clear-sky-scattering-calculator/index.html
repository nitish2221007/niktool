Lord Rayleigh 1871 Molecular Dipole Scattering

Because molecular Rayleigh scattering intensity scales as $1/lambda^4$, blue light ($lambda = 450 ext{ nm}$) is scattered 5.5× more strongly than red light ($lambda = 680 ext{ nm}$), creating the blue color of daytime skies and the deep crimson red of evening sunsets as sunlight traverses high air masses ($AM > 10$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is the sky blue instead of violet?

Although violet light ($lambda = 380 ext{ nm}$) is scattered even more strongly, human eye retinal cones are far more sensitive to blue green wavelengths and the solar spectrum peaks in green blue.
