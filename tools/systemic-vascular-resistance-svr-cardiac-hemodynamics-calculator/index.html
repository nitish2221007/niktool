Ohm's Law Fluid Resistance Analog

SVR represents left ventricular afterload resistance ($Delta P / Q$), distinguishing warm distributive septic shock (low SVR) from cold cardiogenic shock (high compensatory SVR).

Frequently asked questions

Why is SVR multiplied by 80?

80 is the unit conversion factor converting mmHg per L/min into metric CGS units of $ ext{dynes}cdot ext{s/cm}^5$.
