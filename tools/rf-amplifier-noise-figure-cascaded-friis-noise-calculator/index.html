Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle F_{\text{sys}} = F_1 + \frac{F_2 - 1}{G_1} + \frac{F_3 - 1}{G_1 G_2}, \quad \text{NF}_{\text{sys}} = 10 \log_{10}(F_{\text{sys}})$$

Frequently asked questions

Why is the first amplifier stage (LNA) critical in RF design?

As shown by Friis formula, the first stage gain G1 divides subsequent noise contributions, making stage 1 Noise Figure dominate total system noise.

What is the difference between Noise Factor and Noise Figure?

Noise factor F is linear ratio F = SNRin / SNRout; Noise figure NF is expressed in decibels NF = 10 log10(F).

Can attenuators be included in Friis noise calculation?

Yes, an attenuator of X dB has a negative gain (-X dB) and a Noise Figure equal to its loss (+X dB).
