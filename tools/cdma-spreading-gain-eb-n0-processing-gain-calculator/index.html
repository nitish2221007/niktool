Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle SF = \frac{R_c}{R_b}, \quad G_p = 10 \log_{10}(SF) \text{ dB}, \quad \text{SNR} = \frac{E_b}{N_0} - G_p$$

Frequently asked questions

What is CDMA Processing Gain?

Processing gain measures how much a spread-spectrum signal improves signal-to-noise ratio over narrow-band noise.

How does processing gain protect against interference?

Despreading spreads narrow-band interference across the wider bandwidth while collapsing the desired signal back to baseband.

Where is CDMA spreading gain used?

It is used in 3G WCDMA, GPS satellite signals, military anti-jamming radios, and radar applications.
