Svante Arrhenius 1889 Chemical Kinetics Law

The Arrhenius equation links molecular collision theory with Boltzmann statistics ($e^{-E_a/RT}$); as a general rule of thumb in organic synthesis, a $10^circ ext{C}$ temperature increase roughly doubles or triples the reaction velocity.

Frequently asked questions

What is the role of a chemical catalyst in the Arrhenius equation?

A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a significantly lower activation energy ($E_a$), exponentially accelerating the rate constant k.
