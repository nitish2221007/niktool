Lord Rayleigh's 1879 Airy Disk Separation Limit

Two distinct point stars are resolved when the center of one star's central Airy diffraction peak falls on the first dark diffraction ring minimum of the other ($\theta = 1.22\lambda / D$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the theoretical resolution of an 8-inch (200 mm) amateur telescope in visible light?

$\theta = 1.22 imes (550 imes 10^{-9}) / 0.200 = 3.355 imes 10^{-6}\text{ radians} = 0.692\text{ arcseconds}$.
