John Hicks 1937 & Alvin Hansen IS-LM Framework

The IS-LM model synthesizes classical monetary theory with Keynesian aggregate demand, explaining how fiscal expansion (G) raises income but crowds out investment via higher interest rates.

Frequently asked questions

What shifts the IS curve vs LM curve?

Fiscal policy (taxes, government spending) shifts the IS curve; monetary policy (central bank money supply) shifts the LM curve.
