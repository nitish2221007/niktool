How to use Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT System of 3 Congruences x ≡ a_i mod m_i) Calculator

  1. Enter remainders $a_1, a_2, a_3$ for the three congruences.
  2. Enter pairwise coprime moduli $m_1, m_2, m_3$ (e.g. 3, 5, 7).
  3. Inspect unique combined solution $x pmod M$ where $M = m_1 cdot m_2 cdot m_3$ and verify congruence satisfaction across all equations.

Frequently asked questions

Why is CRT used in RSA decryption?

RSA decryption with CRT (computing $m pmod p$ and $m pmod q$ separately) accelerates RSA private key operations by roughly $4 imes$.
