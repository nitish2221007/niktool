How to use Refrigeration Carnot Cycle COP & Compressor Power Calculator

  1. Enter refrigerant evaporating saturation temperature T_C in Celsius.
  2. Enter refrigerant condensing saturation temperature T_H in Celsius.
  3. Enter cooling thermal load capacity Q_c in kilowatts (1 Ton of Refrigeration = 3.517 kW).
  4. Enter compressor isentropic second-law efficiency (typically 0.60 to 0.75).
  5. Inspect thermodynamic COP, Energy Efficiency Ratio (EER), and required compressor electrical horsepower.

Nicolas Léonard Sadi Carnot 1824 Second Law Ceiling

The Carnot cycle defines the absolute theoretical ceiling of heat pump and refrigeration performance; minimizing thermal lift by using water-cooled condensers dramatically boosts COP and slashes electricity bills.

Frequently asked questions

Why does lowering condenser temperature save so much electricity?

Every 1°C reduction in condensing temperature improves refrigeration COP by approximately 2% to 4%.
