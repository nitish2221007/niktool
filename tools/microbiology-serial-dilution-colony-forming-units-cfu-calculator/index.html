Microbiological Quantitative Plate Count Standard

In pharmaceutical quality control and food safety testing, serial dilutions reduce dense bacterial suspensions to the statistically robust $30 ext{ to }300$ colony range, ensuring accurate calculation of viable microbial bioburden.

Frequently asked questions

Why are plates with fewer than 30 colonies rejected?

Counts below 30 have high Poisson random sampling variability ($pm 20%+$ error); counts above 300 suffer from nutrient competition where separate colonies merge.
