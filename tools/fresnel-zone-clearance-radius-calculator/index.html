Augustin-Jean Fresnel 1818 Wave Optics Clearance

Electromagnetic energy travels through a 3D ellipsoidal volume around the visual optical line of sight; obstructing more than 40% of the First Fresnel Zone causes destructive out-of-phase wave cancellation, severely attenuating radio signal strength.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 60% Fresnel clearance the industry standard?

Clearing 60% of the First Fresnel radius guarantees zero diffraction signal loss and provides equivalent performance to completely open free space.
