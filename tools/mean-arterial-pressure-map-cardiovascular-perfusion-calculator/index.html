ICU Critical Care Perfusion Metric

Because the heart spends approximately two-thirds of the cardiac cycle in diastole and one-third in systole ($MAP = DBP + rac{1}{3}PP$), MAP provides a much more accurate physiological measure of capillary organ blood flow than systolic pressure alone.

Frequently asked questions

Why is MAP >= 65 mmHg the standard clinical goal in sepsis?

Surviving Sepsis Campaign clinical trials demonstrate that maintaining $MAP ge 65 ext{ mmHg}$ prevents acute tubular necrosis kidney injury and cerebral hypoperfusion.
