Electric Motor Rotor Torque Foundation

In electric DC and AC induction motors, current-carrying wire coils act as magnetic dipoles (μ = N·I·A); the continuous torque exerted by the stator magnetic field (τ = μ × B) generates mechanical shaft rotation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum torque on a magnetic dipole?

Maximum torque occurs at θ = 90° (perpendicular alignment) where sin(90°) = 1, yielding τ_max = μ · B.
