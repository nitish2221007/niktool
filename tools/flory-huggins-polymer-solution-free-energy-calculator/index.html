Paul Flory & Maurice Huggins 1942 Lattice Mixing Model

Because long polymer chains have extremely low combinatorial entropy of mixing ($1/N o 0$), even small positive enthalpy interactions ($chi > 0.5$) cause polymer solutions and plastic blends to phase separate into two immiscible layers.

Frequently asked questions

Why are most plastic polymer blends immiscible?

Mixing two high-molecular-weight polymers ($N_1, N_2 gg 1000$) provides virtually zero entropy of mixing, so any slight positive $chi$ causes immediate phase separation.
