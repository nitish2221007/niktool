Carrier Diffusion vs Drift Equilibrium Barrier

Mobile majority carriers diffuse across the metallurgical junction, exposing uncompensated fixed ionized donor and acceptor space charges that establish the internal electric barrier V_bi ≈ 0.7 V in silicon.

Frequently asked questions

Why is silicon diode forward drop typically ~0.7V?

Because the built-in potential barrier V_bi created by standard doping levels is ~0.7 to 0.8 V, requiring ~0.7 V forward bias to overcome the barrier.
