Compacted Asphalt Density (145 lb/cu ft)

Compacted Hot Mix Asphalt (HMA) weighs approximately 145 pounds per cubic foot (2.32 metric tons per cubic meter); asphalt batch plants dispatch delivery trucks measured strictly in US Tons.
