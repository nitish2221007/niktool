Architectural Domes and Pressure Vessel Tank Heads

Spherical caps form the roofs of classical basilicas and the curved end-caps (dished heads) of high-pressure boiler vessels to distribute internal stress uniformly.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when cap height h = R?

The spherical cap becomes a perfect hemisphere with volume V = ⅔·π·R³.
