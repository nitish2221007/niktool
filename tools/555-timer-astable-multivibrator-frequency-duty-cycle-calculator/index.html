Frequently asked questions

Why is the standard 555 astable duty cycle always greater than 50%?

Because the capacitor charges through both $(R_1 + R_2)$ but discharges only through $R_2$, ensuring $t_{ ext{high}} > t_{ ext{low}}$ unless a bypass diode is placed across $R_2$.
