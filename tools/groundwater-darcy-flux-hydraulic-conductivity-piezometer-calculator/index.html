Because water can only travel through open interconnecting pore voids, the actual chemical pollutant travel speed ($v_s$) is $3 imes$ to $5 imes$ faster than the apparent bulk Darcy discharge ($q$), critical for environmental plume remediation.

Frequently asked questions

Why is seepage velocity (v_s) always larger than Darcy flux (q)?

Darcy flux assumes flow through the entire cross-section, while seepage velocity accounts for the fact that flow occurs only through effective void pores ($v_s = q / n_e$).
