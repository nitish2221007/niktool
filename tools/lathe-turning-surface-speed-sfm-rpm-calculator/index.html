Constant Surface Speed (CSS) vs Fixed RPM

When facing across a cylinder face toward center zero, fixed RPM causes cutting speed to collapse to zero; CNC lathes use Constant Surface Speed (G96) to accelerate the spindle smoothly as diameter shrinks, preserving constant cutting forces.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum G50 RPM clamp on a CNC lathe?

G50 clamps maximum spindle RPM to prevent huge centrifugal forces from causing chuck jaws to release workpieces at small facing diameters.
