How to use Activated Sludge Food-to-Microorganism (F/M) Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter raw wastewater influent flow rate Q in Million Gallons per Day (MGD).
  2. Enter primary effluent / influent BOD concentration in mg/L.
  3. Enter aeration basin volume V in Million Gallons (MG).
  4. Enter Mixed Liquor Volatile Suspended Solids (MLVSS) active bacterial biomass in mg/L (typically 2,000 to 3,500 mg/L).
  5. Inspect calculated F/M ratio and operational process mode (Conventional, Extended Aeration, or High-Rate).

Bacterial Food-to-Microorganism Balance

Maintaining the F/M ratio within design target limits (0.2 to 0.4 for conventional activated sludge) ensures rapid organic carbon removal while producing dense, readily settleable bacterial flocs in secondary clarifiers.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if F/M ratio is too high?

When F/M > 0.5, bacteria enter the log-growth phase with excess dispersed growth, causing cloudy effluent and poor settling.
