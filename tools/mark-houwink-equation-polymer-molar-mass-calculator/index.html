Frequently asked questions

What does a Mark-Houwink exponent a = 0.5 indicate?

$a = 0.50$ indicates the Flory Theta ($ heta$) condition where polymer-solvent interactions exactly balance polymer-polymer segment attractions, producing ideal random Gaussian coils.
