Peter Lynch's PEG Ratio Valuation

Famed Magellan fund manager Peter Lynch popularized the PEG ratio (P/E / Annual Growth Rate): a PEG of 1.0 represents fair value, while PEG < 1.0 suggests an undervalued growth stock.

Frequently asked questions

What is Earnings Yield?

Earnings yield is the inverse of the P/E ratio (E/P = EPS / Price), allowing direct comparison of stock returns against Treasury bond yields.
