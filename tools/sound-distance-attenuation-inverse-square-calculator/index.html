The 6 dB Doubling Distance Rule

In a free-field acoustic environment without reflective boundaries, sound pressure level drops by exactly 6.02 dB for every doubling of distance from the source (20 · log₁₀(2) ≈ 6.02 dB).

Frequently asked questions

What is the dB loss from 1 meter to 4 meters?

Distance quadruples (2 doublings): attenuation loss is 20 · log₁₀(4) = exactly 12.04 dB.
