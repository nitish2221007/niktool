E. O. Hall & N. J. Petch 1951 Grain Refinement Law

Grain boundaries act as physical pin barriers halting dislocation glide; refining grain size is the only metallurgical strengthening mechanism that simultaneously improves yield strength AND fracture toughness.

Frequently asked questions

What is the inverse Hall-Petch effect at nanocrystalline grain sizes (< 10 nm)?

Below $sim 10 ext{ nm}$, grain boundary sliding replaces dislocation pile-ups, causing metals to soften as grain size decreases.
