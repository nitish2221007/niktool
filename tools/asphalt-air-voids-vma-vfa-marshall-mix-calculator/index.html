Superpave Asphalt Volumetric Mix Design Standard

Targeting exactly $4.0%$ air voids ($V_a$) provides the optimum balance between structural rutting resistance under heavy summer truck axle loads and sufficient binder thickness for durability against cold weather thermal cracking.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if asphalt air voids drop below 3.0%?

Traffic compaction squeezes asphalt binder to the pavement surface ("flushing/bleeding"), causing permanent rutting grooves and slippery hydroplaning hazards.
