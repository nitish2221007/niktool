How to use Monod Microbial Growth Kinetics (μ = μ_max·S / (K_s + S)) & Chemostat Calculator

  1. Enter maximum specific growth rate $mu_{max}$ in $h^{-1}$ (e.g. 0.80 $h^{-1}$ for *E. coli* bacteria).
  2. Enter growth-limiting substrate concentration S in g/L (e.g. glucose nutrient).
  3. Enter Monod affinity constant $K_s$ in g/L (substrate concentration supporting $mu = 0.5 mu_{max}$).
  4. Enter biomass-to-substrate conversion yield $Y_{X/S}$ (g dry cell weight per g substrate consumed).
  5. Inspect specific growth rate $mu$, biomass doubling generation time in minutes, and continuous chemostat steady-state dilution rate ($D = mu$).

Jacques Monod 1949 Microbial Growth Kinetics

The Monod hyperbolic kinetic curve ($mu = rac{mu_{max} S}{K_s + S}$) mirrors Michaelis-Menten enzyme kinetics, allowing industrial biopharmaceutical engineers to optimize continuous bioreactor feeding rates for recombinant protein, insulin, and monoclonal antibody production.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when continuous chemostat dilution rate exceeds μ_max (D > μ_max)?

Cell washout occurs: liquid nutrient pumping flushes microbes out faster than they can divide, dropping biomass concentration in the fermenter to zero.
