Normalizes QT duration for heart rate variations; identifying $QTc > 500 ext{ ms}$ warns clinicians of imminent lethal Torsades de Pointes ventricular fibrillation when prescribing antiarrhythmics, macrolide antibiotics, or psychotropics.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Fridericia preferred at high or low heart rates?

Bazett overcorrects at fast heart rates ($>100 ext{ BPM}$) and undercorrects at slow heart rates ($<60 ext{ BPM}$), whereas Fridericia's cube-root formula maintains physiological stability.
