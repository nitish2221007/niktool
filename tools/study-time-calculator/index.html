How to use Study Time Calculator

  1. Enter the total hours you are awake each day.
  2. Enter your fixed commitments like school, coaching, and breaks.
  3. Click Process to see exactly how many hours you realistically have left for self-study.

Key Features & Privacy

This planner helps you find hidden study hours in your daily routine without prescribing rigid or unhealthy schedules. It simply does the arithmetic so you can plan your timetable realistically. All calculations stay local in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 and 12 students use this at the start of the academic year to build a sustainable daily timetable that balances school, coaching, and self-study.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.
