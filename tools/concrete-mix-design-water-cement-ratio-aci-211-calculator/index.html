Abrams Law of Water-Cement Ratio & ACI 211.1 Mix Standard

Duff Abrams proved in 1918 that concrete compressive strength is determined primarily by the water-cement ratio ($f'_c propto rac{1}{(w/c)}$); reducing $w/c$ from 0.60 to 0.40 nearly doubles structural concrete load-bearing strength.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard ratio of cement to sand to gravel in nominal mix concrete?

Standard M20 nominal concrete uses a 1 : 1.5 : 3 volumetric proportion (1 part cement : 1.5 parts sand : 3 parts coarse aggregate).
