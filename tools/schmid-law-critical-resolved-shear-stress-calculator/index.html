How to use Schmid's Law Critical Resolved Shear Stress (CRSS) Calculator

  1. Enter applied uniaxial tensile stress $sigma$ in MPa.
  2. Enter angle $phi$ between tensile loading axis and the slip plane normal vector in degrees.
  3. Enter angle $lambda$ between tensile loading axis and the slip direction vector in degrees.
  4. Inspect the Schmid orientation factor $m = cosphi cdot coslambda$ and resolved shear stress $ au$ driving dislocation movement.

Erich Schmid 1924 Single Crystal Plasticity Law

Plastic deformation in single crystals occurs when resolved shear stress along the most favorable slip system exceeds the Critical Resolved Shear Stress ($ au_{ ext{crss}}$); maximum shear occurs when $phi = lambda = 45^circ$, yielding a maximum Schmid factor of $m = 0.50$.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if tensile load is perpendicular to the slip plane (φ = 0, λ = 90)?

The Schmid factor is zero ($m=0$), meaning no shear stress is resolved along the slip direction and brittle cleavage fracture occurs instead.
