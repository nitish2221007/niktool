Isoelectric Focusing (IEF) Protein Separation

In an electric field, proteins migrate through a continuous pH gradient until they reach their unique isoelectric point (pI) where net charge becomes zero and electrophoresis migration ceases.

Frequently asked questions

What is the pI of Glycine (pK₁ = 2.34, pK₂ = 9.60)?

pI = (2.34 + 9.60) / 2 = 11.94 / 2 = 5.97.
