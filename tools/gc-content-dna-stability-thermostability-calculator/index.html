DNA Duplex Base Stacking & Hydrogen Bonding

GC base pairs have three hydrogen bonds and stronger base-stacking interactions than AT pairs (two hydrogen bonds), increasing thermal melting denaturation temperatures.

Frequently asked questions

What is GC-skew used for in bacterial genomics?

Asymmetric mutation rates during leading vs lagging strand replication create positive GC-skew, marking bacterial replication origins (*oriC*).
