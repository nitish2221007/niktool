Continuous Well-Mixed Reactor Design

In an ideal CSTR, vigorous mechanical agitation creates uniform composition throughout the tank identical to the exit stream; because reaction occurs entirely at the lowest reactant concentration ($C_A = C_{A0}(1-X)$), CSTRs require larger volumes than Plug Flow Reactors for high conversions.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a CSTR instead of a PFR if CSTR volume is larger?

CSTRs provide excellent temperature control for highly exothermic reactions and easy continuous pH and catalyst slurry handling.
