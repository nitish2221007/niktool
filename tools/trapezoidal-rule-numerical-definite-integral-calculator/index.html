Linear Interpolation Definite Integration Geometry

The trapezoidal rule approximates the area under a curve by a series of trapezoids ($I approx rac{h}{2}[f_0 + 2f_1 + dots + f_n]$), offering a simple numerical quadrature formula with second-order error ($O(h^2)$).

Frequently asked questions

Why does the trapezoidal rule overestimate convex/concave-up functions?

Because chords connecting points on concave-up curves ($f''(x) > 0$) lie entirely above the curve, trapezoids enclose slightly more area than the true integral.
