Frequently asked questions

Why does pH + pOH = 14 at 25°C?

Because the water self-ionization constant is $K_w = [H^+][OH^-] = 1.0 imes 10^{-14}$; taking negative logarithms yields $-log[H^+] - log[OH^-] = 14$.
