Acoustic Destructive Wave Interference

Sound entering the side branch travels length L to the closed end, reflects with zero phase inversion, and returns to the main duct after traveling a round trip of $2L = lambda/2$ ($180^circ$ out of phase), completely canceling the transmitted sound wave at that notch frequency.

Frequently asked questions

Why do quarter-wave silencers attenuate only odd harmonics (1st, 3rd, 5th)?

Because round-trip travel distances of $3lambda/2, 5lambda/2$ also produce exact $180^circ$ phase reversals, whereas even harmonics ($2lambda/2 = lambda$) produce constructive reinforcement.
