Wilfrid Rall 1959 Dendritic Cable Theory

Synaptic potentials (EPSPs) decay exponentially as they propagate along passive dendrites ($V(x) = V_0 e^{-x/lambda}$); thick dendrites with high membrane resistance have large space constants ($lambda > 1 ext{ mm}$), transmitting electrical signals efficiently to the axon hillock to trigger action potentials.

Frequently asked questions

What is the physical definition of the space constant (λ)?

$lambda$ is the distance at which an electrotonic potential decays to $1/e approx 36.8%$ of its initial peak amplitude.
