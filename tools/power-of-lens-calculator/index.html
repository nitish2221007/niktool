How to use Power of Lens Calculator

  1. Enter the focal length of the lens.
  2. Choose the unit (cm or m) and the lens type (convex or concave).
  3. Click Process to get the power in diopters with the correct sign.

Key Features & Privacy

The tool applies P = 1/f with f in metres, converts centimetres automatically, and applies the sign convention so convex lenses are positive and concave lenses are negative. This removes a common source of errors in Class 10 optics. Everything runs locally.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it for the Human Eye and spectacles portion of the Light chapter, and to check lens power numericals.
