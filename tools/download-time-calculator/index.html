Megabits (Mbps) vs Megabytes (MB/s)

Internet service providers advertise bandwidth in Megabits per second (Mbps), while files are measured in MegaBytes (MB). Because 8 bits equal 1 Byte, a 100 Mbps connection yields a theoretical maximum download speed of 12.5 MB/s.

Private browser-based processing
