Sir William Rowan Hamilton 1843 Quaternions (i² = j² = k² = ijk = -1)

Unlike Euler angles (pitch, roll, yaw) which suffer from catastrophic "gimbal lock" (loss of a degree of freedom when two axes align), unit quaternions represent smooth, continuous 3D rotations without singularities.

Frequently asked questions

Why is angle divided by 2 (θ/2) in rotation quaternions?

Rotating a 3D vector requires sandwich multiplication ($v' = q v q^{-1}$), which applies the half-angle rotation twice to yield the full rotation angle $ heta$.
