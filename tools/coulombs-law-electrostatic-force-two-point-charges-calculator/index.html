Charles-Augustin de Coulomb 1785 Fundamental Law

Coulomb's inverse-square law ($F = k_e rac{q_1 q_2}{r^2}$) establishes the electrostatic force holding electrons in atomic orbitals around positively charged atomic nuclei.

Frequently asked questions

How strong is the electrostatic force compared to gravity?

Between two protons, the electrostatic repulsion is approximately $10^{36}$ times stronger than their mutual gravitational attraction.
