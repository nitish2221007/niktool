How to use Hodgkin-Huxley Giant Axon Action Potential Membrane Current Calculator

  1. Enter instantaneous transmembrane cell membrane potential V in mV (e.g. $-65 ext{ mV}$ resting, $+30 ext{ mV}$ peak overshoot).
  2. Enter sodium activation gating variable m ($0.0$ closed, $1.0$ open).
  3. Enter sodium inactivation gating variable h ($1.0$ unblocked, $0.0$ inactivated).
  4. Enter delayed rectifier potassium activation gating variable n ($0.0$ closed, $1.0$ open).
  5. Inspect individual ion channel currents ($I_{ ext{Na}}, I_{ ext{K}}, I_{ ext{L}}$) in $mu ext{A/cm}^2$ and net membrane voltage drive.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the squid giant axon famous in neuroscience?

At up to 1 mm in diameter (100–1000× larger than mammalian axons), it allowed Hodgkin and Huxley to insert glass microelectrodes to record action potential currents directly.
