How to use CNC Spindle Cutting Power & Machinability Power Requirement Calculator

  1. Select workpiece material unit power coefficient K_p (Aluminum, Carbon Steel, Stainless, Titanium).
  2. Enter volumetric metal removal rate MRR in in³/min.
  3. Enter spindle electrical/mechanical transmission efficiency percentage η (typically 80% to 85%).
  4. Inspect required spindle motor electrical horsepower (HP) and kilowatts (kW).

Specific Cutting Energy & Spindle Sizing

Different metal crystal structures require distinct specific cutting energies; cutting Titanium requires 7 times more spindle horsepower per cubic inch removed than Aluminum.

Frequently asked questions

What is unit power coefficient K_p?

K_p is the specific horsepower required to machine away 1 cubic inch of material in 1 minute (HP = MRR · K_p).
