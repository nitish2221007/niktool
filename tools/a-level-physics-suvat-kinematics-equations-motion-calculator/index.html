Uniform Acceleration Kinematics Foundation

SUVAT equations govern all 1D and 2D motion with constant acceleration (projectiles, falling objects, vehicle braking distance), linking displacement, velocity, acceleration, and time.

Frequently asked questions

What does SUVAT stand for?

S = displacement, U = initial velocity, V = final velocity, A = acceleration, T = time.
