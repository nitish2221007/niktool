Negative Feedback & Virtual Ground Concept

Negative feedback forces the differential input voltage to zero ($V_+ approx V_-$), establishing a virtual ground that makes circuit gain depend purely on external precision resistor ratios ($R_f / R_{in}$) rather than erratic internal silicon transistor parameters.

Frequently asked questions

What is the input impedance of an inverting vs non-inverting op-amp?

An inverting amplifier has input impedance equal to $R_{in}$; a non-inverting amplifier has nearly infinite input impedance ($>10^{12} Omega$).
