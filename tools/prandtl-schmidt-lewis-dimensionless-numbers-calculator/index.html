Boundary Layer Similarity & Chilton-Colburn Analogy

When $Le approx 1$ (such as air-water vapor mixtures), thermal and mass boundary layers grow at identical rates, allowing heat transfer coefficients to directly predict evaporative mass transfer rates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Prandtl number for liquid metals vs engine oils?

Liquid metals have tiny $Pr sim 0.01$ (thermal conduction dominates), while viscous engine oils have massive $Pr sim 10,000$ (viscous momentum dominates).
