How to use EPA Landfill Gas Methane Generation First-Order Decay (LandGEM Model) Calculator

  1. Enter total mass of municipal solid waste in place M in metric tons (Mg).
  2. Enter methane generation capacity $L_0$ in $ ext{m}^3/ ext{Mg}$ (standard $100 ext{ m}^3/ ext{Mg}$).
  3. Enter first-order decay rate constant k in $ ext{year}^{-1}$ (0.02 arid climates, 0.04 conventional, 0.06 wet bioreactor).
  4. Enter elapsed landfill age t in years.
  5. Inspect annual methane generation in $ ext{m}^3/ ext{year}$ and Landfill Gas-to-Energy (LFGTE) electric power potential.

EPA Landfill Gas Emissions Model (LandGEM V3.02) Standard

Regulatory standard for estimating fugitive greenhouse gas emissions from landfills and designing commercial landfill gas collection piping and flare destruction systems.

Frequently asked questions

What is the global warming potential (GWP) of landfill methane?

Methane has a GWP of 28–36 over 100 years, making capturing and flaring landfill gas one of the most effective carbon reduction projects.
