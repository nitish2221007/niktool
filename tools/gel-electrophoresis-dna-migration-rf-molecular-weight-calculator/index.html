Ogston Sieving Model of DNA Electrophoretic Mobility

Negative phosphate backbone charges impart a uniform charge-to-mass ratio to DNA; migration speed through agarose pores is inversely proportional to the logarithm of base pair length ($log_{10}( ext{bp}) propto -R_f$).
