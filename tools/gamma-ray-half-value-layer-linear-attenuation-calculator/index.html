Frequently asked questions

What is the radiation Buildup Factor B in thick shielding?

In broad-beam geometry, scattered Compton photons build up in the shield, requiring a multiplicative buildup factor ($I = I_0 B e^{-mu x}$) to prevent under-shielding.
