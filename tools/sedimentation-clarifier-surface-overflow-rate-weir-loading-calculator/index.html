Ten States Standards Clarifier Sizing Rules

Governs discrete particle gravitational sedimentation; any suspended floc particle whose terminal settling velocity exceeds the upward surface overflow rate ($v_s ge ext{SOR}$) is $100%$ captured.

Frequently asked questions

What is the recommended SOR limit for secondary activated sludge clarifiers?

Standard peak design SOR is typically $24 ext{ to }32 ext{ m}^3/( ext{m}^2cdot ext{day})$ ($600 ext{ to }800 ext{ gpd/ft}^2$) to prevent sludge blanket carryover.
