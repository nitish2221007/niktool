James Prescott Joule Electrical Heating Law

Electrical power transforms electrical potential energy into heat, light, or mechanical kinetic work; high-voltage transmission lines step up voltage to minimize current ($I$), reducing $I^2R$ power loss during grid distribution.

Frequently asked questions

Why is P = I²R used to calculate transmission line losses?

Because power lost as resistive heat in wires depends on the square of current; halving the current reduces resistive line heat losses by 75%.
