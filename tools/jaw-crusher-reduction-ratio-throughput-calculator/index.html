Primary Crushing Stage Optimization

Standard jaw and gyratory crushers operate most reliably at reduction ratios between 4:1 and 6:1; exceeding 7:1 reduction causes excessive toggle plate stress, packing in the crushing chamber, and high manganese liner wear.

Frequently asked questions

What is Closed Side Setting (CSS)?

CSS is the minimum distance between the fixed and moving crusher jaws at the bottom discharge opening during the closing cycle stroke.
