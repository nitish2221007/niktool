How to use RF Resonant Cavity Shunt Impedance & Quality Factor (Q₀) Calculator

  1. Enter peak accelerating voltage gap V_acc in Megavolts (MV).
  2. Enter cavity effective shunt impedance R_s in Megohms.
  3. Enter unloaded quality factor Q_0 (30,000 for copper, 10¹⁰ for superconducting niobium cavities).
  4. Inspect continuous RF wall dissipation power loss and geometric R/Q ratio.

Superconducting RF Cryogenic Power Advantage

Normal copper cavities dissipate hundreds of kilowatts in ohmic wall heating; cooling superconducting Niobium cavities with liquid helium to 2 Kelvin boosts Q_0 to 10¹⁰, slashing wall power losses to mere watts.

Frequently asked questions

What is R/Q in RF cavities?

R/Q = R_shunt / Q_0 is a purely geometric figure of merit determined exclusively by the cavity shape.
