Pedro Nunes & Gerardus Mercator's 1569 Sailing Line

A rhumb line (loxodrome) cuts all meridians of longitude at the exact same constant angle, allowing traditional ship captains to lock a single compass heading for the entire oceanic voyage without continuous helm recalculations.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a Great Circle and a Rhumb Line?

A Great Circle is the shortest physical distance on Earth (curved course heading), while a Rhumb Line maintains one constant compass bearing but covers slightly greater total distance.
