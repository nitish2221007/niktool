Resonant Vibration Amplification & Isolation

At resonance ($eta = 1.0$), dynamic amplitude multiplies by $1 / (2zeta) = 10 imes$ for 5% damping; when driving frequency exceeds $eta > sqrt{2} approx 1.414$, the structure enters the vibration isolation regime where transmitted forces drop below static loads ($DAF < 1.0$).

Frequently asked questions

How does damping affect DAF at exact resonance (β=1)?

At $eta=1.0$, $DAF_{ ext{peak}} = rac{1}{2zeta}$; doubling damping from 2% to 4% cuts peak resonant stresses in half ($25 imes o 12.5 imes$).
