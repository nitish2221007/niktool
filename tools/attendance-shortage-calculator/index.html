How to use Attendance Shortage Calculator

  1. Enter how many classes you have attended so far.
  2. Enter the total number of classes conducted to date.
  3. Select your university minimum target percentage (e.g. 75% or 80%).
  4. Click Calculate to see your exact status and recover plan.

Never Get Shortage or Detained

Most colleges mandate a 75% minimum attendance to appear for semester end exams. This tool calculates the exact number of consecutive lectures you must sit for to recover your eligibility without guesswork.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How is attendance shortage recovery calculated?

Formula: Needed Classes = ceil((Target% × Total - Attended) / (1 - Target%))

Is my student data stored?

No, all calculations run in your web browser.
