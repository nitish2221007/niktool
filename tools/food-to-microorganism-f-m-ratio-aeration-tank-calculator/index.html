How to use Food-to-Microorganism (F/M) Organic Loading Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter daily raw wastewater influent flow rate Q in $m^3/ ext{day}$.
  2. Enter primary effluent 5-day Biochemical Oxygen Demand ($BOD_5$) in mg/L.
  3. Enter total biological aeration basin liquid volume V in $m^3$.
  4. Enter Mixed Liquor Volatile Suspended Solids (MLVSS) active bacterial concentration in mg/L (typically 70–80% of MLSS).
  5. Inspect F/M loading ratio and evaluate whether the biological process operates in Extended Aeration, Conventional, or Overloaded mode.

Bacterial Growth Phase & Floc Settleability

Maintaining F/M between 0.20 and 0.40 keeps bacteria in the declining growth phase where microorganisms produce extracellular polymeric slime (EPS) that naturally binds bacteria into dense, rapidly settling flocs in secondary clarifiers.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between MLSS and MLVSS?

MLSS measures total suspended solids (active bacteria + inert grit/sand); MLVSS measures the organic volatile fraction representing living active bacteria.
