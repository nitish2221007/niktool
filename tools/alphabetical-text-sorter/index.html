How to use Alphabetical Text Sorter

  1. Paste your list of items, names, or words into the input area (one item per line).
  2. Choose whether to sort in Ascending (A-Z) or Descending (Z-A) order.
  3. Click Process to instantly alphabetize your list and copy the result.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool quickly organizes any list into alphabetical order, ignoring case sensitivity for natural sorting. It is perfect for organizing bibliographies, glossaries, or name lists. All sorting happens locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to sort reference lists, teachers use it to organize student names, and anyone can use it to alphabetize grocery or task lists.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
