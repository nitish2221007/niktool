Archibald Vivian Hill 1938 Skeletal Muscle Mechanics Standard

Models the fundamental trade-off of human muscle physiology: muscle produces maximum force at zero speed ($F_0$) and maximum speed at zero force ($v_{max}$), with peak power occurring at $sim 30% ext{ to }35%$ of maximum load.
