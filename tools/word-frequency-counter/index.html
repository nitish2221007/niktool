How to use Word Frequency Counter

  1. Paste your text, article, or study notes into the input area.
  2. Click Process to generate a frequency table of all words used.
  3. Review the most common words to analyze the focus and vocabulary of the text.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool counts how many times each word appears in your text and sorts them from most to least frequent. It is an excellent way to analyze text density and keyword usage. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use it for language analysis, SEO beginners use it to check keyword density, and researchers use it to summarize text focus.
