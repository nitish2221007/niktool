Frequently asked questions

Why is a Prandtl-Meyer expansion isentropic while a shock wave is not?

Expansion occurs through an infinite series of infinitesimal Mach waves with zero entropy generation ($Delta s = 0$), preserving total stagnation pressure ($p_{02} = p_{01}$).
