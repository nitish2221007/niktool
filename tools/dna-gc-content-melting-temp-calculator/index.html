PCR Primer Design Rules (3 Hydrogen Bonds in G-C)

G-C base pairs form 3 hydrogen bonds compared to only 2 in A-T pairs, conferring higher thermal stability. Ideal PCR primers possess 40-60% GC content with a Tₘ between 55°C and 65°C.

Frequently asked questions

What is the optimal PCR annealing temperature?

The PCR annealing temperature is typically set 3°C to 5°C below the lower melting temperature (Tₘ) of the primer pair.
