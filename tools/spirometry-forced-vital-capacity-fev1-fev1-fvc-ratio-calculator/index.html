Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Obstructive and Restrictive lung disease?

Obstructive disease (COPD, asthma) limits expiratory air flow ($ ext{FEV}_1/ ext{FVC} < 70%$), while Restrictive disease (fibrosis, scoliosis) reduces overall lung volume ($ ext{TLC} < 80%$, normal ratio).
