Chemical Kinetics & Temperature Sensitivity

Arrhenius kinetics explains why reaction rates increase exponentially with temperature: higher thermal energy enables a greater fraction of colliding molecules to surpass the activation energy barrier Ea.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard rule of thumb for chemical reaction rates?

For many common chemical reactions near room temperature, the reaction rate approximately doubles for every 10 °C increase in temperature.
