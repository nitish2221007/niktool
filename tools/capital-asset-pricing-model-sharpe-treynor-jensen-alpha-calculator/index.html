Modern Portfolio Theory Performance Metrics

A positive Jensen's Alpha ($alpha > 0$) demonstrates that a fund manager generated true abnormal excess returns beyond what is explained by market risk exposures ($eta$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Sharpe Ratio and Treynor Ratio?

Sharpe ratio divides excess return by total volatility ($sigma$), while Treynor ratio divides by systematic market risk ($eta$).
