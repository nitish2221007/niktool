Carl Runge & Martin Kutta 1901 ODE Standard

RK4 evaluates four distinct trial slopes across each step interval to cancel out lower-order Taylor series error terms, delivering high numerical precision ($O(h^4)$) without requiring analytical derivative calculations.

Frequently asked questions

Why is RK4 superior to Euler's method?

Euler's method has first-order error ($O(h)$), requiring millions of steps for decent accuracy, whereas RK4 ($O(h^4)$) achieves greater precision in just a few dozen steps.
