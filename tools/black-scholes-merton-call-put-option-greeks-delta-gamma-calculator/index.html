Fischer Black, Myron Scholes & Robert Merton 1973 Option Greeks

Option Greeks quantify dynamic portfolio risk exposures: Delta ($Delta$) indicates directional exposure and hedging ratio, Gamma ($Gamma$) measures Delta curvature risk, and Theta ($Theta$) measures unavoidable time decay.

Frequently asked questions

What does a Delta of 0.50 indicate?

A Delta of 0.50 means the option price increases by $0.50 for every $1.00 increase in the underlying stock price.
