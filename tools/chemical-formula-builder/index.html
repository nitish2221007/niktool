How to use Chemical Formula Builder

  1. Enter the symbol and valency of the positive ion (cation), e.g., Mg and 2.
  2. Enter the symbol and valency of the negative ion (anion), e.g., Cl and 1.
  3. Click Process to see the cross-over method and the final simplified chemical formula.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool uses the criss-cross (cross-over) method to determine the subscripts of ionic compounds and automatically simplifies them using the greatest common divisor (e.g., Mg2 O2 becomes MgO). It shows the step-by-step logic so students can learn the method. All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use this to practice writing chemical formulas for the "Molecules and Compounds" chapter and to verify their homework answers.
