Konstantin Tsiolkovsky 1903 Rocketry Equation

Tsiolkovsky showed that escaping Earth gravity into Low Earth Orbit requires Δv ≈ 9.4 km/s; because velocity scales logarithmically with mass ratio, staging is mandatory to avoid carrying empty dry tanks.

Frequently asked questions

Why is specific impulse (I_sp) measured in seconds?

Specific impulse is thrust divided by weight flow rate of fuel (I_sp = F / (ṁ · g₀)), canceling units down to seconds across both metric and imperial systems.
