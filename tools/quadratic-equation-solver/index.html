How to use Quadratic Equation Solver

  1. Enter the coefficients a, b, and c for your quadratic equation ax² + bx + c = 0.
  2. Click Solve Equation to calculate the discriminant and roots.
  3. View the nature of roots (real/distinct, real/equal, or complex) and their values.

Key Features & Privacy

This solver uses the quadratic formula to find roots and shows the discriminant to explain the nature of solutions. All calculations happen in your browser — your equations and academic work remain private.

Common Use Cases

Students use this tool to verify their manual calculations, understand the relationship between discriminant and root types, and practice solving quadratic equations from Class 10 mathematics.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Enter the coefficients a, b, and c for your quadratic equation, then click Solve Equation to see the roots and discriminant.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser.

What if the roots are complex?

The tool will show complex roots in the form a + bi when the discriminant is negative.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline.
