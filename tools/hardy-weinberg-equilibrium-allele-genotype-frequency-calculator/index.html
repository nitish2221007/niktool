Frequently asked questions

Why is carrier frequency (2pq) so much higher than disease incidence (q^2)?

Because $q$ is much larger than $q^2$; for Cystic Fibrosis ($q^2 = 1/2500$), the recessive allele is $q = 1/50$, making the carrier frequency $2pq approx 1/25$ ($4%$).
