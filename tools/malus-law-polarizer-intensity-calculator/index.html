Crossed Polarizers and LCD Displays

When two polarizers are oriented perpendicular to each other (θ = 90°), cos(90°) = 0, causing complete optical extinction (0% transmission) — the basis of LCD monitor pixel switching.

Frequently asked questions

What is transmitted intensity at 45 degrees?

Because cos(45°) = 1/√2, cos²(45°) = 0.5, transmitting exactly 50% of the light.
