James Pollard Espy 1841 Thermal Convection Law

As warm surface air parcels rise, they cool at the Dry Adiabatic Lapse Rate ($Gamma_d approx 9.8^circ ext{C/km}$) while the dew point falls much slower ($1.8^circ ext{C/km}$); the LCL is the exact height where parcel temperature meets the dew point and relative humidity reaches 100%.
