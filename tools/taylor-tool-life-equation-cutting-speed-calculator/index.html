Frederick Winslow Taylor 1906 Tool Wear Law

Taylor law (V · Tⁿ = C) proves that cutting speed is the single most destructive parameter driving tool flank wear; increasing speed by 20% can cut carbide insert life in half due to intense Arrhenius thermal degradation.

Frequently asked questions

What is Taylor constant C?

Constant C is the theoretical cutting velocity at which the tool would wear out in exactly 1 minute.
