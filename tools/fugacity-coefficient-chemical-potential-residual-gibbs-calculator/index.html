Gilbert N. Lewis 1901 Fugacity Formulation

Replaces mechanical pressure with chemical fugacity ($dmu = R T dln f$) to account for non-ideal molecular interactions in high-pressure reactors and vapor-liquid phase equilibria.

Frequently asked questions

What is the value of fugacity for an ideal gas?

For an ideal gas, intermolecular forces are zero, so $phi = 1.00$ and fugacity equals mechanical pressure ($f = P$).
