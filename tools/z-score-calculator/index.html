What Does a Z-Score Mean?

A Z-score indicates how many standard deviations an observation lies above or below the mean (z = (x - μ) / σ). A positive Z-score indicates a value above average, and a negative Z-score indicates a value below average.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage falls within ±1 standard deviation?

In a normal distribution, approximately 68.27% of values fall within ±1 standard deviation, and 95.45% within ±2 standard deviations.
