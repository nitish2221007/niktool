Hans Lineweaver & Dean Burk 1934 Linear Kinetic Transformation

Taking the reciprocal of the Michaelis-Menten equation linearizes non-linear enzyme saturation data ($ rac{1}{V} = rac{K_m}{V_{max}} rac{1}{[S]} + rac{1}{V_{max}}$), allowing pharmaceutical biochemists to identify whether drug candidates compete directly at the active binding pocket (Competitive) or bind an allosteric regulatory site (Non-Competitive).

Frequently asked questions

How does competitive inhibition look on a Lineweaver-Burk plot?

Lines for uninhibited and competitively inhibited enzymes intersect at the exact same Y-intercept ($1/V_{max}$), pivoting upwards to a more negative X-intercept (higher apparent $K_m$).
