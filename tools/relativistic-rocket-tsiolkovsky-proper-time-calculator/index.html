Frequently asked questions

Why can a rocket never reach or exceed the speed of light (v = c)?

As velocity approaches $c$, the hyperbolic tangent function asymptotically approaches 1 ($ anh(infty) = 1$), requiring infinite energy and infinite propellant mass to reach $c$.
