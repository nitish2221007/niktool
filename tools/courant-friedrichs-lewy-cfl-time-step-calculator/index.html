How to use Courant-Friedrichs-Lewy (CFL / Courant Number) Time Step Calculator

  1. Enter convective fluid velocity u in m/s.
  2. Enter medium acoustic speed of sound c in m/s (set $c=0$ for incompressible pressure-based solvers).
  3. Enter minimum spatial grid cell spacing $Delta x$ in millimeters.
  4. Enter target numerical Courant number (typically $ ext{CFL} le 1.0$ for explicit time-stepping schemes, $ ext{CFL} sim 5 ext{–}20$ for implicit solvers).
  5. Inspect maximum stable simulation time step $Delta t$ in microseconds ($mu ext{s}$) and milliseconds.

Richard Courant, Kurt Friedrichs, & Hans Lewy 1928 Stability Criterion

The CFL condition mandates that numerical information propagation speed ($Delta x / Delta t$) must exceed physical wave propagation speed ($u + c$); violating CFL in explicit transient solvers triggers numerical instability and floating-point overflow divergence.

Frequently asked questions

Why do implicit CFD solvers allow CFL > 1?

Implicit solvers invert simultaneous matrix equations across the entire grid domain at once, removing the mathematical stability limit on time step size.
