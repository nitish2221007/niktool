How to use Arithmetic Progression Calculator

  1. Enter the first term a and the common difference d.
  2. Enter the number of terms n.
  3. Click Process to get the nth term, the sum of n terms, and a preview of the first terms.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator applies the standard AP formulas: nth term = a + (n-1)d and sum = n/2 [2a + (n-1)d]. It also lists the first few terms so you can verify the sequence visually. Everything is computed locally.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it to check homework on arithmetic progressions, verify nth-term and sum answers, and explore how changing a or d affects the sequence.
