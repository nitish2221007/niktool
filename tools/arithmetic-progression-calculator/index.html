Mathematical Formulas for Arithmetic Progression

An Arithmetic Progression (AP) is a sequence of numbers in which the difference between consecutive terms is constant. This constant value is known as the common difference ($d$).

  • Nth Term Formula ($a_n$): $a_n = a + (n - 1)d$
  • Sum of $n$ Terms Formula ($S_n$): $S_n = \frac{n}{2} \cdot [2a + (n - 1)d]$
  • Alternative Sum Formula: $S_n = \frac{n}{2} \cdot (a + l)$ where $l$ is the last term ($a_n$).

Step-by-Step Worked Example

Problem: Find the 10th term and the sum of the first 10 terms for the sequence starting with $a = 2$ and difference $d = 3$.

  1. First Term $a = 2$, Common Difference $d = 3$, $n = 10$.
  2. Calculation for $a_{10}$: $a_{10} = 2 + (10 - 1) \times 3 = 2 + (9 \times 3) = 2 + 27 = 29$.
  3. Calculation for $S_{10}$: $S_{10} = \frac{10}{2} \times [2(2) + (10 - 1)3] = 5 \times [4 + 27] = 5 \times 31 = 155$.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the Arithmetic Progression Calculator?

Enter the first term (a), common difference (d), and number of terms (n) into the input fields, then click Process to compute the nth term and sum of terms.

What is the formula for the nth term of an AP?

The nth term of an Arithmetic Progression is given by $a_n = a + (n - 1)d$, where $a$ is the first term and $d$ is the common difference.

What is the formula for the sum of n terms in an AP?

The sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP is calculated using $S_n = \frac{n}{2} \times [2a + (n - 1)d]$ or $S_n = \frac{n}{2} \times (a + l)$.

What is the difference between AP and GP?

An Arithmetic Progression (AP) adds a constant common difference between consecutive terms, whereas a Geometric Progression (GP) multiplies consecutive terms by a common ratio.
