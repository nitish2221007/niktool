ROME Method & Winter's Formula ABG Analysis

Using the ROME rule (Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal) allows critical care clinicians to immediately decipher blood gas anomalies and adjust mechanical ventilator ventilation rates.

Frequently asked questions

What is Winter's Formula for metabolic acidosis?

Expected $PaCO_2 = 1.5 imes [HCO_3^-] + 8 pm 2$; if measured $PaCO_2$ is higher, a secondary respiratory acidosis is present.
