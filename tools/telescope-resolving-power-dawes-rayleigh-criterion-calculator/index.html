Frequently asked questions

Why do ground telescopes rarely reach their diffraction limit?

Atmospheric turbulence ("seeing") typically limits ground telescope resolution to $sim 0.5 ext{ to }1.0 ext{ arcseconds}$ unless adaptive optics are used.
