Frequently asked questions

Why is 468 / f(MHz) the famous American ham radio dipole rule of thumb?

468 / f_MHz derives from 0.5 × 983.57 ft × 0.95 ≈ 468, giving total dipole length in feet directly from frequency in MHz.
