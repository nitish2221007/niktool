Ludwig Prandtl 1918 Lifting-Line Vortex Theory

Quantifies aerodynamic drag created as a direct byproduct of generating lift by trailing wingtip vortices ($C_{ ext{Di}} propto C_L^2 / AR$), proving why gliders and U-2 spy planes use ultra-long slender wings ($AR > 20$).

Frequently asked questions

How do winglets reduce fuel burn on commercial airliners?

Winglets diffuse and lift the concentrated high-pressure wingtip vortex, increasing the effective Oswald span efficiency e and reducing induced drag by $4% ext{ to }6%$.
