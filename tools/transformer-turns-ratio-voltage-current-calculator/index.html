  4. Inspect output stepped voltage, turns ratio, and reflected primary current.

Faraday's Law of Mutual Induction

Transformers transfer electrical power between circuits through electromagnetic flux linkage (P_in = P_out in ideal transformers), stepping voltage up or down with inverse current scaling.

Frequently asked questions

Can transformers operate on direct current (DC)?

No, transformers require changing magnetic flux (dΦ/dt) generated by alternating current (AC).
