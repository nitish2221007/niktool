  1. Enter fluid density (ρ) and dynamic viscosity (μ).
  2. Enter mean flow velocity (v) and internal pipe diameter (D).
  3. Inspect the dimensionless Reynolds Number and flow turbulence classification.

Why Reynolds Number is Dimensionless

Reynolds number represents the dimensionless ratio of inertial forces to viscous forces in fluid dynamics: Re = (Inertial Forces) / (Viscous Forces).
