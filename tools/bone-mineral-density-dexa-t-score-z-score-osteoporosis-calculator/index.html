How to use DEXA Bone Mineral Density T-Score & Z-Score Osteoporosis Stage Calculator

  1. Enter patient measured areal Bone Mineral Density (BMD) in $ ext{g/cm}^2$ (from lumbar spine L1-L4 or femoral neck DEXA scan).
  2. Enter peak young adult sex-matched reference mean BMD (typically $sim 1.000 ext{ g/cm}^2$).
  3. Enter population reference standard deviation SD (typically $sim 0.110 ext{ g/cm}^2$).
  4. Inspect calculated T-score and WHO diagnostic classification (Normal, Osteopenia, Osteoporosis).

World Health Organization (WHO) 1994 DEXA Osteoporosis Standard

Provides the universal epidemiological criterion for diagnosing osteoporosis and guiding anti-resorptive (bisphosphonate, denosumab) or anabolic (teriparatide) therapies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a T-score and a Z-score?

A T-score compares BMD to a healthy 30-year-old young adult peak bone mass; a Z-score compares BMD to an age- and sex-matched peer group.
