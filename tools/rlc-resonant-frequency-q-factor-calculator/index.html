Radio Station Selection via LC Tanks

At resonance, inductive reactance cancels capacitive reactance (X_L = X_C), causing series circuit impedance to collapse to pure resistance R and creating a sharp bandpass window for radio receiver tuning.

Frequently asked questions

What does a high Q factor indicate?

A high Q factor (>50) indicates low resistive energy losses and a very sharp, highly selective frequency passband.
