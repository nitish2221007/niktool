Frequently asked questions

Why is C_d ≈ 2.2 in Low Earth Orbit instead of 0.5?

In the free molecular flow regime of upper space, air molecules do not form a continuous fluid boundary layer; they bounce off surfaces with diffuse reflection, yielding C_d ≈ 2.0 - 2.2.
