Frequently asked questions

Why is there a factor of 2 in the radar Doppler formula?

In monostatic radar, the wave travels to the moving target and back, doubling the Doppler shift compared to a one-way transmitter.

What happens when target angle θ = 90°?

When motion is perpendicular to radar line-of-sight, cos(90°) = 0, resulting in zero radial Doppler shift.

What frequencies do police speed radars use?

Police radars typically operate in X-band (10.5 GHz), K-band (24.15 GHz), or Ka-band (34.7 GHz).
