How to use Seismic P-Wave & S-Wave Earthquake Epicenter Distance Calculator

  1. Measure the time delay interval $Delta t = t_S - t_P$ between the initial primary P-wave arrival and the secondary shear S-wave arrival on a seismogram trace in seconds.
  2. Enter local crustal P-wave velocity $v_p$ (typically 5.5 to 6.5 km/s).
  3. Enter local crustal S-wave velocity $v_s$ (typically 3.0 to 3.8 km/s).
  4. Inspect calculated distance radius to the earthquake epicenter in kilometers and miles.

Earthquake Triangulation & Body Wave Velocities

Compressional P-waves travel ~1.7× faster than transverse S-waves; calculating the S-P time lag from three separate seismic stations generates three intersecting distance circles that pinpoint the exact geographic epicenter coordinates.

Frequently asked questions

Why cannot S-waves travel through Earth's outer core?

S-waves are transverse shear waves; liquids have zero shear modulus ($G=0$), proving Earth's outer core is molten liquid.
