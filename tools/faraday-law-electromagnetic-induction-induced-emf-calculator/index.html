Frequently asked questions

What is 1 Weber of magnetic flux?

1 Weber ($1 ext{ Wb} = 1 ext{ T}cdot ext{m}^2$) is the magnetic flux passing through a $1 ext{ m}^2$ area perpendicular to a uniform 1 Tesla magnetic field.
