Spectrophotometer Chemical Wavelength Separation

Diffraction gratings spread multi-wavelength white light into sharply resolved spectral lines according to d·sin θ = mλ, powering laboratory UV-Vis spectrophotometers and astronomical exoplanet atmosphere sensors.
