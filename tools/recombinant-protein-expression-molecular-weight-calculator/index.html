Frequently asked questions

Why is average amino acid molecular weight 110 Da when the mean mass is 128 Da?

During peptide bond condensation synthesis, one water molecule ($18 ext{ Da}$) is removed per residue: $128 - 18 = 110 ext{ Da}$.
