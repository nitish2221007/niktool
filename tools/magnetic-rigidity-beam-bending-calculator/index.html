The 3.3356 Momentum-to-Rigidity Constant

For single-charge relativistic particles, magnetic rigidity simplifies to B · ρ ≈ 3.3356 × p [GeV/c]; at 7 TeV, LHC protons have a massive 23,350 T·m rigidity, demanding 8.33 Tesla superconducting magnets spanning 27 kilometers.
