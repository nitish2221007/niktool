The Famous Unsolved Problem in Mathematics

Proposed by Lothar Collatz in 1937: if n is even, divide by 2; if n is odd, multiply by 3 and add 1. It is conjectured that every positive integer eventually falls into the 4 → 2 → 1 loop.

Frequently asked questions

Has any number been found that does not reach 1?

No, mathematicians have verified the conjecture for all starting integers up to 2⁶⁸ (~2.95 × 10²⁰) with zero counterexamples.
