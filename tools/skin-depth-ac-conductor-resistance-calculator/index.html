Eddy Current Surface Confinement

At high frequencies, internal opposing magnetic eddy currents force electric current into a thin outer annular ring of depth $delta = sqrt{ ho / pi f mu}$; in high-frequency SMPS transformers and induction heaters, using stranded Litz wire with individual insulated strands thinner than $delta$ eliminates skin effect losses.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage of current flows within 1 skin depth?

Approximately 63.2% of total AC current flows within 1 skin depth ($delta$), and over 98% flows within 4 skin depths ($4delta$).
