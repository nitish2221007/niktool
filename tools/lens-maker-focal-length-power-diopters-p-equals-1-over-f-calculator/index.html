Optometric Diopter Refractive Unit

A Diopter ($D = ext{m}^{-1}$) measures reciprocal focal length ($P = 1/f$); when multiple thin lenses are placed together, their total combined power adds linearly ($P_{ ext{total}} = P_1 + P_2$).

Frequently asked questions

What does a -2.50 D prescription mean?

It indicates a diverging concave lens with a focal length of $-40 ext{ cm}$ used to correct nearsightedness (myopia).
