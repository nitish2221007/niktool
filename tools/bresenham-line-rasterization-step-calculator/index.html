Jack Bresenham 1962 Integer Arithmetic Speed

Bresenham algorithm eliminates costly floating-point divisions by tracking an integer decision error variable, allowing hardware display controllers to rasterize lines using purely fast integer addition.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Bresenham algorithm faster than standard DDA?

Digital Differential Analyzers (DDA) require floating-point arithmetic and rounding at every step, whereas Bresenham uses exclusively integer additions.
