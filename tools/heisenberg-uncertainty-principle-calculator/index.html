Werner Heisenberg's 1927 Foundational Principle

The uncertainty principle is a fundamental wave mechanics theorem (not an experimental measurement error): confining an electron within a 1 Ångström atomic diameter automatically induces an intrinsic velocity spread of over 578 km/s.

Frequently asked questions

Why can an electron never be at absolute rest at the center of an atom?

If an electron were completely stationary (Δp = 0), its position uncertainty would be infinite (Δx = ∞), escaping atomic confinement entirely.
