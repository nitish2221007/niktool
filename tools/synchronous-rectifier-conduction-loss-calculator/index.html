Eliminating the 0.5V Forward Diode Barrier

At 20 Amperes, a 0.45V Schottky diode dissipates 9.0 Watts of waste heat; replacing it with a 2.5 mΩ synchronous MOSFET drops the voltage loss to just 50 mV, cutting thermal losses by 89% down to 1.0 Watt.
