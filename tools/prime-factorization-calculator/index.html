How to use Prime Factorization Calculator

  1. Enter a positive integer greater than 1.
  2. Click Process to break it into prime factors.
  3. Review the factor list and the exponent notation.

Key Features & Privacy

The tool uses trial division to find every prime factor and presents the result both as a product of primes and in exponent form, which matches how Class 10 problems are written. It validates the input and runs fully locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use it for the Real Numbers chapter, to find HCF and LCM by the factor method, and to verify their own factorization work.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
