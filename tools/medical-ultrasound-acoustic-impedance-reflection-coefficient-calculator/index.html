Frequently asked questions

Why cannot ultrasound penetrate through bone or lungs?

Bone has very high impedance ($7.8 ext{ MRayl}$) and lung air has very low impedance ($0.0004 ext{ MRayl}$); both create massive impedance mismatches that reflect nearly all ultrasound waves.
