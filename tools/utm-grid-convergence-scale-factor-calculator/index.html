Mapping True North vs Grid North Discrepancy

Because the Transverse Mercator cylinder maps curved meridians onto a flat Cartesian grid, Grid North deviates from True North by the convergence angle $\gamma = \Delta\lambda\cdot\sin\phi$, crucial for land surveyors and GPS navigation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the scale factor at the UTM Central Meridian?

By international convention, the scale factor at the central meridian is exactly $k_0 = 0.9996$ (compressing ground distances by 1 part in 2,500).
