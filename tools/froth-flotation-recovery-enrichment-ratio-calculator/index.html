Two-Product Metallurgical Mass Balance

Flotation operates on hydrophobic surface chemistry; calculating the two-product balance validates whether reagents (collectors, frothers) optimize the trade-off between maximizing grade vs maximizing valuable recovery.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 100% recovery impossible to achieve simultaneously with high grade?

As air bubble residence time increases to capture borderline locked middling particles, gangue rock is entrained, diluting the final concentrate grade.
