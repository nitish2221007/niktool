How to use Creatinine Clearance (Cockcroft-Gault CrCl / eGFR) Kidney Function Calculator

  1. Enter patient age in years.
  2. Enter patient total body weight in kilograms.
  3. Enter serum creatinine concentration $S_{cr}$ in mg/dL from lab blood work.
  4. Select patient biological sex (females have $sim 15%$ less muscle mass, requiring a $0.85$ factor).
  5. Inspect estimated Creatinine Clearance ($CrCl$) in mL/min for antibiotic and chemotherapy dose adjustments.

Donald W. Cockcroft & M. Henry Gault 1976 Renal Metric

FDA drug labeling mandates Cockcroft-Gault Creatinine Clearance for pharmacokinetic dosing adjustments of renally excreted medications (Vancomycin, Gentamicin, Enoxaparin, Novel Oral Anticoagulants NOACs) to avoid lethal drug toxicity.

Frequently asked questions

Why is female CrCl multiplied by 0.85?

Because women have on average 15% less endogenous muscle mass per unit body weight than men, producing less daily metabolic creatinine.
