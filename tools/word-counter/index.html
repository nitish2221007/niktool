How to use Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the input area.
  2. The statistics update automatically as you type, or click Process to refresh.
  3. Review your word count, character count, sentences, and reading time.

Key Features & Privacy

This counter provides a comprehensive breakdown of your text including characters with and without spaces, sentence count, and paragraph count. Because it runs entirely in your browser, your essays and private notes are never uploaded to a server.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to check if their assignments meet word limits, writers use it to track article length, and anyone can use it to estimate reading time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
