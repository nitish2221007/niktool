Richard Stribeck & Mayo D. Hersey Tribology Curve

The Stribeck curve illustrates how fluid film pressure builds with increasing velocity ($H = mu N / P$), lifting metal surfaces out of high-friction boundary contact ($f approx 0.15$) into frictionless hydrodynamic fluid shearing ($f < 0.005$) to ensure zero mechanical wear in automotive crankshaft bearings.

Frequently asked questions

Why do engine start-stops cause the most bearing wear?

At zero speed ($N=0$), the Hersey number is zero, forcing bearings into the boundary lubrication regime where metal asperities touch until the hydrodynamic wedge builds up.
