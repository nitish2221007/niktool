Backmixing Dilution Effect in Continuous Stirred Tanks

Because fresh feed immediately mixes into the bulk liquid inside a CSTR, reaction occurs at the lowest exit concentration ($C_{A,\text{exit}} = C_{A0}(1-X)$), requiring ~4x larger tank volume at 90% conversion compared to an unmixed tubular PFR.

Frequently asked questions

Why use a CSTR if PFR is smaller?

CSTRs provide superior temperature control for highly exothermic reactions and prevent hot spots through continuous agitation.
