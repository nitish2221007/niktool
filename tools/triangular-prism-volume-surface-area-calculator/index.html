Uniform Cross-Section Geometry

Like all right prisms, the 3D volume is strictly the product of the 2D cross-sectional base area and length: V = A_base · L.

Frequently asked questions

What is a right triangular prism?

A 3D polyhedron bounded by two congruent triangular end faces and three rectangular lateral sides joined at 90° angles.
