How to use Lineweaver-Burk Double Reciprocal Kinetics Calculator

  1. Enter biochemical V_max and K_m.

Diagnosing Competitive vs Noncompetitive Inhibitors

Competitive inhibitors increase K_m without altering V_max (swiveling the slope at the same y-intercept), whereas noncompetitive inhibitors decrease V_max with unchanged K_m (shifting the y-intercept upward).
