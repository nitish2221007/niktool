How to use Mechanical Ventilator Dynamic & Static Lung Compliance (C_stat) Calculator

  1. Enter delivered mechanical ventilator Tidal Volume $V_t$ in mL (e.g. 6 mL/kg ideal body weight).
  2. Enter Peak Inspiratory Pressure (PIP / $P_{ ext{peak}}$) in $ ext{cmH}_2 ext{O}$.
  3. Enter inspiratory-hold Plateau Pressure ($P_{ ext{plat}}$) in $ ext{cmH}_2 ext{O}$.
  4. Enter Positive End-Expiratory Pressure (PEEP) in $ ext{cmH}_2 ext{O}$.
  5. Inspect Static Lung Compliance $C_{ ext{stat}}$, Dynamic Compliance $C_{ ext{dyn}}$, and Driving Pressure ($Delta P = P_{ ext{plat}} - ext{PEEP}$).

ARDSNet Lung Protective Mechanical Ventilation Standard

Isolates chest wall/lung parenchymal elasticity ($C_{ ext{stat}}$) from endotracheal airway resistance, ensuring driving pressure stays below $14 ext{cmH}_2 ext{O}$ to avoid ventilator-induced lung injury (VILI).

Frequently asked questions

What causes a sudden drop in Static Compliance?

Pneumothorax, worsening pulmonary edema, ARDS consolidation, or mainstem endotracheal tube intubation decreases $C_{ ext{stat}}$, causing $P_{ ext{plat}}$ to rise.
