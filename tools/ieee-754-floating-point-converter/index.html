Hardware Floating Point Unit (FPU) Representation

IEEE-754 Single Precision (binary32) encodes real numbers as: (-1)^sign × 2^(exponent - 127) × 1.mantissa, offering approximately 7.2 decimal digits of precision across a dynamic range from 1.18×10⁻³⁸ to 3.4×10³⁸.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the exponent biased by 127?

Biasing the 8-bit unsigned integer range (0 to 255) by subtracting 127 allows smooth encoding of both positive and negative powers of 2 without a dedicated sign bit.
