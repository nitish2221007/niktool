Terzaghi 1925 One-Dimensional Consolidation Theory

Unlike porous sands which settle instantly, saturated clay soils squeeze out pore water slowly over years or decades ($S_c = rac{C_c H}{1 + e_0} log_{10} rac{sigma_0' + Deltasigma'}{sigma_0'}$), causing infamous historic structural tilting like the Leaning Tower of Pisa.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between primary and secondary consolidation?

Primary consolidation is caused by the slow dissipation of excess pore water pressure; secondary consolidation (creep) is the slow plastic readjustment of clay particle skeleton bonds.
