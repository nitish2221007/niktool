Frequently asked questions

What is a kinematic singularity in a 2-link arm?

When the arm is fully extended ($r = L_1 + L_2$) or folded back ($r = |L_1 - L_2|$), $ heta_2 = 0^circ$ or $180^circ$ and the arm loses 1 degree of freedom.
