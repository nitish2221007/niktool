How to use Nuclear Reactor Four-Factor Formula (k_∞ = ε·p·η·f) Calculator

  1. Enter fast fission factor $epsilon$ (typically 1.02 to 1.05).
  2. Enter resonance escape probability p (fraction of fast neutrons escaping capture by U-238 during moderation, ~0.80 to 0.90).
  3. Enter neutron reproduction factor $eta$ (fission neutrons produced per thermal absorption in fuel, ~2.0 to 2.1).
  4. Enter thermal utilization factor f (fraction of thermal neutrons absorbed in fuel vs moderator/structure, ~0.85 to 0.95).
  5. Enter total non-leakage probability $P_{ ext{NL}}$ ($P_{ ext{FNL}} cdot P_{ ext{TNL}}$).
  6. Inspect infinite multiplication factor $k_infty$, effective criticality $k_{ ext{eff}}$, and reactivity $ ho$ in pcm.
