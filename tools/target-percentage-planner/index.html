How to use Target Percentage Planner

  1. Enter your dream overall percentage (e.g., 90% or 95%).
  2. Enter the number of subjects and the maximum marks for each.
  3. Click Process to see the exact total marks required and the average you need per subject.

Key Features & Privacy

This planner translates a vague goal like "I want 90%" into concrete, per-subject numerical targets. It warns you if your target is mathematically impossible based on the max marks. All calculations stay in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use this at the start of the academic year to set realistic subject-wise goals for their pre-board and final board exams.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?
