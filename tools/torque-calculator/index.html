How to use Physics Torque & Rotational Power Calculator

  1. Enter applied force in Newtons (N) and perpendicular lever radius in meters (m).
  2. Optionally specify angle θ (90° gives maximum rotational leverage).
  3. Enter motor RPM to calculate dynamic shaft horsepower and kilowatts.

Torque vs Horsepower

Torque represents rotational twisting effort, while Horsepower is the rate at which torque performs mechanical work over time (HP = Torque in lb-ft × RPM / 5252).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is torque maximized at 90 degrees?

Because sin(90°) = 1.0, directing the entirety of applied force perpendicular to the pivot axis.
