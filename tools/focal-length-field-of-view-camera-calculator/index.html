50mm Human Visual Perspective

A 50mm lens on a 35mm full-frame camera produces a diagonal field of view of ~47°, matching the natural perspective magnification of human central vision.

Frequently asked questions

How does crop factor affect field of view?

An APS-C 1.5x crop sensor narrows the field of view: a 50mm lens behaves with the narrower perspective of a 75mm full-frame lens.
