Singapore MOE GCE A-Level 70-Point UAS Modernization

Starting from the 2026 intake, Singapore universities (NUS, NTU, SMU, SUTD) evaluate admission based on 3 H2 content subjects plus General Paper (maximum 70 Rank Points), reducing academic stress by making the 4th H1 subject and Project Work pass/fail.

Frequently asked questions

How does the new 70-point UAS compare to the legacy 90-point system?

Under the 70-point system, $3 ext{ H2 (60 pts)} + ext{GP (10 pts)} = 70 ext{ RP}$, equivalent to scaling a 90-point score without the 4th content subject and PW.
