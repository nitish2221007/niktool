About the exchange rate

This tool uses an approximate indicative rate of 1 USD ≈ ₹83.50. Real-world bank and forex rates vary due to spreads, fees, and market fluctuations. Always verify with your bank or a trusted forex provider before making actual transactions.

Frequently asked questions

Is the conversion rate accurate?

The rate used is indicative and based on recent market averages. Actual rates you receive from banks or money changers will differ slightly due to service fees and spreads.

Is my data sent to any server?

No. All calculations are performed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I convert INR back to USD?

Yes — simply divide your INR amount by 83.50, or use our dedicated INR to USD tool (coming soon).
