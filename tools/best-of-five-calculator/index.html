How to use Best of Five Calculator

  1. Enter marks for all your subjects (5 or more), separated by commas or newlines.
  2. Click Calculate Best 5 to automatically select your top 5 scores.
  3. View the total marks, percentage, and which subjects were excluded from the calculation.

Key Features & Privacy

This calculator automatically identifies your best 5 subjects and calculates percentage based on those. Note: Different boards and institutions may have different rules about which subjects count toward "best of 5". Always verify with your specific board's guidelines.

Common Use Cases

Students use this tool to understand how "best of 5" percentage works, plan which subjects to focus on, and verify their expected board results before official announcements.
