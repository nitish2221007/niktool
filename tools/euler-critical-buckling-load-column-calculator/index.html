How to use Euler Critical Column Buckling Load (P_cr) Calculator

  1. Select column end boundary conditions (Pinned-Pinned K=1.0, Fixed-Pinned K=0.7, Fixed-Fixed K=0.5, Flagpole K=2.0).
  2. Enter Young's Modulus of Elasticity in GPa (Steel = 200 GPa, Aluminum = 69 GPa).
  3. Enter minimum cross-section moment of inertia I_min in cm⁴.
  4. Enter unbraced column height L in meters.
  5. Inspect elastic critical buckling load threshold P_cr.

Leonhard Euler's 1757 Elastic Instability Theorem

Euler proved that slender compressive members fail through sudden lateral elastic buckling instability rather than material crushing yield strength, inversely proportional to the square of the effective length (P_cr ∝ 1/(KL)²).

Frequently asked questions

Why is minimum axis of inertia I_min used for column buckling?

Because a column will always buckle around its weakest geometric axis (lowest moment of inertia I_y) unless laterally braced.
