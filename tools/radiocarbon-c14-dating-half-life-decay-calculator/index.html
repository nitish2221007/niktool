Willard Libby 1960 Nobel Prize in Radiocarbon Dating

Living organisms constantly absorb atmospheric Carbon-14; upon death, C-14 decays exponentially ($t_{1/2} = 5,730 ext{ years}$), providing an unforgeable atomic clock for dating ancient Egyptian pharaohs, prehistoric cave art, and archaeological fossils up to 50,000 years old.

Frequently asked questions

What does "BP" mean in archaeological dating?

"Before Present" (BP) is the standard international radiocarbon timescale referenced to the origin year 1950 CE (before atmospheric nuclear testing altered natural C-14 levels).
