  1. Enter cutting tool shank / cutting edge diameter D in inches (e.g. 0.500" or 0.250").

  4. Enter recommended chip load per tooth (Feed Per Tooth IPT).
  5. Inspect spindle RPM and linear table travel feed rate in Inches Per Minute (IPM) and mm/min.

Optimizing Machining Tool Life & Surface Finish

Running too slow rubs and overheats the carbide cutting edges, while running too fast causes tool chipping; calculating exact chip load per tooth ensures proper thermal chip evacuation and clean mirror finishes.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 3.82 constant in the RPM formula?

3.82 = 12 / pi, which converts tool circumference in inches to surface speed in feet (SFM = pi · D · RPM / 12).
