Frequently asked questions

Why does wide bandgap Silicon Carbide (SiC: Eg = 3.2 eV) operate at much higher temperatures than Silicon?

Higher bandgap $E_g$ suppresses intrinsic carrier thermal leakage ($n_i propto e^{-E_g/2kT}$) by over 16 orders of magnitude, enabling $300^circ ext{C}+$ operation.
