Wave Energy Flux Conservation

Because wave energy flux is conserved as waves enter shallow water, the dramatic drop in wave group velocity forces wave energy to compress into a taller, steeper wave crest until breaking occurs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the breaker depth index?

Waves break when wave height reaches approximately 78% of the local water depth (H_b ≈ 0.78 · d_b).
