L.R. Plitt 1976 Hydrocyclone Modelling

Centrifugal vortex forces accelerate heavy, coarse particles outward to the conical wall to discharge through the apex spigot (underflow), while fine, liberated particles are carried upward in the central air core vortex to the overflow.

Frequently asked questions

How do you achieve a finer d50 cut size?

Using smaller diameter cyclones (e.g. 10cm vs 50cm) and higher inlet operating pressure creates higher G-forces, driving cut sizes down to 20-40 μm.
