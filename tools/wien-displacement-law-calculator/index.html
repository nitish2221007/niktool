Why Hotter Stars Appear Blue

Wien's law demonstrates that peak emission wavelength is inversely proportional to temperature: cooler stars (3,000 K) peak in the red/infrared, while scorching stars (20,000 K) peak in the blue/ultraviolet.

Frequently asked questions

At what wavelength does the Sun peak?

At 5,778 K, the Sun's spectrum peaks at approximately 501.5 nm (green-blue optical visible light).
