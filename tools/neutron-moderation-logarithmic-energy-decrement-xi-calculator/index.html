Neutron Elastic Billiard-Ball Collision Mechanics

Because maximum kinetic energy transfer in elastic collisions occurs between particles of equal mass ($m_n approx m_p$), light water ($A=1$) thermalizes neutrons in only 18 collisions versus 114 collisions for graphite ($A=12$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is Heavy Water (D2O) used if it takes more collisions than Light Water (H2O)?

Although Deuterium takes 25 collisions versus 18 for Hydrogen, Deuterium absorbs 600× fewer neutrons ($Sigma_a approx 0$), allowing natural un-enriched Uranium ($0.7%$ U-235) to reach criticality in CANDU reactors.
