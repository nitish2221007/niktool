CC-CV Charge Curve Efficiency Losses

Lithium charging follows a Constant Current / Constant Voltage (CC-CV) profile; internal chemical resistance and thermal conversion losses consume ~10% of input energy.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1C charging rate?

1C charge current equals the 1-hour numerical capacity of the battery (e.g. 5.0A for a 5,000 mAh pack).
