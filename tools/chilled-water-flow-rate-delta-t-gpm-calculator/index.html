How to use Chilled Water Flow Rate (GPM & ΔT) Sizing Calculator

  1. Enter central chiller cooling plant capacity in refrigeration tons.
  2. Select design temperature differential ΔT (10°F standard = 2.4 GPM/ton; 12°F to 16°F for low-energy variable primary pumping).
  3. Inspect total required hydronic pumping flow in GPM and recommended header pipe diameter.

Hydronic 500 Constant (Q = 500 · GPM · ΔT)

Because water weighs 8.33 lb/gal and specific heat is 1.0 BTU/(lb·°F), flow rate multiplies by 500 (8.33 × 60 min/hr) to directly convert thermal BTU/hr into GPM pump sizing.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 2.4 GPM per ton standard for 10°F ΔT?

GPM/Ton = 24 / 10°F = exactly 2.4 GPM per ton of refrigeration.
