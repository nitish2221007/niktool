Hydrostatic Law Principles

Hydrostatic pressure increases linearly with depth (P = ρ·g·h) due to the gravitational weight of fluid above. In seawater, pressure increases by roughly 1 atmosphere for every 10 meters of descent.

Frequently asked questions

What is the pressure at 10 meters underwater?

At 10 meters in freshwater, total absolute pressure is approximately 2 atmospheres (~200 kPa).
