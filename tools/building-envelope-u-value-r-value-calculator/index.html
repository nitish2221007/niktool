Fourier Conduction & ASHRAE 90.1 Energy Standards

Heat flows via series thermal resistance paths; calculating accurate composite U-values ensures building envelopes comply with IECC and ASHRAE continuous insulation requirements to prevent thermal bridging.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert US R-value to SI R-value?

R_SI = R_US / 5.678. (For example, US R-20 = 3.52 m²·K/W).
