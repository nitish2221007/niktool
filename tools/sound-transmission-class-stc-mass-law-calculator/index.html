The +6 dB per Mass Doubling Law of Acoustics

In the mass-controlled region, sound transmission loss increases by exactly 6 dB for every doubling of surface mass density ($2 imes m$) or doubling of acoustic frequency ($2 imes f$).

Frequently asked questions

Why do double-leaf drywall cavity walls outperform solid mass walls?

Separating the leaves with an air gap and sound-absorbing fiberglass breaks mechanical vibration bridging, yielding high STC (>55) with a fraction of the physical weight.
