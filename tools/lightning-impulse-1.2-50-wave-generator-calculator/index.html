IEC 60060 High-Voltage Lightning Insulation Certification

High-voltage power transformers and substation switchgear must withstand standard $1.2/50 mu ext{s}$ simulated lightning surges (rising in $1.2 mu ext{s}$, decaying to 50% in $50 mu ext{s}$) to prove transformer turn insulation will not suffer catastrophic puncture breakdowns during thunderstorms.

Frequently asked questions

What does "BIL" stand for in power systems?

Basic Impulse Insulation Level (BIL) is the crest voltage of the standard $1.2/50 mu ext{s}$ wave that equipment insulation can withstand without flashover (e.g. 1050 kV BIL for 230 kV grid transformers).
