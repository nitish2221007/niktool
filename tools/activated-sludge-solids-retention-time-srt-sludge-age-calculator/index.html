Biological Wastewater Treatment Primary Control Parameter

SRT ($ heta_c$) controls microbial population ecology: slow-growing nitrifying bacteria (Nitrosomonas / Nitrobacter) require $ heta_c ge 10 ext{ days}$ at $20^circ ext{C}$ to prevent ammonia washout.

Frequently asked questions

What is the typical SRT for an Extended Aeration plant?

Extended aeration oxidation ditches operate at long SRTs of $20 ext{ to }30 ext{ days}$, producing fully aerobically digested sludge.
