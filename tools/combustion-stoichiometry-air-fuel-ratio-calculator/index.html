Lambda Oxygen Sensor Closed-Loop Tuning

Three-way automotive catalytic converters require an exact stoichiometric air-fuel ratio (λ = 1.00 ± 0.01) to simultaneously convert CO, unburnt hydrocarbons (HC), and nitrogen oxides (NOx) into harmless CO₂, H₂O, and N₂.

Frequently asked questions

What is stoichiometric AFR for standard gasoline?

Exactly 14.7 : 1 by mass (14.7 grams of dry air per 1.0 gram of gasoline).
