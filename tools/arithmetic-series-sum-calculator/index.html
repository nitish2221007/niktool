How to use Arithmetic Series Sum Calculator

  1. Enter the starting first term (a₁).
  2. Enter the common difference (d) between consecutive terms.
  3. Enter the total number of terms (n).
  4. Click Calculate to inspect the total sum (Sₙ) and the nth term value.

Gauss Arithmetic Summation Formula

Carl Friedrich Gauss discovered the arithmetic summation method pairing first and last terms: Sₙ = (n/2) × (a₁ + aₙ) = (n/2) × (2a₁ + (n-1)d).
