Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Stability Criterion

In discrete-time sampled signals ($z = e^{sT}$), the continuous imaginary frequency axis maps onto the perimeter of the Unit Circle ($|z| = 1$); poles inside the unit circle guarantee stable bounded-input bounded-output (BIBO) recursive IIR digital filtering.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if a digital filter pole lies outside the unit circle (|z| > 1)?

The filter impulse response grows exponentially without bound, causing digital overflow and system instability.
