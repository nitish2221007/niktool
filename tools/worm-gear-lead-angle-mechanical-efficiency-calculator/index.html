Worm Gear Transmission Kinematics & Self-Locking

When lead angle is smaller than the friction angle ($gamma < phi$), friction prevents the gear from driving the worm backward, creating an inherent fail-safe holding brake for cranes, elevators, and guitar tuning pegs.

Frequently asked questions

Why do single-start worm gears have low efficiency (50-60%)?

Single-start worms operate at shallow lead angles ($gamma < 6^circ$), creating extensive sliding friction between meshing teeth.
