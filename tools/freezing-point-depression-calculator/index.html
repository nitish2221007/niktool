How to use Freezing Point Depression Calculator

  1. Enter the cryoscopic constant (Kf) for the solvent (Water Kf = 1.86 °C·kg/mol).
  2. Enter solution molality (m).
  3. Enter Van 't Hoff ionization factor (i = 1 for non-electrolytes like glucose, i = 2 for NaCl).
  4. Inspect the depressed freezing point.

Why Road Salt Melts Ice

Dissolving ionic salts in water disrupts ice crystal lattice formation, depressing the equilibrium freezing point below 0 °C and causing ambient snow and ice to melt into liquid brine.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Van 't Hoff factor for CaCl2?

Calcium chloride dissociates into 1 Ca²⁺ and 2 Cl⁻ ions, giving an ideal Van 't Hoff factor of i = 3.
