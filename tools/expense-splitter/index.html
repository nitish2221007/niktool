How to use Expense Splitter

  1. Enter the names of all people involved, separated by commas.
  2. List each expense on a new line in the format: Payer Name, Amount, Description.
  3. Click Calculate Split to see the minimized list of who owes whom.

Key Features & Privacy

Splitting bills can be messy, but this tool uses a debt-simplification algorithm to minimize the number of transactions needed to settle up. All calculations happen instantly in your browser, meaning your financial details and friend group names are never sent to any server.

Common Use Cases

This tool is widely used by friends sharing an Airbnb, roommates dividing monthly utility bills, colleagues splitting a lunch order, or groups on a vacation tracking shared costs like gas, groceries, and activities.
