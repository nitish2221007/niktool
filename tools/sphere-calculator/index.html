How to use Sphere Calculator

  1. Enter the radius of the sphere.
  2. Choose the pi value for your calculation.
  3. Click Process to get surface area, volume, and diameter with formulas.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool calculates the two key sphere measurements: Surface Area (4πr²) and Volume (4/3 πr³). It also shows the diameter for reference. All formulas are displayed with substitutions. Processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use this for the Surface Areas and Volumes chapter and real-world problems involving balls, globes, and spherical tanks.
