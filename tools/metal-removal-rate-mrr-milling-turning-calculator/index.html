Roughing Productivity Figure of Merit

MRR is the primary metric evaluating roughing efficiency in high-performance CNC machining centers; balancing high MRR against spindle horsepower limits tool deflection and machine vibration chatter.

Frequently asked questions

What is High Efficiency Milling (HEM)?

HEM uses a light radial width of cut (WOC 5-15%) combined with full axial flute depth (DOC 100-200%) at extreme feed rates to maximize MRR while keeping cutting temperatures low.
