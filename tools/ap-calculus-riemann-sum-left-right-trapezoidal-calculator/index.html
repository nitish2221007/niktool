Bernhard Riemann 1854 Integral Definition

As the number of subintervals approaches infinity ($n o infty, Delta x o 0$), the limit of the Riemann sum converges to the exact definite integral ($lim_{n oinfty} sum f(x_i)Delta x = int_a^b f(x) dx$), forming the core theorem tested in AP Calculus Free Response Questions (FRQ).

Frequently asked questions

When does a Left Riemann Sum underestimate the true integral?

For a strictly increasing function ($f'(x) > 0$), Left Riemann sums underestimate while Right Riemann sums overestimate the true area under the curve.
