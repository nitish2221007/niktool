Orthogonality in Computer Graphics and Game Physics

Two vectors are perpendicular (orthogonal, 90°) if and only if their dot product equals exactly zero (u · v = 0), a property used constantly in 3D surface normal lighting calculations.

Frequently asked questions

What does a dot product of zero signify?

It means the two vectors are strictly perpendicular (orthogonal) at a 90° angle.
