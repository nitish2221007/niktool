Frequently asked questions

What is a support vector in SVM?

Support vectors are the training points that lie on or violate the margin boundary ($y_i(mathbf{w}^Tmathbf{x}_i + b) le 1$); moving any non-support vector has zero effect on the separating hyperplane.
