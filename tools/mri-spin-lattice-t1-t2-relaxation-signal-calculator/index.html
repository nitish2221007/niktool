MRI creates versatile diagnostic soft-tissue contrast without ionizing radiation by adjusting sequence timing ($TR, TE$); short TR highlights differences in longitudinal $T_1$ thermal recovery, while long TE highlights transverse $T_2$ spin dephasing in edema and tumors.

Frequently asked questions

Why is CSF dark on T1-weighted but bright on T2-weighted MRI?

CSF water molecules tumble rapidly, giving them very long $T_1$ ($4,000 ext{ ms}$, unable to recover during short TR) and very long $T_2$ ($2,000 ext{ ms}$, maintaining signal during long TE).
