Albert Betz 1919 Aerodynamic Limit

Because wind must retain residual kinetic velocity to exit behind the rotor blades, no wind turbine can capture more than 16/27 (59.26%) of the kinetic energy in moving wind columns.

Frequently asked questions

Why is wind power proportional to the cube of wind speed (v³)?

Power equals energy per unit mass (½v²) multiplied by mass flow rate (ρAv), causing power to scale with the cube of velocity (P proportional to v³).
