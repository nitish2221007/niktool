Lambert's Cosine & Inverse-Square Photometric Law

Light spreads over the surface of an expanding sphere ($E propto 1/d^2$); doubling the distance from a fixture quarters the illuminance, while tilting the surface reduces flux by $cos heta$, governing energy-efficient LED placement in architectural lighting designs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the conversion factor between Lux and Foot-Candles?

$1 ext{ Foot-Candle} = 10.764 ext{ Lux}$ (since $1 ext{ m}^2 = 10.764 ext{ ft}^2$).
