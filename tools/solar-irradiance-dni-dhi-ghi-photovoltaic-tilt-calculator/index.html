How to use Solar Irradiance (GHI = DHI + DNI·cos θ_z) & PV Array Tilt Angle Calculator

  1. Enter Direct Normal Irradiance (DNI) in $ ext{W/m}^2$ measured perpendicular to the sun.
  2. Enter Diffuse Horizontal Irradiance (DHI) in $ ext{W/m}^2$ from atmospheric sky scattering.
  3. Enter solar zenith angle $ heta_z$ in degrees ($90^circ - ext{Elevation}$).
  4. Inspect Global Horizontal Irradiance ($GHI = DHI + DNI cos heta_z$) in $ ext{W/m}^2$.

Solar Resource Characterization Standard

GHI represents total solar radiation received on a horizontal surface; tracking DNI and DHI components allows solar engineers to model Plane-of-Array (POA) irradiance on fixed-tilt and single-axis tracking photovoltaic farms.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is peak sun hour (1,000 W/m²)?

Standard Test Condition (STC) defines 1 peak sun hour as $1,000 ext{ W/m}^2$ irradiance at $25^circ ext{C}$ cell temperature.
