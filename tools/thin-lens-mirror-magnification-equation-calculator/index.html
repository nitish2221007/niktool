Gaussian Thin Lens Optical Formula

The thin-lens formula ($ rac{1}{f} = rac{1}{d_o} + rac{1}{d_i}$) provides the fundamental geometric optics ray-tracing calculation used in designing camera lenses, microscopes, telescopes, and corrective eyeglasses.

Frequently asked questions

What is the physical difference between a Real and Virtual image?

A Real image ($d_i > 0$) is formed by actual converging light rays and can be projected onto a screen; a Virtual image ($d_i < 0$) is formed by diverging rays that appear to originate from behind the lens.
