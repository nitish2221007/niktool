How to use PID Controller Ziegler-Nichols Closed-Loop Tuning (K_p, T_i, T_d) Calculator

  1. Increase proportional gain with I and D turned off until the closed-loop system reaches continuous marginal sustained oscillation (Ultimate Gain $K_u$).
  2. Measure sustained oscillation period $T_u$ in seconds.
  3. Inspect calculated Proportional ($K_p$), Integral ($K_i$), and Derivative ($K_d$) control gains.

Frequently asked questions

What is the parallel vs ideal PID equation form?

Ideal form: $u(t) = K_p(e + rac{1}{T_i}int e,dt + T_d rac{de}{dt})$; Parallel form: $u(t) = K_p e + K_iint e,dt + K_d rac{de}{dt}$.
