How to use Rocket Engine Thrust & Specific Impulse (I_sp) Calculator

  1. Enter propellant total mass flow rate ṁ in kg/s (oxidizer + fuel).
  2. Enter gas exhaust velocity v_e at the nozzle exit plane in m/s.
  3. Enter nozzle bell exit plane cross-sectional area A_e in m².
  4. Enter pressure imbalance (p_e - p_a) in kPa (0 for perfectly expanded flow).
  5. Inspect total thrust generated in kN and lbf, and engine specific impulse (I_sp) in seconds.

Momentum Thrust vs Pressure Thrust

Total rocket thrust combines bulk momentum ejection and pressure imbalance forces on the nozzle face; when exit pressure matches ambient atmospheric pressure, thrust reaches maximum theoretical efficiency.

Frequently asked questions

What is the thrust of a SpaceX Merlin 1D engine?

A sea-level Merlin 1D produces approximately 845 kN (190,000 lbf) of thrust at an I_sp of 282 seconds.
