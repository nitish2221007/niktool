The True Financial Health Metric

Net worth represents your bottom-line financial standing: Net Worth = Total Assets - Total Liabilities. Tracking net worth over time is the ultimate measure of long-term wealth creation.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Can net worth be negative?

Yes, if debt liabilities exceed accumulated assets (common among recent college graduates with student loans), net worth is negative.
