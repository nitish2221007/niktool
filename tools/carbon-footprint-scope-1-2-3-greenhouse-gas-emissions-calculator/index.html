WRI & WBCSD Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol

Classifying emissions into Scope 1 (direct onsite fuels), Scope 2 (purchased electricity), and Scope 3 (upstream/downstream value chain) fulfills mandatory corporate ESG sustainability reporting and Science-Based Targets (SBTi) net-zero standards.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a metric tonne of CO2 equivalent (tCO2e)?

$ ext{tCO}_2 ext{e}$ standardizes different greenhouse gases into equivalent global warming potential relative to 1 metric ton ($1,000 ext{ kg}$) of Carbon Dioxide.
