Turbulent Boundary Layer Wall-Resolved Meshing

Near solid walls, steep velocity gradients in the viscous sublayer ($y^+ < 5$) dominate drag and separation; setting the correct inflation layer first cell height ($Delta y$) ensures turbulence models accurately predict aerodynamic stall, heat transfer, and skin friction.

Frequently asked questions

Why is y+ ≈ 1 required for SST k-ω and LES models?

Because low-Re turbulence models integrate transport equations directly through the laminar sublayer to the wall without using empirical semi-logarithmic wall function approximations.
