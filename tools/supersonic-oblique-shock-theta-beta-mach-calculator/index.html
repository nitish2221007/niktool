The Classical θ-β-Mach Supersonic Equation

When supersonic flow encounters a wedge, a planar oblique shock forms at angle β, abruptly slowing the normal flow component while compressing and heating the gas.

What happens if wedge angle θ exceeds θ_max?

The shock wave detaches from the wedge tip and curves into a detached subsonic bow shock wave ahead of the vehicle.
