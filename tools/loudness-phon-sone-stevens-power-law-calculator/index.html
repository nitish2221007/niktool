Stanley Smith Stevens 1936 Psychoacoustic Power Law

Human hearing perception is non-linear; an increase of +10 decibels/phons produces a doubling ($2 imes$) of perceived subjective loudness ($S = 2^{(P-40)/10}$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Decibels, Phons, and Sones?

Decibels measure physical acoustic energy; Phons measure frequency-weighted equal loudness contours; Sones measure direct linear subjective perception ($2 ext{ sones} = ext{twice as loud as }1 ext{ sone}$).
