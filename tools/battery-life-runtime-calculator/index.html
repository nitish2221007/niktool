How to use Battery Life & Runtime Calculator

  1. Enter the battery capacity rating (e.g. 2500 mAh 18650 Li-ion cell or 5000 mAh phone battery).
  2. Enter the continuous or average device power consumption load current (in mA or Amps).
  3. Click Calculate to inspect battery life duration with realistic discharge efficiency.

Peukert Effect and Real-World Derating

Batteries rarely deliver 100% of nominal capacity due to chemical internal resistance and voltage sag under high discharge rates. Applying an 85% safety factor provides a dependable real-world runtime estimate.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How long will a 2000 mAh battery run a 100 mA circuit?

(2000 mAh / 100 mA) × 0.85 = ~17 Hours of continuous operation.
