Aqueous pH Equilibrium

In pure water at 25 °C, the ion product Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴. Therefore, pH + pOH = 14 always holds in standard dilute solutions.

Frequently asked questions

What is human blood pH?

Normal arterial blood pH is tightly regulated between 7.35 and 7.45 (slightly alkaline).
