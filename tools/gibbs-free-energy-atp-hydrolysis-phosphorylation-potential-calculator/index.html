Frequently asked questions

Why does cellular ATP hydrolysis release more energy in vivo than in vitro?

Cells continually regenerate ATP, keeping the mass-action ratio $Q = rac{[ ext{ADP}][ ext{P}_i]}{[ ext{ATP}]}$ extremely small ($sim 10^{-4} ext{ M}$), providing an additional $-20 ext{ kJ/mol}$ of thermodynamic driving force.
