Alan Arnold Griffith 1921 Energy Release Rate Theorem

Demonstrates that a crack propagates unstably when elastic strain energy released by crack growth exceeds the surface energy needed to create new crack faces ($G ge 2gamma_s$).

Frequently asked questions

Why do metals have much higher fracture toughness than ceramics?

In ductile metals, significant plastic dissipation energy ($gamma_p gg gamma_s$) absorbs energy at the crack tip, increasing critical stress.
