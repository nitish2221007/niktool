How to use Solar Panel Array Daily Output & Yield Calculator

  1. Enter total solar array nameplate rating in Watts (e.g. 15 panels × 400W = 6,000W).
  2. Enter average local Peak Sun Hours (typically 3.5 to 5.5 hours per day).
  3. Select overall system derate efficiency.
  4. Inspect expected daily and monthly electricity harvest in Kilowatt-Hours (kWh).

Peak Sun Hours (1,000 W/m² Standard)

One Peak Sun Hour represents the equivalent solar irradiance of 1,000 Watts per square meter for 1 hour, standardizing solar calculation across different latitudes and seasons.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What factors cause solar derating?

High ambient temperature losses, inverter DC-to-AC conversion losses, dust/soiling, and cable resistance typically reduce output by 15-20% below STC nameplate ratings.
