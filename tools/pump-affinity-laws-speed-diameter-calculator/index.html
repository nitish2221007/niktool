Cubic Power Law (P ∝ N³) of VFD Variable Speed Drives

Because pump power consumption scales with the cube of speed (P ∝ N³), reducing pump motor speed by just 20% slashes electrical power consumption by nearly 50% (0.80³ = 0.512), making VFDs a massive industrial energy efficiency upgrade.

Frequently asked questions

What are the three pump affinity laws?

1) Flow varies linearly with speed (Q₂/Q₁ = N₂/N₁); 2) Head varies quadratically (H₂/H₁ = (N₂/N₁)²); 3) Power varies cubically (P₂/P₁ = (N₂/N₁)³).
