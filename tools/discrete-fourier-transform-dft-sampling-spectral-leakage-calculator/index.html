Discrete Fourier Transform Sampling Resolution Law

Frequency resolution depends solely on total time record duration ($Delta f = 1/T_{ ext{record}}$); applying smooth Hanning/Blackman windows tapers the signal edges to zero, suppressing discontinuous side-lobe spectral leakage by $>30 ext{ to }58 ext{ dB}$.

Frequently asked questions

Does zero-padding increase true physical frequency resolution?

No; zero-padding interpolates the spectrum with smoother sinc curves without adding new spectral information.
