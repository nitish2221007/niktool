Hazen's Surface Settling Principle

According to Allen Hazen's sedimentation theory, a particle settles out of suspension if its discrete settling velocity exceeds the upward fluid velocity, which is governed exclusively by the surface overflow rate (SOR = Q/A) regardless of tank depth.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard EPA design SOR for secondary clarifiers?

Standard design peak overflow rate is typically 600 to 800 gpd/ft² (1,000 to 1,200 gpd/ft² peak hourly flow) to prevent sludge solids washout.
