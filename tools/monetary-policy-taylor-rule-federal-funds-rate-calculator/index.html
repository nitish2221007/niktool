John B. Taylor 1993 Systematic Monetary Rule

The Taylor Principle states that when inflation rises by $1%$, the central bank must raise nominal interest rates by more than $1%$ (coefficient $1.5$) to ensure real interest rates rise and dampen macroeconomic demand.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GDP output gap?

The percentage difference between actual real GDP and potential full-employment GDP ($y - y^*$).
