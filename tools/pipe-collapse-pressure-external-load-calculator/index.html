Cubic D/t Ratio Collapse Sensitivity

Unlike internal burst pressure which scales linearly with (t/D), external collapse resistance scales with the cube of wall thickness ratio ($(t/D)^3$), making thick-walled pipe mandatory for deepwater ocean installations.

Why does a slight ovality drastically reduce collapse strength?

Even a 1% out-of-roundness imperfection creates asymmetric bending moments that trigger premature plastic collapse at less than half theoretical yield.
