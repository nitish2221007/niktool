Gross vs Net Salary Understanding

Gross salary is total stated compensation before mandatory withholding, whereas Net Pay represents actual disposable income deposited into your bank account.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

What is effective tax rate vs marginal tax rate?

Effective tax rate is the total percentage of overall income paid in tax, while marginal tax rate applies only to your highest tax bracket.
