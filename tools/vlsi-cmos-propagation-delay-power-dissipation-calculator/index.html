Frequently asked questions

What is CMOS dynamic power dissipation?

Dynamic power is consumed when charging and discharging internal gate and interconnect capacitance during switching transitions.

Why is supply voltage (VDD) so critical for IC power?

Dynamic power scales quadratically with VDD (P ∝ VDD²), so lowering VDD is the most effective way to reduce power consumption.

What is activity factor α?

Activity factor is the average fraction of clock cycles in which a gate output makes a power-consuming 0-to-1 transition.
