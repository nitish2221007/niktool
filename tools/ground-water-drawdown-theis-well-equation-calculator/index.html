How to use Theis Confined Aquifer Groundwater Well Drawdown Calculator

  1. Enter continuous well pumping discharge rate Q in $m^3/ ext{day}$.
  2. Enter confined aquifer transmissivity T in $m^2/ ext{day}$.
  3. Enter dimensionless storativity / storage coefficient S (typically $10^{-5}$ to $10^{-3}$ for confined aquifers).
  4. Enter observation distance r from pumping well in meters.
  5. Enter continuous pumping duration in days.
  6. Inspect groundwater head drawdown s in meters and feet.

Charles Vernon Theis 1935 Transient Groundwater Solution

The Theis solution integrates transient heat flow analogies into hydrogeology, predicting the expanding cone of depression around pumping wells to prevent aquifer overdraft and well interference.

Frequently asked questions

What is Storativity in a confined aquifer?

Storativity is the volume of water an aquifer releases from storage per unit surface area per unit decline in hydraulic head.
