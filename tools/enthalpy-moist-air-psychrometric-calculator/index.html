Thermodynamic Latent Enthalpy of Vaporization

At room temperature, the latent heat of water evaporation (2,501 kJ/kg) means that even small changes in humidity ratio dominate total air enthalpy, driving HVAC cooling energy consumption.

What is the specific enthalpy of standard comfort air (25°C, 50% RH, W = 10 g/kg)?

h = 1.006(25) + 0.010(2501 + 1.86×25) = 25.15 + 25.48 = 50.63 kJ/kg (21.77 BTU/lb).
