Sir Isaac Newton 1687 Centripetal Motion Principle

Even at constant speed, circular motion involves continuous acceleration ($a_c = v^2/r$) directed toward the center of the curve because the velocity vector direction changes continuously.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Centripetal and Centrifugal force?

Centripetal force is the real inward force (like tire friction or string tension) causing circular motion; centrifugal force is the fictitious outward inertia experienced in a rotating non-inertial frame of reference.
