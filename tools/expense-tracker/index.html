    How to use Expense Tracker

    1. Enter the amount you spent in the input field.
    2. Select the appropriate category and add a short description.
    3. Click Add Expense to save it locally and view your running summary.

    Key Features & Privacy

    This expense tracker stores all your financial data directly in your browser's LocalStorage. No data is ever transmitted to external servers, ensuring your personal spending habits remain completely private and secure.

    Common Use Cases

    Students and professionals use this tool to maintain a quick, lightweight daily spending log without needing to download heavy apps or create accounts. It is perfect for tracking monthly budgets, identifying spending leaks, and keeping a simple financial diary.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I use this tool?

    Simply enter your expense amount, select a category, and click Add Expense to log it and view your summary.

    Is my data secure?

    Yes, all processing and storage happen locally in your browser via LocalStorage.

    Is this tool free to use?

    Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
