Jacobus Henricus van 't Hoff 1887 Osmotic Pressure Law

Desalination only occurs when applied hydraulic pressure exceeds osmotic pressure ($Delta P > Pi$), driving pure water molecules across semipermeable polyamide thin-film composite membranes against the natural concentration gradient.

What is typical seawater osmotic pressure at 25°C?
