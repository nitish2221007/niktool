How to use Deep Foundation Pile Bearing Capacity (Meyerhof Method Q_u = Q_b + Q_s) Calculator

  1. Enter circular pile diameter D in meters (e.g. 0.50 m).
  2. Enter embedded foundation depth length L in meters.
  3. Enter representative Standard Penetration Test (SPT) soil N-value.
  4. Enter engineering safety factor FS (typically 2.5 to 3.0).
  5. Inspect allowable working load capacity in kN and metric tonnes, along with the percentage breakdown between toe end-bearing ($Q_b$) and shaft skin friction ($Q_s$).

G.G. Meyerhof 1976 Deep Foundation SPT Analysis

Pile foundations bypass weak surficial soils by transferring skyscraper column loads deep into dense bearing strata via simultaneous shaft perimeter shear friction ($ au = 2N ext{ kPa}$) and high-pressure toe end-bearing ($q_p = 400N ext{ kPa}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is a safety factor of 2.5 to 3.0 standard for piles?

High safety factors account for subterranean soil heterogeneity, pile installation group shadow effects, and down-drag negative skin friction.
