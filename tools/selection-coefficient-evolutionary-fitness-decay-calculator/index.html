J.B.S. Haldane & Sewall Wright Selection Trajectory

Natural selection changes allele frequencies deterministically according to relative fitness differentials ($W = 1 + s$); beneficial mutations spread with sigmoidal logistic dynamics across generations until reaching 100% species fixation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical selection coefficient in nature?

Most strongly selected adaptive mutations (such as lactase persistence in humans or antibiotic resistance in bacteria) have selection coefficients between $s = 0.01$ and $s = 0.15$.
