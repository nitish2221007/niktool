How to use Cohen-Coon Reaction Curve Open-Loop PID Controller Tuning Calculator

  1. Enter open-loop step test steady-state process gain K ($Delta ext{PV} / Delta ext{CO}$).
  2. Enter process dominant lag time constant $ au$ in seconds (time to reach 63.2% of steady-state).
  3. Enter pure transportation dead-time delay $ heta$ in seconds.
  4. Inspect tuned Cohen-Coon proportional gain $K_p$, integral reset time $T_i$ (s), and derivative rate time $T_d$ (s).

G.H. Cohen & G.A. Coon 1953 Dead-Time Tolerant PID Tuning

Unlike classic Ziegler-Nichols tuning which becomes wildly oscillatory when dead time exceeds 20% of the time constant ($ heta/ au > 0.2$), Cohen-Coon incorporates the dead-time ratio directly into the gain equations to deliver quarter-amplitude decay response in slow thermal and chemical processes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the dead time ratio (θ/τ) rule of thumb?

Processes with $ heta/ au < 0.3$ are easy to control with PID; processes with $ heta/ au > 1.0$ are dead-time dominated and require Smith Predictors or Model Predictive Control (MPC).
