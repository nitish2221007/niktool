Alveolar-Arterial Oxygen Gas Diffusion Barrier

A normal A-a gradient with low $PaO_2$ indicates pure hypoventilation (narcotic overdose), whereas an elevated A-a gradient confirms intrinsic lung pathology (pulmonary embolism, pneumonia, pulmonary edema).
