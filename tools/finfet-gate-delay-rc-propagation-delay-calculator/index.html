Why are multiple fins used in FinFETs instead of widening the gate?

FinFET width is quantized in discrete fin heights ($W_{ ext{eff}} = N imes (2 H_{ ext{fin}} + T_{ ext{fin}})$); drive strength increases by adding parallel fins.
