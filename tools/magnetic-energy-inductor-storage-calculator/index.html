Switch-Mode Power Supply (SMPS) Energy Transfer

Flyback and Boost DC-DC converters store electrical energy in an inductor's magnetic field during the switch-ON phase (E = ½LI²) and discharge it into the output capacitor during switch-OFF.

Frequently asked questions

What is the energy in a 10 mH coil carrying 2 Amps?

E = ½ × 0.010 H × (2A)² = 0.5 × 0.010 × 4 = 0.020 Joules (20 millijoules).
