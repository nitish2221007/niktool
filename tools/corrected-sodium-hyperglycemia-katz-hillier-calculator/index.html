Hyperglycemic Intracellular-Extracellular Osmotic Shift

High extracellular glucose draws water out of cells into vascular space by osmosis, diluting measured serum sodium; calculating corrected sodium prevents mistakenly infusing hypotonic fluids in DKA/HHS.
