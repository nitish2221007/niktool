American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM) Equations

The ACSM metabolic running model calculates the precise volume of oxygen consumed during steady-state aerobic exertion, accounting for horizontal kinetic work (0.2·v) and vertical gravitational climbing work (0.9·v·grade).

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 MET in oxygen consumption?

1 MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) represents resting basal oxygen uptake: exactly 3.5 mL of O₂ per kilogram of body mass per minute.
