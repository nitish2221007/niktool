USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) Standard

Standard empirical method for estimating watershed storm flood runoff volumes based on hydrologic soil group (A, B, C, D), land use, and antecedent moisture condition (AMC).

Frequently asked questions

What is the physical meaning of Curve Number CN = 100?

$CN = 100$ represents a completely impervious surface ($S = 0, I_a = 0$) where $100%$ of rainfall immediately converts to runoff ($Q = P$).
