How to use B.Tech CGPA Calculator

  1. Enter the total credits and your SGPA for each semester you have completed.
  2. If you are still studying, simply leave future semesters blank or remove them using the "Remove" button.
  3. Click Calculate CGPA to instantly see your overall CGPA and estimated percentage.

How is CGPA calculated?

The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated by multiplying the SGPA of each semester by its respective total credits, summing those values, and dividing by the total number of credits across all semesters.

CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) / Σ(Credits)

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert CGPA to percentage?

This tool uses the standard AICTE formula: Percentage = (CGPA - 0.75) × 10. Note that some universities use a different multiplier (e.g., CGPA × 10). Always check your university's official grading policy.

Can I use this for diploma or M.Tech?

Yes! The math is the same. Just enter the credits and SGPA for your specific course structure. You can add or remove semesters as needed.

Is my academic data stored anywhere?

No. This tool works completely offline in your browser. Your grades and credits are never uploaded to a server.

What if I failed a subject or have a backlog?

Enter the SGPA exactly as it appears on your official semester marks memo. The calculator relies on your final semester SGPA, which already factors in any backlogs or grade deductions.
