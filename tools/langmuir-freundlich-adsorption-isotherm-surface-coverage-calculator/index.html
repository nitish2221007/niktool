Irving Langmuir 1918 Nobel Prize Surface Chemistry Model

The Langmuir isotherm assumes monolayer coverage on a homogeneous surface with zero adsorbate-adsorbate lateral interactions, providing the theoretical basis for catalytic converters and water purification activated carbon filters.

Frequently asked questions

What happens at high pressure in the Langmuir isotherm?

At high pressure ($KP gg 1$), the surface coverage approaches $100%$ ($ heta ightarrow 1.0$), reaching saturation where adsorption rate equals desorption rate.
