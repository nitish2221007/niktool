Michael Faraday Field Theory Formulation

The electric field ($ ec{E} = - abla V$) represents the physical force per unit positive test charge ($ ec{F} = q ec{E}$), mediating electrostatic action-at-a-distance across empty space at the speed of light.

Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between Electric Field and Electric Potential?

The electric field is the negative spatial gradient (slope) of electric potential ($E = -dV/dr$).
