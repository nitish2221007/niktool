Biot Number Criterion (Bi < 0.1)

Lumped capacitance assumes spatial temperature uniformity within the body, which is mathematically valid whenever the internal conduction resistance is negligible compared to external convection (Biot Number Bi = h·L_c/k < 0.1).

What happens at t = τ (one time constant)?
