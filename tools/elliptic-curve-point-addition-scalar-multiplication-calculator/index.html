Frequently asked questions

What is the Point at Infinity (O) in elliptic curves?

The Point at Infinity $mathcal{O}$ acts as the additive identity element ($P + mathcal{O} = P$ and $P + (-P) = mathcal{O}$) in the abelian group of points on the curve.
