Charles-Augustin de Coulomb 1776 Soil Shear Strength Law

The Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion models soil shear resistance as the sum of particle interlocking friction ($sigma anphi$) and electrostatic clay cohesion (c), governing slope stability, retaining walls, and foundation bearing capacity.

Frequently asked questions

What is the friction angle of a purely cohesive clay vs dry sand?

Saturated undrained clay has $phi_u = 0^circ$ (pure cohesion $c = S_u$), while clean sand has $c = 0 ext{ kPa}$ (pure friction $phi approx 30^circ ext{–}38^circ$).
