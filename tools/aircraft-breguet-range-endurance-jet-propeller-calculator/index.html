How to use Breguet Aircraft Range Equation (Jet & Propeller Range / Endurance) Calculator

  1. Enter true airspeed cruise velocity V in km/h (e.g. 850–900 km/h).
  2. Enter engine Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption (TSFC) in $ ext{hr}^{-1}$ (typically 0.50–0.60 for modern high-bypass turbofans).
  3. Enter aerodynamic lift-to-drag cruise efficiency ratio L/D (16 to 20 for commercial airliners).
  4. Enter initial takeoff gross weight $W_0$ in kg.
  5. Enter final empty/landing gross weight $W_1$ in kg.
  6. Inspect total maximum non-stop cruise range in km and Nautical Miles (NM), flight endurance time, and burned fuel weight.

Louis Charles Breguet 1921 Aircraft Range Standard

The universal performance equation balancing structural lightweighting ($ln(W_0/W_1)$), aerodynamic efficiency ($L/D$), and engine fuel efficiency ($ ext{TSFC}$) for airline route planning.

Frequently asked questions

Why is cruising at high altitude optimal for jet aircraft?

In cold, thin stratospheric air, jet engines operate at peak thermodynamic efficiency while maintaining high true airspeed at optimal lift coefficient.
