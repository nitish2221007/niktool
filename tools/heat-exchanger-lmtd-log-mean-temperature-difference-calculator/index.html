Thermal Engineering Heat Exchanger Design

Because the temperature difference between hot and cold streams varies logarithmically along the pipe length, simple arithmetic mean temperature difference overestimates heat transfer; LMTD provides the exact integrated logarithmic average.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Counter-Flow more efficient than Parallel-Flow?

Counter-flow maintains a larger, more uniform temperature driving force ($Delta T$) along the entire heat exchanger, allowing the cold fluid outlet temperature to exceed the hot fluid outlet temperature.
