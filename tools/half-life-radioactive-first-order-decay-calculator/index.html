How to use First-Order Reaction Kinetics & Half-Life (t₁/₂) Calculator

  1. Enter initial reactant concentration C₀ (in Molarity or percentage %).
  2. Enter first-order kinetic rate constant k ($s^{-1}$, $min^{-1}$, or $h^{-1}$).
  3. Enter elapsed reaction time duration t.
  4. Inspect remaining concentration $C_t$, half-life $t_{1/2}$, and elapsed half-life cycles.

Concentration-Independent Half-Life

For all first-order chemical reactions and nuclear decay processes, half-life is completely independent of starting concentration ($t_{1/2} = ln(2)/k approx 0.693/k$), decaying by exactly 50% every cycle.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How much drug remains after 5 half-lives?

After 5 half-lives, $(1/2)^5 = 1/32 = 3.125%$ of the original drug remains in the bloodstream (96.875% cleared).
