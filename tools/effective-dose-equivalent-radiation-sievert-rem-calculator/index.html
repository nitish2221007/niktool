How to use Radiation Effective Dose Equivalent (Sieverts & Rems: E = ∑ w_T·H_T) Calculator

  1. Enter physical absorbed radiation dose D in milliGray (mGy, 1 mGy = 1 mJ/kg).
  2. Enter radiation quality weighting factor $w_R$ ($1.0$ photons/electrons, $10.0$ fast neutrons, $20.0$ alpha particles).
  3. Enter ICRP 103 tissue radiosensitivity weighting factor $w_T$ ($0.12$ lungs/colon/bone marrow, $0.04$ bladder/thyroid, $0.01$ skin).
  4. Inspect Equivalent Dose $H_T$ and whole-body Effective Dose E in milliSieverts (mSv) and millirem.

ICRP Publication 103 Radiological Protection Standard

Quantifies stochastic biological cancer risk by weighting physical energy deposition against radiation ionization density ($w_R$) and specific organ biological radiosensitivity ($w_T$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the average natural background radiation dose for humans?

Global average natural background radiation is approximately $2.4 ext{ mSv/year}$ ($240 ext{ mrem/year}$) primarily from radon, cosmic rays, and food.
