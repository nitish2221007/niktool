Paraxial Helmholtz Equation TEM₀₀ Spatial Profile

Diffraction dictates an inescapable trade-off: tightly focused laser spots ($w_0 < 10 mu ext{m}$) have immense optical intensity but short Rayleigh depths of focus ($z_R propto w_0^2$), while collimated laser beams require large initial beam diameters to suppress far-field angular beam expansion ($ heta = lambda / pi w_0$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the definition of Rayleigh Range (zR)?

$z_R$ is the distance from the waist where the beam cross-sectional area doubles and beam radius expands by $sqrt{2} approx 1.414 imes w_0$.
