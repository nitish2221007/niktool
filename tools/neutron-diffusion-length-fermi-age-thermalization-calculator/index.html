Frequently asked questions

Why does Heavy Water (D2O) have a much larger diffusion length than Light Water (H2O)?

Deuterium has a nearly 600× smaller thermal neutron absorption cross-section than hydrogen ($Sigma_a approx 0.00003 ext{ cm}^{-1}$ vs $0.022 ext{ cm}^{-1}$), allowing neutrons to diffuse over $100 ext{ cm}$.
