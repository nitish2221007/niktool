Harriet Chick & Herbert E. Watson 1908 Disinfection Law

Chick-Watson law ($N_t / N_0 = e^{-k C^n T}$) proves that pathogen kill rate depends directly on the mathematical product of chemical concentration and contact time ($CT$), ensuring municipal drinking water safety against waterborne pathogens.

Frequently asked questions

Why is higher CT required in cold water (0.5°C vs 20°C)?

Cold water slows down chemical oxidation reaction rates of chlorine with microbial cell membranes, requiring longer contact time.
