Frequently asked questions

What is the ITU-R P.1238 propagation model?

ITU-R P.1238 is an international standard empirical model for indoor radio propagation loss between 300 MHz and 100 GHz.

What does the distance power loss coefficient N represent?

N represents how quickly signal power decays with distance; N=20 is free space, whereas obstacles increase N up to 40.

Why is indoor path loss higher than free space?

Walls, floors, furniture, and multipath interference add attenuation compared to open air.
