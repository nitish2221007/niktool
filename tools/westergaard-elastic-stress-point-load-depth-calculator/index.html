H.M. Westergaard 1938 Layered Soil Stress Distribution

While Boussinesq assumes an isotropic elastic half-space, Westergaard models stratified layered soils interspersed with rigid horizontal clay/silt sheets that resist lateral expansion ($epsilon_x = epsilon_y = 0$), providing more realistic settlement calculations for sedimentary soil deposits.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Westergaard stress lower than Boussinesq directly beneath the load (r=0)?

Directly under the load ($r=0$), Westergaard yields $sigma_z = rac{1}{2pi} rac{P}{z^2} approx 0.318 rac{P}{z^2}$, whereas Boussinesq yields $ rac{3}{2pi} rac{P}{z^2} approx 0.477 rac{P}{z^2}$ because horizontal reinforcement spreads stress wider.
