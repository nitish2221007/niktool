The Crucial Fourth-Power Radius Law (r⁴)

Flow rate scales with the fourth power of radius (Q ∝ r⁴): widening a pipe or blood vessel by just 20% increases volumetric flow rate by more than double (1.2⁴ ≈ 2.07x).

Frequently asked questions

Why is Poiseuille's law important in cardiovascular medicine?

Mild arterial plaque narrowing causes massive increases in vascular resistance, forcing the heart to generate dangerously high blood pressure.
