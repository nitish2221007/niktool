How to use Appliance Electricity Cost & Bill Calculator

  1. Enter the appliance wattage (listed on the device label).
  2. Enter daily operating hours.
  3. Enter your local utility cost per kilowatt-hour ($/kWh).
  4. Click Calculate to inspect daily, monthly, and annual utility bills.

How to Reduce Household Energy Consumption

Energy Consumption (kWh) = (Watts × Hours) / 1000. Heating and cooling appliances (AC, space heaters, water heaters) consume the majority of residential power.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 1500W space heater cost to run for 8 hours at $0.15/kWh?

(1.5 kW × 8 hrs) × $0.15 = $1.80 per day (~$54 per month).
