Emergency Medicine Toxicology Triage Metric

An elevated Osmolar Gap ($>10 ext{ mOsm/kg}$) provides an immediate emergency diagnostic flag for lethal antifreeze (Ethylene Glycol) or windshield wiper fluid (Methanol) poisoning hours before gas chromatography lab results return.

Frequently asked questions

Why are glucose and BUN divided by 18 and 2.8?

To convert clinical concentration units (mg/dL) into molar osmotic concentrations (mOsm/L) based on molecular weights ($180 ext{ g/mol}$ for glucose and $28 ext{ g/mol}$ for nitrogen).
