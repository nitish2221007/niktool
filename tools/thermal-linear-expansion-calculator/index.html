Why Civil Engineers Use Bridge Expansion Joints

A 1,000-meter steel bridge subjected to a 40 °C seasonal temperature change expands by nearly half a meter (48 cm). Finger expansion joints prevent destructive compressive buckling.

Frequently asked questions

Why is steel reinforced concrete so durable?

Steel and concrete share nearly identical thermal expansion coefficients (α ≈ 12 × 10⁻⁶ / °C), preventing thermal shear delamination during temperature swings.
