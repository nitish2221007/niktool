How to use ROI (Return on Investment) Calculator

  1. Enter the initial investment or campaign spend amount.
  2. Enter the total revenue generated or ending asset value.
  3. Optionally specify the investment period in years.
  4. Click Calculate to inspect your net return and annualized efficiency.

How to Interpret ROI

Return on Investment (ROI) evaluates the efficiency of an investment: ROI (%) = ((Net Profit) / (Cost of Investment)) × 100. An ROI greater than 0% means the investment was profitable.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI for online marketing?

A 5:1 ratio (500% ROI or $5 revenue for every $1 spent) is generally considered strong for digital ad campaigns.
