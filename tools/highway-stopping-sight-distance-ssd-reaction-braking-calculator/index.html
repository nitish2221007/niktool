AASHTO Green Book Highway Safety Geometric Standard

Designing vertical crest curves and horizontal turns with sight distance exceeding SSD ($SSD = d_{ ext{reaction}} + d_{ ext{braking}}$) ensures motorists can spot a 15 cm stationary obstacle and safely stop without collision.

Frequently asked questions

Why is perception-reaction time set to 2.5 seconds?

2.5 seconds accommodates the 90th percentile of human driver reaction capabilities across complex unexpected highway conditions.
