Relativistic Doppler Blue-Shift in Undulators

Frequently asked questions

What does K < 1 vs K > 1 mean in undulators?

When K <= 1, the device is a pure linear undulator emitting a single sharp spectral peak; when K >> 1, it acts as a wiggler emitting a broadband synchrotron spectrum.
