How to use Color Contrast Checker

  1. Enter or pick your Foreground (Text) and Background colors in HEX format.

  3. Evaluate your score against WCAG AA (4.5:1 for normal text) and AAA (7:1) guidelines.

WCAG 2.1 Contrast Standards

Accessible color contrast ensures readability for users with visual impairments or color blindness. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) Level AA requires a minimum ratio of 4.5:1 for standard text and 3:1 for large headings.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is WCAG AA threshold?

Level AA requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for regular text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold).
