Depth of Discharge (DoD) & Battery Lifespan

Discharging lead-acid batteries beyond 50% causes rapid sulfation and destroys cells in months; modern Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) chemistry safely operates at 90% Depth of Discharge for over 6,000+ daily charge cycles (15+ years).

Frequently asked questions

Why is Round-Trip Efficiency (RTE) important in solar batteries?

RTE measures energy lost as heat during chemical charge and discharge; LiFePO4 batteries deliver ~95% RTE compared to only ~75-80% for lead-acid.
