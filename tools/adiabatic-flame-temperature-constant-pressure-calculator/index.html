First Law Combustion Enthalpy Balance

Adiabatic flame temperature assumes complete chemical reaction with zero thermal heat loss to the environment ($Delta H_R = Delta H_P$); calculating $T_{ ext{ad}}$ is essential for selecting superalloy thermal barrier coatings in jet engine combustors and industrial furnaces.

Frequently asked questions

Why is actual flame temperature always lower than theoretical Tad?

High temperatures ($>1800 ext{ K}$) trigger endothermic chemical dissociation ($CO_2 ightleftharpoons CO + rac{1}{2}O_2, H_2O ightleftharpoons OH + H$), absorbing heat alongside radiative wall losses.
