The Dual of the Cube

A regular octahedron is one of the five classical Platonic solids and is the geometric dual of the cube: connecting the center points of the six faces of a cube creates an octahedron.

Frequently asked questions

How many faces, vertices, and edges does an octahedron have?

An octahedron has 8 equilateral triangular faces, 6 vertices, and 12 edges (V - E + F = 6 - 12 + 8 = 2).
