François-Marie Raoult 1887 Ideal Solution Law

Raoult's Law establishes that the vapor above an ideal mixture is always enriched in the more volatile component ($y_A > x_A$), providing the thermodynamic operating principle behind petrochemical crude oil distillation columns.

Frequently asked questions

What causes positive or negative deviations from Raoult's Law?

Strong intermolecular attractions between dissimilar molecules (A-B > A-A) cause negative deviations; repulsive interactions cause positive deviations and azeotropes.
