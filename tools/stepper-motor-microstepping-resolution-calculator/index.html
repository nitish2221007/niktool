  5. Inspect exact steps/mm calibration value for 3D printer / CNC firmware and required microcontroller step pulse frequency in kHz.

Microstepping Torque vs Positional Resolution

Microstepping subdivides full step angles by modulating dual sine-cosine coil currents; this eliminates low-speed mechanical resonance and quietens motor operation.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 80 steps/mm the standard value on Ender 3 printers?

200 steps/rev × 16 microsteps = 3,200 pulses; dividing by a 40 mm/rev GT2 belt pulley (20 teeth × 2mm) yields exactly 80.0 steps/mm.
