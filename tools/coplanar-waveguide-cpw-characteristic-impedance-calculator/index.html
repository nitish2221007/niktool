Coplanar Waveguide Microwave Planar Geometry

CPW lines place signal and ground conductors on the same top metal layer, eliminating the parasitic inductance of ground vias and providing low dispersion up to 100+ GHz for monolithic microwave integrated circuits (MMICs).

Frequently asked questions

Why is CPW favored over standard microstrip for high frequencies?

CPW confines electric field lines in the narrow surface slot gaps, significantly reducing radiation loss and dielectric substrate thickness sensitivity.
