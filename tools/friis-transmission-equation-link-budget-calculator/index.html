How to use Friis Transmission Equation Free-Space Link Budget Calculator

  1. Enter transmitter output power P_t in dBm (e.g. 20 dBm = 100 mW).
  2. Enter transmitter antenna gain G_t and receiver antenna gain G_r in dBi.
  3. Enter carrier radio frequency in MHz (e.g. 2400 MHz for 2.4 GHz WiFi, 868/915 MHz for LoRa).
  4. Enter line-of-sight propagation distance in kilometers.
  5. Inspect received power in dBm and verify positive link budget margin over receiver sensitivity.

Harald T. Friis 1946 Bell Labs Radio Transmission Formula

Free-space path loss expands quadratically with distance as electromagnetic wave fronts expand over the surface area of a sphere, attenuating received power predictably in line-of-sight channels.

Frequently asked questions

Why do higher frequencies suffer more free-space loss?

Because effective antenna aperture area shrinks inversely with frequency squared, capturing fewer photons at higher frequencies for an isotropic receiver.
