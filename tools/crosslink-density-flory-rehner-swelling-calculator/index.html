Paul Flory & John Rehner Jr. 1943 Rubber Theory

At swelling equilibrium, thermodynamic osmotic mixing forces that drive solvent inside the rubber network are exactly balanced by elastic conformational entropic retraction forces of stretched polymer chains.

Frequently asked questions

What does a higher crosslink density mean?

More crosslinks restrict polymer chain expansion, leading to lower solvent swelling ($v_r$ closer to 1), higher elastic modulus, and increased rubber hardness (Shore A durometer).
