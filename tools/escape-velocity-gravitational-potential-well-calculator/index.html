Newtonian Gravitational Potential Well

Frequently asked questions

What is the escape velocity of a black hole?

At a black hole's Schwarzschild event horizon ($R = 2GM/c^2$), the escape velocity equals the speed of light ($c approx 300,000 ext{ km/s}$).
