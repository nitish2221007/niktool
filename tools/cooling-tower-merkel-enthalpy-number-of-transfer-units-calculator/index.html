Friedrich Merkel 1925 Cooling Tower Theory

Combines sensible heat transfer and latent evaporative mass transfer into a single air enthalpy driving potential ($h_s - h_a$), defining the international design standard for HVAC and power plant cooling towers.

Frequently asked questions

Why can water never be cooled below the wet-bulb temperature?

At the wet-bulb temperature, ambient air is $100%$ saturated with water vapor ($ ext{RH}=100%$), halting net evaporation.
