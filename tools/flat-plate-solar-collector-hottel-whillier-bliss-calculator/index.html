How to use Flat-Plate Solar Collector (Hottel-Whillier-Bliss) Efficiency Calculator

  1. Enter total solar irradiance on tilted collector plane $G_T$ in $W/m^2$.
  2. Enter collector inlet water circulation temperature $T_{ ext{in}}$ in Celsius.
  3. Enter ambient outdoor air dry-bulb temperature $T_a$ in Celsius.
  4. Enter total collector aperture gross area in $m^2$.
  5. Inspect instantaneous thermal efficiency $eta$, useful thermal power output $Q_u$ in kW, and maximum stagnation safety temperature.

The HWB linear efficiency curve ($eta = F_R( aualpha) - F_R U_L rac{T_{ ext{in}} - T_a}{G_T}$) is the worldwide ISO 9806 certification standard for rating solar domestic hot water collectors and glazed solar thermal panels.

Frequently asked questions

What is Collector Stagnation Temperature?

Stagnation is the maximum equilibrium temperature reached when fluid pump circulation stops ($Q_u = 0$), where all incident solar energy is dissipated as thermal radiation/convection loss.
