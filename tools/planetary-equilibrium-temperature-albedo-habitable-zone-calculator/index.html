Frequently asked questions

Why is Earth's actual average temperature (+15°C) warmer than its equilibrium temp (-18°C)?

Atmospheric greenhouse gases ($H_2O, CO_2$) trap outgoing infrared radiation, providing $+33 ext{ K}$ of natural greenhouse warming.
