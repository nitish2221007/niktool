Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Secular and Transient equilibrium?

Secular equilibrium occurs when the parent half-life is thousands of times longer than the daughter ($T_1 gg T_2$), causing daughter activity to equalize exactly with parent activity ($A_2 = A_1$).
