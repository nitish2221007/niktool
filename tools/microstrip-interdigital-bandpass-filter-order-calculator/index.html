Chebyshev Equal-Ripple Polynomial Steepness

By allowing small controlled ripple in the passband, Chebyshev filters produce a vastly steeper cutoff skirt than Butterworth filters, reducing the number of inductor/capacitor resonant tank stages required for sharp RF channel isolation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the trade-off of using a Chebyshev filter over a Butterworth filter?

Chebyshev filters have non-linear phase response and group delay distortion near the band edges, whereas Butterworth filters offer smooth linear phase response.
