Osmotic Pressure in Physiology

Osmotic pressure governs fluid movement across cell membranes. Intravenous IV infusions must be isotonic (~7.8 atm at 37 °C) to prevent red blood cells from swelling (hemolysis) or shrinking (crenation).

Frequently asked questions

What is Reverse Osmosis (RO)?

Applying hydrostatic pressure exceeding natural osmotic pressure forces pure water backwards through a semipermeable membrane, filtering out salts and contaminants.
