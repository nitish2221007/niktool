Friedrich Pockels 1893 Linear Electro-Optic Effect

Applying an electric field directly alters the crystal's birefringent refractive indices, rotating polarized laser light by 90° in sub-nanosecond timescales to switch giant pulses out of laser cavities.

Frequently asked questions

Why is quarter-wave voltage (V_π/2) used in laser cavities?

Because light passes through the Pockels crystal twice (forward and backward reflecting off the cavity end mirror), applying $V_{\pi/2}$ delivers the full half-wave 90° polarization rotation.
