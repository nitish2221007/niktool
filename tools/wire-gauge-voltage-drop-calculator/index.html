How to use Wire Gauge & Voltage Drop Calculator

  1. Enter the source supply voltage (e.g. 12V DC automotive/solar or 120V/230V AC).
  2. Enter the operating circuit current in Amperes.
  3. Enter the one-way cable run distance in meters.
  4. Select your conductor AWG gauge.
  5. Click Calculate to verify if voltage drop stays within the recommended 3% threshold.

NEC Voltage Drop Recommendations

The National Electrical Code (NEC) recommends a maximum voltage drop of 3% for branch circuits and 5% for combined feeder and branch circuits to prevent equipment malfunction and overheating.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How to reduce excessive voltage drop?

Increase the wire conductor thickness (choose a thicker gauge with a lower AWG number) or shorten the circuit run length.
