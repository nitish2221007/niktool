Henri Pitot's 1732 River Velocity Discovery

Pitot tubes measure the impact stagnation pressure of moving fluid brought to rest; by Bernoulli's equation, dynamic pressure (P_stag - P_stat = ½ρv²) provides direct airspeed readout on all commercial airliners.
