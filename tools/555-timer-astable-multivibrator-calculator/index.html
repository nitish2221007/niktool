NE555 Astable Formulas

In astable mode, the 555 timer continuously oscillates: Frequency f = 1.44 / ((R₁ + 2R₂) · C), with High Time t₁ = 0.693·(R₁+R₂)·C and Low Time t₂ = 0.693·R₂·C.

Frequently asked questions

Can a standard 555 timer achieve 50% duty cycle?

Placing a diode in parallel with R₂ allows independent charge and discharge paths to achieve an exact 50% square wave.
