Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between scientific Q and engineering Q (Q_eng)?

Scientific Q evaluates only thermal plasma power ($P_{ ext{fus}}/P_{ ext{aux}}$); engineering $Q_{ ext{eng}}$ accounts for plant electrical grid power input and conversion efficiency, requiring $Q_{ ext{plasma}} > 20 ext{ to }30$ for net electricity generation.
