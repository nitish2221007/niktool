How to use Belt Drive Capstan Friction (Euler-Eytelwein T₁ / T₂ = e^(μ·β)) Calculator

  1. Enter tight side belt tension $T_1$ in Newtons.
  2. Enter friction coefficient $mu$ between belt and pulley.
  3. Enter belt contact wrap angle $eta$ in degrees (e.g. $180^circ = pi ext{ rad}$).
  4. Enter driving pulley radius R in meters.
  5. Inspect tension holding ratio ($T_1/T_2 = e^{mueta}$), slack side tension $T_2$, and maximum transferable torque without slip.

Leonhard Euler & Johann Eytelwein 1808 Capstan Formula

Because holding force scales exponentially with wrap angle ($T_1 = T_2 e^{mueta}$), winding a mooring rope just 3 full turns around a harbor capstan ($eta = 6pi$) multiplies a sailor's pulling force by over 300 times.

Frequently asked questions

Why do V-belts transmit far more torque than flat belts?

The $40^circ$ V-groove wedges the belt, increasing virtual friction to $mu_{ ext{virtual}} = mu / sin( heta/2) approx 3mu$.
