How to use Electricity Units Converter

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Select the From and To units (power or energy units).
  3. Click Process to see the conversion and all equivalent values.

Key Features & Privacy

This converter handles both power units (W, kW, MW) and energy units (J, kJ, kWh) separately, preventing invalid cross-type conversions. It shows all equivalents in the same category for easy reference. This is especially useful for Class 10 electricity problems where students confuse power and energy units. All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Students use this for electricity numericals involving unit conversions between watts and kilowatts, joules and kilowatt-hours, and understanding the relationship between power and energy.
