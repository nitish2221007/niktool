Frequently asked questions

What is the physical meaning of the constant 360 in the Rational formula?

360 converts rainfall in mm/hr ($10^{-3} ext{ m/3600 s}$) over hectares ($10^4 ext{ m}^2$) directly into cubic meters per second ($ ext{m}^3/ ext{s}$).
