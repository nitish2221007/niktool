Stanley Smith Stevens' Power Law of Hearing

While decibels and Phons are logarithmic, Sones provide a direct linear scale of human hearing perception: an 8-Sone sound is perceived by human listeners as exactly twice as loud as a 4-Sone sound.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 Sone defined as?

1 Sone is defined as the perceived loudness of a 1,000 Hz pure tone at a sound pressure level of 40 dB SPL (40 Phons).
