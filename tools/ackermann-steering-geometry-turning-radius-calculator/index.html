Frequently asked questions

Why must the inner wheel turn sharper than the outer wheel?

The inner wheel traces a tighter circular radius path than the outer wheel ($R_{ ext{inner}} < R_{ ext{outer}}$), requiring a steeper angle ($delta_i > delta_o$).
