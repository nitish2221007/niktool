Molecular Orbital (MO) Theory Power

MO theory accurately predicts that oxygen (O₂, Bond Order = 2.0) contains two unpaired electrons in degenerate π* antibonding orbitals, explaining why liquid oxygen is visibly attracted to magnetic fields (paramagnetic).

Frequently asked questions

Why does Helium dimer (He₂) not form?

He₂ has 2 bonding and 2 antibonding electrons; Bond Order = ½·(2 - 2) = 0, meaning no net chemical bond can hold the atoms together.
