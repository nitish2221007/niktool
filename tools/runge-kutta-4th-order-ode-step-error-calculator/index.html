Carl Runge & Martin Kutta 1901 Higher-Order ODE Solver

RK4 samples four trial tangent slopes across each time step to cancel truncation terms up to 4th order ($mathcal{O}(h^4)$ global error), providing the backbone integrator for orbital dynamics, flight simulators, and computational fluid dynamics.

Frequently asked questions

Why is RK4 favored over forward Euler integration?

Euler has massive first-order error ($mathcal{O}(h)$) that diverges quickly, whereas halving the step size in RK4 reduces error by a factor of 16 ($2^4$).
