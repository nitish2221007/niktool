At $u = u_{mf}$, the upward hydrodynamic drag force exactly balances the downward buoyant weight of the bed, transforming stationary packed solids into a boiling liquid-like suspension with high heat and mass transfer.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when gas velocity exceeds u_mf?

Gas bubbles form and rise through the bed, transitioning the reactor into the bubbling fluidized regime (and eventually slugging or pneumatic transport).
