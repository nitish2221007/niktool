Peter Debye & Erich Hückel 1923 Quasineutrality Law

Defines the fundamental criteria for matter to behave as a collective plasma: electrostatic perturbations are shielded out within distance $lambda_D$, and the Debye sphere contains millions of interacting particles ($N_D gg 1$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the cutoff frequency for electromagnetic waves penetrating a plasma?

Radio/microwaves with frequency below the plasma frequency ($f < f_{pe}$) reflect completely off the plasma, which is why the ionosphere reflects AM radio waves.
