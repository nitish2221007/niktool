Jack Treynor's 1965 Systematic Risk Metric

For well-diversified portfolios where unsystematic company-specific risk has been eliminated, the Treynor ratio evaluates how efficiently the fund converts market beta exposure into excess alpha.

Frequently asked questions

When is Treynor ratio better than Sharpe ratio?

Treynor is preferred when evaluating a sub-portfolio that is part of a broader well-diversified total fund.
