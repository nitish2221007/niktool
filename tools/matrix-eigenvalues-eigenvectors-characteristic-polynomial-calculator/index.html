Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between eigenvalues and the matrix determinant?

The product of all eigenvalues equals the matrix determinant ($lambda_1 imes lambda_2 = det(A)$), and their sum equals the trace ($lambda_1 + lambda_2 = ext{Tr}(A)$).
