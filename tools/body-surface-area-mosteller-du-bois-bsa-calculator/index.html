Clinical Oncology & Hemodynamic Normalization Standard

Basal metabolic rate and cardiac output correlate closer with Body Surface Area ($BSA$) than raw body weight; Mosteller's formula ($BSA = sqrt{ rac{Ht imes Wt}{3600}}$) is universally used to calculate narrow-therapeutic-index chemotherapy protocols (Cisplatin, Doxorubicin).

Frequently asked questions

What is the average BSA for adult men and women?

The average adult male BSA is approximately $1.9 ext{ m}^2$, while the average adult female BSA is approximately $1.6 ext{ m}^2$.
