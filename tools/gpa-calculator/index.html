How to use GPA Calculator

  1. Enter your grades and corresponding credits on each line (e.g., A, 4).
  2. Select your institution's grading scale (4.0 or 10-point).
  3. Click Process to calculate your weighted Grade Point Average.

Key Features & Privacy

This calculator supports both the standard 4.0 scale (with +/- variants) and the 10-point scale common in Indian universities. It multiplies grade points by credits to give a true weighted GPA. All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

High school and college students use this to calculate their semester GPA or cumulative GPA for scholarship applications and higher studies.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?
