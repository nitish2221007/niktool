Frequently asked questions

Why does plasma resistivity decrease at higher temperatures?

Fast-moving electrons undergo much less Coulomb deflection angle during ion encounters ($ heta_{ ext{scatter}} propto 1/v^2$), reducing collisional momentum loss.
