Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Macrodrip and Microdrip tubing?

Macrodrip delivers large drops ($10 ext{ to }20 ext{ gtt/mL}$) for rapid adult hydration; Microdrip delivers tiny drops ($60 ext{ gtt/mL}$) for precise pediatric medication delivery.
