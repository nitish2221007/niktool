Pilot Crosswind Landing Safety Limits

Every aircraft type specifies a Maximum Demonstrated Crosswind Component (e.g. 15 knots for a Cessna 172, 38 knots for a Boeing 737) to prevent runway veer-off excursions during landing rollouts.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the crosswind for wind 300° at 20 knots on Runway 27?

Angle diff is 30°; Crosswind = 20 × sin(30°) = 10.0 knots from the right; Headwind = 20 × cos(30°) = 17.3 knots.
