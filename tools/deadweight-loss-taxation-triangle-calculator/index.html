Arnold Harberger's 1964 Welfare Triangle

Deadweight loss represents mutual gains from trade completely destroyed because the tax drives a wedge between buyer willingness-to-pay and seller cost, shrinking total societal economic surplus.

Frequently asked questions

Why does deadweight loss increase quadratically with tax rate?

Because both the tax wedge T and the quantity drop ΔQ increase linearly with the tax rate, their product (DWL = ½·T·ΔQ) scales with the square of the tax rate ($T^2$).
