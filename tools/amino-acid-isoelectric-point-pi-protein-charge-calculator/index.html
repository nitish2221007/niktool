Protein Isoelectric Focusing & Ion Exchange Chromatography

At its isoelectric point ($pH = pI$), a protein has net zero electrical charge and minimum solubility; adjusting pH above or below $pI$ allows cation or anion exchange chromatography separation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Zwitterion?

A zwitterion is an electrically neutral molecule carrying equal numbers of positive ($-NH_3^+$) and negative ($-COO^-$) ionized functional groups.
