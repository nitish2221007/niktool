How to use Hemodialysis Urea Kinetic Modeling (Single-Pool Daugirdas Kt/V Adequacy) Calculator

  1. Enter pre-dialysis Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN) $C_0$ in mg/dL.
  2. Enter immediate post-dialysis BUN C in mg/dL.
  3. Enter total hemodialysis treatment duration t in hours (typically 3.5–4.5 hours).
  4. Enter ultrafiltration fluid volume removed (UF) in Liters.
  5. Enter patient post-dialysis target dry body weight W in kg.
  6. Inspect single-pool Daugirdas $spKt/V$ dose and Urea Reduction Ratio (URR%).

Frequently asked questions

What do the letters K, t, and V stand for in Kt/V?

K is dialyzer urea clearance rate (mL/min), t is treatment time (min), and V is the patient's total body water volume in which urea is distributed (mL).
