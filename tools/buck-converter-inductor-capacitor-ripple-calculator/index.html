Continuous Conduction Mode (CCM) Buck Design

Sizing the inductor for a 30% current ripple ($Delta I_L = 0.30 I_{ ext{out}}$) provides an optimal balance between maintaining Continuous Conduction Mode (CCM) at light loads while minimizing physical inductor magnetic core size and $I^2R$ copper losses.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Buck converter efficiency typically >90% compared to linear regulators?

Buck converters toggle power MOSFETs fully ON or OFF with virtually zero voltage drop across the switch, eliminating the massive $(V_{ ext{in}} - V_{ ext{out}}) cdot I$ thermal dissipation of linear LDOs.
