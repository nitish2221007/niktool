Division is undefined in modular arithmetic ($mathbb{Z}/mmathbb{Z}$); multiplying by the modular inverse ($a^{-1} pmod m$) replaces division, forming the mathematical backbone of RSA private key generation ($d equiv e^{-1} pmod{phi(n)}$).

Frequently asked questions

When does a modular inverse fail to exist?

A modular inverse fails to exist if and only if $gcd(a, m) > 1$ (the numbers share a common factor greater than 1).
