Why RF Engineers Prefer dBm

dBm expresses power referenced to 1 milliwatt (0 dBm = 1 mW). A 3 dB increase doubles power (23 dBm = 200 mW), while a 10 dB increase represents a 10x power jump (30 dBm = 1 Watt).

What is 0 dBm in Watts?
