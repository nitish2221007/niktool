How to use Discriminant Calculator

  1. Enter the coefficients a, b, and c of the quadratic equation.
  2. Click Process to calculate D = b² - 4ac.
  3. Read the nature of the roots based on the discriminant value.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator shows the full substitution into D = b² - 4ac and classifies the roots as two distinct real, two equal real, or no real roots. This is the exact classification used in Class 10 exams, and everything runs locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to quickly check the nature of roots before solving, and to verify homework answers from the quadratic equations chapter.
