Spherical Earth Geometric Swath Projection

Accounting for Earth's spherical curvature is essential for wide FOV sensors; flat-earth approximations underestimate ground swath width by over 15% at orbital altitudes above 500 km.

Frequently asked questions

Why is ground track velocity slower than orbital velocity?

Because the satellite traverses an orbit at radius $(R_E + h)$, projecting its motion onto the smaller spherical Earth surface ($R_E$) compresses the linear velocity by $R_E / (R_E + h)$.
