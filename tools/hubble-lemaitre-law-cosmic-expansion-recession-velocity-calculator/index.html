Frequently asked questions

What is the Hubble Tension in modern astrophysics?

The Hubble Tension is the persistent discrepancy between cosmic microwave background measurements ($H_0 approx 67.4 ext{ km/s/Mpc}$) and local Cepheid/supernova measurements ($H_0 approx 73.0 ext{ km/s/Mpc}$).
