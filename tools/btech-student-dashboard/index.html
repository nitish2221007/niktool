How to use B.Tech Student Dashboard

  1. Enter your current or target CGPA and your current attendance percentage.
  2. List any pending assignments, lab records, or study goals in the text area.
  3. Click Update Dashboard to generate your personalized academic status report.

Key Features & Privacy

This dashboard acts as a central command center for engineering students. It provides instant feedback on whether your attendance is above the mandatory 75% threshold and categorizes your CGPA. All your academic metrics are saved locally, ensuring your performance data remains completely private.

Common Use Cases

B.Tech and B.E. students use this dashboard at the start of every semester to set goals, during mid-terms to track attendance shortages, and before placements to maintain a quick summary of their academic profile and pending backlogs.
