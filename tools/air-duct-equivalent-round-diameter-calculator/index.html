Huebscher's Equivalent Round Duct Friction Law

Round ductwork is the most aerodynamically efficient shape with minimal surface perimeter friction; the Huebscher equation allows HVAC engineers to swap rectangular trunk ducts for round spiral pipe without altering static pressure drop.
