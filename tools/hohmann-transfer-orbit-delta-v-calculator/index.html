Walter Hohmann 1925 Optimal Orbital Transfer

A Hohmann transfer is the most fuel-efficient two-impulse orbital maneuver connecting two coplanar circular orbits, using an elliptical transfer orbit tangent to both initial and final trajectories.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Delta-v required to go from LEO to GEO?

Transferring from a 300 km Low Earth Orbit to Geostationary Orbit requires exactly Δv₁ = 2.44 km/s and Δv₂ = 1.49 km/s (total 3.93 km/s).
