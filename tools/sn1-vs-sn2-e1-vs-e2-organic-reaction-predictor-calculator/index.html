Organic Chemistry Reaction Decision Matrix

Predicting substitution vs elimination balances steric accessibility against base strength: unhindered $1^circ$ carbons undergo bimolecular backside $S_N2$ attack, while sterically crowded $3^circ$ carbons and strong bases exclusively undergo $E2$ elimination.

Frequently asked questions

What stereochemical outcome occurs in pure SN2 reactions?

Complete 100% Walden inversion of configuration occurs because the nucleophile attacks from the exact opposite backside ($180^circ$) of the leaving group.
