How to use Chapter Progress Tracker

  1. Enter the total number of chapters in a specific subject.
  2. Enter how many you have fully revised and how many you have practiced with PYQs.
  3. Click Process to see your revision versus practice completion rates.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool separates "reading/revision" from "actual PYQ practice," which is a common blind spot in student preparation. It highlights exactly how many chapters are still untouched. All tracking is done locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use this to audit their Science or Math preparation a few weeks before boards to ensure they aren't just reading theory but also solving previous year questions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
