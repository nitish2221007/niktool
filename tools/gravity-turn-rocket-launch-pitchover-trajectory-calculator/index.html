Zero-Aerodynamic-Load Orbital Ascent Trajectory

By initiating a tiny pitchover kick at low altitude and aligning the rocket engine thrust vector exactly with the velocity vector ($alpha = 0$), gravity naturally arcs the vehicle horizontal with zero aerodynamic angle of attack, eliminating destructive bending moments on the rocket fairing.
