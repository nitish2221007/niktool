How to use Soil 1D Primary Consolidation Settlement (S_c) Calculator

  1. Enter clay stratum thickness H₀ in meters.
  2. Enter initial in-situ void ratio e₀ (ratio of volume of voids to volume of solids).
  3. Enter compression index C_c obtained from oedometer consolidation testing.
  4. Enter initial effective vertical overburden stress σ₀ in kPa at mid-layer depth.
  5. Enter net vertical stress increase Δσ imparted by building foundation footing loads.
  6. Inspect total ultimate primary consolidation settlement in millimeters and centimeters.

Terzaghi 1D Pore Water Dissipation Consolidation

Foundation loads initially pressurize incompressible water inside saturated clay pores; as pore water slowly squeezes out over years, soil skeleton void ratio decreases exponentially.
