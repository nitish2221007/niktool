Wilhelm Wien 1893 Thermal Radiation Displacement Law

Demonstrates that hotter objects radiate peak energy at shorter wavelengths ($lambda_{max} propto 1/T$), explaining why cool stars glow red ($3,000 ext{ K}$), medium stars shine yellow-white ($6,000 ext{ K}$), and hot stars blaze blue-violet ($20,000 ext{ K}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why did human vision evolve to see 400 to 700 nm wavelengths?

Because the Sun's surface temperature ($5,778 ext{ K}$) emits its peak photon irradiance at $501.5 ext{ nm}$ (green-yellow visible light).
