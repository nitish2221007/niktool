AP Statistics Two-Sample Inference Standard

Welch's two-sample t-test does not assume equal population variances ($sigma_1^2 e sigma_2^2$), making it the most robust and mandatory test on AP Statistics exams for comparing treatment vs control experimental groups.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Welch's t-test preferred over the pooled t-test?

The pooled t-test requires the strict assumption of equal population variances; if variances are unequal, pooled tests give severely inflated false positive rates.
