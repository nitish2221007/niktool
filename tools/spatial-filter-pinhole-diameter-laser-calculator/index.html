Fourier Plane Spatial Frequency Filtering

A spatial filter transforms laser dust scattering and diffraction rings into high-frequency spatial components that focus outside the central Airy spot; a pinhole placed at the focal point transmits only the clean, pristine TEM₀₀ Gaussian beam.

Frequently asked questions

Why is pinhole size chosen at 1.5x the Airy disk diameter?

Sizing the pinhole at 1.5x the Airy disk transmits ~99% of the desired TEM₀₀ Gaussian laser beam power while comfortably aligning without clipping.
