Frequently asked questions

Why can a particle enter and leave the Ergosphere without falling into the black hole?

Because the ergosphere lies outside the true event horizon ($r_E > r_+$), particles can enter negative-energy orbits and escape with more kinetic energy than they arrived with (Penrose effect).
