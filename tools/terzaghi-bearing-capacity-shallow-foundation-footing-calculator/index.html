Karl von Terzaghi 1943 Father of Soil Mechanics

Terzaghi's bearing capacity equation divides soil resistance into three distinct components: cohesion along slip planes ($c N_c$), overburden surcharge weight ($q N_q$), and soil self-weight wedge friction ($ rac{1}{2}gamma B N_gamma$), preventing catastrophic building foundation shear failure.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a safety factor of 3.0 typically applied to soil bearing capacity?

Because soil is a natural, highly variable anisotropic geological material with non-uniform shear strength properties.
