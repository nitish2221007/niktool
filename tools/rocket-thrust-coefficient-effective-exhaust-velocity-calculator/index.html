How to use Rocket Thrust Coefficient (C_F) & Effective Exhaust Velocity (c) Calculator

  1. Enter rocket combustion chamber total pressure $p_0$ in bar (e.g. 100 bar for gas generator, 300 bar for staged combustion).
  2. Enter nozzle throat cross-sectional area $A_t$ in $ ext{cm}^2$.
  3. Enter dimensionless thrust coefficient $C_F$ (typically 1.30–1.50 at sea level, 1.70–1.95 in vacuum).
  4. Enter total propellant mass flow rate $dot{m}$ in kg/s.
  5. Inspect total engine thrust force in kN and lbf, Specific Impulse $I_{ ext{sp}}$, and characteristic velocity $c^*$.

Frequently asked questions

Why is vacuum thrust higher than sea-level thrust?

In vacuum, ambient backpressure is zero ($p_a = 0$), eliminating the negative atmospheric pressure penalty on the nozzle exit plane ($(p_e - p_a) A_e$).
