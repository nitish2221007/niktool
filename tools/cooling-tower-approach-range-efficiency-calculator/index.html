Cooling Technology Institute (CTI) Evaporative Limits

An evaporative cooling tower can never cool water below the ambient wet-bulb temperature; the Approach measures how closely the tower approaches physical thermodynamic perfection (typically 3°C to 5°C).

Frequently asked questions

Why is wet-bulb temperature lower than dry-bulb temperature?

Evaporation absorbs latent heat from the water droplets, chilling the wet-bulb sensor below the ambient dry air temperature.
