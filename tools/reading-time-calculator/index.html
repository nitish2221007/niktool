How to use Reading Time Calculator

  1. Paste your text, article, or study material into the input area.
  2. Adjust the Words Per Minute (WPM) slider if needed (default is 200 WPM for average adults).
  3. The reading time updates automatically in minutes and seconds.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool estimates how long it will take to read a piece of text based on average reading speeds. It updates live as you type or paste, making it perfect for planning study sessions. All text processing happens locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use this to estimate how long a chapter will take to read, and writers use it to check if their articles fit within publication time guidelines.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
