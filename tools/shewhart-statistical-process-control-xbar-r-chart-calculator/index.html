How to use Shewhart Statistical Process Control (X̄ and R Chart Limits) Calculator

  1. Enter overall Grand Average Mean $ar{ar{X}}$ across all sample subgroups.
  2. Enter average subgroup range $ar{R}$ (difference between max and min within subgroup).
  3. Select subgroup sample size n (typically $n=5$ parts per inspection interval).
  4. Inspect Upper and Lower Control Limits (UCL / LCL) for both X-bar and R charts.

Walter A. Shewhart 1924 Statistical Process Control

Control charts distinguish natural random variation (common cause) from out-of-control shifts (special cause); plotting subgroup averages against $3sigma$ control limits detects machine tool wear and process drifts before defective parts are produced.

Frequently asked questions

Why are Control Limits different from Specification Limits?

Control Limits ($3sigma/sqrt{n}$) represent the natural statistical voice of the process; Specification Limits represent the customer's tolerance requirements.
