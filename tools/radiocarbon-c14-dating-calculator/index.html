Willard Libby's Nobel Prize-Winning Method

Living organisms continuously incorporate atmospheric C-14 through respiration and food. When an organism dies, C-14 ingestion ceases, and the isotope decays with a half-life of 5,730 years.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum limit for C-14 dating?

Beyond approximately 50,000 to 60,000 years (~10 half-lives), residual C-14 drops below 0.1%, requiring Potassium-Argon or Uranium-Lead radiometric dating for older rocks.
