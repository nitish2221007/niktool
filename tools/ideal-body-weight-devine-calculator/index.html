Why Devine IBW is Used in ICU Mechanical Ventilation

Lung capacity scales strictly with vertical height and biological sex (not adipose body fat). Setting ICU ventilator tidal volumes (6-8 mL/kg) using Devine IBW prevents barotrauma.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Devine baseline height?

5 feet (60 inches / 152.4 cm) provides the baseline 50 kg (male) or 45.5 kg (female), adding 2.3 kg per additional inch.
