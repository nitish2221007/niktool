William Henry Bragg & Louis Harold Gray Dosimetry Cavity Law

Bragg-Gray cavity theory proves that when an ionization chamber is small compared to the range of secondary electrons, the absorbed dose in surrounding tissue is directly proportional to the ionization created in the cavity gas scaled by the stopping power ratio.

Frequently asked questions

What is the W/e constant for dry air?

$W/e = 33.97 ext{ J/C}$ (or 33.97 eV per ion pair) is the fundamental physical constant relating ionization charge to absorbed energy in air.
