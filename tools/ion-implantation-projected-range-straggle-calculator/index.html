  2. Enter ion accelerator kinetic energy in keV (typically 10 to 150 keV).

Lindhard, Scharff & Schiøtt (LSS) 1963 Stopping Theory

Implanted ions lose kinetic energy via electronic drag and nuclear elastic collisions with silicon atoms; heavy arsenic ions stop rapidly within a few dozen nanometers, making them ideal for ultra-shallow source/drain junctions.

Frequently asked questions

Why is high-temperature rapid thermal annealing (RTA) required after ion implantation?

Bombarding ions destroy the silicon crystalline lattice into an amorphous state; thermal annealing recrystallizes the lattice and electrically activates the dopants into substitutional lattice sites.
