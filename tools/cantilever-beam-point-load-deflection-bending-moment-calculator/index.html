How to use Cantilever Beam End Point Load Deflection (δ_max = P·L³ / (3·E·I)) Calculator

  1. Enter point load P applied at the free tip in kilonewtons (kN).
  2. Enter cantilever unsupported beam length L in meters (m).
  3. Enter material Young's Modulus of Elasticity E in GPa (e.g. 200 GPa for structural steel, 70 GPa for aluminum).
