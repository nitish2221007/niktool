Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle C = \min(N_t, N_r) \cdot B \cdot \log_2\left(1 + \frac{\text{SNR}_{\text{lin}}}{N_t}\right)$$

Frequently asked questions

What is Spatial Multiplexing in MIMO?

Spatial multiplexing transmits separate independent data streams simultaneously over multiple antennas on the same frequency channel.

What limits the maximum MIMO streams?

The maximum spatial streams are limited by min(Nt, Nr), the smaller of transmit or receive antenna counts.

Why is MIMO used in 4G, 5G, and WiFi 6?

MIMO multiplies wireless throughput without requiring extra spectrum bandwidth.
