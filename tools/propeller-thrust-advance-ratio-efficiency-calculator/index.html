William Froude Propeller Actuator Disk Momentum Theory

The advance ratio ($J = rac{v}{nD}$) represents the forward distance traveled per propeller revolution normalized by diameter; constant-speed variable-pitch propellers adjust blade angle to maintain peak propulsive efficiency ($eta approx 85%$) across all flight regimes.
