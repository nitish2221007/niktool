Pierre-Simon Laplace Electromagnetic Conductor Force

When charge carriers drift through a magnetic field, the sum of individual microscopic Lorentz forces produces macroscopic mechanical force on the wire ($ ec{F} = I ec{L} imes ec{B}$), driving all industrial electric motors and loudspeaker voice coils.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the force zero when the wire is parallel to the magnetic field (θ = 0°)?

Because $sin(0^circ) = 0$, current flowing parallel to magnetic field lines experiences zero deflecting Lorentz force.
