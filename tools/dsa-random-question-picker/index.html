Why random practice?

Interleaved practice (mixing topics and difficulties) builds stronger problem-solving skills than blocked practice. This picker simulates the randomness of real interviews.

FAQ

How many questions are in the bank?

The built-in bank includes 50+ classic DSA problems across 8 topic categories and 3 difficulty levels.

Can I add my own questions?

Currently the bank is built-in. A custom question feature may be added in a future update.
