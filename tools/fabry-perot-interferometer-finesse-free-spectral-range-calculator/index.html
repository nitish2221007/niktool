Charles Fabry & Alfred Pérot 1899 Multiple-Beam Interferometry

Constructive multi-beam interference inside high-reflectivity Fabry-Pérot etalons ($mathcal{F} = rac{pisqrt{R}}{1-R}$) narrows resonant transmission fringes to sub-megahertz linewidths, providing ultra-narrow spectral optical filters for laser wavelength stabilization and gravitational wave detectors (LIGO).

Frequently asked questions

What is the physical meaning of Optical Finesse (ℱ)?

Finesse represents the average number of back-and-forth round trips a photon bounces inside the optical cavity before escaping through the mirrors.
