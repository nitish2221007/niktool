How to use Notice Format Checker

  1. Paste your complete notice draft into the input area.
  2. Click Process to run a structural checklist.
  3. Review the checklist to ensure you have included the word NOTICE, date, heading, body, and sign-off.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool checks for the essential structural elements of a standard school notice. It does not grade your grammar or content quality, but ensures you don't lose marks for missing format elements. All checking is done locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use this to self-evaluate their notice writing practice before board exams and school tests.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
