HVAC Air Conditioning & Building Comfort Engineering

Calculating psychrometric enthalpy ($h = c_p T + omega h_{fg}$) and dew point allows HVAC engineers to determine exact cooling coil refrigeration tonnage required for indoor thermal dehumidification.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when air temperature drops below the dew point?

Water vapor condenses out of the air as liquid fog, dew droplets, or window condensation.
