How to use Inventory Turnover & DSI Calculator

  1. Enter annual Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) from your income statement.
  2. Enter average inventory balance ((Beginning Inventory + Ending Inventory) / 2).
  3. Inspect how many times inventory turns over annually and the average days required to sell through warehouse stock.

High vs Low Inventory Turnover

A higher turnover ratio indicates strong sales velocity and minimal dead capital locked up in storage, whereas low turnover points to overstocking and potential product obsolescence.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy inventory turnover ratio for retail?

Grocery and apparel retailers typically average between 6 and 12 turns per year (30 to 60 days DSI).
