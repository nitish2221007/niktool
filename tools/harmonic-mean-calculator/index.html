Why Use Harmonic Mean for Average Speed?

If you drive to a destination at 40 km/h and return along the same distance at 60 km/h, your average speed is NOT the simple average (50 km/h), but the Harmonic Mean: 2 / (1/40 + 1/60) = 48.0 km/h, because more time is spent driving at the slower speed.

What is the relation between AM, GM, and HM?
