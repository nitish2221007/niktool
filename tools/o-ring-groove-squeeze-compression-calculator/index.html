Parker O-Ring Handbook Design Guidelines

Proper elastomeric squeeze establishes the initial zero-pressure contact seal without over-compressing the rubber (15-25% for static face/radial seals; 10-15% for dynamic moving pistons to reduce friction and wear).

Frequently asked questions

What is the recommended squeeze for static O-ring seals?

Standard recommended static squeeze is 15% to 30% (ideally ~20-22%).
