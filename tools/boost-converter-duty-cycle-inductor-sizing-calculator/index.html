Energy Storage Inductive Flyback Principle

When the Boost switch closes, energy stores in the inductor magnetic field ($E = rac{1}{2} L I^2$); when it opens, inductor collapse voltage sums with the input supply to pump electrons across the diode to the higher output voltage.

Frequently asked questions

Why is average input current in a boost converter higher than output current?

By conservation of power ($P_{ ext{in}} approx P_{ ext{out}}$), stepping voltage up ($V_{ ext{out}} > V_{ ext{in}}$) requires drawing proportionately higher input current ($I_{ ext{in}} = I_{ ext{out}} / (1 - D)$).
