Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle \text{FSPL (dB)} = 20 \log_{10}(d_{\text{km}}) + 20 \log_{10}(f_{\text{GHz}}) + 92.45, \quad P_r = P_t + G_t + G_r - \text{FSPL}$$

Frequently asked questions

What is Friis Transmission Equation?

The Friis equation calculates power received by one antenna from another under ideal line-of-sight conditions in free space.

What is EIRP?

Equivalent Isotropically Radiated Power (EIRP = Pt + Gt) is the total effective power radiated by a directional transmitter antenna.

Why does path loss increase with frequency?

Higher frequency EM waves have smaller wavelengths and smaller effective aperture areas for isotropic receiving antennas.

Is this calculation done on a remote server?

No, all calculations execute 100% locally in your browser.
