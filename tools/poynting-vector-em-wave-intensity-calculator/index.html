John Henry Poynting's 1884 Energy Flow Theorem

The Poynting vector S = (1/μ₀) · (E × B) defines the directional energy flux density (Watts/m²) transported across space by propagating electromagnetic waves, explaining solar radiation and radio RF transmissions.
