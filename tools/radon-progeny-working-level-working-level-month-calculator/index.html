How to use Radon Daughter Working Level (WL) & Working Level Month (WLM) Calculator

  1. Enter measured indoor Radon-222 gas concentration in pCi/L (or divide Bq/m³ by 37).
  2. Enter indoor air equilibrium fraction factor F (typically 0.40 in homes, 0.20 in high ventilation mines).
  3. Enter total cumulative annual exposure time in hours (2000 hours for workplace, 7000 for home).
  4. Inspect Working Level (WL) and cumulative annual exposure in Working Level Months (WLM).

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 Working Level Month (WLM)?

$1 ext{ WLM}$ is the cumulative alpha radiation exposure from breathing an atmosphere of $1.0 ext{ WL}$ for 1 occupational working month ($170 ext{ hours}$).
