Julius Caesar's Military Cryptography

Used by Julius Caesar in 58 BC to protect military dispatches, the Caesar shift is a monoalphabetic substitution cipher where each letter in the plaintext is shifted down the alphabet by a fixed integer key.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROT-13?

ROT-13 is a special Caesar cipher with a shift of 13; because the English alphabet has 26 letters, applying ROT-13 twice returns the original text.
