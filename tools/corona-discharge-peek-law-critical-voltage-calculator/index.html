Frank W. Peek Jr. 1929 High-Voltage Corona Law

When local electric fields exceed air breakdown strength ($E > 30 ext{ kV/cm}$), air molecules ionize into a glowing violet corona sheath, creating audible hissing noise, radio interference, and multi-megawatt transmission power losses that power utilities prevent by bundling 2 to 4 sub-conductors per phase.

Frequently asked questions

Why does conductor bundling suppress corona discharge?

Bundling multiple parallel sub-conductors increases the effective electrical radius ($r_{ ext{eff}} = sqrt{r cdot s}$), reducing the surface electric field gradient below the corona threshold.
