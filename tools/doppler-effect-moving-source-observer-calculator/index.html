Christian Doppler 1842 Acoustic Wave Compression

As a moving vehicle emits sound waves, each successive wave crest is launched closer to the previous crest, compressing the effective wavelength ahead ($lambda' = (c - v_s)/f_0$) to produce a sharp upward pitch shift.
