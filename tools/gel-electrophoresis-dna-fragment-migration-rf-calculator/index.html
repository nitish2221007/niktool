Electrophoretic Molecular Sieve Sizing Standard

Porous agarose and polyacrylamide gels act as molecular sieves: smaller nucleic acid fragments migrate faster and further through the matrix, producing a linear semi-logarithmic relationship ($log( ext{MW}) propto -R_f$) used to verify PCR restriction digests and plasmids.

Frequently asked questions

Why do larger DNA fragments migrate slower in gel electrophoresis?

Larger DNA fragments experience greater physical friction and steric entanglement with the polymer agarose mesh network under the electric field.
