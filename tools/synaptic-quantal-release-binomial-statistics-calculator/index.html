Bernard Katz 1954 Quantal Release Hypothesis

Synaptic transmission is stochastic and quantized; calculating quantal content ($m = n cdot p$) allows neuroscientists to determine whether synaptic plasticity (LTP/LTD) is driven by presynaptic increases in release probability ($p$) or postsynaptic receptor insertion ($q$).

Frequently asked questions

What is a "miniature" mEPSP?

A "mini" is the miniature postsynaptic electrical voltage depolarization generated by the spontaneous fusion of a single synaptic vesicle containing ~5,000 neurotransmitter molecules (glutamate/acetylcholine).
