Latent Heat of Vaporization Barrier

Liquid Helium has an extremely small latent heat of vaporization ($Delta H_{ ext{vap}} = 20.9 ext{ kJ/kg}$); a tiny heat leak of just 1.0 Watt evaporates over 4.1 Liters of liquid helium per day, requiring multi-layer vacuum insulation (MLI) and liquid nitrogen radiation thermal shields in MRI superconducting magnets.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Liquid Hydrogen boil-off difficult to eliminate completely?

Hydrogen has small molecular size causing high vapor pressure and undergoes slow exothermic ortho-to-para spin isomer conversion ($527 ext{ kJ/kg}$) that self-boils stored liquid.
