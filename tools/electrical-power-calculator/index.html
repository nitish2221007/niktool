How to use Electrical Power Calculator

  1. Select the formula that matches the values you know: P=VI, P=I²R, or P=V²/R.
  2. Enter the two required values.
  3. Click Process to get the power in watts with the formula shown.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator supports all three common power formulas used in Class 10 electricity and clearly labels which formula was applied. It prevents division by zero and rejects negative inputs, and it processes everything locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to solve power numericals, compare which formula fits a given problem, and check answers for homework and exam practice.
