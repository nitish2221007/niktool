How to use Bragg's Law X-Ray Diffraction (nλ = 2d·sin θ) & Interplanar Spacing Calculator

  1. Enter experimental X-ray diffractometer peak position $2 heta$ in degrees.
  2. Enter X-ray source radiation wavelength $lambda$ in Ångströms (typically 1.5406 Å for Copper $Kalpha$).
  3. Enter reflection integer diffraction order n (standard $n = 1$).
  4. Inspect interplanar atomic spacing d in Å and nm.

William Lawrence Bragg 1913 Nobel Prize Equation

Constructive wave interference occurs when path differences between parallel atomic crystal planes equal integer multiples of wavelength ($nlambda = 2dsin heta$), enabling atomic structure determination of crystalline materials.

Frequently asked questions

Why is XRD reported in 2-theta instead of theta?

Diffractometer detectors rotate by an angle of $2 heta$ relative to the incoming incident beam while the sample rotates by $ heta$.
