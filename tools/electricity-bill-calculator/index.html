How to use Electricity Bill Calculator

  1. Enter the number of units (kWh) consumed.
  2. Enter the rate per unit and any fixed monthly charge.
  3. Click Process to see the estimated bill breakdown.

Key Features & Privacy

The tool multiplies units by the rate and adds the fixed charge. Because tariffs differ by provider, state, and slab, the rate is fully editable rather than hard-coded, and the result is clearly labelled an estimate. All processing stays in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use it for electricity project work, and households use it for quick what-if estimates of their monthly bill.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
