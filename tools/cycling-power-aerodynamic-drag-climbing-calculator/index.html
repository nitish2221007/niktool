The Cubic Velocity Aerodynamic Wall (P ∝ v³)

Because aerodynamic drag power scales with the cube of velocity ($P_{ ext{aero}} propto v^3$), overcoming air resistance consumes over 85% of a cyclist's energy output above 35 km/h on flat ground.

Frequently asked questions

Why are climbing speeds governed by W/kg rather than pure Watts?

On steep climbs (>7% grade), gravitational work ($m cdot g cdot v cdot sin heta$) dominates power demand; carrying less body mass drastically reduces required climbing wattage.
