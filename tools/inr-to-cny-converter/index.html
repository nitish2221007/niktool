Frequently asked questions

Is CNY the same as Yuan or Renminbi?

Yes. CNY is the ISO currency code. Renminbi (RMB) is the official name of the currency, and Yuan is the primary unit. They refer to the same currency.

Why is the INR to CNY rate so low?

One Chinese Yuan is worth significantly more than one Indian Rupee, which is why the conversion factor is a small decimal (around 0.086).

Is this converter accurate for actual transactions?

The rate shown is indicative. Banks, credit cards, and money changers apply their own spreads and fees, so the actual amount you receive may vary.

Is my input stored anywhere?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser — no data is uploaded to any server.
