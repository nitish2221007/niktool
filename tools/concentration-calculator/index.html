How to use Concentration Calculator

  1. Select what you want to find: Concentration, Mass of solute, or Volume of solution.
  2. Enter the two known values as prompted.
  3. Click Process to see the result with formula and unit conversions.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool uses C = m/V and rearranges it for any unknown. It shows results in both g/L and mg/L (or L and mL) for practical understanding. All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Students use this for chemistry numericals involving solution concentration, dilution problems, and preparing solutions of specific concentrations.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
