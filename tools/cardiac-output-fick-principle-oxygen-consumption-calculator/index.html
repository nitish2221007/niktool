How to use Fick Principle Cardiac Output (CO = VO₂ / (CaO₂ - CvO₂)) & Stroke Index Calculator

  1. Enter total body oxygen consumption rate $V ext{O}_2$ in mL/min (typically $125 ext{ mL/min/m}^2$ or $sim 250 ext{ mL/min}$ at rest).
  2. Enter blood hemoglobin concentration Hb in g/dL.
  3. Enter arterial oxygen saturation $S_a ext{O}_2$ (e.g. 0.98 for 98%).
  4. Enter mixed venous oxygen saturation $S_v ext{O}_2$ measured from a Swan-Ganz pulmonary artery catheter (e.g. 0.70 for 70%).
  5. Enter patient Body Surface Area (BSA) in $ ext{m}^2$.
  6. Inspect Cardiac Output in L/min and normalized Cardiac Index in $ ext{L/min/m}^2$.

Adolf Fick 1870 Mass Balance Hemodynamic Standard

The gold-standard reference method for measuring human blood flow and diagnosing cardiogenic shock during right heart catheterization.

Frequently asked questions

Why is low mixed venous oxygen saturation (SvO2 < 60%) alarming?

When cardiac output drops, tissues must extract a higher percentage of oxygen from each milliliter of circulating blood, causing $S_v ext{O}_2$ to drop steeply.
