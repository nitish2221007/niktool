Peter Debye & Irving Langmuir Collective Plasma Behavior

For an ionized gas to behave as a true plasma, the Debye length must be far smaller than the system dimensions ($lambda_D ll L$) and the Debye sphere must contain millions of particles ($N_D gg 1$), ensuring quasi-neutrality and high-frequency collective shielding of electric fields.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to radio waves below the plasma frequency f_pe?

Electromagnetic waves with frequency below $f_{ ext{pe}}$ cannot propagate through the plasma and are 100% reflected (explaining how the ionosphere bounces AM radio signals across the globe).
