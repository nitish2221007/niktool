How to use Electricity Consumption Calculator

  1. Enter the wattage of the appliance (e.g., a 100W bulb).
  2. Enter how many hours it runs per day and for how many days.
  3. Optionally enter your local electricity tariff per unit (kWh) to see the estimated cost.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool calculates the exact electrical energy consumed in kilowatt-hours (commercial "units") and estimates the cost based on your tariff. It bridges the gap between Class 10 physics formulas (E = P x t) and real-world electricity bills. All processing is local.

Common Use Cases

Students use this for school projects on energy conservation, and households use it to estimate the running cost of ACs, heaters, or refrigerators.
