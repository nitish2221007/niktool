Sir Ronald Fisher's ANOVA Foundation

The F-test evaluates whether two population variances are equal (H₀: σ₁² = σ₂²), providing the statistical engine for Analysis of Variance (ANOVA) multi-group significance testing.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the larger variance placed in the numerator?

Placing the larger variance in the numerator guarantees F ≥ 1.0, enabling standardized one-tailed F-distribution table lookup.
