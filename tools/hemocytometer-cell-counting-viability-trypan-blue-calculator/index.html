Cell Culture Hemocytometer Volume Calibration

A Neubauer hemocytometer chamber has a precise depth of $0.1 ext{ mm}$, meaning each $1 ext{ mm} imes 1 ext{ mm}$ grid square contains exactly $0.1 ext{ mm}^3 = 10^{-4} ext{ mL}$; multiplying average cell counts by $10^4$ gives standard cells/mL.

Frequently asked questions

How does Trypan Blue dye work for viability?

Intact live cell membranes exclude the dye, while damaged/dead permeable cell membranes allow Trypan Blue to enter and stain the cytoplasm blue.
