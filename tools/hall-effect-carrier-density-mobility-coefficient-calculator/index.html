Frequently asked questions

Why is the sign of the Hall voltage opposite for electrons and holes?

Magnetic Lorentz force ($mathbf{F} = q mathbf{v} imes mathbf{B}$) deflects moving electrons and holes to the SAME physical side of the wafer, but opposite electrical charge polarities generate opposite sign Hall voltages.
