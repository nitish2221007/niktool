Centrifugal Gravity Ore Classification

Hydrocyclones use tangential fluid swirl to generate centrifugal forces hundreds of times greater than gravity, splitting coarse heavy sand to the spigot underflow for regrinding while fine liberated minerals overflow to froth flotation.

Frequently asked questions

What is cut size d₅₀?

d₅₀ is the particle diameter that has an exact 50/50 equal chance of reporting to either the cyclone overflow or underflow streams.
