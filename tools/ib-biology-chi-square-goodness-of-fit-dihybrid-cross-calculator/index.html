Hypothesis Testing in High School Biology

The Chi-Square test determines whether deviations between observed phenotype ratios and Mendelian predictions are due to random statistical chance ($p > 0.05$) or indicate genetic linkage, gene crossover, epistasis, or lethal alleles ($p < 0.05$).
