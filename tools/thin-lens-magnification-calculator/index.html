  1. Enter lens focal length f in cm (positive for converging convex lenses, negative for diverging concave lenses).

Gaussian Lens Formula

The thin lens equation 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ accurately predicts focal behavior when lens thickness is negligible compared to surface radii of curvature.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when an object is placed inside the focal length (dₒ < f)?

A magnifying glass effect occurs: a virtual, upright, and enlarged image forms behind the object.
