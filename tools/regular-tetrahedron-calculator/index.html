The Simplest 3D Platonic Solid

A regular tetrahedron is the simplest of all ordinary convex polyhedra and the only one with 4 faces, 4 vertices, and 6 edges. In molecular chemistry, carbon sp³ hybridization forms a tetrahedral geometry (109.5° bond angle).

Frequently asked questions

Is a regular tetrahedron self-dual?

Yes, connecting the centers of the 4 faces of a regular tetrahedron creates another inverted regular tetrahedron.
