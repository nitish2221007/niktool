Why Gasoline Tanks Require Headspace

Gasoline has a high volumetric expansion coefficient (β ≈ 950 × 10⁻⁶ / °C). Fuel pumped from cold underground tanks expands significantly when parked under hot sunshine, creating hazardous overflow without expansion volume.

Frequently asked questions

What is the relationship between linear (α) and volumetric (β) expansion for solids?

For isotropic solids, the volumetric expansion coefficient is approximately three times the linear coefficient: β ≈ 3α.
