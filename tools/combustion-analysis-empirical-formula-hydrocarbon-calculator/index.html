Lavoisier & Liebig Combustion Analysis Method

Combustion analysis converts all carbon into $CO_2$ and all hydrogen into $H_2O$; by calculating elemental mass conservation ($m_O = m_{ ext{sample}} - [m_C + m_H]$), chemists determine the fundamental empirical formula of unknown organic compounds.

Frequently asked questions

Why is oxygen calculated by difference rather than direct measurement?

Because combustion requires excess atmospheric oxygen gas ($O_2$), all oxygen produced comes from both the reactant sample and the injected flame gas.
