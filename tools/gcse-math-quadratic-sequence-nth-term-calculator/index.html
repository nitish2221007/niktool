GCSE High School Mathematics Algebra Method

A constant second difference ($2a = Delta^2$) proves a sequence is quadratic; solving the linear system $2a = ext{diff}_2, 3a+b = ext{diff}_1, a+b+c = T_1$ provides an foolproof method for UK GCSE and Edexcel exam papers.

Frequently asked questions

What if the second difference is not constant?

If the second difference varies, the sequence is not quadratic and may be cubic ($n^3$), geometric ($r^n$), or Fibonacci.
