Nephron Sodium Clearance Ratio

Measures the fraction of filtered sodium that escapes renal tubular reabsorption, providing the classic clinical test to guide IV fluid hydration vs diuretic therapy in oliguric renal failure.

Frequently asked questions

Why is FeNa invalid in patients taking loop diuretics (furosemide)?

Loop diuretics block tubular sodium reabsorption, causing falsely high FeNa; use Fractional Excretion of Urea (FeUrea $< 35%$) instead.
