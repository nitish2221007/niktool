Acoustic Standing Wave Boundary Conditions

Open ends and string fixed ends enforce boundary displacement nodes and antinodes, ensuring open pipes produce full harmonic integer spectra ($1f, 2f, 3f$) while closed pipes produce exclusively odd harmonics ($1f, 3f, 5f$).

Frequently asked questions

Why do closed-end organ pipes sound deeper than open pipes of the same length?

A closed pipe fundamental wavelength is twice as long ($lambda = 4L$) as an open pipe ($lambda = 2L$), producing an octave lower fundamental pitch ($f_{ ext{closed}} = rac{1}{2} f_{ ext{open}}$).
