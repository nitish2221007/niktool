Enrico Fermi & Paul Dirac 1926 Pauli Exclusion Statistics

Because electrons are spin-1/2 fermions subject to the Pauli Exclusion Principle, states cannot be multiply occupied; at the Fermi energy ($E = E_F$), the state occupancy probability is always exactly $f(E_F) = 0.50$ (50%) regardless of temperature.

Frequently asked questions

When can the Fermi-Dirac distribution be approximated by Maxwell-Boltzmann?

When $(E - E_F) ge 3 k_B T$, the "$+1$" in the denominator becomes negligible, simplifying $f(E) approx e^{-(E - E_F)/k_B T}$.
