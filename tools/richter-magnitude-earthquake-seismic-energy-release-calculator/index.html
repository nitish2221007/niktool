Charles Richter & Beno Gutenberg 1935 Energy Scale

Because the Richter magnitude is semi-logarithmic with a $1.5$ exponent factor, an increase of $+1.0$ on the magnitude scale corresponds to $10^{1.5} approx 31.62$ times more energy, and a $+2.0$ increase releases exactly $1,000 imes$ more energy.
