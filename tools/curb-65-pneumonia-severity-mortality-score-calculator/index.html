Provides an evidence-based risk stratification score preventing unnecessary hospitalizations for low-risk pneumonia patients while identifying high-risk sepsis candidates.

What is the CRB-65 score without urea laboratory testing?

CRB-65 omits blood urea testing ($0 ext{–}4 ext{ scale}$), allowing rapid triage in primary care clinic settings.
