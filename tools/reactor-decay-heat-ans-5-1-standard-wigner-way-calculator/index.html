Eugene Wigner & Katharine Way 1946 Decay Heat Standard

Even after immediate chain reaction shutdown ($k=0$), beta/gamma decay of radioactive fission fragments produces $sim 6.5%$ power at $t=1 ext{ second}$ ($sim 200 ext{ MW}$) and $sim 1.5%$ at $1 ext{ hour}$, requiring continuous long-term cooling.
