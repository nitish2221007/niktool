Frequently asked questions

What happens when DO2 drops below critical threshold (DO2_crit)?

When $DO_2$ falls below $sim 300 ext{–}400 ext{ mL/min}$, cells switch to anaerobic glycolysis, generating dangerous lactic acidosis.
