Mathematical & Theoretical Physics Formulas

Battery runtime estimation relies on electrical energy density, Peukert's law, and weighted duty cycle power dissipation:

1. Basic Battery Energy & Capacity Conversions

$$\displaystyle E_{\text{Wh}} = \frac{C_{\text{mAh}} \cdot V_{\text{nominal}}}{1000}, \quad C_{\text{mAh}} = \frac{E_{\text{Wh}} \cdot 1000}{V_{\text{nominal}}}$$

2. Duty Cycle Weighted Average Power Dissipation

When microcontrollers (e.g., ESP32, STM32, Nordic nRF52) alternate between active RF transmission and deep sleep states, average current $I_{\text{avg}}$ is calculated as:

$$\displaystyle I_{\text{avg}} = \left( I_{\text{active}} \cdot \frac{D_{\text{active}}}{100} \right) + \left( I_{\text{sleep}} \cdot \frac{100 - D_{\text{active}}}{100} \right)$$

3. Battery Runtime Formula with Efficiency

$$\displaystyle \text{Runtime (Hours)} = \frac{C_{\text{mAh}} \cdot \eta_{\text{discharge}}}{I_{\text{avg}} \cdot 100}$$

4. Peukert's Law for High Discharge Currents

For lead-acid batteries and high C-rate discharges (e.g., drones and EV powerpacks), available capacity shrinks under heavy loads according to Peukert's equation:

$$\displaystyle t = H \left( \frac{C}{I \cdot H} \right)^k$$

where $k$ is Peukert's exponent ($1.1$ to $1.3$ for lead-acid, $\approx 1.02$ to $1.05$ for lithium-ion).

Step-by-Step Worked Example

Scenario: An ESP32 environmental sensor is powered by a 3.7V 2500 mAh Li-Ion battery. The sensor draws 120 mA during 5% of the time (active WiFi transmission) and 15 µA (0.015 mA) during 95% of the time (deep sleep). Converter efficiency is 85%.

  1. Calculate Weighted Average Current: $I_{\text{avg}} = (120 \times 0.05) + (0.015 \times 0.95) = 6.00 + 0.01425 = 6.01425\text{ mA}$.
  2. Calculate Usable Capacity: $C_{\text{usable}} = 2500\text{ mAh} \times 0.85 = 2125\text{ mAh}$.
  3. Calculate Total Operating Hours: $\text{Runtime} = \frac{2125}{6.01425} = 353.33\text{ Hours}$.
  4. Convert to Days: $353.33 \div 24 = 14.72\text{ Days}$.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate battery runtime from mAh and load current?

Divide the battery capacity in milliamp-hours (mAh) by the load current in milliamps (mA), then multiply by the battery discharge efficiency factor (typically 80% to 90%): Runtime (hours) = (Capacity_mAh * Efficiency) / Load_mA.

What is Peukert's Law and why does it affect battery life?

Peukert's Law explains that as the discharge rate of a lead-acid or chemical battery increases, its available capacity decreases non-linearly. Higher load currents deplete batteries faster than simple linear equations predict.

How do you calculate IoT sensor battery life with sleep modes?

Compute average power by combining active mode power consumption and sleep mode power consumption based on duty cycle percentage: Average Current = (Active_Current * Active_Time%) + (Sleep_Current * Sleep_Time%). Then divide capacity by Average Current.

How do I convert battery capacity from mAh to Watt-hours (Wh)?

Multiply capacity in mAh by nominal battery voltage in Volts and divide by 1000: Wh = (mAh * V) / 1000.
