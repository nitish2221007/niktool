Peter Debye's Molecular Polarity Unit

Named after Nobel laureate Peter Debye (1936), the Debye unit quantifies molecular polarity: non-polar molecules like CO₂ have μ = 0 D, whereas highly polar water has a permanent dipole moment of μ = 1.85 D.
