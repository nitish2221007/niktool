How to use Heat Exchanger Effectiveness-NTU (Number of Transfer Units) Method Calculator

  1. Enter overall heat transfer conductance product UA in W/K ($U imes A$).
  2. Enter minimum fluid heat capacity rate $C_{min} = dot{m} c_p$ in W/K.
  3. Enter capacity ratio $C_r = C_{min} / C_{max}$ ($0 le C_r le 1$).
  4. Inspect dimensionless Number of Transfer Units (NTU) and thermal effectiveness $ arepsilon = q / q_{max}$.

Kays & London Effectiveness-NTU Method

When fluid outlet temperatures are unknown, the LMTD method requires tedious iterative guessing; the Effectiveness-NTU method calculates heat transfer directly from inlet temperatures ($q = arepsilon C_{min} (T_{h, ext{in}} - T_{c, ext{in}})$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the significance of NTU > 3?

When $ ext{NTU} > 3$, the effectiveness curve plateaus; adding more surface area yields diminishing thermodynamic returns.
