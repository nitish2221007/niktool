  1. Enter desired target gripper position coordinates (X, Y) in cm.

  3. Inspect calculated shoulder (θ₁) and elbow (θ₂) joint angles required to reach the target.

Elbow-Up vs Elbow-Down Dual Solutions

Planar 2-link robotic arms have two symmetric mathematical IK solutions for any reachable point (Elbow-Up and Elbow-Down), allowing motion planners to avoid physical obstacles.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if the target is outside l₁ + l₂?

The target lies outside the reachable workspace boundary and the mathematical inverse cosine has no real solution.
