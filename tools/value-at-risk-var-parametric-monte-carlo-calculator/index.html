J.P. Morgan 1994 RiskMetrics & Basel Regulatory Capital

VaR quantifies market risk into a single dollar figure ($VaR = P cdot z cdot sigma sqrt{t}$); because standard VaR ignores the magnitude of catastrophic tail risk beyond the cutoff, modern Basel III / IV banking rules mandate Expected Shortfall (CVaR).

Frequently asked questions

What is the square root of time rule in VaR?

Under the random walk assumption of independent daily returns, multi-period volatility scales with the square root of time ($sigma_T = sigma_1 sqrt{T}$).
