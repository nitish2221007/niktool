  1. Enter measured laboratory 5-day standard Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD₅) in mg/L.
  2. Enter base-e degradation kinetic rate constant k₂₀ at 20°C (typically 0.20 to 0.25 day⁻¹ for domestic wastewater).
  3. Enter river/wastewater ambient temperature in Celsius.
  4. Inspect total ultimate carbonaceous oxygen demand (BOD_u) and exerted fraction.

First-Order Organic Decomposition Kinetics

Microorganisms consume dissolved oxygen to oxidize organic matter following first-order kinetics (d[BOD]/dt = -k·BOD); standard 5-day BOD captures ~68% of total ultimate carbonaceous oxygen demand at 20°C.

Frequently asked questions

Why is 5 days the standard incubation period for BOD tests?

Five days was chosen historically in Great Britain because no British river takes more than 5 days to reach the sea from source, and 5 days avoids interference from secondary nitrifying bacteria.
