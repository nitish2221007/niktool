Amedeo Avogadro Molar Solution Chemistry

Molarity ($M = ext{mol/L}$) allows chemists to relate liquid reagent volumes directly to stoichiometry numbers of interacting molecules ($N = n imes 6.022 imes 10^{23}$).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Molarity (M) and Molality (m)?

Molarity is moles of solute per liter of total solution ($ ext{mol/L}$); Molality is moles of solute per kilogram of pure solvent ($ ext{mol/kg}$).
