Harry Nyquist 1928 & Claude Shannon 1949 Sampling Theorem

To faithfully reconstruct any continuous band-limited analog signal without distortion, the sampling rate must strictly exceed twice the highest frequency component (F_s > 2·F_max); otherwise, ultrasonic frequencies reflect back into the audible spectrum as false ghost tones.

How do anti-aliasing filters prevent this issue in ADCs?
