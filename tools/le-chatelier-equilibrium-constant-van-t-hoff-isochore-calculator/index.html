Frequently asked questions

Why does increasing temperature decrease yield in the Haber process?

Ammonia synthesis is highly exothermic ($Delta H^circ = -92.4 ext{ kJ/mol}$); heating shifts equilibrium backward toward $N_2$ and $H_2$ reactants.
