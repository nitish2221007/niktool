How to use Gaussian Laser Beam Waist, Divergence & Rayleigh Range (z_R) Calculator

  1. Enter laser wavelength $\lambda$ in nanometers (e.g. 532 nm Green, 633 nm HeNe, 1064 nm Fiber).
  2. Enter focused beam waist radius $w_0$ ($1/e^2$ intensity radius) in micrometers.
  3. Enter propagation target distance z in meters.
  4. Inspect Rayleigh range $z_R$ (depth of focus where beam area doubles) and expanded beam radius $w(z)$.

Helmholtz Paraxial Wave Equation

Diffraction dictates that tighter laser focal spots diverge more rapidly into the far field ($\theta = \lambda / \pi w_0$); the Rayleigh range $z_R = \pi w_0^2/\lambda$ defines the confocal parameter over which the beam remains tightly focused.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Rayleigh range (z_R)?

The Rayleigh range is the distance from the focal waist where the beam cross-sectional radius expands by $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414\times$ (beam cross-sectional area doubles).
