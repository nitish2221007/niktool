The Fundamental Equal Superposition Creator

The Hadamard gate is an involuntary self-inverse unitary operator; applying H to ground state |0⟩ rotates it into equal 50/50 superposition, forming the opening step of Grover and Shor algorithms.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when you apply two Hadamard gates in a row?

Because H² = I (Identity matrix), applying two consecutive Hadamard gates reverses the superposition and returns the qubit to its original state.
