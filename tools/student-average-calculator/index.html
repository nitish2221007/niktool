How to use Student Average Calculator

  1. Enter your marks separated by commas or new lines.
  2. Click Process to calculate the sum, count, and average.
  3. Review the highest and lowest scores alongside the mean.

Key Features & Privacy

This tool computes the arithmetic mean of any list of marks and also reports the count, total, highest, and lowest values, which is useful for spotting performance spread. It runs fully in your browser with no data sent anywhere.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to average test scores, subject marks, or assignment grades, and teachers use it to compute class or test averages quickly.
