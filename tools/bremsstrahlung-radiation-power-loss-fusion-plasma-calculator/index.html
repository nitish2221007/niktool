Frequently asked questions

Why do heavy metal impurities like Tungsten (Z=74) quench fusion plasmas?

Because Bremsstrahlung radiates proportional to $Z^2$, even trace parts-per-million ($10^{-5}$) of high-Z tungsten impurities radiate tens of megawatts, cooling the plasma below fusion ignition.
