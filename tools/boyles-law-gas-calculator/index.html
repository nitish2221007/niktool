Boyle's Law Principle

At constant temperature, the absolute pressure and volume of a fixed mass of ideal gas are inversely proportional (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ = constant). Halving the gas volume doubles its pressure.

Frequently asked questions

Does Boyle's Law hold during temperature changes?

No, Boyle's Law strictly assumes an isothermal process with constant temperature (T₁ = T₂).
