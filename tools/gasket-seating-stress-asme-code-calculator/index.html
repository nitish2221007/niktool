How to use ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Gasket Seating Stress Calculator

  1. Enter internal design pressure P in psi.
  2. Enter mean gasket contact diameter G in inches.
  3. Select ASME gasket material factor preset (Spiral wound, Compressed sheet, Corrugated metal).
  4. Inspect ASME Section VIII operating load W_m1 and initial seating load W_m2 to size total flange bolt area.

ASME Section VIII Div 1 Appendix 2 Rules

Flange bolt cross-sectional area must satisfy the greater of the operating load $W_{m1}$ (internal hydrostatic push plus gasket pressure retention) or initial atmospheric gasket seating yield load $W_{m2}$.

Frequently asked questions

What is the gasket factor m?

Factor m defines the ratio of residual gasket contact stress over internal fluid pressure required to maintain a leak-tight seal during operation.
