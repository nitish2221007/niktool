Fundamental Euclidean Geometry Theorem

In any right-angled Euclidean triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares of the other two sides ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$), forming the foundation of 2D/3D coordinate distance formulas, trigonometry, and computer graphics vector math.
