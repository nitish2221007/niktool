Square-Law MOSFET Current Model

In saturation, the conductive inversion channel pinches off at the drain end, making drain current I_D independent of V_DS and controlled exclusively by the square of the gate overdrive voltage (I_D ∝ (V_GS - V_th)²).

Frequently asked questions

What is the condition for MOSFET saturation?

V_GS ≥ V_th (channel turned on) and V_DS ≥ V_GS - V_th (drain pinch-off).
