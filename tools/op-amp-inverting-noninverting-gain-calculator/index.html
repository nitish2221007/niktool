Negative Feedback Stability

Operational amplifiers have immense open-loop gain (>100,000x). Applying negative feedback through R_f provides predictable, linear, and temperature-stable closed-loop gain.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Voltage Follower (Buffer)?

Setting Rf = 0 Ω in a non-inverting op-amp yields a gain of exactly Av = +1.0x with near-infinite input impedance and zero output impedance.
