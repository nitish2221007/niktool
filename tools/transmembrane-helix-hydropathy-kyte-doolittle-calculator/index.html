Frequently asked questions

Why is a 19-21 residue window used to find transmembrane helices?

An alpha-helix advances $0.15 ext{ nm}$ per residue; spanning a $3.0 ext{ nm}$ hydrophobic lipid bilayer core requires exactly $3.0 / 0.15 = 20 ext{ residues}$.
