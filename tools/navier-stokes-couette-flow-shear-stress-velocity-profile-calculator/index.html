Maurice Couette 1890 Exact Navier-Stokes Solution

Plane Couette flow is one of the rare exact analytical solutions to the non-linear Navier-Stokes equations, forming the physical basis for rotational cylinder viscometers and journal bearing lubrication oil film analysis.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the Couette velocity profile strictly linear?

In the absence of a pressure gradient ($ rac{dP}{dx} = 0$), viscous momentum diffusion across the gap creates a constant velocity gradient ($ rac{du}{dy} = rac{U}{h}$).
