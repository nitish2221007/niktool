Circular Shaft Torsion Theory

Shear stress varies linearly from zero at the center to a maximum at the outer surface ($ au propto r$), guiding sizing of automotive driveshafts and industrial gearbox spindles.

Frequently asked questions

Why are hollow shafts more weight-efficient for torsion than solid shafts?

Material near the central core experiences near-zero shear stress; removing it in a hollow tube drastically cuts weight with minimal loss in torsional strength.
