How to use Bioreactor Volumetric Oxygen Mass Transfer (k_L·a) & Oxygen Transfer Rate (OTR) Calculator

  1. Enter volumetric gas-liquid oxygen mass transfer coefficient $k_L a$ in $h^{-1}$.
  2. Enter dissolved oxygen saturation equilibrium concentration $C^*$ in mg/L (typically $sim 7.8 ext{ mg/L}$ for air-saturated water at 30°C).
  3. Enter actual operational dissolved oxygen setpoint $C_L$ in mg/L (typically 20% to 30% air saturation).
  4. Inspect volumetric Oxygen Transfer Rate (OTR) in $ ext{mg/(L}cdot ext{h)}$ and $ ext{mmol/(L}cdot ext{h)}$ and maximum sustainable high-density aerobic biomass.

Two-Film Gas-Liquid Mass Transfer in Bioreactors

Because oxygen has very low solubility in aqueous broth ($sim 8 ext{ ppm}$), oxygen transfer from sparged air bubbles into the liquid broth ($OTR = k_L a [C^* - C_L]$) is the primary rate-limiting bottleneck in industrial antibiotic, vaccine, and microbial biofuel fermentations.

Frequently asked questions

How do fermenter operators increase kLa during peak cell growth?

By increasing Rushton turbine impeller agitation RPM (shearing bubbles into higher interfacial area $a$) and boosting sterile compressed air sparging flow rate.
