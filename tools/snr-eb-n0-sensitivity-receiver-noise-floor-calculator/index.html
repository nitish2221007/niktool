Mathematical & Theoretical Formulas

This calculator evaluates performance using the following core formulas:

$$\displaystyle N_{\text{floor}} = 10 \log_{10}(k \cdot T \cdot B) + 30 \text{ dBm}, \quad \text{Sensitivity} = N_{\text{floor}} + \text{NF} + \text{SNR}_{\text{req}}$$

Frequently asked questions

What is thermal noise floor kTB?

Thermal noise floor is Johnson-Nyquist noise generated by thermal agitation of charge carriers inside passive components.

What is standard room temperature noise density?

At 290 K, thermal noise power density is -174 dBm/Hz.

How does receiver sensitivity relate to coverage?

More sensitive receivers (e.g. -110 dBm vs -95 dBm) can detect fainter signals over longer ranges.

Is this calculation local?
