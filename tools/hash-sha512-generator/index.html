Why Use SHA-512?

SHA-512 offers 256 bits of collision resistance against brute-force and quantum attacks, making it the preferred standard for certificate authorities and digital signatures.

Frequently asked questions

Is SHA-512 faster than SHA-256 on 64-bit CPUs?

Yes, because SHA-512 operates on native 64-bit arithmetic words, it often executes faster on modern 64-bit processors than SHA-256.
