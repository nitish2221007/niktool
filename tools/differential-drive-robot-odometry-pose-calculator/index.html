Frequently asked questions

What causes wheel odometry drift in mobile robots?

Wheel slip on slick floors, uneven tire wear, and floor bumps accumulate boundless position drift over time, requiring periodic correction from LiDAR SLAM or AprilTag fiducial landmarks.
