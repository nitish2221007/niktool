Frequently asked questions

Why is infrared astronomy effective at peering through dust clouds?

Infrared wavelengths are much longer than dust grain sizes ($lambda gg a$), passing cleanly through dense nebulae with minimal extinction ($A_K approx 0.1 A_V$).
