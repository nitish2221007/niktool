Frequently asked questions

Why is IHD = 4 a classic signature for a benzene ring?

A benzene ring ($C_6H_6$) consists of 1 six-membered ring plus 3 conjugated double bonds ($1 + 3 = 4 ext{ degrees of unsaturation}$).
