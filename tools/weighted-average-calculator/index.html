How to use Weighted Average Calculator

  1. Enter each score and its weight on a new line as Score, Weight.
  2. Use weights that reflect importance, such as assignment, test, and exam percentages.
  3. Click Process to get the weighted average and total weight.

Key Features & Privacy

The tool multiplies each score by its weight, sums the results, and divides by the total weight. It warns you when weights do not add up to 100 so you can double-check your setup. All calculations stay local.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to compute final grades where tests, projects, and exams carry different weights, and teachers use it to build transparent grading breakdowns.
