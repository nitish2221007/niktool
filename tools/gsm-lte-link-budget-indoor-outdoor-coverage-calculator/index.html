Frequently asked questions

What is MAPL in cellular engineering?

Maximum Allowable Path Loss (MAPL) defines the total attenuation a radio signal can sustain before dropping below receiver sensitivity.

How does frequency impact cellular cell size?

Lower frequencies (e.g., 700 MHz) propagate much further with lower path loss than high frequencies (e.g., 3.5 GHz).

What fade margin is recommended for cellular links?

A log-normal shadow fading margin of 8 dB to 15 dB is typically added for 90-95% cell coverage reliability.
