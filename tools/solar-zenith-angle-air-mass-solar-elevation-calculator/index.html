How to use Solar Zenith Angle (θ_z), Elevation & Optical Air Mass (AM) Calculator

  1. Enter geographic observer Latitude $phi$ in degrees.
  2. Enter solar declination angle $delta$ in degrees ($-23.45^circ$ winter solstice to $+23.45^circ$ summer solstice).
  3. Enter solar hour angle $omega$ ($15^circ$ per hour before/after local solar noon).
  4. Inspect Solar Zenith Angle $ heta_z$, Solar Elevation altitude above horizon, and optical Air Mass path coefficient (AM).

Fritz Kasten & Andrew Young 1989 Atmospheric Solar Path

Air Mass ($AM = 1/cos heta_z$) measures the direct optical path length of sunlight through Earth's atmosphere relative to the zenith; $AM 1.5$ (corresponding to $ heta_z = 48.2^circ$, $1,000 ext{ W/m}^2$) is the universal IEC 60904 Standard Test Condition (STC) for rating all commercial solar panels.

Frequently asked questions

What is AM0 vs AM1.5?

AM0 ($1,361 ext{ W/m}^2$) represents extraterrestrial sunlight in outer space above the atmosphere, while AM1.5 ($1,000 ext{ W/m}^2$) represents mid-latitude terrestrial sunlight filtered through 1.5 atmospheric air masses.
