  3. Enter shop air supply line gauge pressure in bar (typically 6.0 bar / ~90 PSI).
  4. Enter stroke length in millimeters and cycling speed in cycles per minute (CPM).
  5. Inspect extension push force, retraction pull force, and compressed air consumption in SCFM.

ISO 15552 Pneumatic Actuator Sizing

Double-acting cylinders produce less force on retraction because the piston rod occupies cross-sectional volume; accounting for rod annular area ensures automated clamping mechanisms maintain necessary holding forces.

Frequently asked questions

Why is standard industrial air pressure set at 6 bar (87 PSI)?

6 bar provides an optimal economic balance between actuator force output, compressor power consumption, and seal durability.
