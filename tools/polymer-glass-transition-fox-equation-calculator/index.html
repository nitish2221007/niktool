Thomas G. Fox's 1956 Free Volume Theorem

The Fox equation models how mixing hard, rigid monomers (like Styrene, T_g = 100°C) with soft, flexible monomers (like Butadiene, T_g = -60°C) proportionally shifts polymer chain segmental mobility and rubbery flexibility.
