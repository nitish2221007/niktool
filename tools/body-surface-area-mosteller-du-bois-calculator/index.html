Robert D. Mosteller's 1987 Simplified Formula

Mosteller demonstrated that the simple square-root formula $\text{BSA} = \sqrt{(H\cdot W)/3600}$ matches complex multi-exponential models within 1%, becoming the universal hospital standard for oncology medication prescribing.
