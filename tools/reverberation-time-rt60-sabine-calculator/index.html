Wallace Clement Sabine's Formula

Sabine discovered in 1898 that acoustic clarity requires matching RT60 to purpose: 0.3-0.5s for speech/podcasts, 0.8-1.2s for chamber music, and 1.8-2.2s for symphonic concert halls.

Frequently asked questions

What does RT60 mean?

The time in seconds required for sound reflections to drop by 60 dB (1 millionth of original intensity).
