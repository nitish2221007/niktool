How to use Karvonen Target Heart Rate Zones Calculator

  1. Enter your chronological age.

  3. Inspect your custom 5-zone cardiovascular training heart rate ranges.

Why Karvonen is Superior to Simple % Max HR

The Karvonen formula incorporates individual Heart Rate Reserve (HRR = HR_max - HR_rest), customizing training zones to match your true aerobic fitness baseline.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the optimal heart rate zone for weight loss?

Zone 2 (60-70% of HRR) maximizes lipid fatty-acid oxidation as the primary muscular energy source.
