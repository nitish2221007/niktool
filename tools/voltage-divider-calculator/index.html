Resistive Voltage Divider Formula

A voltage divider produces an output voltage that is a fraction of its input: Vout = Vin × (R₂ / (R₁ + R₂)). When R₁ = R₂, Vout is exactly half of Vin.

Frequently asked questions

Can a voltage divider be used as a power supply?

No, connecting a heavy load in parallel with R₂ alters the effective resistance and sags Vout unless buffered by an op-amp follower.
