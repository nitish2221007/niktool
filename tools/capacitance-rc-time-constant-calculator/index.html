RC Circuit Behavior

In one time constant (1τ = R·C), a capacitor charges to approximately 63.2% of its maximum voltage. After 5 time constants (5τ), the capacitor is practically fully charged (99.3%).

What is the cutoff frequency formula for an RC filter?
