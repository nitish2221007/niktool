How to connect phone to laptop & chat anonymously

  1. Enter your preferred display name in the input box.

Key Features & Privacy

This anonymous temporary room chat (ChatNest) is designed for instant text transfers between mobile and desktop devices. Messages are transmitted securely and stored in an encrypted ephemeral storage buffer that automatically wipes all room history 24 hours after room disconnect. No account creation, email address, or phone number is ever required.

Common Use Cases

Users frequently use this tool to quickly send links, notes, and text clips from phone to laptop. Friends and gamers use it as an instant anonymous chat room to talk with strangers or group members without sharing personal phone numbers or social media handles.
