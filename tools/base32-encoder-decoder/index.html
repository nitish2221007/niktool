Why Base32 in Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

Base32 avoids visually ambiguous characters like 0/O and 1/I, making it easy for humans to manually read and transcribe 2FA secret seeds into authenticator apps.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What characters are used in RFC 4648 Base32?

Uppercase letters A through Z and digits 2 through 7 (total 32 symbols).
