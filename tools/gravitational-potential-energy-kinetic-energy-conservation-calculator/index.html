Law of Conservation of Mechanical Energy

In a closed conservative system with negligible air resistance, potential energy converts completely into kinetic energy ($mgh = rac{1}{2}mv^2$); terminal speed depends strictly on drop height and is completely independent of object mass.

Frequently asked questions

Why is final free-fall speed independent of mass?

Because mass appears linearly on both sides of the energy conservation equation ($mgh = rac{1}{2}mv^2$), canceling out to yield $v = sqrt{2gh}$.
