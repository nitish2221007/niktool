  5. Inspect required total packed column bed height Z in meters and feet.

Allan Colburn 1939 Continuous Mass Transfer Integration

Packed columns transfer mass continuously rather than in discrete equilibrium trays; factoring column height into $Z = HTU imes NTU$ separates the equipment mass transfer efficiency ($HTU$) from the thermodynamic separation difficulty ($NTU$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is structured packing preferred over random dumping rings in modern columns?

Structured corrugated sheet metal packings offer much higher surface area per unit volume with lower pressure drop, cutting HTU in half.
