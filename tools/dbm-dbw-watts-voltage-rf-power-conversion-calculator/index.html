Frequently asked questions

What is 0 dBm in Watts?

0 dBm corresponds exactly to 1 milliwatt (0.001 Watt).

Why is 50 Ohms assumed for voltage conversion?

RF transmission lines, spectrum analyzers, and power meters are calibrated to a standard 50-ohm characteristic impedance.

How do you convert dBm to dBW?

Subtract 30 from dBm to get dBW (since 1 W = 1000 mW, which is a 30 dB difference).

Is this conversion processed locally?

Yes, all calculations occur strictly in your browser.
