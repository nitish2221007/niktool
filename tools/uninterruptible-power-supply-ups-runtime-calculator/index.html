Peukert's Effect and Inverter Thermal Efficiency

High discharge rates drain lead-acid batteries faster than nominal C20 ratings due to Peukert's law; factoring an 85% combined inverter/thermal efficiency delivers realistic, dependable server shutdown timelines.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How long will a 1500VA (24V 18Ah) UPS run a 400W load?

Usable energy is (24V × 18Ah × 0.85) ≈ 367 Wh; 367 Wh / 400W ≈ 0.92 hours (~55 minutes).
