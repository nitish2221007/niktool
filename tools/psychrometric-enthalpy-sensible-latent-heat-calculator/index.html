How to use Psychrometric Sensible & Latent Cooling Load (CFM) Calculator

  1. Enter supply fan airflow volume in CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute).
  2. Enter dry-bulb temperature drop ΔT across the cooling coil in °F (typically 18°F to 22°F).
  3. Enter moisture removal humidity differential ΔW in grains of water per pound of dry air (7000 grains = 1 lb water).
  4. Inspect Sensible Cooling, Latent Dehumidification, Total AC Tonnage, and Sensible Heat Ratio (SHR).

Willis Carrier 1911 Rational Psychrometric Formulae

Air conditioners must simultaneously cool dry air (sensible load) and condense water vapor into liquid drain pan runoff (latent load); matching equipment Sensible Heat Ratio (SHR) prevents humid clammy indoor spaces.

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical Sensible Heat Ratio for residential AC?

Standard residential comfort cooling operates at an SHR of ~0.70 to 0.75 (70% sensible cooling, 30% moisture condensation).
