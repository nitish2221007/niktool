Understanding Baker's Percentages

In professional baking, all ingredient weights are expressed as a percentage of total flour weight: 70% hydration means 70g of water per 100g of flour.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is beginner vs artisan sourdough hydration?

Beginner dough is typically 65-70% hydration (easy to shape), while open-crumb artisan sourdough ranges between 75% and 85% hydration.
