How to use Focal Length Calculator

  1. Select whether you are working with a Lens or a Mirror.
  2. Enter the object distance (u) and image distance (v) using the correct Cartesian sign convention.
  3. Click Process to calculate the focal length and determine the nature of the optic.

Key Features & Privacy

This calculator applies the standard lens and mirror formulas, automatically identifying whether the resulting focal length indicates a converging or diverging optic. Remember to apply your textbook's sign convention (usually distances against incident light are negative). All processing is 100% local in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use this to quickly verify ray optics numericals involving concave/convex lenses and mirrors before board exams.
