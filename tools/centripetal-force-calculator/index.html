How to use Centripetal Force Calculator

  1. Enter the rotating body mass in kilograms.
  2. Enter tangential velocity in meters per second (m/s).
  3. Enter the radius of the circular path in meters.
  4. Click Calculate to inspect the required inward centripetal force.

Centripetal Force in Curvature

Any object moving in a circle must experience an inward net force (Fc = m·v²/r) directed toward the center of curvature to constantly change its velocity vector.

Frequently asked questions

What provides centripetal force for a turning car?

Static friction between the car tires and the road surface provides the necessary inward centripetal force.
