James Cooley & John Tukey 1965 FFT Algorithm

By exploiting symmetry and periodicity of twiddle factors ($W_N^{k + N/2} = -W_N^k$), Cooley and Tukey slashed DFT complexity from $O(N^2)$ to $O(N log_2 N)$, enabling real-time digital audio processing, Wi-Fi 6 OFDM modulation, and MRI medical imaging.
