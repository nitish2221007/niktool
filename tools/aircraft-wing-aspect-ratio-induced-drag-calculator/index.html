Prandtl Lifting-Line Induced Drag Theory

High aspect ratio wings ($AR > 10$) reduce wingtip vortices and lift-induced drag ($C_{Di} propto 1/AR$), which is why long-range gliders and U-2 spy planes use slender wings.
