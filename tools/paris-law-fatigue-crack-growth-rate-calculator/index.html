Frequently asked questions

What are the three regions of a fatigue crack growth curve?

Region I: Near-threshold ($Delta K_{ ext{th}}$) microcrack initiation; Region II: Linear Paris law power regime; Region III: Rapid unstable fracture approaching $K_{IC}$.
