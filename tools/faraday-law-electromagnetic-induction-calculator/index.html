Michael Faraday's 1831 Induction Law & Lenz's Rule

Electromagnetic induction underpins all modern electric power generators and transformers: moving a magnetic field through a conductive coil induces an opposing electric current (Lenz's Law).

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 Weber per second in SI units?

A magnetic flux change of 1 Weber per second (1 Wb/s) across a single turn induces exactly 1 Volt of electromotive force.
