Acoustic Inverse-Square Geometrical Spreading Law

Sound power spreads outward over an expanding spherical surface area ($A = 4pi r^2$), which reduces intensity by a factor of 4 ($6.02 ext{ dB}$ drop) every time the distance to the sound source is doubled.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the decibel scale logarithmic?

Human hearing spans an immense intensity range of $10^{12}$ ($1 ext{ to }1,000,000,000,000 ext{ W/m}^2$); the logarithmic decibel scale compresses this into a manageable 0 to 120 dB scale.
