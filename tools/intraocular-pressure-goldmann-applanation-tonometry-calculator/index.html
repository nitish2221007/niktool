Frequently asked questions

Why does Goldmann applanation use a 3.06 mm diameter tip?

At exactly $3.06 ext{ mm}$ applanation diameter, the corneal surface tension tear meniscus capillary force exactly cancels the corneal bending stiffness resistance.
