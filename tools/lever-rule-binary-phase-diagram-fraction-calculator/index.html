How to use Lever Rule Binary Alloy Phase Diagram Mass Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter overall nominal alloy bulk composition $C_0$ in weight percentage (wt %).
  2. Enter composition of solid phase $alpha$ at the left tie-line boundary $C_alpha$ in wt %.
  3. Enter composition of liquid/solid phase $eta$ at the right tie-line boundary $C_eta$ in wt %.
  4. Inspect equilibrium mass fractions of phase $alpha$ ($w_alpha$) and phase $eta$ ($w_eta$) computed via the inverse lever arm ratio.

The Inverse Lever Arm Conservation Rule

By conservation of mass along an isothermal tie-line, the fraction of each phase is proportional to the length of the opposite lever arm divided by the total tie-line length ($w_alpha = (C_eta - C_0) / (C_eta - C_alpha)$).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it called the inverse lever rule?

Because the fraction of the left phase ($alpha$) is calculated using the length of the right-hand tie-line segment ($C_eta - C_0$).
