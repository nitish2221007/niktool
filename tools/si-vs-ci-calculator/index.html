How to use SI vs CI Calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount (P).
  2. Enter the annual rate (R) and time in years (T).
  3. Click Process to compare simple interest, compound interest, and their difference.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator computes SI = PRT/100 and CI using A = P(1 + r/100)^t with yearly compounding, then shows the exact difference so you can see why compound interest grows faster. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Class 10 students use it for the comparison problems that frequently appear in exams, and anyone can use it to understand how compounding affects savings.
