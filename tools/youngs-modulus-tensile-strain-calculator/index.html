Hooke's Law Linear Elastic Regime

Named after Thomas Young (1807), Young's modulus measures a material's intrinsic resistance to elastic deformation under uniaxial tension (Structural Steel ≈ 200 GPa, Titanium ≈ 116 GPa, Aluminum ≈ 69 GPa).

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 GPa in Pascals?

1 GigaPascal (GPa) = 1,000 MegaPascals (MPa) = 10⁹ N/m².
