Force Plate Impulse-Momentum Verification

Jump height is governed strictly by the vertical impulse ($int (F - mg) dt = m v_{ ext{takeoff}}$) generated against the ground; elite basketball and volleyball athletes routinely produce peak ground reaction forces exceeding 2.5× to 3.0× body weight.

Frequently asked questions

Why does measuring jump height by hang time sometimes overestimate height?

Tucking legs or bending knees before landing artificially extends flight hang time, distorting the $h = g t^2 / 8$ calculation.
