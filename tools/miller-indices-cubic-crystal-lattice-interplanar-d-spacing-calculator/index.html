William Hallowes Miller 1839 Crystallographic Notation

Miller indices $(hkl)$ uniquely define parallel planes of atoms in 3D lattices; identifying allowed XRD reflections reveals whether a metal crystallizes in Body-Centered Cubic (BCC) or Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) symmetry.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the (100) reflection forbidden in Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) crystals?

In FCC lattices, destructive interference from atoms on face centers cancels out the (100) diffracted wave, requiring all indices to be unmixed (all even or all odd).
