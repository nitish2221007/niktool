Temperature Independence of Molality

Because volumetric molarity (mol/L) changes with thermal liquid expansion/contraction, thermodynamics and colligative properties strictly employ temperature-invariant Molality (mol/kg mass of solvent).

Frequently asked questions

When are Molarity and Molality nearly identical?

In dilute aqueous solutions at room temperature where density ≈ 1.00 g/mL and solute mass is negligible, Molarity (M) ≈ Molality (m).
