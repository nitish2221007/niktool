How to use Wastewater Activated Sludge Mean Cell Residence Time (MCRT / Sludge Age) Calculator

  1. Enter biological aeration basin volume V in $m^3$.
  2. Enter Mixed Liquor Suspended Solids (MLSS) concentration X in mg/L (typically 2,500 to 4,000 mg/L).
  3. Enter Waste Activated Sludge (WAS) concentration $X_w$ from clarifier underflow in mg/L (typically 6,000 to 10,000 mg/L).
  4. Enter target Solids Retention Time / MCRT in days (e.g. 5–8 days for BOD removal, 10–15 days for complete nitrification).
  5. Inspect daily waste activated sludge (WAS) volumetric pumping rate in $m^3/ ext{day}$ and total basin solids inventory.

Solids Retention Time (SRT) & Nitrification Control

MCRT ($ heta_c$) represents the average time nitrifying bacteria spend in the aeration system; keeping MCRT above 10 days ensures slow-growing autotrophic Nitrosomonas and Nitrobacter bacteria multiply faster than they are wasted, ensuring 100% ammonia removal.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if MCRT is too short (<3 days)?

Nitrifying bacteria wash out of the system, floc settling degrades in secondary clarifiers, and effluent turbidity surges.
