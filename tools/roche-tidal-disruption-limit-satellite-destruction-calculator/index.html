Édouard Roche 1848 Gravitational Tidal Limit

When a moon crosses inside a planet's Roche limit, differential gravitational tidal pull exceeds the moon's self-gravitational cohesion, shredding it into debris that flattens into planetary rings (e.g. Saturn's rings).

Frequently asked questions

Why do artificial satellites not break apart inside the Roche limit?

Artificial satellites are tiny and held together by electromagnetic chemical tensile bonds of metal/composite materials, not self-gravity.
