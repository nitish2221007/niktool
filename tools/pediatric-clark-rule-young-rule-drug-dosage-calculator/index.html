Pediatric Pharmacology Dosing Principles

Children metabolize medications differently than adults due to developing liver enzymes and renal clearance; Clark's Rule based on body weight provides a safer dosing approximation than chronological age alone.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Clark's Rule preferred over Young's Rule?

Body weight is a far better physiological indicator of drug distribution volume than chronological age, especially for children who are above or below average height.
