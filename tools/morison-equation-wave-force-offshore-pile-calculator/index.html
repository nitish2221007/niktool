J.R. Morison 1950 Offshore Hydrodynamic Wave Loading

The Morison equation superimposes quadratic velocity form drag ($u|u|$) and unseparated fluid acceleration mass inertia ($dot{u}$); structural engineers use it to design offshore wind turbine monopiles and jacket foundations against 100-year North Sea storm waves.

Frequently asked questions

When is Morison's equation valid vs MacCamy-Fuchs diffraction theory?

Morison's equation is valid for slender piles where $D/lambda < 0.2$ (diameter is less than 20% of the ocean wavelength); large gravity base structures require full diffraction analysis.
