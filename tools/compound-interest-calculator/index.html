How to use Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount (P).
  2. Enter the annual rate (R) and time in years (T).
  3. Choose the compounding frequency and click Process to get the amount and CI.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator uses A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) and supports yearly, half-yearly, and quarterly compounding, which covers the common Class 10 cases. It displays the formula and every input so the result is easy to verify, and it runs fully locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to understand how compounding frequency affects the final amount, and to check compound interest homework problems.
