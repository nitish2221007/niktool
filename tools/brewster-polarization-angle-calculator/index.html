Why Polarized Sunglasses Eliminate Water Glare

Sir David Brewster showed in 1815 that when light reflects off water or glass at θ_B = arctan(n₂/n₁) (~53° on water), reflected light is 100% horizontally polarized. Vertically oriented polarizing sunglass filters eliminate 100% of this blinding glare.

Frequently asked questions

What is Brewster's angle for glass in air?

For standard window glass (n = 1.52) in air (n = 1.0), Brewster's angle is arctan(1.52) = 56.66°.
