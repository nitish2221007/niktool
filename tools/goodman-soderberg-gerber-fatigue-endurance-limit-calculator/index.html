How to use Goodman, Soderberg & Gerber Mean Stress Fatigue Endurance Limit Calculator

  1. Enter alternating stress amplitude $sigma_a = (sigma_{max} - sigma_{min})/2$ in MPa.
  2. Enter mean static stress $sigma_m = (sigma_{max} + sigma_{min})/2$ in MPa.
  3. Enter fully reversed endurance limit $S_e$ in MPa.
  4. Enter ultimate tensile strength $S_{ut}$ in MPa.
  5. Inspect Goodman, Soderberg, and Gerber fatigue life safety factors.

Frequently asked questions

What is the effect of compressive mean stress on fatigue life?

Compressive mean stress ($sigma_m < 0$) closes microcracks and substantially INCREASES fatigue endurance life.
