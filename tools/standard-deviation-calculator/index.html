How to use Standard Deviation Calculator

  1. Paste your dataset (numbers separated by commas, spaces, or tabs).
  2. Click Calculate to inspect both sample standard deviation (Bessel's correction n-1) and population standard deviation (n).
  3. Review additional stats including Mean, Sum, and Variance.

Sample vs Population Standard Deviation

Use Sample Standard Deviation (s) with divisor (n-1) when your data represents a sample of a wider population to prevent underestimating variance. Use Population Standard Deviation (σ) with divisor (n) when your dataset encompasses the entire population.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard deviation formula?

Sample SD = sqrt(sum((x - mean)^2) / (n - 1))
