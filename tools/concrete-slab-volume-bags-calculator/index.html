Premix vs Ready-Mix Concrete Truck Ordering

For jobs over 1.5 to 2.0 cubic yards, ordering a ready-mix concrete delivery truck is significantly more cost-effective and structurally uniform than hand-mixing dozens of individual 80lb bags.

Frequently asked questions

How many 80 lb bags are in 1 cubic yard of concrete?

One cubic yard of concrete (27 cu ft) requires approximately 45 bags of 80 lb premix concrete (0.60 cu ft per bag).
