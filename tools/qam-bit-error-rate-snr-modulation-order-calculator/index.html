Frequently asked questions

What is Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM)?

QAM modulates both amplitude and phase of two orthogonal carrier waves (I and Q) to pack multiple bits into each symbol.

Why is higher order QAM faster?

256-QAM carries 8 bits per symbol compared to QPSK which carries 2 bits per symbol, quadrupling data throughput at the cost of higher required SNR.

What SNR is needed for 64-QAM?

64-QAM typically requires ~18 dB to 20 dB SNR for low error rates.
