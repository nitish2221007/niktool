Inverse Viscosity Relationship

Melt Flow Rate is inversely proportional to molecular weight and melt viscosity: high molecular weight polymers flow slowly with low MFR (< 1.0 g/10 min), while low molecular weight plastics melt into runny liquids with high MFR (> 20 g/10 min).

Frequently asked questions

What is a "fractional melt" polymer?

A polymer with an MFR of less than 1.0 g/10 min, offering maximum impact strength and environmental stress crack resistance (ESCR).
