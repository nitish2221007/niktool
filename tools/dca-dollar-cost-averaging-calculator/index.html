Lowering Cost Basis in Market Dips

DCA eliminates market-timing emotional anxiety: purchasing fixed dollar amounts at regular intervals acquires more shares when prices are low and fewer when prices are high.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.
