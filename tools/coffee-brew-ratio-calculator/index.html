Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) Golden Cup Standard

SCA defines the optimal extraction ratio as 55 to 60 grams of coffee per 1,000 grams of water (1:16 to 1:18), achieving a total dissolved solids (TDS) concentration between 1.15% and 1.45%.
