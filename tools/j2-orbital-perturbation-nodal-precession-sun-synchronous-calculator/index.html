Earth Equatorial Bulge J2 Geopotential Standard

Earth's equatorial bulge precesses the orbital plane at exactly $+0.9856^circ/ ext{day}$ (360° per year), matching Earth's revolution around the Sun so satellites pass over ground targets at the exact same local solar time every day.

Frequently asked questions

Why are all Sun-Synchronous Orbits slightly retrograde (i > 90°)?

To achieve positive eastward precession ($dot{Omega} > 0$), $cos(i)$ must be negative, requiring an inclination between $96^circ$ and $99^circ$.
