Erwin Schrödinger 1D Quantum Confinement Standard

Compressing electron wavefunctions within nanometer barriers ($L le lambda_{ ext{de Broglie}}$) discretizes continuous bulk energy bands into distinct subband levels, creating tunable laser diodes and Quantum Cascade Lasers (QCLs).

Frequently asked questions

Why is energy inversely proportional to the square of well width (1/L²)?

De Broglie wavelength is constrained to $lambda = 2L/n$; since kinetic energy $E propto p^2 propto 1/lambda^2$, confinement energy scales as $1/L^2$.
