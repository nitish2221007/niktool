How to use Bishop's Simplified Method of Slices Circular Slope Stability Factor of Safety Calculator

  1. Enter soil effective cohesion $c^prime$ in kPa and internal friction angle $phi^prime$ in degrees.
  2. Enter total sliding soil mass weight W in kN.
  3. Enter mean inclination base angle of slices $alpha$ in degrees.
  4. Enter pore water pressure ratio $r_u = u / (gamma h)$ (0 for dry embankment, 0.2–0.4 for steady seepage).
  5. Inspect Bishop's iteratively solved Factor of Safety (FS).

Alan W. Bishop 1955 Rigorous Slices Formulation

Satisfies vertical force equilibrium on each individual slice ($m_alpha = cosalpha (1 + rac{ analpha anphi^prime}{FS})$), providing substantially higher accuracy than the simplified Ordinary (Fellenius) method.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Bishop's method called simplified if it requires iteration?

It assumes interslice shear forces are zero ($Delta X = 0$) while fully satisfying overall moment equilibrium and vertical slice force balance.
