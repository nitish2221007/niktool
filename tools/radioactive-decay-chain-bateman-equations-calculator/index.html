Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Secular and Transient Equilibrium?

Secular equilibrium occurs when parent half-life is thousands of times longer than daughter ($T_1 gg T_2$), locking daughter activity equal to parent; Transient equilibrium occurs when parent is moderately longer ($T_1 sim 10 imes T_2$).
