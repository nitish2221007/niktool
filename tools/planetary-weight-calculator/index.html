Mass vs Weight in Astronomy

Your mass (the quantity of matter in your body) remains constant anywhere in the universe. Weight is the downward gravitational force (W = m · g) exerted by the celestial mass beneath you.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you weigh less on the Moon?

The Moon has roughly 1/81 of Earth's mass, producing surface gravity that is only 16.6% (1/6th) of Earth's gravity.
