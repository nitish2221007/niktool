How to use Screen DPI / PPI Pixel Density Calculator

  1. Enter native horizontal and vertical pixel resolution (e.g. 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 3840×2160).
  2. Enter diagonal screen measurement in inches.
  3. Inspect display sharpness density (PPI) and individual subpixel dot pitch.

Apple Retina and Visual Acuity Limits

A display achieves "Retina" resolution when the human eye (with 20/20 vision) cannot distinguish individual pixels at typical viewing distances (e.g. >300 PPI for phones held at 12 inches, >220 PPI for laptops at 20 inches).
