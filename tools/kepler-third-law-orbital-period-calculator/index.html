Johannes Kepler's 1619 Harmonic Third Law

Kepler proved that the square of orbital period is directly proportional to the cube of semi-major axis distance ($T^2 propto a^3$), harmonizing planetary motions across our solar system and thousands of newly discovered exoplanets.
