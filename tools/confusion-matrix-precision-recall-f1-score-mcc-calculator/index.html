Frequently asked questions

Why is Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) considered the most robust single metric?

MCC evaluates all four quadrants of the confusion matrix symmetrically, yielding values from -1.0 (total disagreement) to +1.0 (perfect prediction).
