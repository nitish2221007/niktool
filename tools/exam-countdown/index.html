How to use Exam Countdown

  1. Select the date of your upcoming board or school exam.
  2. Optionally set the exact start time (default is 9:00 AM).
  3. Click Process to see the exact days, hours, and minutes remaining.

Key Features & Privacy

This countdown calculates the exact time left down to the minute and shows the total hours remaining, which helps in dividing your syllabus into hourly targets. It runs entirely locally without needing an internet connection after loading.

Common Use Cases

Students use this to track days left before CBSE/ICSE board exams, pre-boards, or half-yearly tests to pace their revision.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.

Is this tool free to use?

Yes, NikTool is 100% free with no account or registration required.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once loaded, the tool works completely offline in your browser.
