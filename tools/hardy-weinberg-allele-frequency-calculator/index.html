How to use Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium Allele Frequency Calculator

  1. Enter the homozygous recessive disease or trait frequency (q²) as a decimal fraction (e.g. 0.0004 for 1 in 2,500).
  2. Inspect dominant allele frequency (p), recessive allele frequency (q), and heterozygous carrier percentage (2pq).

G. H. Hardy and Wilhelm Weinberg's 1908 Law

Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium proves that in the absence of evolutionary influences (mutation, selection, drift), allele frequencies remain constant across generations: p² + 2pq + q² = 1.0.

Frequently asked questions

If a recessive condition affects 1 in 2,500 people, what is the carrier frequency?

q² = 0.0004 => q = 0.02, p = 0.98; Carrier frequency 2pq = 2 × 0.98 × 0.02 = 0.0392 (~1 in 25 people are carriers).
