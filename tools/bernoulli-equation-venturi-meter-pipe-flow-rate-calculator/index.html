How to use Bernoulli Equation & Venturi Meter Pipe Flow Rate Calculator

  1. Enter upstream pipe inlet diameter $D_1$ in millimeters (mm).
  2. Enter constricted throat nozzle diameter $D_2$ in millimeters (mm).
  3. Enter measured differential static pressure drop $Delta P$ across inlet and throat in kPa.
  4. Enter fluid density $ ho$ in $ ext{kg/m}^3$ (e.g. 1,000 for water, 1.225 for air).
  5. Inspect calculated volumetric flow rate Q in Liters/second and accelerated throat fluid velocity $v_2$.

Daniel Bernoulli 1738 Hydrodynamics Principle

As fluid velocity increases through a pipe constriction ($v_2 > v_1$), static pressure decreases ($P_1 + rac{1}{2} ho v_1^2 = P_2 + rac{1}{2} ho v_2^2$), enabling non-intrusive flow measurement without moving mechanical parts.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a discharge coefficient (Cd ≈ 0.98) included?

The discharge coefficient accounts for minor boundary layer friction and non-uniform velocity profile head losses in real fluids.
