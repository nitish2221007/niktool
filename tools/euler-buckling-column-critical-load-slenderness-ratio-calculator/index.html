Leonhard Euler 1757 Structural Column Buckling Theorem

Slender columns subjected to compressive axial forces fail by sudden sideways geometric buckling long before the material reaches its compressive yield strength; doubling effective length cuts buckling capacity by $75%$ ($propto 1/L^2$).

Frequently asked questions

Why is a Fixed-Fixed column 4 times stronger than a Pinned-Pinned column?

Because clamping both ends halves the effective buckling length ($K = 0.5$), quadrupling the critical load ($P_{ ext{cr}} propto 1/K^2 = 1/0.25 = 4$).
