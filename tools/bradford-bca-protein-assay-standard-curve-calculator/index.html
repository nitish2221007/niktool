Frequently asked questions

Why is the BCA assay preferred over Bradford in detergent buffers?

The Bicinchoninic Acid (BCA) assay is compatible with up to $5%$ ionic and non-ionic detergents (SDS, Triton X-100) that interfere with Bradford.
