How to use Wilke-Chang Liquid Diffusivity (D_AB = 7.4·10⁻⁸·√(φ·M_B)·T / (μ_B·V_A^0.6)) Calculator

  1. Enter solvent molecular weight $M_B$ (e.g. 18.0 for water, 32.0 for methanol).
  2. Enter solvent association parameter $phi$ (2.6 for water, 1.9 for methanol, 1.0 for unassociated solvents).
  3. Enter solute molar volume at normal boiling point $V_A$ in $ ext{cm}^3/ ext{mol}$.
  4. Enter solvent dynamic viscosity $mu$ in centipoise (cP) and temperature in $^circ ext{C}$.
  5. Inspect liquid molecular diffusivity $D_{AB}$ in $ ext{m}^2/ ext{s}$ and $ ext{cm}^2/ ext{s}$.

Frequently asked questions

Why does water have a high association factor phi of 2.6?

Extensive hydrogen-bonding networks cause water molecules to diffuse as clustered supramolecular aggregates.
