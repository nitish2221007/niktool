Telescope Exit Pupil and Human Eye Pupil Match

The exit pupil (Aperture / Magnification) should match the observer's dark-adapted eye pupil (~5mm to 7mm for humans) to capture maximum photons without clipping the light beam.

Frequently asked questions

What is a "fast" telescope in astrophotography?

Telescopes with low focal ratios (f/4 to f/5) collect photons faster across a wider field of view than "slow" high focal ratio (f/10 to f/15) planetary scopes.
