Lawrence Joseph Henderson & Karl Albert Hasselbalch Equation

Buffer solutions resist changes in hydronium ion concentration when small amounts of strong acid or base are added; the human blood bicarbonate buffer system maintains strict physiological pH ($7.35 ext{ to }7.45$) using this exact chemical equilibrium.

Frequently asked questions

When is a buffer solution at its maximum buffering capacity?

When $[A^-] = [HA]$, the ratio is 1 ($log 1 = 0$), so $pH = pK_a$, providing equal protection against added acids and bases.
