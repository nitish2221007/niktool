Enrico Fermi Neutron Moderation Theory

Light water (Hydrogen-1) has an atomic mass nearly identical to a neutron (A=1), maximizing kinetic energy transfer per collision (ξ = 1.0) so fast 2 MeV neutrons thermalize in just 18 collisions compared to 114 in graphite.

Frequently asked questions

Why is heavy water (D₂O) used in CANDU reactors despite needing 25 collisions?

Because Deuterium has a neutron absorption cross-section hundreds of times smaller than light hydrogen, allowing reactors to run on unenriched natural Uranium.
