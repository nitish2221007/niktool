How to use Chemical Equilibrium Constant (Kp / Kc) Converter

  1. Enter concentration equilibrium constant Kc.
  2. Enter net change in gaseous stoichiometric coefficients Δn = (Moles Gas Products - Moles Gas Reactants).
  3. Enter absolute Kelvin temperature T.
  4. Inspect the partial pressure equilibrium constant Kp.

Gas Mole Change and Kp = Kc Equality

When Δn = 0 (equal number of gas moles on reactant and product sides), (RT)⁰ = 1 and therefore Kp = Kc regardless of temperature.

Frequently asked questions

What is Δn for Haber process N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃?

Δn = 2 (products) - (1 + 3) (reactants) = 2 - 4 = -2.
