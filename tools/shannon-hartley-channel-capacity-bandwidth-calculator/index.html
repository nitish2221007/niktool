The Shannon-Hartley Telecommunications Ceiling

No modulation scheme can transmit data faster than C = B · log₂(1 + SNR) without corrupted packet errors; doubling channel bandwidth doubles data rate linearly, while increasing SNR yields logarithmic gains.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30 dB SNR in linear signal power ratio?

30 dB = 10^(30/10) = 1000:1, meaning the signal power is 1,000 times stronger than background thermal noise power.
