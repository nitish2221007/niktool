Thomas Young's 1801 Wave Theory Experiment

Young's double slit experiment proved conclusively that light behaves as a wave: overlapping wavefronts from the two slits interfere constructively when optical path difference is an integer wavelength (d·sin θ = mλ).

Frequently asked questions

What happens to fringe spacing when moving the screen farther away?

Fringe spacing increases linearly with distance L (Δy ∝ L), making fringes wider and easier to see.
