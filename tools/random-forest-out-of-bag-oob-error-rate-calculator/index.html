Because each tree is trained on a bootstrap sample of $63.2%$ of data, the remaining $36.8%$ out-of-bag samples act as an internal validation set, providing unbiased generalization error estimation without cross-validation overhead.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the OOB probability exactly 1/e as N increases?

From calculus: $lim_{N o infty} (1 - 1/N)^N = e^{-1} approx 0.3678794412$.
