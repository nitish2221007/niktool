Steel Density in Petroleum Units (65.5 ppg)

Because structural drill pipe steel weighs 490 lbs/cu ft (65.5 ppg), drilling mud floats and supports a significant fraction of heavy drill strings, reducing derrick mast loads and rig tension.

Frequently asked questions

What is the buoyed weight of a 300,000 lb drill string in 13.1 ppg mud?

BF = 1 - (13.1 / 65.5) = 0.80; Buoyed hook load = 300,000 × 0.80 = 240,000 lbs (60,000 lbs buoyed reduction).
