Frequently asked questions

Why is electron Larmor radius so much smaller than ion Larmor radius?

Because electrons have 3,600× smaller mass than deuterons ($r_L propto sqrt{m}$), their gyroradius is under $50 mu ext{m}$, while deuterons trace larger $2 ext{ mm}$ orbits.
