Frequently asked questions

Why is Gate-All-Around (GAA) replacing FinFET at 2nm and below?

GAA nanosheets provide complete electrostatic gate control on all four sides of the channel, eliminating parasitic corner leakage and allowing continuous tuning of nanosheet width.
