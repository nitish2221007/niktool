Parametric Equalizer Audio Precision

Robert Bristow-Johnson's Audio EQ Cookbook formulas establish the mathematical link between filter sharpness Q and musical bandwidth: higher Q values narrow the filter to surgically remove resonant hum without affecting neighboring musical pitches.

What Q-factor corresponds to a 1.0 octave bandwidth?

Q = 1 / (2 · sinh(ln(2)/2)) ≈ 1.4142 (√2).
