Frequently asked questions

Why is Hydrogen-1 universally imaged in clinical MRI?

The human body is over $60%$ water ($H_2O$) and lipids, giving Hydrogen an enormous natural abundance and the highest gyromagnetic ratio of all non-radioactive stable nuclei.
