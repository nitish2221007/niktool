Alfred Marshall Midpoint Elasticity Formula

The midpoint formula ($ rac{Delta Q / Q_{ ext{avg}}}{Delta P / P_{ ext{avg}}}$) guarantees that elasticity is identical whether price increases or decreases, avoiding base-dependent percentage calculation distortions.

Frequently asked questions

Why is PED usually a negative number?

By the Law of Demand, price and quantity demanded move in opposite directions; economists frequently quote the absolute magnitude $|PED|$.
