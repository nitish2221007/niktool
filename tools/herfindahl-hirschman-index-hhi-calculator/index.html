US Department of Justice & FTC Merger Guidelines

Squaring market share percentages places greater mathematical weight on dominant large firms; mergers that increase HHI by more than 100-200 points in highly concentrated markets trigger antitrust regulatory challenges.

Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum possible HHI score?

A pure 100% monopoly market has an HHI of 100² = 10,000.
