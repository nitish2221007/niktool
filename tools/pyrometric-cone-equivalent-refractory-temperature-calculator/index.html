Pyrometric cones measure integrated "heat-work" (combined temperature + time sintering kinetics) rather than raw air temperature; ASTM C24 PCE tests classify furnace refractory bricks based on the cone number where the tip softens and bends to touch the plaque.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Cone 06 cooler than Cone 6?

In the Orton cone numbering system, the leading "0" denotes negative sequence (like negative numbers); Cone 06 ($999^circ ext{C}$) is much cooler than Cone 6 ($1,222^circ ext{C}$).
