  1. Enter circular duct internal diameter in millimeters (or converted inches).
  2. Enter design air volume flow rate in CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute).
  3. Enter straight duct run length in meters.
  4. Inspect total static air friction pressure loss in Pascals and inches of water gauge (in. w.g.), duct airflow velocity in FPM, and check compliance with the industry 0.08 - 0.10 in.w.g./100ft equal friction sizing standard.

Equal Friction Duct Sizing Method

ASHRAE recommends sizing commercial supply ducts to a constant pressure drop rate of 0.08 to 0.10 in. w.g. per 100 ft to balance fan motor electrical power consumption against duct fabrication capital cost.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if duct air velocity exceeds 1,500 FPM (7.6 m/s)?

Air velocities above 1,500 FPM cause turbulent boundary vortex shedding that generates loud rumble and whistling noise in office ceiling diffusers.
