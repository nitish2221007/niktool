Willebrord Snellius 1621 Optical Refraction Law

Snell's law ($n_1 sin heta_1 = n_2 sin heta_2$) governs the bending of light at optical boundaries; when light attempts to exit a denser medium at angles exceeding the critical angle ($ heta_1 > heta_c$), $100%$ of optical power is reflected with zero loss, enabling worldwide high-speed fiber-optic internet communication.

Frequently asked questions

What is Total Internal Reflection (TIR)?

TIR occurs when light traveling through an optically denser medium strikes a boundary with a less dense medium at an angle greater than $ heta_c = arcsin(n_2/n_1)$, completely trapping the light inside.
