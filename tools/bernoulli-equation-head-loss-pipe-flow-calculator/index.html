Daniel Bernoulli 1738 Energy Conservation Principle

Quantifies mechanical energy conservation in fluid streams ($P + rac{1}{2} ho v^2 + ho g z = ext{const}$), accounting for conversion between pressure, kinetic, gravitational potential, and frictional dissipation energies.

Frequently asked questions

What causes velocity head changes in pipe systems?

Changes in pipe cross-sectional diameter cause fluids to accelerate or decelerate (continuity equation $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$).
