Standard Atmospheric Lapse Rate (-6.5°C / km)

Atmospheric pressure drops exponentially with altitude following the hydrostatic equation and the standard lapse rate (0.0065 K/m), allowing precise barometric altimetry in aircraft and GPS smartwatches.
