Balance of Gravity and Centripetal Acceleration

A satellite in orbit is in continuous freefall toward Earth, but its horizontal tangential velocity (v = √(GM/r)) ensures the surface curves away beneath it at the exact same rate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the orbital speed of the International Space Station (ISS)?

At an altitude of ~400 km, the ISS orbits Earth at approximately 7.67 km/s (27,600 km/h), completing an orbit every 92 minutes.
