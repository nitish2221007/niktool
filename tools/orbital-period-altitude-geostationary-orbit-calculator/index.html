Arthur C. Clarke 1945 Geostationary Orbit Concept

At an altitude of exactly $35,786 ext{ km}$, a satellite's orbital period matches Earth's sidereal rotation ($23.93 ext{ hours}$), allowing stationary ground satellite TV dishes.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Geostationary Orbit located strictly above the equator?

Because the orbital plane must pass through Earth's center of mass, only an equatorial orbit remains stationary over a single ground latitude ($0^circ$).
