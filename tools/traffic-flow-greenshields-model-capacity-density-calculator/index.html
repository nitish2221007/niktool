Bruce D. Greenshields 1935 Macroscopic Traffic Stream Model

Greenshields proved that flow follows a parabolic curve ($q = v_f k - rac{v_f}{k_j} k^2$); highway throughput peaks at half the free-flow speed ($v_{ ext{opt}} = rac{1}{2}v_f$) and half jam density ($k_{ ext{opt}} = rac{1}{2}k_j$), beyond which shockwaves cause congestion collapse.

Frequently asked questions

Why does flow decrease as density increases past k_opt?

When vehicle packing exceeds optimal spacing, drivers tap brakes, slowing average speed faster than the rate of density increase and cutting total flow.
