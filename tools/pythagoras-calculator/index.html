How to use Pythagoras Calculator

  1. Choose whether you are finding the hypotenuse or one of the other sides.
  2. Enter the two known sides.
  3. Click Process to find the missing side using the Pythagorean theorem.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator applies a² + b² = c² and rearranges it when solving for a non-hypotenuse side. It validates that the hypotenuse is the longest side when relevant, and runs fully locally.

Common Use Cases

Students use it for the Triangles chapter and right-triangle numericals across maths and science.
