Frank Sortino's Downside Asymmetry Focus

While Sharpe ratio penalizes large upward spikes as "volatility", the Sortino ratio isolates only negative downside drawdowns, providing a much more accurate evaluation for growth strategies with positive skew.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Sortino preferred over Sharpe for growth stocks and crypto?

Because large upward jumps are beneficial to investors, the Sortino ratio does not penalize positive upside volatility.
