First-Order Pharmacokinetic Clearance Dynamics

Half-life determines how long a drug remains in the body and dictates dosing intervals; approximately 5 half-lives ($97%$) are required to reach therapeutic steady state during continuous dosing.

Frequently asked questions

How many half-lives are required to eliminate a drug from the body?

After 5 half-lives, $96.875%$ of the drug is eliminated; after 7 half-lives, over $99%$ is cleared.
