How to use Rankine Lateral Earth Pressure (K_a & K_p) & Retaining Wall Thrust Calculator

  1. Enter retaining wall vertical stem height H in meters.
  2. Enter retained backfill soil unit weight $gamma$ in $ ext{kN/m}^3$ (typically 17 to 20 $ ext{kN/m}^3$).
  3. Enter soil internal friction angle $phi$ in degrees (typically 28° to 36° for granular soils).
  4. Inspect Rankine active coefficient $K_a$, at-rest $K_0$, passive resistance coefficient $K_p$, total lateral horizontal active thrust $P_a$ in kN/m, and overturning moment about the toe.

William John Macquorn Rankine 1857 Earth Pressure Theory

Rankine assumes cohesionless soil in plastic equilibrium; as a retaining wall yields slightly outward ($0.001 H$), active earth pressure ($K_a$) drops to its minimum state, providing the baseline lateral load for designing reinforced concrete cantilever and gravity retaining walls.

Frequently asked questions

Why is active pressure (Ka) so much smaller than passive pressure (Kp)?

Active failure occurs when soil expands outward along slip planes, while passive failure requires compressing the entire soil mass against internal shear resistance.
