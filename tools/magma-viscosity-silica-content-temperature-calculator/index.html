Silica Polymerization & Explosive Volcanism

Higher silica ($SiO_2$) content forms dense interconnected silicon-oxygen tetrahedral polymer networks, increasing magma viscosity by over six orders of magnitude ($10^2 o 10^8 ext{ Pa}cdot ext{s}$) and trapping dissolved volcanic gases until explosive catastrophic fragmentation occurs.

Frequently asked questions

Why is basaltic lava so fluid compared to rhyolite?

Basalt has low silica content and high eruptive temperatures (~1,200°C), making it fluid like warm honey compared to stiff rhyolite paste.
