  3. Click Calculate to inspect the dew point condensation temperature and mugginess rating.

Dew Point vs Relative Humidity

Dew point is the absolute temperature to which air must be cooled to become 100% saturated with water vapor. Unlike relative humidity (which changes with air temperature), dew point gives a true measure of atmospheric moisture.

Frequently asked questions

At what dew point does air feel muggy?

Dew points above 18 °C (65 °F) feel noticeably sticky and humid to humans.
