Frequently asked questions

What is the "10-degree rule" in chemistry?

As an empirical approximation for reactions with $E_a approx 50 ext{ kJ/mol}$, chemical reaction rate roughly doubles for every 10°C rise in temperature ($Q_{10} approx 2$).
