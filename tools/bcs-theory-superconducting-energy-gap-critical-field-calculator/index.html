Below the critical temperature ($T_c$), electron-phonon lattice interactions bind conduction electrons into zero-spin Cooper pairs that condense into a macroscopic quantum ground state protected by an energy gap ($2Delta_0 = 3.53 k_B T_c$), permitting infinite DC electrical current with zero electrical resistance.

Frequently asked questions

Why does magnetic field destroy superconductivity?

Magnetic fields exert Lorentz forces that penetrate the superconducting wavefunction, breaking Cooper pairs when the magnetic energy density ($ rac{1}{2}mu_0 H^2$) exceeds the superconducting condensation energy.
