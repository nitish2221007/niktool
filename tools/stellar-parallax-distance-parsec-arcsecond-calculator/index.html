1 Parsec ("parallax of one second") is the distance at which 1 Astronomical Unit subtends an angle of 1 arcsecond ($1 ext{ pc} = 206,265 ext{ AU} approx 3.26 ext{ light-years}$), forming the first rung of the cosmic distance ladder.

Frequently asked questions

What is the precision of the ESA Gaia astrometry mission?

The Gaia space telescope measures stellar parallaxes with microarcsecond precision ($mu ext{as}$), mapping over 1.8 billion stars across the Milky Way.
