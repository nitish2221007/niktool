Sir Godfrey Hounsfield 1979 Nobel Prize CT Scale

Calibrates X-ray attenuation on a standardized scale: Air is defined as $-1000 ext{ HU}$, Water as $0 ext{ HU}$, and dense cortical bone as $+1000 ext{ to }+3000 ext{ HU}$, enabling automated tissue segmentation.

Frequently asked questions

What is CT Windowing (Window Width and Level)?

Human eyes can distinguish only $sim 30$ shades of gray; windowing maps a narrow HU range (e.g. Width 350, Level 40 for soft tissue) to full display brightness to maximize tumor contrast.
