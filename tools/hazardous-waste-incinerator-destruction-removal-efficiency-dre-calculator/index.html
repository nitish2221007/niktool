EPA Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) Standard

Enforces strict thermal destruction compliance ($99.99%$ for toxic organics and $99.9999%$ six-nines for PCBs and dioxins) in hazardous waste rotary kilns.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 3 Ts of successful incineration?

Temperature ($> 1100^circ ext{C}$), Time ($> 2.0 ext{ seconds}$ gas residence time), and Turbulence (thorough air-fuel mixing).
