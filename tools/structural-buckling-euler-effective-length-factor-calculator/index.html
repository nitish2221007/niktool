How to use Euler Column Buckling Critical Load (P_cr) & Slenderness Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter column unbraced physical length L in meters.
  2. Enter material Young's modulus of elasticity E in GPa (200 GPa for structural steel, 30 GPa for concrete, 70 GPa for aluminum).
  3. Enter minimum cross-sectional area moment of inertia $I_{min}$ in $ ext{cm}^4$.
  4. Select end boundary support conditions (Pinned-Pinned $K=1.0$, Fixed-Fixed $K=0.5$, Fixed-Pinned $K=0.7$, Fixed-Free $K=2.0$).
  5. Inspect Euler elastic critical buckling load $P_{ ext{cr}}$ in kN and kips.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the minimum moment of inertia (I_min) used in buckling calculations?

Columns buckle around the weakest principal geometric axis where resistance to bending is lowest.
