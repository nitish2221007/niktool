Christian Doppler 1842 Wave Frequency Shift Principle

Wavefronts compress together in front of a moving source ($lambda = (v - v_s)/f$) and stretch behind it, producing the characteristic high-pitch to low-pitch drop heard as emergency sirens pass by.
