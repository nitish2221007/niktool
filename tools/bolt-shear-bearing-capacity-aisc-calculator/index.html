How to use AISC High-Strength Bolt Shear & Bearing Strength Calculator

  1. Select high-strength bolt specification (ASTM A325 or A490 with threads included or excluded).
  2. Select nominal bolt shank diameter d (3/4", 7/8", 1").
  3. Enter connected steel plate thickness t in inches and shear plane condition (Single or Double shear).
  4. Inspect LRFD design bolt shear capacity (ϕR_n in kips / kN) and hole bearing tearout capacity.

AISC structural steel specifications define nominal shear stresses for high-strength bolts (F_nv = 54 ksi for A325-N, 68 ksi for A325-X); double shear connections double the shear load capacity per fastener.

Frequently asked questions

What is the LRFD design shear capacity of a 3/4" A325-N bolt in single shear?

ϕR_nv = 0.75 × 54 ksi × (π/4 × 0.75²) = 0.75 × 54 × 0.4418 ≈ 17.89 kips (79.6 kN).
