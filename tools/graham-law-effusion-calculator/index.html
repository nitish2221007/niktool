Molecular Mass and Kinetic Velocity

Because lighter molecules travel with higher root-mean-square kinetic velocity at the same thermal temperature (v_rms = √(3RT/M)), they effuse through porous barriers significantly faster.

Frequently asked questions

How much faster does Helium effuse compared to Oxygen?

Helium (4 g/mol) effuses √(32/4) = √8 ≈ 2.83 times faster than Oxygen (32 g/mol).
