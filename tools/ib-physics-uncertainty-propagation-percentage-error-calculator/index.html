IB Physics Internal Assessment (IA) Uncertainty Standard

In experimental science, uncertainties must always be propagated correctly: absolute uncertainties add during addition and subtraction ($Delta z = Delta x + Delta y$), while percentage fractional uncertainties add during multiplication and division ($ rac{Delta z}{z} = rac{Delta x}{x} + rac{Delta y}{y}$).

Frequently asked questions

Why do uncertainties add during subtraction (Z = X - Y)?

Errors and uncertainties always accumulate and can never cancel each other out; subtracting two numbers with uncertainty increases the relative fractional error.
