The Stefan-Boltzmann T⁴ Law on the HR Diagram

Because thermal radiant flux scales with the fourth power of temperature, hot O-type and B-type stars radiate hundreds of thousands of times more energy than cooler red dwarf stars.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Betelgeuse over 100,000× more luminous than the Sun despite being cooler?

Betelgeuse has a lower surface temperature (~3,500K), but its colossal supergiant radius provides an enormous surface area that overwhelms the T⁴ temperature difference.
