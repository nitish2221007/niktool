How to use Readability Score Calculator

  1. Paste your draft article, blog post, or school paper.
  2. Click Calculate to inspect the Flesch Reading Ease score and grade level.
  3. Aim for a score between 60 and 70 (8th–9th grade level) for public web content.

Why Readability Matters for SEO

Search engines and users prefer content that is easy to scan and digest. Scoring between 60 and 80 on Flesch Reading Ease reduces bounce rate and improves user engagement metrics.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good Flesch Reading Ease score for web blogs?

Scores between 60.0 and 70.0 are considered easily understood by 13- to 15-year-old students, making it ideal for general web audiences.
