The Central Mole Concept in Chemistry

The mole is the SI base unit connecting macroscopic measurable lab masses in grams with submicroscopic atom counts ($1 ext{ mole} = 6.022 imes 10^{23} ext{ particles}$), forming the essential bridge for all chemical reactions.
