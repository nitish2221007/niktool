Why Heat Pumps Deliver 300% to 400% Efficiency

Unlike resistive heaters that create heat directly from electric current (1 kW electric = 1 kW heat), a heat pump uses refrigeration compression to move ambient outdoor heat indoors, delivering 3 to 4 kW of warmth for every 1 kW of consumed electricity (COP = 3.0 - 4.0).

Frequently asked questions

What does a COP of 4.0 mean?

A COP of 4.0 means the heat pump outputs 4.0 Kilowatt-hours of heat energy into your home for every 1.0 Kilowatt-hour of electricity consumed.
