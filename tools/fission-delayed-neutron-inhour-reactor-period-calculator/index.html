Delayed Neutrons & Nuclear Reactor Controllability

Delayed neutrons emitted seconds after fission by precursor fragments (e.g. Br-87, I-137) lengthen the reactor response time from microseconds to tens of seconds ($T sim 60 ext{ s}$), making nuclear reactors safely controllable by mechanical motor-driven control rods.

Frequently asked questions

What is Prompt Critical ($1.00 Dollar Reactivity)?

When inserted reactivity equals the delayed neutron fraction ($ ho = eta$, or $1.00), the reactor goes critical on prompt neutrons alone without waiting for delayed neutrons, triggering explosive millisecond power spikes.
