Coaxial Power Transmission & High Power Density

Planetary gear trains share input torque across multiple planet gears simultaneously, delivering immense torque density, high efficiency (>95%), and coaxial shaft alignment in compact robotic actuators and automatic transmissions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the assembly rule for 3-planet gearboxes?

The sum of sun and ring teeth (N_s + N_r) must be evenly divisible by the number of planet gears so planets mesh symmetrically without binding.
