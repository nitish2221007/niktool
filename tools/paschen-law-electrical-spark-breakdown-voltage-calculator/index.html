Friedrich Paschen 1889 Townsend Avalanche Spark Law

Paschen's curve exhibits a universal "U-shaped" breakdown minimum ($sim 327 ext{ V}$ in air at $pcdot d approx 0.57 ext{ Pa}cdot ext{m}$); at ultra-low pressures (vacuum), electrons rarely collide with gas molecules, while at high pressures, frequent collisions prevent electrons from gaining ionization kinetic energy, requiring kilovolts to strike an arc.

Frequently asked questions

Why is SF6 used in high-voltage circuit breakers and switchgear?

Sulfur Hexafluoride ($SF_6$) is strongly electronegative, rapidly capturing free electrons to form heavy negative ions and delivering $3 imes$ higher dielectric breakdown strength than air.
