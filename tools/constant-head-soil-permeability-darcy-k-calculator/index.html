Henry Darcy 1856 Law of Seepage Flow

Constant-head permeameters test coarse granular soils where discharge is substantial; Darcy law relates volumetric flow rate to hydraulic gradient via Q = k · i · A · t.

Frequently asked questions

Why is a falling-head test used instead of constant-head for fine clay?

Clay hydraulic conductivity is so low that water volume collected in a constant head test is too tiny to measure accurately.
