How to use Mohr-Coulomb Soil Shear Strength (τ_f = c' + σ'·tan φ') Calculator

  1. Enter soil effective cohesion $c^prime$ in kPa.
  2. Enter effective angle of internal friction $phi^prime$ in degrees.
  3. Enter total normal overburden/foundation stress $sigma$ in kPa.
  4. Enter pore water pressure u in kPa.
  5. Inspect effective normal stress $sigma^prime = sigma - u$ and ultimate failure shear strength $ au_f$.

Mohr-Coulomb Failure Criterion & Effective Stress Principle

Water pressure in soil pores bears no shear stress; positive pore water pressure directly reduces effective normal contact stress ($sigma^prime = sigma - u$), triggering sudden slope landslides during heavy rainstorms.

What is the shear strength of clean sand (c = 0)?
