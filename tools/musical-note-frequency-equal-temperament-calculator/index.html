12-Tone Equal Temperament Semitone Ratio (2^(1/12))

Equal temperament divides the octave into 12 semitones, each spaced by a frequency factor of 2^(1/12) ≈ 1.059463, enabling instruments to play in all 12 musical keys without retuning.
