How to use Marks to Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter the marks you obtained in the first field.
  2. Enter the total maximum marks in the second field.
  3. Click Calculate Percentage to see your percentage instantly.

Key Features & Privacy

This simple calculator converts raw marks into percentage using the formula: (Marks Obtained / Total Marks) × 100. All processing happens in your browser, ensuring your academic data remains private.

Common Use Cases

Use this tool to calculate percentage for individual tests, assignments, exams, or any assessment where you know your marks and the total possible marks.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Enter your marks obtained and total marks, then click Calculate Percentage to see the result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser.

What if my marks are greater than total?

The tool will show an error. Marks obtained cannot exceed total marks.
