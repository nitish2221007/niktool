How to Use the Marks to Percentage Calculator

  1. Type the score or marks you obtained in the Marks Obtained field.
  2. Enter the maximum possible score in the Total Marks field (e.g. 100, 500, or 800).
  3. Click Calculate Percentage to instantly view your percentage formatted up to two decimal places.

Mathematical Formula for Marks Percentage

Calculating the percentage of obtained marks relative to maximum marks uses the fundamental proportion formula:

Percentage (%) = (Marks Obtained / Total Maximum Marks) × 100

Worked Examples

Here are two common scenarios demonstrating how exam scores convert to percentage:

  • Example 1 (Board Exam Total): A student scores 440 marks out of 500 total marks.
    Calculation: (440 / 500) × 100 = 0.88 × 100 = 88.00%
  • Example 2 (Class Test / Theory Paper): A student scores 67.5 marks out of 75 maximum marks.
    Calculation: (67.5 / 75) × 100 = 0.90 × 100 = 90.00%

Key Features & Privacy

NikTool's Marks to Percentage Calculator processes all computations strictly inside your web browser. No academic data or scores are ever transmitted to an external server or saved in databases. The utility features instant execution, clean output formatting, and works completely offline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate percentage from total marks?

Divide the marks obtained by the total maximum possible marks, then multiply the resulting decimal by 100.

Can I calculate percentage for decimal marks like 42.5 out of 50?

Yes! You can enter fractional or decimal marks obtained (e.g. 42.5) and any total maximum marks.

What if obtained marks are greater than total marks?

Under standard exam evaluations, obtained marks cannot exceed maximum marks. The tool alerts you if an invalid entry is detected.

Does this calculator save my test scores?

No. All calculations are executed strictly inside your local browser tab and never sent to any server.
