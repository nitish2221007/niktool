Method of Joints Static Equilibrium Principle

Because pin joints cannot transmit bending moments, every truss node is in concurrent coplanar static equilibrium ($sum F_x = 0, sum F_y = 0$), allowing straightforward determination of axial tension ties and compression struts in bridge trusses.

What is a Zero-Force Member in a truss?
