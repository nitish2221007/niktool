Official LSAC Credential Assembly Service (CAS) Standard

Unlike college transcripts that cap grades at 4.0, LSAC standardizes all undergraduate coursework across institutions, converting A+ grades into 4.33 quality points and recalculating punitive withdrawals to generate a universal CAS GPA reported to American Bar Association (ABA) law schools.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Does LSAC recalculate repeated courses?

Yes; LSAC includes all original and repeated college grades in your cumulative GPA, regardless of whether your undergraduate college used grade forgiveness.
