Why Oversizing DC Solar Arrays Boosts ROI

Solar arrays rarely operate at full 100% STC wattage due to thermal heat derates and low sun angles; oversizing DC panels by 20% to 35% (ILR 1.25 to 1.35) allows the inverter to reach peak capacity hours earlier in the morning and later in the afternoon.

Frequently asked questions

What is inverter clipping?

Clipping occurs when DC solar production exceeds the inverter's maximum AC capacity; the inverter throttles DC voltage away from the maximum power point to cap power safely.
