How to use HCF Calculator

  1. Enter two or more positive integers separated by commas.
  2. Click Process to compute the highest common factor.
  3. Copy the result if you need it for your working.

Key Features & Privacy

The calculator uses the Euclidean algorithm to find the HCF (also called GCD) of two or more integers, which is fast and reliable even for large numbers. It validates that all inputs are positive integers and runs entirely in your browser.

Common Use Cases

Students use it to check HCF answers in the Real Numbers chapter, simplify fractions, and solve word problems involving grouping or distribution.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this tool?

Simply paste or type your input in the input area, click Process, and copy your result.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all calculations happen locally in your browser without server uploads.
