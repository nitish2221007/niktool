Driving Nutrient Uptake via Transpiration

VPD represents the drying power of the air: maintaining VPD between 0.8 and 1.2 kPa ensures plant stomata stay open for continuous CO₂ assimilation and calcium nutrient uptake without causing dehydration stress.

Private browser-based processing

Frequently asked questions

What is ideal VPD for cannabis and vegetative crops?

0.8 to 1.1 kPa for vegetative growth, increasing to 1.2 to 1.5 kPa during late flower/fruiting.
