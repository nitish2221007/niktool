  2. Enter Strong Acid Cation (SAC) resin bed volume in Liters.

  4. Inspect total treated softened water volume in $ ext{m}^3$, Bed Volumes (BV), and required regeneration rock salt mass.

Cation Exchange Water Softening Stoichiometry

Exchanges dissolved scaling hardness ions ($ ext{Ca}^{2+}, ext{Mg}^{2+}$) for non-scaling sodium ($ ext{Na}^+$), protecting domestic plumbing boilers and industrial cooling towers from mineral scaling.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Bed Volume (BV)?

Bed Volume is the volume of water treated divided by the volume of resin media ($BV = V_{ ext{water}} / V_{ ext{resin}}$); typical softening runs achieve 300–600 BV.
