Svante Arrhenius 1889 Kinetic Activation Barrier

Chemical reactions require reactant molecules to possess sufficient kinetic collision energy to cross the transition state energy barrier ($E_a$); raising temperature exponentially increases the fraction of molecules with $E ge E_a$.

Frequently asked questions

Why do chemical reaction rates roughly double for every 10°C rise?

For typical activation energies (~50 to 80 kJ/mol), the Arrhenius exponential term yields a $Q_{10}$ temperature coefficient between 2.0 and 3.0.
