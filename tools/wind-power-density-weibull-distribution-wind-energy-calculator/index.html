Cubic Wind Velocity Energy Law

Because wind kinetic power scales with the cube of velocity ($P propto v^3$), a small $20%$ boost in wind speed increases harvestable energy by $72.8%$ ($1.2^3 = 1.728$), making hub height wind resource assessment critical.

Frequently asked questions

What is the theoretical maximum limit for wind turbine energy capture?

Albert Betz proved in 1919 that no wind turbine can capture more than $16/27 approx 59.26%$ of the kinetic energy in wind (the Betz limit).
