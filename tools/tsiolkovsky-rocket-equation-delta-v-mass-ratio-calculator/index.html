Konstantin Tsiolkovsky 1903 Rocket Equation Standard

The foundational law of astronautics governing orbital launch, lunar landing, and interplanetary space exploration by linking momentum conservation ($m , dv = -c , dm$) to exponential fuel mass requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Why is getting to Low Earth Orbit (LEO) so difficult?

LEO requires $Delta v approx 9.4 ext{ km/s}$ (including gravity/drag losses); with chemical propellants ($I_{ ext{sp}} approx 350 ext{s}$), over $90%$ of total liftoff weight must be pure propellant.
