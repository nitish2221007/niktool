How to use Airy Isostasy Mountain Crustal Root & Moho Depth Calculator

  1. Enter mountain topography elevation height h above sea level in kilometers.
  2. Enter standard undeformed continental crust reference thickness $T_0$ in km (typically ~30 to 35 km).
  3. Enter continental crust density $ ho_c$ in $g/ ext{cm}^3$ (typically 2.7 to 2.8 $g/ ext{cm}^3$).
  4. Enter upper mantle density $ ho_m$ in $g/ ext{cm}^3$ (typically 3.3 $g/ ext{cm}^3$).
  5. Inspect deep buoyant crustal root thickness and depth to the Mohorovičić seismic boundary.

Frequently asked questions

What is the root-to-height ratio for typical continental mountains?

Because $ ho_c / ( ho_m - ho_c) approx 2.75 / (3.30 - 2.75) approx 5.0$, every 1 km of mountain elevation requires approximately 5 km of deep subterranean crustal root.
