How to use GPS Dilution of Precision (GDOP / PDOP) Positioning Accuracy Calculator

  1. Enter 3D Position Dilution of Precision (PDOP) from GPS receiver NMEA stream.
  2. Enter Time Dilution of Precision (TDOP).
  3. Enter User Equivalent Range Error (UERE) in meters (typically ~2 to 5 meters for single-frequency GPS, <1 meter for dual-frequency RTK).
  4. Inspect total Geometric Dilution of Precision (GDOP) and calculated 3D real-time spatial positioning accuracy error in meters.

Satellite Constellation Tetrahedron Geometry

DOP measures the geometric distribution of visible satellites; when satellites are widely spaced across all four quadrants of the sky, the volume of the intersecting user tetrahedron is maximized, minimizing error amplification (low PDOP < 2.0).

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why is vertical GPS error (VDOP) always higher than horizontal error (HDOP)?

Because receivers cannot see satellites below the Earth horizon, the geometric lack of downward baseline angles naturally doubles vertical height uncertainty.
