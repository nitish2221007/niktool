How to use Reverse GST Calculator

  1. Enter the final gross billing amount that already includes GST.
  2. Select your applicable GST slab (5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%).
  3. Click Calculate to separate the pre-tax base price from the CGST/SGST taxes.

Reverse GST Formula

Extracting GST from an inclusive price uses the formula: Net Base Price = Gross Price / (1 + GST Rate / 100), and GST Tax = Gross Price - Net Base Price.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

How to calculate 18% reverse GST on ₹1,180?

₹1,180 / 1.18 = ₹1,000 Net Base Price and ₹180 GST (₹90 CGST + ₹90 SGST).
