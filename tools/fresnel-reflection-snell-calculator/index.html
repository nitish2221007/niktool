Augustin-Jean Fresnel's 1823 Wave Optics Equations

Fresnel formulas describe how light splits between reflection and refraction at optical interfaces; at Brewster's angle ($\theta_B = \arctan(n_2/n_1)$), p-polarized light transmits with 100% transmission and zero reflection.

Frequently asked questions

What is normal incidence reflection for glass (n = 1.5) in air?

$R = ((1.5 - 1) / (1.5 + 1))^2 = (0.5 / 2.5)^2 = 0.04 = 4.0%$ reflected light per surface.
