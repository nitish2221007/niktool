Earth Equatorial Bulge J₂ Orbital Dynamics

Earth's equatorial centrifugal bulge creates the $J_2$ gravitational perturbation that torques satellite orbital planes; tuning retrograde inclination ($i approx 97^circ ext{–}99^circ$) causes the orbital plane to precess eastwards by exactly $+0.9856^circ/ ext{day}$ ($360^circ/ ext{year}$), ensuring spy and weather satellites (Landsat, Sentinel) pass overhead at the exact same local solar time daily.

Frequently asked questions

Why must Sun-Synchronous Orbits be retrograde (i > 90°)?

Because $cos(i)$ must be negative to make the precession rate $dot{Omega} propto -cos(i)$ positive (eastward matching the Sun's apparent motion).
