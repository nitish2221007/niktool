Landscape Hyperfocal Focusing Technique

Focusing your camera at the exact hyperfocal distance (H) renders everything acceptably sharp from half the hyperfocal distance (H/2) all the way to infinity.

Frequently asked questions

What is Circle of Confusion (CoC)?

CoC is the maximum diameter of an optical blur circle on the sensor that the human eye still perceives as a sharp point in the final print.
