SEO Best Practices for URL Slugs

Clean, descriptive, hyphen-separated URL slugs improve click-through rates (CTR) in search engine result pages (SERPs) and provide clear keyword context for both crawlers and users.

Private browser-based processing

All calculations and conversions run 100% locally in your browser. No personal data or numbers are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.

Frequently asked questions

Why are hyphens preferred over underscores in URLs?

Google officially recommends hyphens (-) because their algorithms treat hyphens as word separators, whereas underscores (_) combine words together.
