Combustion Thermodynamics Equivalence Ratio

Equivalence ratio ($Phi = ext{AFR}_{ ext{stoich}}/ ext{AFR}$) normalizes fuel stoichiometry; race engines tune rich ($Phi approx 1.15 ext{–}1.25$) to suppress knock and lower exhaust valve temps, while lean-burn gas turbines operate lean ($Phi approx 0.5 ext{–}0.7$) to minimize thermal $NO_x$ emissions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Equivalence Ratio (Φ) and Lambda (λ)?

$Phi$ measures fuel excess ($Phi > 1$ is fuel-rich), while $lambda = 1/Phi$ measures air excess ($lambda > 1$ is air-rich / fuel-lean).
