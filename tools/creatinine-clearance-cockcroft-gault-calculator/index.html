  5. Inspect estimated creatinine clearance in mL/min and FDA renal drug dosing impairment classification.

Donald W. Cockcroft & M. Henry Gault 1976 Equation

Cockcroft-Gault remains the gold standard FDA regulatory formula for calculating patient renal clearance and adjusting antibiotic/chemotherapy dosages to prevent drug toxicity.

Frequently asked questions

Why is female clearance multiplied by 0.85?

Females have approximately 15% less lean skeletal muscle mass per kilogram than males, producing less endogenous baseline creatinine breakdown.
