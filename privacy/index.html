Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

At NikTool (accessible from https://niktool.in/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by NikTool and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via our Contact Page or by email at contact@niktool.in.

2. Browser-First Local Processing Architecture

NikTool is built with a privacy-first model. The vast majority of our utilities—including text converters, calculators, JSON formatters, PDF manipulation tools, and generators—execute 100% locally within your client browser using JavaScript.

  • Your input data (text, documents, JSON payload, numbers, passwords) is never uploaded or stored on any remote server.
  • Calculations and conversions happen in your browser memory and are discarded when you close or refresh the tab.

3. Log Files and Web Analytics

NikTool follows a standard procedure of using log files and Google Analytics (gtag.js). These logs log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and Version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamp
  • Referring/exit pages and click count

These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to https://niktool.in/ and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to NikTool and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Cookie Policy & Local Storage

NikTool uses cookies and browser localStorage solely for operational preferences (such as saving user theme choices or recent tool configurations locally). We do not sell, rent, or trade any cookie data.

6. Third Party Privacy Policies

NikTool's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

7. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. NikTool does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
